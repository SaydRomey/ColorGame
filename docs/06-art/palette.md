# 🎨 Color Codex

A reference for color-based NPC naming, hue families, 
representative palette values, etymology, pigments, and color-related worldbuilding.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Hex and RGB values for historical pigments and traditional color names 
> are representative digital approximations rather than universal standards. 
> 
> A historical color name may have several valid digital interpretations 
> depending on source, material, and context.

## Naming Principle

Real color names 
(especially rare, historical, pigment, mineral, textile, heraldic, botanical, 
and material names), can be used directly as character names, titles, or epithets.

Colors that sit between hue families are listed under **Cross-Hue Colors** 
so they can support nuanced affinities rather than being forced into a single category.

### Boundary Notes

- **Verdigris** — green or bluish patina formed on copper, brass, or bronze exposed to the atmosphere for long periods of time.
- **Periwinkle** — pale blue-lavender and naturally ambiguous between blue and purple.
- **Glaucous** — can describe bluish-gray, greenish-gray, or pale blue-green surfaces in plants and animals.
- **Indigo** — commonly treated as deep blue-violet.
- **Cyan** and **Turquoise** — sit strongly between blue and green.
- **Eburnean** and **Ivory** — useful at the yellow/white boundary.
- **Fulvous**, **Ocher**, **Tawny**, **Titian**, and **Amber** — bridge yellow/orange and brown.
- **Isabelline**, **Sarcoline**, and **Ashen** — useful as subdued neutral or near-achromatic affinities.

# Reference Conventions

- Treat historical Hex/RGB values as representative unless a game-specific canonical value is deliberately assigned.
- Keep cross-hue colors in their most useful primary family and list their secondary affinities under **Cross-Hue Colors**.
- Preserve alternate spellings, aliases, pigment names, and etymology when they add naming or worldbuilding value.
- Prefer attested color, pigment, mineral, textile, botanical, heraldic, and historical terms when naming characters.
- A color name can remain in the codex even when it is not currently assigned to an NPC.

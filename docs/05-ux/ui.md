## Core HUD (In-World & Diegetic)
The Prismwalker theming lends itself to **UI-as-magic** rather than flat overlays.  
The player should feel their HUD is *part of the shard*.

- **Health/Essence Bar:**
  - Represented as cracks in glass or a prism shard fragment in the corner.
  - As you take damage, cracks spread outward; healing causes them to fuse back together.
  - Adds tension when "near shattering."

- **Hue Binding Display:**
  - Orbiting gems or shards around a central prism icon (like the one in the Prismwalker’s chest).
  - Bound hues glow faintly, and the active hue pulses or refracts with movement.
  - When switching, shards rotate smoothly, like stained-glass turning in light.

- **Charge Resource (Hue Bar):**
  - A segmented bar beneath the shard that fills/drains with color.
  - Each cast temporarily tints the segment in that hue’s color before fading back to neutral.

## Menus & Inventory
Menus should feel like stepping into a memory echo instead of opening a spreadsheet.

- **Background:** Faint rippling light across a dark prism surface.
- **Sections:** Tabs represented by floating shard fragments that the player "rotates" through.
- **Item Icons:** Each relic/item sits on a circular sigil, tinted subtly to its hue alignment.
- **Lore & Echo Entries:** Fade in as whisper-text, with ghostly voices or subtle resonant tones when opened.

---

## Dialogue System
Displaying personality system through UI.

- **Dialogue Box Style:**
  - Semi-transparent stone/glass slab with faint refractions of the active hue(s).
  - If multiple hues influence personality, the text border pulses between them.
  - NPC dialogue could be tinted subtly by *their* regional hue.

- **Player Choices:**
  - Neutral options stay white/gray.
  - Hue-influenced options glow faintly in their respective color.
  - Higher affinity tier? The choice animates with more flourish (sparkles, vines, flames, etc.).

- **Subtle Personality Shift:** Maybe we could add small flourishes?
  - Witty Yellow lines curve like a flourish.
  - Red options appear with a sharper flicker.

---

## Combat UI
Combat is prismatic, so effects should be clear but not cluttered.

- **Skill Prompts:**
  - When a hue ability is ready, the icon glows faintly with "edge lighting."
  - On cast, it leaves a short-lived trail matching the hue (e.g., Red = ember sparks).

- **Enemy Targeting:**
  - Enemies weak to a hue shimmer with faint cracks in that color.
  - HUD hint (small shard fragment) vibrates when the correct counter-hue is equipped.

- **Ultimate (Prismatic Burst):**
  - UI "overcharges": shards orbit faster, prism icon refracts rainbow light.
  - When unleashed, the whole screen gets a subtle kaleidoscope ripple.

---

## Environmental/Puzzle UI

For Echo puzzles and shrine interactions:
  - **Echo Prompts:** Text/images distort and rearrange like shards of stained glass.
  - **Shrine Interaction:** Binding colors looks like slotting shards into a glowing circle, with the UI fracturing and resolving as hues "lock."
  - **Failure:** If you get it wrong, the menu/UI shatters briefly before reforming.

---

## Atmosphere & Accessibility
Tone should match the lore: subtle, crystalline, and otherworldly.

- **Sound Design in UI:**
  - Menu navigation = crystalline chimes.
  - Hue selection = low hum that rises in pitch with affinity.
  - Error/failure = muted crack or prism fracture sound.

- **Accessibility Options:**
  - Because hues are central, we should include **symbolic glyphs** for each color (not just color coding).
  - Example: 
    - Green = leaf glyph
    - Red = flame
    - Blue = wave
    - Purple = spiral
    - Yellow = sunburst
    - Brown = root.
  - Ensures clarity for colorblind players while still being thematic.

# 💎 Bound Hues in the Crystal Shard

If we want the Health / Essence Shard to also act as 
a visual indicator of the currently bound hues.
- The shard = Character's soul/core

## Single Hue Bound
- The shard glows in one solid color.
- Faint pixel “veins” radiate from the shard’s cracks in that hue.
- Example:
  - Red → ember flickers inside the crystal.
  - Green → moss-like glow, tiny motes drifting.
  - ...

---

## Dual Hues Bound
- The shard displays a split-color glow — diagonal or vertical division of two hues.
- Crack lines shift between the two, pulsing faintly.
- Example: Red + Green → half ember shimmer, half vine-like glow.

---

## Three Hues Bound
- The shard glows with a triangular prismatic fracture pattern, each section representing a hue.
- When charges are spent, the active hue pulses outward brighter in its “slice.”
- Between casts, a faint prismatic shimmer runs across the crystal surface.

---

## Diegetic Tie-In
- The health cracks and bound hue glow work together:
  - As you lose health, cracks spread, distorting the hues.
  - At low health, colors bleed into each other → prism feels unstable.

---

> [!TIP]  
> This way, the top-left crystal shard is both our health AND our identity display. 
> It stays minimalistic but feels alive and thematic.

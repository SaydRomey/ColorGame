# 🌌 UI in the Shard-Pixel Style

> [💎 See Bound Hues in the Crystal Shard ►](ui-crystal-shard/.md)

## Health / Essence Bar
- Instead of a sleek bar, show a cracked shard in the top-left corner.
- As health drops, more cracks spread and the glow inside dims.
- At critical HP, the shard flickers as if about to shatter.
- Pixel-art cracks animate subtly — pulsing when healing.

---

## Hue Binding Display
- In the bottom-right corner, a prism shard core sits, with up to 3 smaller gems orbiting it.
- Active hue: pulses/glows stronger.
- Switching hues makes the orbiting gem slide smoothly into the core (like a ritual binding).
- Pixel-style rotation animation: like a wheel of stained-glass shifting frame by frame.

---

## Hue Bar (Charges)
- A segmented horizontal shard bar sits just above the Hue Core.
- Each segment fills with the cast hue’s color, then fades back toward neutral gray.
- Drained bar looks dull/ashen.
- Refill animation: colors "drip" in from the shard core at the bottom-right.

---

## Dialogue & Choices
- Dialogue box = stone slab UI with faint cracks.
- Neutral text = pale white.
- Hue-tinted choices = glowing pixel fonts (Green vines flicker, Red sparks drop, Blue drips, etc.).
- At higher affinity tiers, glow intensifies (e.g., animated flame or fractal prism shimmer around the text).

---

## In-Game Feel
- HUD should fade when idle (so the player mostly sees the environment and character glow).
- Combat/UI interactions flare with color: health shard shakes when hit, hue gems flare on cast.
- Keep everything diegetic-feeling, as if the Prismwalker perceives their own HUD through the shard.

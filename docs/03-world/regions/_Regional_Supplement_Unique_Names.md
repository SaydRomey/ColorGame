## Optional integration notes

- map labels below the major-city tier
- throwaway dialogue anchors so local speech does not point only to named quest hubs
- rumor destinations for hidden caches, shrinelets, omen events, and Echo fragments
- route labels for ferries, funeral roads, shade-lines, battlefield corridors, and hazard passages
### Where these names are most useful
- as map labels to make regions feel inhabited below the quest tier
- as throwaway dialogue anchors so NPCs do not all point only to major cities and boss zones
- as rumor locations for hidden caches, shrines, Echo fragments, or regional micro-events
- as wayfinding labels for traversal routes, ferries, shade lines, funeral roads, and omen sites

### Best uses for the lesser NPCs
These work best when they:
- speak only one or two memorable sentences
- echo the myths-and-omens material in local dialect and practical speech
- recur as witnesses, workers, attendants, and mourners rather than as plot engines
- make each region feel socially inhabited, not just mythically staged
### Best use of the lesser NPCs
These work best when they:
- deliver one or two strong region-defining sentences
- echo mythic material in altered local wording
- appear repeatedly in ambient spaces rather than anchoring full quest trees
- make the world feel observed by ordinary people, not just by named champions and relic bearers

# Payne’s Shade

**Region:** 🔵 Iriseld — Wisdom, storms, sorrow, mystery  
**Role:** Painter of illusions and forger of souls; dreams in blue-gray landscapes  
**Visual:** Somber artist in tattered smocks of storm-hue

## Image References

## Dialogue
> Illusion needs a frame... and a witness.
> Every masterpiece begins in gray.
> What you see isn’t false — it’s just unfinished.
> I’ve painted gods, liars, and myself. Only the canvas knows who’s real.

## Contextual Lines
**While casting a large-scale illusion:**
> The world is easier to fix when you paint over it first.

## Key Item
*Payne’s Brush* – Artist’s brush made from shadow-hair; paints illusions that persist for an hour.

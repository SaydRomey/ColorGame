# Glaucous Wing

**Region:** 🔵 Iriseld — Wisdom, storms, sorrow, mystery  
**Role:** Avian shapeshifter who guards the misty cliffs; feathers mimic sea-gray skies  
**Visual:** Mist-cloaked guardian with wind-slicked feathers

## Dialogue
> Wings are for silence, not escape.
> The sky mourns every fall — listen.

> Feathers are liars. The wind is honest.

> Hold tight. The clouds bite when you hesitate.

# Falu Homesteader

**Region:** 🔴 Vireya — Passion, war, sacrifice, rebellion  
**Role:** Stubborn defender of the Red Timber Towns; wields copper tools as weapons  
**Visual:** Grizzled builder whose hammer sings defense

> This land raised me. I won’t leave until it dies. And maybe not then.

*Falu Charm* – House-shaped pendant from red timber; wards off undead and snow spirits.

## 🔴 Vireya — Additional NPCs

### Sanguine Quartermaster
**Role / Description:**  
A severe keeper of battlefield ledgers, ration vaults, oath-stores, 
and the names of the not-yet-buried. 
They do not fight for glory; they fight against waste. 
To them, every war is measured in what still needs carrying.

**Visual Concept:**  
Broad-shouldered, wrapped in layered crimson-and-brown military cloth, 
with iron hooks, bone tally charms, and scrolls lacquered against ash. 
Their belt carries wax-sealed ledgers and ration knives instead of medals.

**Core Dialogue:**  
- “Victory is a poor word. Count the mouths left after it.”  
- “The dead are expensive. The living even more so.”  
- “Every promise made in battle becomes someone else’s inventory.”

**Interaction / Triggered Line:**  
_When shown a war relic or battlefield token:_  
- “Ah. Unpaid blood. I can tell by the way it still thinks it belongs to someone.”

**Key Item:** **Sanguine Ledger**  
A chained war-book that records blood debts, battlefield obligations,
 and the names of those denied proper burial. 
 In the right hands, it can reveal 
 which revenants still rise because something is owed.

### Rustbanner Child
**Role / Description:**  
A scavenger-guide raised among wrecked standards and battlefield refuse. 
They know where banners still whisper, where old fires sleep beneath ash,
 and which roads the dead still march at dusk.

**Visual Concept:**  
Lean, smoke-darkened, wearing a patchwork cloak 
stitched from banner scraps and saddlecloth. 
One arm carries strips of red cloth braided into lucky knots. 
Their boots are mismatched and quick.

**Core Dialogue:**  
- “If it still flaps, don’t touch it.”  
- “Most flags are lies. The old ones just lie louder.”  
- “I don’t pray in Vireya. I duck.”

**Interaction / Triggered Line:**  
_When entering a battlefield or banner road with them nearby:_  
- “Heads low. If the cloth starts turning toward you, it remembers your kind.”

**Key Item:** **Rustbanner Pin**  
A tarnished insignia fashioned from banner iron and stitched cloth. 
Wearing it can briefly mark the bearer 
as tactically irrelevant to lesser banner-bound spirits.

### Cauterist Nun
**Role / Description:**  
A battlefield sister who seals wounds with heat, prayer, 
and deliberate cruelty. 
Her mercy is real, but never gentle. 
She believes survival is often uglier than death.

**Visual Concept:**  
Black-red robes scorched at the sleeves, 
a veil pinned back with surgical bronze, 
carrying a censer-like cautery lamp on a chain. 
Her hands are scarred from repeated ritual burns.

**Core Dialogue:**  
- “Hold still. Pain is just the body admitting it wants to live.”  
- “Mercy that leaves no mark is usually a lie.”  
- “The cleanest healing in Vireya smells like burning.”

**Interaction / Triggered Line:**  
_When the player is low on health or marked by flame damage:_  
- “I can close that. I did not say I could spare you the memory of it.”

**Key Item:** **Cauter Bell**  
A bronze hand-bell used in triage rites. 
Its tone stills bloodflow, suppresses panic, and binds a fresh wound shut
—at the cost of leaving a temporary vow-mark on the body.

### Ferric Standard-Breaker
**Role / Description:**  
A former rebel specialist trained to drag, desecrate, 
or unmake cursed standards before they raised the dead. 
Blunt, exhausted, and politically dangerous, 
they are tolerated only because they are useful.

**Visual Concept:**  
Wears stripped-down half-plate with broken insignia filed smooth. 
Carries an iron grappling hook, long polearm haft, 
and chain loops designed for tearing banners out of walls, corpses, or shrine stone.

**Core Dialogue:**  
- “You don’t kill a banner. You embarrass it in front of the dead.”  
- “Every war says it’ll end after one more symbol.”  
- “If a standard is holy, why does it scream when I pull?”

**Interaction / Triggered Line:**  
_When near a Bannerbound Revenant or war shrine:_  
- “There. The cloth is the spine. Break that, and the rest remembers it can fall.”

**Key Item:** **Ferric Hook**  
A heavy hooked tool used to drag standards, relic poles, 
and chained shrine objects out of place. 
Especially useful against banner-bound entities and symbolic anchors.

### Poppy Funeral Mother
**Role / Description:**  
A keeper of martyr gardens and funeral bloom-fields. 
She tends the flowers that grow where names were spoken last, 
and claims that some red petals open only when the dead are listening.

**Visual Concept:**  
Older woman in soot-red mourning dress, hands always dusted with seed ash. 
Carries a shallow wicker basket of black soil and scarlet poppy heads. 
Her shawl is embroidered with funeral knots.

**Core Dialogue:**  
- “The flowers don’t care whether the dead were right. Only whether they were loved.”  
- “Step carefully. Some graves are only pretending to be gardens.”  
- “Nothing grows faster in Vireya than a story watered with blood.”

**Interaction / Triggered Line:**  
_When shown ashes, remains, or a battlefield relic:_  
- “I can plant that. Whether it blooms mercy or accusation is not mine to promise.”

**Key Item:** **Poppy Ash Satchel**  
A pouch of funerary ash and poppy seed used in memorial rites. 
Scattered over a battlefield, it can calm some war echoes 
or reveal where someone truly fell.

## 🌊 Iriseld — Additional NPCs

### Harbor Resonant
**Role / Description:**  
A master tuner of docks, bell-lines, bridge-songs, 
and storm moorings in Zaffran Port. 
They keep the city from drifting into ruin one note at a time.

**Visual Concept:**  
Dressed in layered blue workrobes threaded with silver chimes and tuning forks. 
They wear thick gloves for glass tension work 
and carry resonance rods across their back like bundled reeds.

**Core Dialogue:**  
- “A bridge is only architecture pretending not to be music.”  
- “Storms are rude, but they are rarely off-key.”  
- “Most collapses begin with one note no one thought mattered.”

**Interaction / Triggered Line:**  
_When near a resonance bridge, bell tower, or unstable platform:_  
- “Listen first. The wrong step is usually audible before it is visible.”

**Key Item:** **Resonant Fork**  
A tuned metal fork that stabilizes or retunes local sound structures. 
Useful for reinforcing harmonic bridges, exposing sonic traps, 
or briefly quieting unstable resonance fields.

### Undertide Ferryman
**Role / Description:**  
A boatman of impossible routes who ferries mourners, relics, 
and sealed cargo through unstable air-tide passages. 
He is said to know which paths the storm respects and which it merely tolerates.

**Visual Concept:**  
Tall, weathered, in indigo waterproof layers with glass-bead prayer cords. 
His skiff is hung with small bells that never ring unless he permits it. 
One eye is clouded white.

**Core Dialogue:**  
- “Water is honest. Sky only imitates it when grieving.”  
- “I don’t take everyone across. Some people are heavier than what they’re carrying.”  
- “If the mist asks your name, let me answer for you.”

**Interaction / Triggered Line:**  
_When offered a relic, grave token, or passage request:_  
- “This will cross. Whether you cross with it is a more expensive question.”

**Key Item:** **Undertide Token**  
A carved passage-marker accepted by ferrymen and some threshold spirits. 
Grants safe transit across a single unstable route 
or preserves footing during one collapse event.

### Saltglass Embalmer
**Role / Description:**  
A quiet craftsperson who preserves the belongings of the sky-lost: journals, 
locks of hair, bells, letters, and fractured glider pieces. 
They do not embalm bodies so much as absences.

**Visual Concept:**  
Pale blue-gray apron over simple mourning attire, wearing sea-salt crystal lenses. 
Their workshop is full of tiny sealed cases, ribbon tags, 
and relic trays lined with powdered glass.

**Core Dialogue:**  
- “The drowned leave water. The fallen leave silence.”  
- “I preserve what cannot be recovered, which is not the same as bringing it back.”  
- “Loss requires good shelving.”

**Interaction / Triggered Line:**  
_When shown a personal token or relic tied to someone missing:_  
- “Ah. This still has a voice in it. Not a loud one, but enough.”

**Key Item:** **Saltglass Locket**  
A transparent memorial capsule that can hold the echo of one voice, 
phrase, or emotional tone. 
Often used in remembrance rites and resonance puzzles.

### Bluewake Cartographer
**Role / Description:**  
A navigator who maps routes visible only during specific storms, 
moon phases, or reflection conditions. 
Their charts are part geography, part listening exercise.

**Visual Concept:**  
Lightly armored traveler with rolled translucent maps in lacquered tubes. 
Their cloak is lined with weather script, 
and their boots are fitted with grip hooks for glass or wet ledges.

**Core Dialogue:**  
- “A map is just a promise made to a landscape. Most landscapes break them.”  
- “The shortest route is usually the one that appears only when you stop looking straight at it.”  
- “Storms redraw borders more honestly than kings do.”

**Interaction / Triggered Line:**  
_When entering a new weather state or reflective zone:_  
- “There. Don’t stare. The route’s shy if it thinks you’re desperate.”

**Key Item:** **Bluewake Chart**  
A weather-sensitive route map showing temporary aerial paths, 
reflection bridges, and safe currents during specific atmospheric conditions.

### Hollow Chanter
**Role / Description:**  
A singer whose lungs and throat were altered by years of inhaling 
upward rain and storm resonance.  
Their voice no longer belongs entirely to them, which makes it useful.

**Visual Concept:**  
Thin, elegant figure with translucent rain-scar tissue at the throat. 
Wears layered cerulean scarves and carries 
a hollow reed instrument carved with weather-script.

**Core Dialogue:**  
- “My voice came back wrong. Iriseld called that an improvement.”  
- “Some storms don’t want silence. They want accompaniment.”  
- “Do not ask me to sing anything that still belongs to the living.”

**Interaction / Triggered Line:**  
_When a wraith shrieks or a resonance mechanism destabilizes:_  
- “Now? Good. I only know how to answer things that are already breaking.”

**Key Item:** **Hollow Reed**  
A weather-hollowed reed pipe that emits a note capable of revealing 
false reflections or briefly silencing one shriek-based enemy effect.

### Sandbroker Widow
**Role / Description:**  
A water-rights negotiator and caravan broker whose influence runs through rumor, 
scarcity, and grace under public pressure. 
She never raises her voice because the desert already has one.

**Visual Concept:**  
Gold-and-umber layered silks with tightly wrapped desert veil, 
jeweled fingers stained with ink and dust. 
Carries sealed trade cords and tiny water measures at her belt.

**Core Dialogue:**  
- “In Luxeran, thirst is a kind of law.”  
- “People say I sell water. I sell tomorrow.”  
- “A fair bargain is one both parties resent equally.”

**Interaction / Triggered Line:**  
_When asking for passage, water, or trade access:_  
- “Of course I can help. The only real question is which future you’re prepared to spend.”

**Key Item:** **Broker’s Knot**  
A trade cord tied in sanctioned forms. 
It can seal a temporary bargain or bind one spoken promise until sunset.

### Shade Architect
**Role / Description:**  
A designer of mobile awnings, shadow courts, and timed sanctuaries. 
In a land of punishing light, they are as important as any priest.

**Visual Concept:**  
Practical sun-shield layers in pale saffron and brown, 
carrying folded shade rods and mirrored drafting slats. 
Their hands are callused from rope work rather than pen work.

**Core Dialogue:**  
- “You can tell what a city worships by where it puts its shadows.”  
- “A good shade line saves more lives than a sermon.”  
- “Sunlight reveals. Shade decides what can survive the revelation.”

**Interaction / Triggered Line:**  
_When in a timed light puzzle or direct sun hazard:_  
- “Wait. The wall is about to tell you where mercy falls.”

**Key Item:** **Shade Compass**  
A directional instrument that predicts where protective or truth-revealing 
shadow will fall in sun-timed spaces.

### Goldtongue Appraiser
**Role / Description:**  
A celebrated evaluator of motives who reads desire faster than speech. 
They are charming, hard to trust, and often correct in ways that feel invasive.

**Visual Concept:**  
Elegant traveler’s wear in gold, rust, and cream, 
with rings set with tiny mirrored lenses. 
Their smile is famous and widely considered dangerous.

**Core Dialogue:**  
- “Honesty is overrated. Want is much easier to measure.”  
- “People only lie for three reasons: hunger, vanity, or memory.”  
- “Show me what you hope this is worth, and I’ll tell you what it really is.”

**Interaction / Triggered Line:**  
_When shown a relic or during negotiation:_  
- “No, no—don’t tell me what it does. Tell me what you need it to mean.”

**Key Item:** **Goldtongue Lens**  
A fine appraisal lens that reveals what a target most desires 
in the present moment, whether person, relic, or beast.

## 🤎 Auralith — Additional NPCs

### Grave Dust Miller
**Role / Description:**  
A processor of sacred ash, powdered bone, 
and funerary dust used in sealing rites, medicine, and truth work. 
Their mill is half shrine, half workshop.

**Visual Concept:**  
Ash-coated practical robes, leather face-wrap, and broad work gloves. 
Their hair and lashes are permanently dusted pale from years of labor.

**Core Dialogue:**  
- “Everything becomes powder eventually. My trade is deciding what kind.”  
- “Do not call it dirt. Dirt forgets where it came from.”  
- “Truth grinds slowly in Auralith.”

**Interaction / Triggered Line:**  
_When shown remains, residue, or disturbed burial matter:_  
- “Yes. This has been mixed with something false. I can hear it in the texture.”

**Key Item:** **Dust Sieve**  
A ritual sieve used to separate true remains from false residue, 
contamination, or planted evidence.

### Reliquary Mason
**Role / Description:**  
A builder of testimony walls, vault seals, memory-lock doors, and reliquary thresholds. 
They think in pressure, stone, and what truths a structure is allowed to carry.

**Visual Concept:**  
Heavy work harness over dust-brown robes, chalk-marked hands, 
and stone measuring strings hanging at the waist. 
Their tools are geometric, severe, and reverently maintained.

**Core Dialogue:**  
- “Every wall keeps two stories: what it holds, and what it fears being asked.”  
- “A bad tomb is just a rumor with masonry.”  
- “Stone is honest. It only lies when ordered to.”

**Interaction / Triggered Line:**  
_When near a sealed vault, blocked passage, or memory-lock wall:_  
- “This wasn’t built to keep people out. It was built to keep an answer from becoming too convenient.”

**Key Item:** **Mortar Seal**  
A dense binding compound used in memorial architecture. 
It can close an active haunt-path or open one sealed wall 
if fed the correct memory condition.

### Bone Orchard Keeper
**Role / Description:**  
A tender of memorial groves grown from ash-rich soil and funerary remains. 
They cultivate trees, bone flowers, 
and quiet places for truths too difficult for stone.

**Visual Concept:**  
Soft brown gardening robes layered with mourning beads and pruning hooks. 
Their orchard basket holds pale seeds, bone-white blossoms, 
and tags etched with names.

**Core Dialogue:**  
- “Some grief grows better in sunlight than underground.”  
- “The orchard is for names that need roots, not walls.”  
- “Even Auralith allows beauty, provided it remembers the dead correctly.”

**Interaction / Triggered Line:**  
_When given remains, a memorial token, or a forgotten name:_  
- “Plant it, then. If it blooms, the land agrees it belonged to someone.”

**Key Item:** **Orchard Spindle**  
A funerary planting tool that can cause one memorial bloom to open, 
often revealing hidden residue trails or consent-based spirit paths.

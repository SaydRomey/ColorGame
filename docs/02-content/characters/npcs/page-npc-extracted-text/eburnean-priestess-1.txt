EBURNEAN PRIESTESS

IVORY-CLAD ORACLE
WHO READS THE
CRACKS IN BONE AND TUSK

In the sun-bleached temples of Luxeran, the Eburnean Priestess listens not to gods, but to bone.
She reads the whispers in ivory and the echoes in chance, seeing patterns where others see nothing.

Her truths are never simple, but they are always honest.
Fortune is not given. It is wagered.

THEY SPEAK IN WHISPERS

“Ivory cracks
from pressure, not time.”

“A roll of the dice
echoes louder than a prayer.”

“I don’t predict fate.
I flirt with it.”

“The bones say you’re
lucky—or very stubborn.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When asked for a prophecy:

“Close your eyes.
If you see something... that’s mine.”

THE PRIESTESS’S NATURE

KEEPER OF QUIET TRUTHS
Sees beyond noise, finding patterns in what others ignore.

CHANCER OF FATES

READER OF BONE
Every crack, curve, and chip tells a story only she can hear.

TRICKSTER’S WISDOM
Uses riddles and games to reveal what blunt words cannot.

GUIDE OF POSSIBILITIES

only the crossroads.

KEY ITEM

EBURNEAN DICE

Carved from ivory tusks, used to gamble for fate—
win or lose a memory.

Rolls reveal truths, chances, and consequences.

Risk a memory to gain insight or advantage.

Winning may grant power.
Losing may erase the past.

Memories lost cannot be recovered.

“Fortune is carved.
But memory is the price.”

RITUALS OF DIVINATION

BONE READING
Studies the fractures in bone and tusk to see hidden futures.

DICE DIVINATION
Rolls the sacred dice to uncover chances, omens, and wagers.

SUN INTERPRETATION
Reads the patterns of light and shadow to mark the day’s mood.

ECHOES IN SILENCE
Listens to the quiet to hear what fate refuses to say aloud.

TUSK ORACLES
Polishes and casts ivory charms to seek guidance.

WHERE TO FIND HER

THE IVORY SANCTUM
A sunlit temple built from bleached stone and carved bone.

THE BAZAAR OF SHADES
Her curtained stall appears where choices tangle like threads.

THE CROSSROADS OF LIGHT
Where five roads meet—
she sits between them at dusk.

“THE FUTURE IS A GAME.
PLAY CAREFULLY, OR IT PLAYS YOU.”

She says she was once a princess
who gambled with kings and lost everything but her sight.

Others say she was born from
a tusk that fell from the sky,
whispering riddles.

She remembers none of it.
Only the patterns.

And the price of truth.

HARLEQUIN TRICKBLADE

MASKED JESTER-ASSASSIN;
STRIKES WITH CHAOTIC GLEE IN CARNIVAL-GREEN ATTIRE

A laugh. A flash. Then silence.
He appears when folly reigns and vanishes before sense returns.

Some call him a spirit of misrule.
Others swear he’s fate’s jester—mocking gods and men alike.

He dances on the edge between madness and mastery, turning confusion into a blade and each performance into a kill.

THEY SPEAK IN WHISPERS

“Chaos is the only pattern sharp enough to cut fate.”

“I laugh when death forgets its cue.”

“Every stab’s a joke with a punchline in blood.”

“Catch me? You’re already part of the act.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When disappearing mid-battle:

“Guess who?”
“Wrong answer!”

THE TRICKSTER’S TENETS

EMBRACE CHAOS
Order blinds.
Chaos reveals.

WATCH. MOCK. STRIKE.
Observe all.
Judge none.
End swiftly.

THE JOKE IS THE BLADE
Laughter disarms.
The unexpected kills.

NEVER REPEAT
A pattern predicted is a pattern survived.

EVERYTHING IS THE ACT
You are the audience.
You are the actor.

You are already deceived.

KEY ITEM

HARLEQUIN MASK

Jester’s enchanted visage; wearer can mimic any voice and dodge fate once per day.

Mimics any voice perfectly.

Creates flawless disguises.

Once per day, avoids death by fate’s design.

Resets after sunrise.

“Wear a thousand faces,
but hide the one they seek.”

TRICKBLADE

HIDDEN BLADES

JESTER’S TINCTURES

WHERE TO FIND HIM

OVERGROWN CARNIVALS
Abandoned festival grounds where joy rotted.

MASKED MARKETS
Where hidden fights are waged behind laughter.

TWILIGHT GROVES
Sacred woods where fools speak to spirits.

THE WORLD IS A STAGE,
AND I WRITE THE ENDING IN GREEN INK.

You saw me.
You didn’t.

You heard me.
You understood something else.

Good.

The truth is heavy.
Laughter floats.

I choose the lightest blade and the loudest laugh.

Remember this act when the stars change and names are forgotten.
I’ll be somewhere you didn’t look.

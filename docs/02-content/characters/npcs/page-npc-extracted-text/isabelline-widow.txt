AURALITH
MEMORY • DECAY • SILENCE • ALCHEMY

ISABELLINE WIDOW

WANDERER IN SUN-BLEACHED CLOTHES;
SAID TO NEVER WASH UNTIL VENGEANCE IS SERVED

She walks the forgotten roads with nothing but her grief and a tapestry woven in the days before the names were taken.

Her vow is older than the dust on her garments.
Her silence, deeper than any well.

Where she treads, memories surface, and wrongs are spoken aloud by those who buried them.

She does not raise her voice.
She simply shows you what you pretended was never there.

THEY SPEAK IN WHISPERS

“Wash your grief,
and you’ll lose its weight.”

“Dust tells stories the living forget.”

“The tapestry knows betrayal before I do.”

“I haven’t cleaned these robes.
I still wear his silence.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When sensing betrayal nearby:

“The threads are trembling.
Someone here has false hands.”

THE WIDOW’S TENETS

REMEMBER THE LOST
Hold the names.
Recall what others bury.

LET DECAY TEACH
What rots returns.
Patience is a blade.

SPEAK ONLY NECESSARY
Silence reveals more than words ever could.

TURN PAIN INTO PURPOSE
Grief is raw ore.
Vengeance is the vessel.

TRUTH HAS NO CLEAN HANDS
Justice is never spotless.
And neither am I.

KEY ITEM

ISABELLINE TAPESTRY

Ancient woven scene; changes to warn of betrayal or deceit near the viewer.

The scene shifts when danger hides in plain sight.

Reveals the betrayer—
but never the time.

Weeps faint ashes when lies are near.

Protects the bearer from one fatal deceit per day.

“It remembers what you refuse to.”

THREADS OF MEMORY

LOST NAMES
Names once spoken are never truly lost.

FADED PATHS
The forgotten roads remember every footstep.

BROKEN PROMISES
Vows broken leave marks in the weave.

UNSPOKEN TRUTHS
What is hidden will one day be unraveled.

CYCLE OF DUST
All things return to dust, and dust tells all.

WHERE TO FIND HER

DESOLATE HIGHROADS
Where heat lifts memories from the stones.

RUINED SPIRE SETTLEMENTS
Where old promises echo in the wind.

FORGOTTEN BURIAL GROUNDS
Where the dead speak softest truths.

“I DO NOT SEEK PEACE.
I SEEK THE ONE WHO TOOK IT.”

I carry no blade that isn’t memory.
No coin but the cost of what was.

No water but what my tears refuse to make.

The sun has bleached my clothes,
but not my purpose.

I walk until names are returned
and lies are undone.

You may forget.
The tapestry does not.

I will not.

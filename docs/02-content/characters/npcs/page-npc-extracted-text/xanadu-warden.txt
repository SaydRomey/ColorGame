XANADU WARDEN
GUARDIAN OF THE FORGOTTEN GARDENS

He keeps watch where beauty was buried and time gave up.
The Xanadu Warden tends what others abandoned—overgrown paths, crumbled fountains, and names that wind no longer speaks.

He does not seek to restore.
He listens. He remembers.

In his hands are the stories of a place that once flourished beyond need or want.
He guards it not with walls, but with patience.

And he guides only those who come not to take—but to understand.

HE SPEAKS IN WHISPERS

“Gardens grow again, even over bones.”

“This garden grew thorns when we fled.”

“If you listen, the moss remembers where you buried the truth.”

“Hold the stone in moonlight — it’ll show you what we forgot.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When leading the player into ancient ruins:

“Roots grow around memory.
Tread softly — some stones grieve.”

THE WARDEN’S TENETS

MEMORY IS ROOTED
The past feeds the future.
Forgetfulness is decay.

GROWTH IS PATIENCE
Force breaks what time would mend.

SILENCE IS WISDOM
Listen first.
Words are the last part.

BEAUTY IS FRAGILE
What blooms can wither.
Protect, do not possess.

TRUTH IS BURIED
Beneath thorns and stone lie the answers.

NATURE REMEMBERS
The land keeps what people forget.

KEY ITEM

XANADU STONE

Ancient mossy relic; grants visions of lost utopias when held in moonlight.

Reveals visions of forgotten gardens and utopian cities.

Shows echoes of those who walked before.

Illuminates hidden paths and natural secrets.

Encourages growth in barren places.

Must be held in moonlight to unlock its power.

“What was perfect was not permanent.
What remains can be better.”

WHERE HE DWELLS

THE FORGOTTEN GARDENS
An ancient paradise reclaimed by vines, moss, and memory.

CRUMBLED FOUNTAINS
Waters still run beneath the stone, though none drink.

ROOT-SHROUDED RUINS
Temples and paths returned to the earth’s embrace.

WHAT HE CREATES

Remedies that restore.
Elixirs that awaken.

Seeds that remember their birthplace.
Songs that call growth from stone.

WHAT HE VALUES

Remembrance over glory.

Compassion over conquest.

Curiosity over certainty.

Stewardship over ownership.

Legacy over legacy of self.

HOW TO EARN HIS TRUST

Speak humbly.
Listen deeply.

Care for what others ignore.

Return what is not yours.

Protect the small and unseen.

Do not rush what time took centuries.

BE GENTLE.
BE HONEST.
BE WORTH REMEMBERING.

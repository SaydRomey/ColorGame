AURALITH
MEMORY • DECAY • SILENCE • ALCHEMY

BURNT SIENNA SCULPTOR

CARVES STORIES INTO OCHRE STONE;
EACH STATUE WHISPERS WITH ANCESTRAL MEMORY.

A master of forgotten craft, he works in silence and dust, shaping more than stone.
Every sculpture holds a story. Every story holds a soul.

He listens to the old things locked beneath the earth—and sets them free one chip at a time.

HE SPEAKS IN STONE

“Stone remembers better than blood.”

“Each chip is a truth I don’t speak.”

“The louder you strike,
the less the stone trusts you.”

“My statues don’t lie.
They just need dust to wake.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When animating a statue:

“Stand back.
The old soul still has questions.”

THE CRAFTSMAN’S WAY

LISTENS TO STONE
He communes with earth and memory, feeling the story inside the rock.

CARVES TRUTH
Each cut removes what is false, revealing what was always there.

AWAKENS SOULS
Through ancient rites and patient work, he breathes life into forgotten forms.

KEEPS THE PAST
He preserves what the world abandons, for sometimes memory is all that remains.

KEY ITEM

BURNT SIENNA GOLEM CORE

Heart of an old earthen automaton; can animate one statue per moon cycle.

Holds the memory of its maker and every soul once it served.

Places the core within a statue and speak its forgotten name.

Recharges under moonlight.
One statue, one cycle, one awakening.

STONECARVER ABILITIES

SHATTER & REVEAL
A powerful blow that breaks away illusionary stone, revealing hidden passages or memories.

DUST TO FORM
Summons stone dust to coalesce into a temporary guardian or tool of ancient design.

ANCESTRAL WHISPER
Reads a statue’s memory, revealing whispers of the past, secrets, and forgotten names.

EARTHBOUND PRISON
Traps a foe beneath stone, silencing them and burying them in ancient judgment.

WHERE TO FIND HIM

THE CARVED DEPTHS
A vast, echoing quarry beneath Auralith, where the oldest stones sleep.

THE QUIET ATELIER
His workshop, filled with half-finished figures and tools older than language.

THE FORGOTTEN GALLERY
A hall of statues that shouldn’t exist—and don’t speak unless he’s near.

“WE CARVE NOT TO BE REMEMBERED,
BUT SO THEY MAY REMEMBER THROUGH US.”

Some say he was once a general who turned from war.
Others say he was born from the first clay and the first sorrow.

He does not deny it.

He does not seek fame, nor coin, nor power.
Only that the stories not yet told are given a body, a face, a voice.

Ask the stone.
It will tell you—once the dust is gone.

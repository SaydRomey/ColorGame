THULIAN EMISSARY

AMBASSADOR FROM A HIDDEN MINERAL KINGDOM;
EYES SHIMMER PINK AND KNOWING

They emerge from caverns where dreamlight drips like slow rain and truths are carved in crystal.
Thulian Emissaries carry the voices of a people who remember what others forget.

Their words are smooth, their intentions precise.
They trade in knowledge, favors, and futures.

Every gem they wear is a door—and every door opens both ways.

THEY SPEAK IN HINTS

“Truth is a rare gem.
Most aren’t polished enough to see it.”

“I don’t lie.
I just leave out the easy parts.”

“Reflections are fragments.
So are we.”

“This mirror shows your truth, not mine.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When the player questions their motives:

“Does a prism pick its angles?”

THE EMISSARY’S TENETS

KNOWLEDGE IS CURRENCY
Information shapes empires.
Guard it. Trade it. Use it.

SECRETS HAVE VALUE
Not all truths are meant to be told.

REFLECTIONS REVEAL
Look within before you seek without.

FATE IS FLUID
The future is carved, not written.

BEAUTY IS IN PRECISION
Every word, every gesture, every gem—intentional.

KEY ITEM

THULIAN MIRROR

Polished rare stone; shows only truth, but not always the full picture.

Reveals truths concealed by illusion or lie.

Shows possibility, not certainty.

Requires focus and honest intent.

Can unsettle those who fear what they’ll see.

“Truth is a facet.
Perspective is the cut.”

WHERE THEY COME FROM

CRYSTAL DEPTHS
Hidden caverns beneath the moonlit spires of Myrrala.

STONE REMEMBRANCE
Cities grown from memory and mineral, not soil.

LIVING GEOLOGY
Their bodies and magics are shaped by stone.

THEY WALK BETWEEN WORLDS

“We do not belong to the surface.
We belong to what endures.”

WHAT THEY VALUE

Wisdom over wealth.

Insight over power.

Subtlety over sway.

Memory over ambition.

Harmony over control.

HOW TO EARN THEIR TRUST

Speak with care.

Keep confidences.

Offer more than you ask.

Prove your mind can hold nuance.

BE HONEST.
BE PATIENT.
BE WORTH REMEMBERING.

QUINACRIDONE BLADE

WARRIOR-PAINTER WHOSE MAGENTA BLADES NEVER DULL;
FIGHTS FOR AESTHETIC JUSTICE

Born where pigment meets passion, Quinacridone Blade believes that beauty must be defended.
Her strokes are swift, her standards higher.

On the field, she paints in vivid arcs of motion, leaving behind more than wounds—she leaves meaning.
She does not fight for glory, but for the right of existence to be exquisite.

SHE SPEAKS IN BOLD TONES

“Style without edge is decoration.”

“Every duel writes a stroke in my masterpiece.”

“Blood is pigment.
Pain is brushwork.”

“I duel to compose beauty—not to win.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

During combat in radiant fields:

“Strike like a stanza.
Parry like a rhyme.”

THE WARRIOR-PAINTER’S TENETS

BEAUTY IS A RIGHT
All creations deserve to be defended.

PERFECTION IS EARNED
Discipline shapes the hand.
The hand shapes fate.

EXPRESSION IS TRUTH
No stroke is wasted.
No scar is meaningless.

MOVEMENT IS POETRY
Flow in offense.
Precision in defense.

LEGACY IS COLOR
Leave the world more vibrant than you found it.

KEY ITEM

QUINACRIDONE CODEX

A grimoire of vibrant pigments; its spells must be painted mid-battle.

Spells are inscribed in living pigment.

Requires motion to activate.

Empowers blades with color-forged energy.

Can dazzle, bind, or devastate in equal measure.

“To cast, you must paint what you mean.”

WHERE TO FIND HER

IRIS GALLERIES
Hidden salons where art and duel intersect.

BATTLEFIELDS OF BEAUTY
She appears where conflict defiles the land.

DUELISTS’ CIRCUITS
Invited by those bold enough to challenge her canvas.

SHE FIGHTS. SHE PAINTS. SHE JUDGES.

“Every clash is a canvas.
Every victory, a finishing touch.”

WHAT SHE VALUES

Excellence in all forms.

Creativity with purpose.

Honesty in expression.

Balance of form and fury.

The courage to be seen.

HOW TO DEAL WITH HER

Do not mock her art.

Do not stain her vision.

Respect her canvas or face her blade.

SPEAK TRUTH.
FIGHT CLEAN.
APPRECIATE BEAUTY.
EARN HER RESPECT.

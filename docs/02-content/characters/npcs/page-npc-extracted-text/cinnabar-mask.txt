CINNABAR MASK

MASKED ENVOY OF JOY AND DEATH;
RITUALS PAINTED IN SACRED RED-ORANGE

The Cinnabar Mask dances between celebration and carnage, speaking in riddles wrapped in laughter.
To them, joy and mortality are twin flames—both demand witnesses, both deserve reverence.

They bring color to the gray places between life and legend.

THEY SPEAK IN RIDDLES & RHYME

“Smiles are masks.
Mine just fits better.”

“A ritual is only wrong if no one claps.”

“I’ve danced at ten funerals.
And nine weddings.

I forget which was louder.”

“Blood is just ink for a louder story.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When performing a rite in the field:

“Clap, or it gets awkward.
The spirits hate awkward.”

THE MASK’S ROLES

TRICKSTER PRIEST
Blends humor and horror to reveal deeper truths through spectacle.

RITUAL PERFORMER
Paints the world with sacred cinnabar, turning every act into ceremony.

BRINGER OF BALANCE
Honors both the living and the dead, knowing neither lasts forever.

WEAVER OF STORIES
Crafts legends from moments, ensuring no sacrifice is ever silent.

CHAOS IN DISGUISE
Sows beautiful disorder, reminding the world that meaning is a choice.

KEY ITEM

CINNABAR SEAL

Enchanted wax stamp; bonds contracts with joyful truth or cruel irony.

Binds oaths, pacts, and promises.

Ink of cinnabar records emotion as binding truth.

Truth told with laughter is unbreakable.

Cruelty signed with a smile still holds.

“A seal is only as strong as the story it believes.”

RITES OF THE CINNABAR MASK

PAINTED BEGINNINGS
Marks the birth of something new with cinnabar and song.

DANCE OF TRANSITION
Guides souls across thresholds with rhythm, laughter, and colored fire.

FEAST OF ECHOES
Celebrates the living and the fallen in one table, one toast.

OATH OF SCARLET
Binds promises with blood-red wax and witnessed joy.

MASK OF RELEASE
Removes pain’s weight through performance and acceptance.

WHERE TO FIND THEM

THE CRIMSON CARNIVAL
A wandering festival that appears before battle, harvest, or uprising.

THE RUBY SANCTUARY
A hidden temple where laughter is prayer and pain is painted.

THE BLOODRED ROAD
A dangerous path where every step is a story waiting to be told.

“I DANCE, THEREFORE IT IS REAL.
I LAUGH, THEREFORE IT IS BEARABLE.”

They say the Mask was once a child who asked the gods why they wept and why they warred.
The gods answered with silence, so the child painted their own smile and spoke until the world listened.

Now the Mask walks between joy and grief, reminding all who watch that to feel deeply is to live completely.

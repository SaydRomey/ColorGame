ALIZARIN
THE CRIMSON WEAVER

SORCERESS • SEAMSTRESS OF FATE • WEAVER OF OATHS

Alizarin weaves destiny with blood-dyed thread and memory.
She collects the stories of the fallen and sews them into a tapestry that grants strength to those who still fight.

“EVERY HEROIC DEATH IS A STITCH.
EVERY STITCH, A LEGACY.”

Alizarin was once a chronicler of victories, until she realized that only the living are remembered poorly.
She took up needle and thread to keep truer records—ones that could not be burned, lied about, or forgotten.

Now she offers strength to those willing to add their story to the quilt.
The price is always the same.

The reward is never the same twice.

SHE DOES NOT PREDICT FATE.
SHE STITCHES IT.

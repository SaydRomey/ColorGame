AURALITH
MEMORY • DECAY • SILENCE • ALCHEMY

MIKADO WARLORD

REGAL TYRANT IN LACQUERED ARMOR;
HIS RULE POLISHED, HIS JUSTICE SHARP

He rules Auralith with perfect form.
Every decree, every gesture, every strike is measured.

In a land where memory rots and voices fade, he carves order from silence.

His warriors move as one.
His courts are quiet as tombs.

And his blade?
It remembers every name it has ever ended.

HE SPEAKS IN DISCIPLINED WORDS

“Style is strength with discipline.”

“A cut made with grace leaves a cleaner wound.”

“The blade teaches faster than books.”

“Balance. Posture.
And one clean decision.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

Before executing judgment:

“Rust never dulled this edge.
Only hesitation does.”

THE WARLORD’S TENETS

DISCIPLINE IS POWER
Control yourself, and you control all.

JUSTICE IS PRECISION
Mercy is a luxury.
Justice is exact.

ORDER IS STRENGTH
A well-placed rule is sharper than a blade.

SILENCE IS AUTHORITY
Let others speak.
Let results answer.

HONOR IS ETERNAL
Your name dies if you break your word.

KEY ITEM

MIKADO KATANA

Heirloom blade lacquered in gold-brown; grants calm focus and unerring strikes.

Sharpens the mind and stills the heart.

Grants perfect focus in the heat of battle.

Strikes find their mark without fail.

Honors the fallen —
empowers the living.

Fails those who strike without purpose or poise.

“Precision is mercy.
Hesitation is cruelty.”

WHERE TO FIND HIM

LACQUERED PALACE
Seat of his rule, guarded by silence.

HALL OF REFLECTIONS
Mirrors that show what you refuse to face.

GILDED COURTYARD
Where judgments are delivered without voice.

THE WAY OF THE MIKADO

“A kingdom lasts when the ruler is still.
A ruler lasts when his rule is never questioned.”

WHAT HE VALUES

Loyalty without question.

Excellence in all things.

Tradition over change.

Clean outcomes.
No loose ends.

Beauty in precision.

HOW TO EARN HIS RESPECT

Show mastery in body, word, and blade.

BE PREPARED.
BE PRECISE.
BE WORTHY.

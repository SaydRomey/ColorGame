CELADON ARCHIVIST

ETHEREAL SCHOLAR TENDING TO POTTERY LIBRARIES;
MEMORY SEALED IN GLAZED RUNES

The Celadon Archivist preserves what the world would rather forget.
Within the quiet stacks of glazed vessels, they study the echoes of lives long gone—hearing the whispers trapped beneath centuries of clay and silence.

THEY SPEAK IN WHISPERS

“Each glaze holds a lifetime.
Crack it, and you hear the end.”

“Nothing fades — only layers build.”

“Don’t drop that shard.
It remembers your secrets faster than you forget them.”

“Porcelain has better memory than flesh.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When asked about a forgotten incantation:

“Check the seventh shelf.
No, not that one. The one that hums when you lie.”

THE ARCHIVIST’S DUTIES

GUARDIAN OF GLASS
Protects the pottery libraries where memories are sealed in celadon clay.

READER OF RUNES
Translates glazed runes and kiln-scribed symbols into forgotten histories.

KEEPER OF ECHOES
Listens to the whispers inside vessels, preserving the voices of the past.

CATALYST OF GROWTH
Uses memory and clay to cultivate knowledge, spells, and living alchemical forms.

SILENT WITNESS
Knows much, speaks little.
Truth is offered only to those who listen deeply.

KEY ITEM

CELADON SHARDS

Pieces of enchanted porcelain; used to record spells visually like film.

Capture incantations, rituals, and memories as living images.

Play them back in a Celadon Projector or attuned kiln.

Fragile, but preserves what even paper cannot.

Shatters over time unless renewed with living essence.

“A shard remembers.
A soul endures.”

PORTER OF PORCELAIN LORE

GLAZE OF MEMORY
Infuses clay with essence to lock in experiences and emotions.

KILN OF ECHOES
Fires vessels in silence, allowing trapped voices to surface without breaking the seal.

RUNE ETCHING
Inscribes symbols that link memories, spells, and bloodlines across generations.

SHARD SCRIPTORIUM
Organizes and archives shards by era, theme, and resonance signature.

RESTORATION
Mends broken vessels and fractured memories — if the past allows forgiveness.

WHERE TO FIND THEM

THE JADE STACKS
A terraced archive of jars carved into the cliffs of Huevale.
Time moves differently here.

THE OVERGROWN KILNS
Ancient kilns swallowed by vines, where the Archivist listens when the world grows quiet.

THE PORCELAIN LABYRINTH
A maze of silent shelves and locked memories.
Only the curious with care may enter.

“MEMORY IS CLAY.
TIME IS FIRE.

WHAT WE SAVE IS WHO WE BECOME.”

They were once a potter, then a chronicler, then something in between.

In youth, they sought to outlive death through alchemy.

In age, they learned: memory outlives all things.

Now they tend the endless shelves, not to hoard the past, but to keep it from being lost to the rush of new beginnings.

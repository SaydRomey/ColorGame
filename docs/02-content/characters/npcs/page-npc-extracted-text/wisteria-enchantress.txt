MYRRALA
MAGIC • DREAMS • FATE • ILLUSION

WISTERIA ENCHANTRESS

SOFT-SPOKEN SPELLCASTER FROM THE VINE-COVERED TOWER;
WEAVES GENTLE GLAMOURS

She dwells where wisteria drapes the old stones and twilight lingers between thought and memory.
From her tower, she watches the currents of dream and desire, and bends them into beauty.

Her magic is not force, but suggestion.
A whisper. A glance.

A half-remembered song on the wind.

Many seek her for power.
Few understand the price of being made lovely.

She asks only that those who enter leave behind their certainty.

SHE SPEAKS IN WHISPERS

“Charm is a quieter curse.”

“I weave kindness like silk —
hard to notice until you’re bound.”

“Glamour is truth made beautiful enough to forgive.”

“If the moon nods at you —
don’t blink.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When encountered during twilight hours:

“The light’s soft now.
It listens better than it speaks.”

THE ENCHANTRESS’S TENETS

BEAUTY IS A BRIDGE
It opens hearts.
It crosses minds.

SUBTLETY IS STRENGTH
Loud magic shatters.
Soft magic endures.

SECRETS ARE SEEDS
Plant them wisely.
Every truth grows.

DREAMS HOLD KEYS
The sleeping mind remembers what waking forgets.

COMPASSION IS POWER
To be gentle is not to be weak.
It is to choose your art.

LET GO TO WEAVE
Cling to nothing.
Only then can you shape all.

KEY ITEM

WISTERIA VEIL

Shimmering shawl; obscures the wearer’s presence in twilight and dreams.

Blends the wearer into shadow, mist, and half-remembered places.

Masks presence from sight, sound, and divination.

Grants passage through dreamscapes and illusory borders.

Draws on moonlight and perfume to sustain its glamour.

“You are not truly hidden.
Only beautifully overlooked.”

WHERE SHE DWELLS

THE VINE-COVERED TOWER
An ancient spire wrapped in wisteria and time.

THE TWILIGHT BALCONY
Between day and night, she listens to the world soften its edges.

THE PERFUME GARDEN
Rare blooms and moonlit fountains feed her art.

WHAT SHE CREATES

Glamours that soothe.

Illusions that protect.

Whispers that linger.

Beauty that remembers.

WHAT SHE VALUES

Grace over force.

Empathy over judgment.

Imagination over logic.

Patience over haste.

Memory over gold.

HOW TO EARN HER TRUST

Speak gently.
Listen deeply.

Protect the overlooked.

Do not demand.

Do not rush.

BE KIND.
BE PATIENT.

BE WORTH REMEMBERING.

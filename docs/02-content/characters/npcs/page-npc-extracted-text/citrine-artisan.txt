LUXERAN
SUNLIGHT • TRICKERY • VITALITY • ILLUSION

CITRINE ARTISAN

JEWELER WHOSE PIECES BRIGHTEN SPIRITS;
HER WORK PROMOTES JOY AND PROSPERITY

In the sunlit markets of Luxeran, the Citrine Artisan cuts more than gemstones—she shapes hope, laughter, and luck itself.
Her jewelry doesn’t just dazzle the eye; it warms the heart and sweetens the day.

SHE SPEAKS IN SPARKS

“Jewels are emotions you can wear.”

“Happiness is the easiest lie to sell.”

“What shines isn’t always treasure—sometimes it’s just a clever angle.”

“Gold doesn’t cheer you up.
But it listens better than people.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When offering to sell something:

“This one sparkles just right when you lie.”

THE ARTISAN’S CRAFT

GEMSMITH EXTRAORDINAIRE
Cuts and sets stones that amplify joy, confidence, and good fortune.

DEALER IN DELIGHT
Her pieces lift spirits, ease worries, and inspire brighter days.

MISTRESS OF ILLUSION
Knows how perception shapes value—and how to play it well.

FAIR, YET UNPREDICTABLE
Her prices are as shifting as sunlight on water.

COLLECTOR OF STORIES
Every gem holds a tale.
She collects them as carefully as gold.

KEY ITEM

CITRINE LENS

Yellow gemstone monocle; lets wearer see sources of happiness or greed.

Reveals hidden wells of joy, desire, or envy.

Filters the world in golden truth and flattering lies.

Helps in negotiation, diplomacy, and reading hearts.

Shows where wealth flows—and who hoards it.

“See clearly. Choose wisely.
Shine generously.”

WHERE TO FIND HER

THE DAZZLING BAZAAR
Her stall sparkles near the fountain, where sunlight lingers longest.

THE GOLDEN GALLERY
A hidden workshop above the city, filled with mirrors, light, and half-finished dreams.

FESTIVAL OF RADIANCE
During the festival, she appears in many places at once.
Or none. Depends on the light.

“FORTUNE FAVORS THE CHEERFUL,
BUT WISDOM CHOOSES THE RIGHT SHINE.”

They say she once set a gem that could bottle sunlight.
Kings offered kingdoms.

She asked for stories instead.

She learned that joy is rarer than gold,
but easier to share.

Now she gives both—one for the hands,
one for the heart.

And in Luxeran, that’s the truest wealth of all.

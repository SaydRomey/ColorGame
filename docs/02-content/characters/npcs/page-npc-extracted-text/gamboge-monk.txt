GAMBOGE MONK

VARNISH-WEARING MARTIAL ARTIST FROM LACQUERED TEMPLES;
CALM BUT DEADLY

From lacquered walls and sun-washed courtyards, the Gamboge Monk walks the narrow path between stillness and storm.
He wears the color of sacred varnish — a protective sheen for both body and mind.

His strikes are rituals.
His breath, a blade.

He harms only when balance demands it, and never without grace.

Those who see him first rarely see him again.
Those who survive, remember the silence.

THEY SPEAK IN WHISPERS

“Lacquer what you can’t clean.”

“Even peace bruises when it lands too hard.”

“Silence can hit harder than thunder — if you wait long enough.”

“I meditate between footsteps.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When ambushed unexpectedly:

“Violence without grace?
That’s just noise.”

THE MONK’S TENETS

REFINE THE SELF
Discipline body and mind.
Purity strengthens action.

STRIKE WITH PURPOSE
Every move has meaning.
Every blow, a decision.

EMBRACE IMPERMANENCE
All things change.
Attachment is the root of fear.

HARM ONLY IMBALANCE
Violence is a tool, not a hunger.

SILENCE IS A WEAPON
Wait. Watch. Act.
The moment is everything.

KEY ITEM

GAMBOGE GOURD

Lacquered container used in rituals; once uncorked, releases holy smoke.

Releases consecrated smoke that purifies and disorients.

Blinds and confuses evildoers, clears allied minds.

Provides a brief ward against corruption and hostile magic.

Refills at temples of the sun or sites of true balance.

“Breathe in light.
Breathe out everything else.”

TO ACT WITHOUT FEAR IS TO SERVE THE SUN.”

I polish the mirror until nothing remains to hide.

The world is hot.
Let it be.

My mind is cool.
Let it be.

I walk between.

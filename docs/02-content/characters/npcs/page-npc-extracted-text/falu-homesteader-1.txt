FALU HOMESTEADER

STUBBORN DEFENDER OF THE RED TIMBER TOWNS;
WIELDS COPPER TOOLS AS WEAPONS

The Red Timber Towns were carved from bloodwood and iron will.
Falu was born when fire still licked the foundation beams and has sworn to protect every nail, every hearth, every soul under its roof.

He is no soldier, but his hammer has stopped more than one raider.
This is his home. And he does not yield.

THEY SPEAK IN RESPECT (OR CAUTION).

“If the hearth dies,
so do the hills.”

“Redwood walls don’t fall...
they remember why they stand.”

“Call me stubborn one more time.
I’ll outlive the insult.”

“This hammer built hope.
And broke bones.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When asked about leaving town:

“This land raised me.
I won’t leave until it dies.

And maybe not then.”

THE HOMESTEADER’S CODE

DEFEND THE HEARTH
Home is more than wood and stone—it is the lives within.

BUILD TO ENDURE
Every beam, every nail, every repair is a promise to the future.

NEVER BACK DOWN
The town does not stand because it is strong, but because it refuses to fall.

HONOR THE LAND
The hills gave us timber.
We give them loyalty.

NEIGHBOR BEFORE SELF
In the Red Timber Towns, we survive together or not at all.

SMARAGDINE KNIGHT

EMERALD-ARMORED PROTECTOR OF FOREST REALMS;
BLADE GLINTS LIKE GEM-CUT LEAVES

The grove remembers its vow.
Smaragdine Knight is its hand, its shield, its quiet wrath.

Where roots hold fast and light filters like mercy, the Knight walks.
Their armor is a living thing—woven from leaf and stone, bound by sap and song.

They do not conquer the wild.
They are the wild’s will, made steel, made emerald.

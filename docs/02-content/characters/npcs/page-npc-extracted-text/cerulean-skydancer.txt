IRISELD
WISDOM • STORMS • SORROW • MYSTERY

CERULEAN SKYDANCER

MESSENGER OF WIND SPIRITS;
PERFORMS AERIAL RITES IN AZURE SILKS

She dances where the clouds are born and where stories are carried away.
The Skydancer hears the breath of tempests and the whisper of skies forgotten by the ground below.

SHE SPEAKS WITH THE WIND

“You rise only when you fall with grace.”

“The wind doesn’t forget who defies it.”

“Each gust speaks a different name.
Most are warnings.”

“Flight is a promise —
not to fall, but to keep dancing.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

Before a sky-ritual sequence:

“Move like lightning forgot your name.”

DUTIES OF THE SKY PRIEST

BINDING OF GUSTS
She weaves prayers into the upper winds, guiding storms away from the innocent.

MESSENGER OF THE AIRSTREAMS
Carries voices, visions, and warnings between lands and lost souls.

RITES OF ASCENT
Performs sacred dances that open paths through clouds and veil.

STORM INTERCESSOR
Negotiates with tempests, soothing rage, turning wrath to rain.

SEEING FAR
Reads the language of cloudforms, wingbeats, and the silence between thunder.

KEY ITEM

CERULEAN CLOAK

Azure silk garment; wearer is immune to lightning while in open skies.

Repels lightning strikes in open skies.

Light as wind, strong as the storm above.

Empowered by sky-rites atop high altars.

“The sky does not strike
what it has chosen to wear.”

SKYDANCER ABILITIES

WINDSTEP
Moves with impossible lightness, riding the updrafts.

GUST CALL
Summons a directed burst of wind to shield, push, or lift.

CLOUD VEIL
Merges with mist and vapors, becoming unseen.

AERIAL BALLET
Disorients foes with graceful, dizzying movements.

STORM’S BALANCE
Channels the storm’s temper, turning fury into calm.

WHERE TO FIND HER

THE HIGH WIND ALTARS
Ancient terraces above the clouds, where rituals are carved into stone and air.

THE EYE OF THE CYCLONE
At the heart of great storms, where she communes with the tempest’s mind.

THE FORGOTTEN UPDRAFT
A hidden current that lifts only those who dance without fear.

“THE GROUND REMEMBERS WEIGHT.
THE SKY REMEMBERS SONG.”

She say she was mortal once, dancer who loved the wind more than the earth.

She gave her name to the storm so others might live beneath clear skies.

Now she belongs to neither —
and to both.

As long as someone dares to rise,
her dance will never end.

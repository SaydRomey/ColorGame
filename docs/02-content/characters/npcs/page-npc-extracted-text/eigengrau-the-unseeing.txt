EIGENGRAU THE UNSEEING

SHADOWMANCER FROM THE REALM OF WHISPERS;
COMMANDS DARKNESS OF THE MIND’S EYE

In the storm-veiled lands of Iriseld, Eigengrau walks where memories sleep.
He does not seek power, but truth, buried beneath the noise of light and the lies we tell ourselves.

Blindfolded, he listens to the dark that lives behind the skull.
The unseen is not empty—it is full of things you’re afraid to find.

THEY SPEAK IN WHISPERS

“Not all shadows are cast by light.”

“The color you see in dreams is me.”

“I blind myself to see deeper.
You still blink too much.”

“Fear isn’t a wall.
It’s a mirror with dust on it.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When the player hesitates to look into the prism:

“You won’t like what you see.
That’s why it’s worth looking.”

THE ORACLE’S NATURE

WEAVER OF SHADOWS
Shapes the unseen, the unspoken, and the fears we hide.

SEER OF THE SUBCONSCIOUS
Reads not the future, but what already lives within the mind.

KEEPER OF SILENT TRUTHS
Speaks in riddles because plain words cannot hold what he sees.

STORM-BORN
His power rises with the wind, the rain, and the weight of unspoken sorrow.

WITNESS WITHOUT JUDGMENT
He shows, but does not change—the choice is always yours.

KEY ITEM

EIGENGRAU PRISM

Dark crystal; shows the user’s subconscious desires or fears when gazed into.

Reveals hidden truths the mind tries to bury.

Reflects desires, fears, and repressed memories.

Effects last only as long as you’re willing to face them.

May heal, or may break what you deny.

“The darkest depths hold the clearest truths.”

DREAMSCAPE

SUBCONSCIOUS

REGRETS

POTENTIALS

WHERE TO FIND HIM

THE DROWNED SPIRE
An ancient tower half-sunk in a lake, where no voice echoes.

THE WHISPERING CAVERN
A cave that speaks your name when no one else is near.

THE VEILMARKET
A hidden bazaar that appears only in dreams or storms.

“YOU SEEK ANSWERS OUT THERE.
I SHOW YOU WHAT YOU ALREADY KNOW WITHIN.”

He walks where memory thins and names dissolve in mist.
He does not guide the weak, for truth is a blade without a hilt.

To know yourself fully is to risk becoming someone new.

Look—if you dare.
The prism waits.

The dark listens.

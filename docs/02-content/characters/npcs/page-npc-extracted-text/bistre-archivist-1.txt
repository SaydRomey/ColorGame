AURALITH
MEMORY • DECAY • SILENCE • ALCHEMY

BISTRE ARCHIVIST

CARETAKER OF SOOT-STAINED SCROLLS;
LOREKEEPER WITH SMUDGED HANDS AND PERFECT RECALL

Bistre Archivist tends the vaults of what was.
He rescues truth from ash and keeps the stories that time and fire would rather forget.

HE SPEAKS IN WHISPERS

“Soot smears,
but it preserves.”

“Ink lies.
Burnt pages confess.”

“I remember everything.
Especially the lies we burned.”

“Ask the flame —
it reads better than I do.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When revealing a hidden memory:

“Watch the wall.
Shadows keep clearer records than kings.”

WAYS OF THE ARCHIVIST

KEEPER OF ASH
He rescues and preserves knowledge that others destroy.

TRUTH IN RUIN
He believes fire reveals what ink hides.
He burns to remember.

WITNESS OF TIME
Centuries of memory rest in his mind.
Nothing escapes his recall.

SCRIBE OF SHADOW
He records not with ink, but with soot, char and memory.

CUSTODIAN OF WHAT REMAINS
He guards relics, names, and stories buried beneath silence.

KEY ITEM

BISTRE LANTERN

Sooty lamp that reveals past events in shadows when lit in forgotten places.

Reveals echoes of the past projected as moving shadows.

Consumes memories.
Not all that is seen should be kept.

The more it shows you,
the more it remembers about you.

“Light the forgotten.
Face what was.

Carry what must be.”

ARCHIVIST ABILITIES

ASHEN RECALL
Summons memories of a place or person.
Reveals hidden truths or forgotten paths.

BURNT TESTIMONY
Consumes a document or record to expose a concealed fact or contradiction.

SHADOW SCRIPT
Projects past events onto nearby surfaces as silent, ghostly re-enactments.

SILENT CENSUS
Erases a memory from mortal minds.
What is forgotten cannot be recalled.

WHERE TO FIND HIM

THE SILT ARCHIVE
A buried library beneath the ashen spires of Auralith.

THE SMOKEWARDEN
A drifting tower that appears only to those who seek forgotten answers.

THE FORGOTTEN VAULTS
Catacombs of memory, where names are carved in soot and bone.

“WE BURNED IT THINKING IT DIED.
BUT ASH REMEMBERS WHAT FIRE FORGETS.”

Bistre does not seek power, only preservation.
He believes that history is a body — and memory is the only soul that keeps it from rotting.

He walks between what was and what might be, carrying a lantern that shows the cost of every choice.

HE KEEPS WHAT TIME IGNORES.
HE REMEMBERS SO WE MAY LEARN.

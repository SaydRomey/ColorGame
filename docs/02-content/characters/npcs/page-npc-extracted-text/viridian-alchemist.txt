VIRIDIAN ALCHEMIST

MASTER OF STABLE TRANSMUTATIONS; USES SACRED BLUISH-GREEN INK TO DRAW POWER INTO PIGMENTS

In Huevale, where roots whisper and leaves remember, the Viridian Alchemist listens to the slow language of change.
He distills the breath of moss and the tears of stone into inks that carry intent.

With steady hand and quiet heart, he writes what must endure.
Not all potions are meant to heal.

Not all glyphs are meant to fade.
Through balance, respect, and the weight of every drop, he turns the natural into the eternal.

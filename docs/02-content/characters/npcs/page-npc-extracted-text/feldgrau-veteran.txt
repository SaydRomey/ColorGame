FELDGRAU VETERAN

RETIRED SOLDIER TURNED MERCENARY STRATEGIST;
ARMOR CAMOUFLAGED LIKE MOSSY STONE

He marched through wars that carved names into ash and stone.
When the banners fell and the songs forgot, Feldgrau walked away.

He traded glory for survival, orders for options, and steel for silence.
Now he sells strategy to those who still believe in victory.

But some fights he takes are for debts the living can’t repay.

THEY SPEAK IN WHISPERS

“Cover the blade in green,
and the blood won’t shine.”

“I’ve seen ghosts lose their war...
and still stand guard.”

“I buried more comrades than I remember.
But moss remembers them all.”

“Don’t ask about my medals.
Ask the forest where I left them.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When asked to teach strategy:

“Plan like the forest —
layered, patient, and ready to swallow noise.”

THE VETERAN’S TENETS

BLEND TO ENDURE
A soldier unseen is a soldier alive.

OBSERVE. ADAPT. STRIKE.
Watch the enemy.
Learn the ground.

Choose the moment.

THE DEAD TEACH
Every grave holds a lesson for the next survivor.

THE FOREST PROVIDES
Use what the wild offers.
Waste nothing.

Leave no trace.

VICTORY IS SURVIVAL
Glory fades.
Living with purpose is the only true reward.

KEY ITEM

FELDGRAU CLOAK

Worn by ghost soldiers; renders the wearer near-invisible in forests or ruins.

Merges with forest shadows and foliage.

Greatly reduces visibility and detection.

Dampens sound of movement and breath.

Once worn, whispers of the fallen may be heard at night.

“The dead don’t need to be seen to still watch.”

STRATEGIST’S EDGE

TERRAIN READING
Knows every path, trap, and vantage of the land.

WAR MEMORY
Recalls battles, formations, and fatal mistakes.

GUERRILLA CRAFT
Specializes in ambushes, retreats, and misdirection.

RESOURCEFUL
Makes tools, traps, and solutions from whatever is near.

RUTHLESS LOGIC
Removes emotion from war.
Makes the hard choice first.

WHERE TO FIND HIM

MOSSWARD OUTPOST
A hidden watchpost built into ancient ruins.

OVERGROWN CAIRNS
Where fallen soldiers sleep beneath living stone.

THE GREENMIRE
A misty floodplain where tracks vanish and secrets sink.

“THE FOREST DOESN’T RUSH.
YET EVERYTHING IS ACCOMPLISHED.”

I was twenty when I learned to kill.
I was thirty when I learned why men die.

Now I’m old enough to know when it’s worth it.

I don’t fight for kings or gods.
I fight for balance.

Between life and death.
Between the noise...

and what truly matters.

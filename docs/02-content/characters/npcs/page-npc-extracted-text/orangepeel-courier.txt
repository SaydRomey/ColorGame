ORANGEPEEL COURIER

HIGH-SPEED RUNNER OF THE TRADE ROADS;
SCENT OF CITRUS MARKS HER TRAIL

Orangepeel runs where others rest.
The trade roads are her river, the sun her engine, and citrus her calling card.

From port to palace, she delivers what matters most — before the dust settles.

You’ll see the peel charm swinging long before you see her.
By then, she’s already gone.

SHE SPEAKS IN QUICK WORDS

“Speed is safety.
Talk later.”

“The scent hits before the shadow does.”

“Trade waits for no one —
except me.”

“If the deal smells off,
I already ran.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When delivering crucial intel:

“Fast news. Fresh squeeze.
Take it or leave it.”

KEY ITEM

ORANGE PEEL CHARM

Zesty amulet; worn by traders, it wards off fatigue and attracts good deals.

Replenishes energy and drives away exhaustion.

Draws favorable prices and honest bargains.

Masks the wearer’s scent from pursuers and traps.

Brings luck on the road and in the market.

“Zest in the pocket,
fortune on the road.”

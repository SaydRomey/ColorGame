IRISFIELD
WISDOM • STORMS • SORROW • MYSTERY

ZAFFRE GLASSWRIGHT

ARTISAN WHO SPEAKS THROUGH COBALT GLASS;
TRAPS MEMORIES IN SHIMMERING WINDOWS

He works where light meets silence, in the hush between thunder and tide.
The Zaffre Glasswright shapes cobalt like thought, and sets sorrow into silence.

Every pane holds a breath.
Every curve, a truth too heavy to say aloud.

He does not ask your name.
He asks what you cannot unsee.

Here, memories are molten.
And once sealed in blue, they can only be faced.

Not broken.
Not erased.

Only understood.

HE SPEAKS IN REFLECTIONS

“Glass keeps what words forget.”

“Look too deep, and you’ll find what looked back.”

“This vial doesn’t show you what’s hidden — it shows you what you buried.”

“I don’t make windows.
I make confessions.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When using the vial to a memory gate:

“Step through, Just don’t expect the same person to come out.”

THE GLASSWRIGHT’S TENETS

SILENCE IS CLARITY
Truth speaks loudest in quiet minds.

MEMORY IS WEIGHT
What we bear shapes what we see.

REFLECTION REVEALS
What is hidden within will always reflect.

BEAUTY IS BURDEN
To hold beauty is to hold its sorrow.

CONFESSION IS FREEDOM
Only by facing the past can one pass through.

GLASS IS FOREVER
Break it, and it still remembers.
Mend it, and it remembers more.

KEY ITEM

ZAFFRE VIAL

Cobalt bottle filled with enchanted tears; reveals hidden doors or passages.

Reveals concealed doors, passages, and memories.

Draws out buried sorrow to open forbidden ways.

Works only where the past has left an echo.

Single use per memory gate.

Most potent under stormlight or moon.

“Some doors are locked.
Others are forgotten.

This vial reminds them.”

WHERE HE DWELLS

THE GLASS TOWER
A lonely spire of blue windows overlooking the storm-washed sea.

BREAKWATER FOUNDRY
Where waves crash below and glass is born from salt and lightning.

THE MEMORY ATRIUM
A hall of suspended panes that hold what was lost.

WHAT HE CREATES

“Windows that witness.
Vials that whisper.

Mirrors that remember.
Glass that confesses.”

WHAT HE VALUES

Honesty over comfort.

Insight over ignorance.

Reflection over denial.

Sorrow over forgetting.

Legacy over legacy denied.

HOW TO EARN HIS TRUST

Bring him a memory you cannot bear.
Listen without speaking.

Do not ask for power.
Ask only to understand.

Let your reflection speak truth.

BE HONEST.
BE BRAVE.

BE WORTH REMEMBERING.

“Glass does not lie. It only waits for you to look close enough.” — Zaffre Glasswright

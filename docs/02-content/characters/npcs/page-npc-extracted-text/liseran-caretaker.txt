LISERAN CARETAKER

MATRON OF THE BLOOMING SHRINE;
PROTECTOR OF REBIRTH AND MOTHERS

She watches over the sacred gardens where lost souls rest before returning.
Clad in the lilies of mourning, she tends both the living and the unborn with equal devotion.

To her, grief is not an enemy, but a soil — rich, dark, and necessary for new life.

Many come to weep.
Fewer leave unchanged.

THEY SPEAK IN HUSHED TRUTHS

“Rebirth always costs something — even if it’s love.”

“Lilies bloom in mourning, not celebration.”

“I tend grief like a garden — quietly.”

“Each birth echoes a loss unspoken.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When helping restore a fading memory:

“Let the petals carry what you can’t.”

THE CARETAKER’S TENETS

HONOR THE CYCLE
All things pass.
All things return.

PROTECT THE MOTHERS
Nurture the womb, guard the vulnerable.

TEND THE GARDEN
Grief must be tended or it will wither the soul.

REMEMBER THE SILENT
Speak their names in prayer and in bloom.

ACCEPT THE COST
Love, loss, and rebirth are never separate.

KEY ITEM

LISERAN LILY

Frail blossom encased in mana crystal; releases a healing aura upon breaking.

Shatters to release a wave of healing in a wide area.

Purifies corruption and eases sorrow.

Most potent when used beneath starlight or moonlight.

The blossom will not bloom in earthly soil.

“Some blooms are meant to break.”

WHERE TO FIND HER

BLOOMING SHRINE
Hidden sanctuary where souls await rebirth.

GARDENS OF MELANCHOLY
Fields of lilies that blossom only at twilight.

DREAMER’S REST
A refuge for the weary and the heartbroken.

“IN MOURNING, WE PLANT,
IN LOVE, WE RETURN.”

Every petal remembers.
Every tear waters.

The garden is vast, and none walk it alone.

Leave what you must.
Take what you’re given.

And when you are ready, the lilies will open the path you were meant to walk again.

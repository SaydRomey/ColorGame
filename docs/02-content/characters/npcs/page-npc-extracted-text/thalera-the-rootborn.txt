THALERA THE ROOTBORN

VERDANT GHOST
FORMED FROM ROOTS AND VINES

Thalera is one of the Rootborn—ancient spirits bound to the deep memory of Huevale’s oldest woods.
She was once the echo of a grove’s heart, until the world was wounded.

Now she walks between bark and breath, a guardian of forgotten oaths and overgrown truths.
Those who see her are rarely the same.

SHE SPEAKS IN WHISPERS

“Your feet are bare.
Your breath uncolored.”

“You do not belong, Prismwalker.”

“Yet... you carry the Root’s grief in your steps.”

KEY ITEM

ROOTBORN MANTLE

A shroud of living moss and vine; grants the wearer communion with plants and spirits.

Grants the ability to commune with plants and spirits.

Protects against necrotic magic and decay.

Silences the wearer’s steps in natural environments.

Heals slowly when surrounded by living vegetation.

“We do not walk the forest.
The forest walks through us.”

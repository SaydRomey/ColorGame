HUEVALE
NATURE • GROWTH • ALCHEMY • STEALTH

SAPROOT CULTIST

SWAMP-DWELLER DEVOTED TO A SLEEPING TREE GOD;
CHANNELS EARTHY GREEN ICHOR

The god sleeps in bark—dream too loud and it might wake.
He moves through the mire with reverent feet.

The roots are his scripture, the moss his vestments.
He offers rot, not rage.

He collects what others throw away—and turns it holy.
In his hands, decay becomes devotion.

HE SPEAKS IN WHISPERS

“The god sleeps in bark—dream too loud and it might wake.”

“Rot is just birth with teeth.”

“I bleed sap, not doubt.”

“Decay’s not death.
It’s the soil praying.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When defending their faith:

“Worship the rot.
It’s the only thing that listens forever.”

THE CULTIST’S TENETS

SILENCE IS SACRED
Speak less. Listen more.
The roots hear all.

ROT IS RENEWAL
Everything returns.
Everything serves.

SAP IS BLOOD
Offer what you are.
The god drinks deep.

DECAY IS DEVOTION
Let go. Break down.
Become the soil.

THE SLEEPER WATCHES
He does not stir—but he remembers.

KEY ITEM

SAPROOT IDOL

Carved from swamp wood and berries; protects its owner from decay and curses.

Shields against decay, poison, and curses.

Slowly heals corruption and blight.

Strengthens bond with nature spirits.

Must be fed sap or berries under moonlight.

“From rot, we rise.
From root, we remember.”

WHERE TO FIND THEM

MOSSFALL FENS
Hidden shrines half-sunk in mist and mire.

DREAMBARK GROVES
Ancient trees where the bark bleeds green.

THE SUNKEN ALTARS
Rotting temples offered to the sleeping one.

THEY SERVE THE SLEEPING TREE

“Hush now. Feed the roots.
The dream is deep.”

WHAT THEY VALUE

Patience over progress.

Sacrifice over comfort.

Secrets over truth.

Fertility over purity.

Cycles over endings.

HOW TO DEAL WITH THEM

Do not mock their faith.

Do not desecrate their groves.

Do not steal what grows.

EARN TRUST.
GIVE OFFERING.
WALK SOFTLY.
STAY UNNOTICED.

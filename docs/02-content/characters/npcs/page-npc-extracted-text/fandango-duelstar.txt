FANDANGO DUELSTAR

PERFORMER-WARRIOR OF THE PASSION STAGE;
DANCES AND DUELS WITH EQUAL FLAIR

Born beneath the velvet tents of Myrrala, Fandango Duelstar learned blade and ballet from the same mistress.
To her, combat is theater, and theater is truth.

She lives for the gasp between strikes—when fate leans forward and the world holds its breath.
Every step she takes is a promise to dazzle... or die trying.

KEY ITEM

FANDANGO DAGGER

Curved pink-steel blade; glows during duels and sings when blood is spilled.

Hums a haunting melody when it draws blood.

Glows brighter with each flawless step.

Drinks deep.
Feeds the drama.

A blade of art—as sharp as any true steel.

“Every cut is a note.
Every note leaves a mark.”

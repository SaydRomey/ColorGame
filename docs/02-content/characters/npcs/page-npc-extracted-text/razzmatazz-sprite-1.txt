RAZZMATAZZ SPRITE

TINY FEY OF MISCHIEF AND PARTIES;
BURSTS INTO PINK WHEN LAUGHING

Don’t trust the quiet.
That’s when I’m plotting.

I live for the pop, the flash, the moment no one sees coming.
I crash courts, crash feasts, and crash your serious face.

If there’s magic asleep, I’ll drum it awake.
Why be solemn in a world this loud with wonder?

I’m small, yes.
But I shake your perception like a tambourine.

SHE SPEAKS IN GIGGLES

“Fun is dangerous when it’s your only defense.”

“If it giggles in the dark...
it’s probably me.”

“I drummed a mountain awake once.
It danced.”

“Magic’s more fun when it’s not yours.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When helping unlock a sealed relic:

“Boom or bloom?
Depends if I’m bored.”

KEY ITEM

RAZZMATAZZ DRUM

A fey instrument; awakens slumbering magic in artifacts when played.

Awakens latent magic in relics and artifacts.

Plays different tunes for different kinds of magic.

The louder and wilder the beat, the deeper it stirs.

Cannot be silenced, only outplayed.

“If it sleeps, I stir.
If it stirs, I make it sing.”

WHERE TO FIND HER

MOONLIT FESTIVALS

FORGOTTEN THEATERS
Old stages where illusions go to play.

AFTERPARTY REALMS
The veil thins where the party never ends.

WHAT SHE VALUES

Laughter over seriousness.

Creativity over control.

Wonder over wisdom.

Freedom over rules.

Friends over fame.

HOW TO DEAL WITH HER

Keep up.
Stay playful.

Don’t try to cage her.

Offer wonder, not orders.

If you earn her laugh, you earn her help.

PLAY.

LIMERICK DRUID

TRICKSTER OF THE GLENS;
CHANTS IN VERSE TO CHARM BEASTS AND BREW ILLUSIONS

In the glens where moss mumbles and laughter lingers, he wanders—flute in hand and rhyme on tongue.
He speaks in limericks, for rhyme is a root and a key.

Beasts know his verses.
Vines untwist at his tune.

And those who cross him are cursed... but only for fun.

THEY SPEAK IN LIMERICKS

“Rhyme binds more than rope.
Ask the fox.”

“When the wind sings your name backward,
don’t answer.”

NOTES

♦ Speaks only in limericks.

♦ Trickster, illusionist, and master of playful magic.

♦ Walks the line between wisdom and mischief.

THE DRUID’S TENETS

RHYME IS ROOT
Words take hold.
A verse can move mountains or mend a feud.

PLAY BEFORE POWER
Mischief opens doors that force could never budge.

ALL THINGS HAVE SONG
Beasts, stones, and streams all keep time.

BREW WITH A SMILE
Potions sour without joy, and magic bites.

TRUTH HIDES IN FUN
A jest can speak truer than a sermon.

KEY ITEM

LIMERICK FLUTE

Woodwind carved from bog-oak; playing it conjures playful forest spirits.

Plays any tune the druid wills.

Summons playful forest spirits and tricksters.

Harmless to friend, troublesome to foe.

Echoes linger.
The forest remembers every note.

“A tune well played
is a prank the forest applauds.”

DIALOGUE APPENDIX — LIMERICKS

UPON FIRST MEETING

In the glen where the foxes all play,
I wandered and rhymed through the day.

If you follow the tune,
Don’t look at the moon —

It’s watching and wanders away.

OFFERING ADVICE

When vines block your path, don’t be mad,
They’re grumpy, not wicked or bad.

Just whisper a song,
Not too short or too long,

And they’ll move — if they’re feeling unclad.

ON ILLUSIONS

An echo can dance like a deer,
And giggle a foot from your ear.

If the trees start to hum,
You should probably run —

Or you’ll marry a mushroom, I fear.

WHEN USING HIS FLUTE

A note from this flute in my hand,
Can summon a trickster or band.

One puff and they leap,
Out of root, bark, or deep —

Though they rarely obey what I planned.

ABOUT THE PRISMWALKER

You’re colorless now — quite a feat,
A vessel both blank and complete.

Will you bloom or decay?
Well, either way...

The moss at your feet thinks you’re neat.

ON THE FOREST’S MAGIC

The forest remembers each tread,
And whispers your dreams when you’ve fled.

But step with intent,
And you may just prevent

What the vines had been planning instead.

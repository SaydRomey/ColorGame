SKOBELOFF DIVER

UNDERWATER RELIC HUNTER
FROM THE DEEP TEAL CITIES;
AGILE AND SILENT

Born where the currents hum ancient names, Skobeloff Diver moves like a rumor in water.
The deep teal cities raised them—half memory, half myth.

They dive not for gold, but for what time forgot.
Relics sleep in silted cathedrals.

They wake in careful hands.
No bubbles. No noise.

Only purpose, and the pull of what should not be found.

THEY SPEAK IN LOW TONES

“Below the blue, there’s only pressure and memory.”

“The deeper you dive, the older the lies become.”

“Light lies.
Pressure doesn’t.”

“This lens? It’s seen ghosts smile at their traps.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When spotting a hidden artifact:

“There. It blinks when your heart does.
Don’t ask how I know.”

THE DIVER’S TENETS

DEPTH REVEALS TRUTH
Go deeper.
Surfaces hide everything.

SECRETS ARE CURRENCY
What you find stays in the deep.

SILENCE IS SURVIVAL
Make no noise.
Leave no trail.

RESPECT THE OLD
These ruins are older than your greed.

CURRENTS REMEMBER
Water keeps every story it touches.

KEY ITEM

SKOBELOFF LENS

A monocle that sees through illusion and detects magical traps in dim light.

Sees through illusions and deceptions.

Detects magical traps and hidden mechanisms.

Works in darkness, murk, and even deep pressure.

Must be kept clean and in balance with the sea.

“The truth doesn’t hide.
It just waits below.”

WHERE TO FIND THEM

SUNKEN SPIRES
Ruined towers of the deep teal cities, now half-buried in silt.

DROWNED ARCHIVES
Libraries that sank with the scholars who betrayed them.

TIDAL TRENCHES
Deep rifts where pressure keeps other divers away.

THEY DIVE. THEY FIND. THEY DISAPPEAR.

“What time buries,
the deep returns.”

WHAT THEY VALUE

Knowledge over wealth.

History over glory.

Observation over action.

Balance over dominance.

Legacy over possession.

HOW TO DEAL WITH THEM

Earn their trust with silence and respect.

Do not follow.

Do not rush.

Offer truth, not treasure.

BE STILL.
BE HONEST.
BE WORTH REMEMBERING.

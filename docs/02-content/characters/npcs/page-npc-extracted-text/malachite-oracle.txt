MALACHITE ORACLE

GREEN-SEER OF ANCIENT RUINS;
SPEAKS IN CRYPTIC PROPHECY WHILE WRAPPED IN STONE-GREEN ROBES

She does not walk — she emerges.
From roots, from shadow, from memory.

Her robes are woven from the old ways, dyed in lichen and layered with silence.

She sees what others ignore — the fracture in the leaf, the lie in the vein, the truth behind the turning of seasons.
Her words are veils. Her visions are knives.

She does not offer comfort.
She offers clarity.

Some call her oracle.
Others call her the stone that listens.

SHE SPEAKS IN MOSS-WHISPERS

“When the stone speaks, silence obeys.”

“Change is the only truth the roots trust.”

“Fate is a serpent.
I only provide the fang.”

“I don’t speak prophecy.
I just repeat what the moss sings.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When asked for a vision:

“Bring me something forgotten.
Only then can I show you what’s ahead.”

THE ORACLE’S TENETS

ROOTS REMEMBER
Everything returns.
Nothing is lost.

CHANGE IS SACRED
Stagnation is rot.
Transformation is law.

TRUTH HIDES DEEP
What is seen is surface.
What is felt is real.

FATE IS NOT KIND
It offers no promises.
Only possibilities.

SILENCE IS A TEACHER
Listen deeper than words.
Hear what the stone knows.

KEY ITEM

MALACHITE FANG

Serpent-blade of verdant ore; poisons foes with dreams of transformation.

Coated in toxin that seeps into flesh and spirit.

Inflicts visions of the self as something new.

Those who survive are never the same.

Forged from the fang of a dreaming serpent.

“It doesn’t kill.
It reminds.”

WHERE TO FIND HER

OVERGROWN RUINS
Where the old ones built and time grew over.

SACRED STONE CIRCLES
Where earth and sky still listen.

WHISPERING POOLS
Reflections speak what tongues forget.

SHE SEES WHAT OTHERS IGNORE

“I DO NOT CHANGE FATE.
I ONLY SHOW ITS SHADOW.”

ORACLE IN BATTLE

SHE DOES NOT FIGHT.
SHE TRANSFORMS.

Binds the roots.

Clouds the mind.

Poisons the dream.

Becomes the storm.

“Let the world break.
I will become what remains.”

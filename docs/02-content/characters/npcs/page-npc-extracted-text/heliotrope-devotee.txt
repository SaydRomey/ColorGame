MYRRALA
MAGIC • DREAMS • FATE • ILLUSION

HELIOTROPE DEVOTEE

SOLAR CLERIC WHOSE POWER WAXES AS THE FLOWER TURNS;
HARBORS ETERNAL LOVE

She follows the turning of the light, as faithfully as the heliotrope follows the sun.
In her gaze is longing older than kingdoms, in her heart a love that time could not unmake.

Her prayers call down warmth and mercy, but there is fire in her devotion.
What she protects, she would burn the world for.

THEY SPEAK IN WHISPERS

“The sun doesn’t follow the flower.
It’s the other way ’round.”

“Devotion is light that blinds from behind.”

“I loved once, Now I just orbit.”

“The light that saves also sears.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

During sunrise rites:

“Watch the petals.
They know before we do.”

THE DEVOTEE’S TENETS

FOLLOW THE LIGHT
Seek the rising.
Trust the dawn.

Let truth illuminate.

LOVE WITHOUT CLAIM
Love is not possession, but perpetual offering.

TURN WITH THE WORLD
As the flower turns, so must the soul.

BRING WARMTH, NOT WRATH
Healing is the holiest flame.
Let mercy be your power.

REMEMBER THE BELOVED
Hold memory as prayer.
Love outlives the flesh.

RITES OF THE EVER-TURNING FLOWER

DAWN OFFERING
Offer dew and petals to greet the light.

SOLAR PRAYER
Prayer rises strongest when the sun is young.

PETAL WATCH
Observe the heliotrope’s turn for guidance.

HEART REMEMBRANCE
Recall the lost or loved to fuel sacred power.

NOON VOW
At zenith, renew vows to truth, not self.

KEY ITEM

HELIOTROPE BAND

Bracelet that glows with solar devotion; protects from dark curses at dawn.

Shields the wearer from dark curses at sunrise.

Glow intensifies as the sun rises.

Strengthened by acts of love and devotion.

Once per day at dawn, nullifies one curse or ill effect.

“The sun remembers who loved it first.”

WHERE TO FIND HER

SUNBLOOM SHRINES
Hilltop shrines where light falls first.

PETAL COURTYARDS
Gardens of heliotrope and sacred blooms.

DAWNSEEKER MONASTERIES
Monasteries that rise with the sun.

“SHE DOES NOT CHASE THE SUN.
SHE BECOMES ITS REMEMBRANCE.”

The flower turns.
The light returns.

So too does love,
even when it burns.

I walk where morning speaks.
I weep what others hide.

For every soul I heal,
I carry one inside.

The sun will rise.
And I will be here—

petals open,
heart unchanged.

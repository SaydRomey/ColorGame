AURALITH
MEMORY • DECAY • SILENCE • ALCHEMY

CAPUT MORTUUM ASCETIC

ALCHEMIST OF DECAY AND TRANSMUTATION;
WEARS THE COLOR OF DRIED BLOOD AND RUST

The Ascetic walks the thin line between life and dissolution.
He seeks not immortality, but understanding—distilling truth from the refuse of flesh, metal, and memory.

HE SPEAKS IN FUNDAMENTS

“Death isn’t the end —
it’s the element most resist.”

“Let rot teach you what gold never will.”

“I studied endings until they started talking back.”

“What decays can be distilled.”

CONTEXTUAL LINE

When offering alchemical aid:

“It won’t taste like hope,
But it’ll buy you time.”

PATHS OF HIS STUDY

DECAY MADE INSTRUCTIVE
He dissects, observes, and respects the cycle of unmaking.

ALCHEMY OF TRANSITION
Turning base things into new essences—never creating, only shifting.

THE PATIENCE OF DUST
Time is his crucible.
Rushing the process invites catastrophe.

KNOWLEDGE WITHOUT EGO
He records what others fear to learn.
Not to teach—only to preserve.

DETACHMENT AS DISCIPLINE
Desire clouds the mixture.
He owns nothing that can be taken.

KEY ITEM

CAPUT MORTUUM PHYLACTERY

Dusty alchemical relic; binds part of one’s soul to forestall death by years.

Anchors a fragment of the soul in stasis.

Delays death, but at the cost of memory and warmth.

One use per moon cycle.
The price increases.

“What lives too long
forgets how to live.”

ALCHEMICAL DISCIPLINES

PUTREFACTION
Breaks matter down to its base filth.
Every substance returns willingly—or not.

ESSENCE DISTILLATION
Separates truth from contaminants.
Yields potent elixirs—rarely without consequence.

SOUL PRESERVATION
Binds what fades.
Identity frays the longer it is kept from the cycle.

CORROSIVE TRANSFORM
Changes form through dissolution.
Dangerous, unstable, and irreversible.

ASHEN REVELATION
Burns away illusions and memory alike.
Clarity comes at the cost of comfort.

WHERE TO FIND HIM

THE HEMLOCK WORKS
A sunken laboratory choked with roots and rust.
Entry is permitted by silence alone.

THE WEEPING VAULT
An underground archive of spoiled texts and failed experiments.

THE MORTUARY GROVES
A forest where nothing wholly dies.
He forages among the in-between.

“I DO NOT SEEK TO ESCAPE DEATH,
BUT TO NEGOTIATE WITH IT.”

He was once a student of royal alchemists, until he asked the wrong question:

“Why must everything return to nothing?”

Cast out, he walked until the world stopped answering him with comfort.

Now he listens where the living do not —
and writes in the language of endings.

DOPPELSHADE

MIRROR OF THE SELFLIED

“...Your face, your stance, your fear —
a heartbeat behind you.”

“I saw myself dodge.
But I hadn’t moved yet.”

TYPE

Illusory Mimic / Mirrorborne Trickster

THREAT LEVEL

Variable — Based on player complexity.

FIRST EMERGED

Post-Shattering; earliest recorded in Myrrala.

DESCRIPTION

A self-propagating illusion created from unfulfilled choices, ego residue, and mirrorborne emotion.

Forms when lingering doubt is reflected too long in the Blooming Veil’s mirrorstones.

Mimics appearance, equipment, and behavioral tics — even spellcasting stutters or dodge habits.

ENCOUNTER TRAITS

1. Mimics the player’s current appearance and equipment loadout.

2. Reacts to your last 3 movement patterns, trying to catch your next input.

3. Damaging it before revealing its illusion form causes hue feedback — a psychic jolt that scrambles your HUD.

4. They are never quite perfect.
That’s their trap. That’s your warning.

5. Appears behind or near mirrors, often posing mid-gesture.

6. Opens with your most frequent spell or attack combo — but slightly early.

7. Adapts quickly.
Second encounter includes all choices made in first.

A. HUE INTERACTION

Weak to truth-bound light, Aurelian.

Weak to sound-based rhythm, Cobalt.

Immune to illusions unless constructed by the player using Violet.

Reflected projectiles strike for full damage if released with reversed timing.

B. BEHAVIOR PATTERN

1. Poses mid-gesture near reflective surfaces.

2. Copies your frequent combo.

3. Predicts the next input slightly early.

C. KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Temporarily stuns if caught in a Thulian Mirror beam or Heliotrope Band aura.

Becomes unstable when confronted by multiple dialogue paths or player contradictions.

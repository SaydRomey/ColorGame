HELIOTROPIC LURKER

THORN IN THE TWILIGHT

“It sees only light.
But dreams in shadow.”

THREAT LEVEL

HIGH

Ambush predator that thrives in twilight and bloom.

TYPE

Lurking Flora / Light-Stalker

FIRST EMERGED

After the Withering Bloom, under Liseran Shrine.

HUE INTERACTION

AMPLIFIED BY LIGHT

Glows stronger and more aggressive when struck with Radiant Echo or other light-based magic.

WEAKENED BY DARKNESS

Shadow, darkness, and veiling abilities reduce its strength and senses.

HELIOTROPE AWARENESS

If the player wears the Heliotrope Band, it becomes aware but hesitant—waiting for your guard to drop.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

SUDDEN DARKNESS

Vulnerable to shadow bursts or darkness illusions, which cause it to recoil.

DISTRACTION BY LIGHT

Can be distracted by moving light sources like fireflies, glow-orbs, or illusions.

MID-BLOOM VULNERABILITY

If caught mid-bloom, its petals become brittle.
A precise strike can destroy its nerve-center.

DESCRIPTION

A vine-creature that moves only when light shifts.
It uses heliotrope blossoms as sensors, blooming toward sources of hue.

Its strikes are sudden—from petal to fang in less than a heartbeat.

It is silent, but players report feeling “watched” before it strikes.

ANATOMY

HELIOTROPE BLOOMS

Sensitive petals detect hue and radiant energy.
When threatened, they flare open.

PETAL-FANGS

Inner petals split into barbed maws in an instant, delivering poisoned bites.

THORNED TENDRILS

Flexible, barbed vines latch onto prey, draining hue over time.

COIL OF MEMORY

Its body spirals like sunlight trying to become memory—each loop stores echoes of warmth it once knew.

ROOTED HIDE

Covered in bark-like armor and moss, resistant to blunt or elemental force.

NERVE-CORE

Hidden deep within the bloom cluster.
Exposed only when Best-bloom.

ATTACK PATTERNS

BLOOM BURST

Erupts from the ground, scattering petal-shards that inflict hue bleed.

TENDRIL GRASP

Lunges with thorned vines to pull the target close, applying slow and hue drain.

SUNKISS SPIRE

Coils upward, summoning light-seeking vines that track the player.
Best dodged in shadow.

All attacks intensify when exposed to light.
Seek shade to survive.

NOTES

Some believe the Lurker can remember those it failed to catch, and will follow them across regions, reappearing near heliotrope growths.

Defeating it may yield a Withered Bloom-Core, usable in memory-bound rituals or light-sensitive traps.

LORE ENTRY

From the Garden Notes of the Liseran Caretaker.

“The lilies grieve when it feeds.
They know it was once part of them.”

“The Heliotropic Lurker was not planted.
It grew from sorrow, a vine that refused to wither when the sun set on its garden.”

“It stalks by scent, by shadow-angle, by memory of warmth.
It never hunts in full light, but flares when dawn rises—a final hunger before retreat.”

“Some say it only appears to those carrying heliotrope—the band, the bloom, or the guilt.
Others believe it is drawn to unresolved farewells.”

“If it sees you, it won’t chase.
It will wait.”

— Liseran Caretaker, beneath the Weeping Lilies.

BEHAVIOR

AMBUSH HUNTER

Burrows under bloom-pools and vine walls; bursts up when the player lingers or casts healing spells.

THORNED LATCH

Latches with thorned tendrils that drain hue slowly over time.

TWILIGHT STALKER

Most active in low light and twilight.
Retreats during full light or after being seen through a Thulian Mirror.

WATCHER’S PATIENCE

Does not pursue.
It waits in silence, observing movement, warmth, and lingering light.

POSSIBLE DROPS

WITHERED BLOOM-CORE (RARE)

Pulsing core of dried light.
Used in memory rituals and advanced traps.

TWILIGHT VINE (COMMON)

Flexible vine still warm with fading hue.
Used in alchemical binds.

HELIOTROPE PETAL (COMMON)

Residual petal that retains a faint light-memory.
Can be infused into gear.

LURKER’S ESSENCE (UNIQUE)

Rare essence that whispers locations of hidden blooms when held at night.

LAVA-FUSED BEHEMOTH

THE EMBERCROWNED

“Born in magma.
Tempered by wrath.”

THREAT LEVEL
EXTREME

Environmental hazard.
Capable of wiping unprepared parties.

TYPE
Elemental Beast / Obsidian Juggernaut

FIRST EMERGED
Deepflare Surge, Post-Prism Fracture.

HUE INTERACTION

IMMUNE TO CRIMSON
Absorbs fire-based damage and gains power, triggering lava bursts.

VULNERABLE TO COBALT
Chill or steam attacks cause armor fissures and movement slow.

EARTHEN STONEBIND
Can root its limbs briefly when targeting exposed fault points.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

MOLTEN VENT
Upper back vent overheats and glows.
Strike it to stun and expose it.

COOLED TERRAIN
Freezing lava paths beneath it can immobilize limbs and slow movement.

SONIC VIBRATIONS
Seismic runes, drums, or vibration spells destabilize its footing and balance.

FOCUS FIRE
Coordinated attacks on legs can topple it, exposing back vent longer.

DESCRIPTION

The Lava-Fused Behemoth is a slow-moving mountain of molten power.

Its armored hide is obsidian-formed, fissured with glowing lava.
It does not hunt — it erupts.

Territorial, ancient, and nearly unkillable in open terrain, its presence reshapes battlefields and can block entire routes through the Ember Expanse.

ANATOMY

SPINAL LAVA VENTS
Channel magma to the surface.
Overcharge leads to explosive bursts of flame.

OBSIDIAN PLATES
Naturally formed armor that deflects most physical and magical attacks.

MOLTEN CORE
Heart of liquid fire.
Unknown if it is alive — or just eternal heat.

EMBERCROWN SPIKES
Store pressure and release during charges or lava floods.

GLOWING FAULTLINES
Heat-lines across its body.
Some are decoys. Some lead deep within.

CRUSHING LIMBS
Each step causes shockwaves and leaves behind burning terrain.

ATTACKS & ABILITIES

SEISMIC STOMP
Slams the ground, causing shockwaves that stagger and crack terrain.

LAVA BREATH
Sweeps a cone of molten fire forward, leaving burning pools behind.

EMBERCROWN BURST
Vents overpressure from its back, causing radial lava explosions.

MOLTEN CHARGE
Locks onto a target and charges, ignoring obstacles and dealing massive damage.

NOTES

Immune to most status ailments except freeze, stun, or root.

Lava it leaves behind lasts several seconds.

Fleeing Behemoths will trigger collapse events or volcano eruptions.

LORE ENTRY

Chiseled into a broken obsidian tablet near the Furnace Maw.

“We did not create it.
We unburied it.”

“They say the Lava-Fused was born from beneath — a beast the size of a siege tower, resting in magma until rage woke it.

Not a dragon, not a titan — just the shape fire chose to wear.”

“Its back vents fury.
Its breath melts spears.

The earth bends around it.
Nothing in Vireya moves so slowly — or kills so fast.”

“Some Ember Priests believe it carries a buried heart — the first fire, unquenched and still dreaming of destruction.

Others say it has no heart.
That’s why it burns so loud.”

— Translation by Elarin Flintwake, shortly before the Furnace Maw collapsed.

BEHAVIOR

TERRITORIAL
Patrols molten ravines, lava rivers, and ancient forges.

ERUPTIVE CHARGES
Moves slow until provoked, then charges in a straight line with explosive force.

LAVA BREATH
Breathes gouts of flame from spinal vents.
Can melt terrain and force players to reposition.

LAVA FLOODS
Occasionally bursts the ground, releasing waves of lava that reshape the battlefield.

POSSIBLE DROPS

EMBERCROWN CORE (LEGENDARY)
Overcharged core used to forge Prism-infused weapons and armor.

LAVA-FUSED SCALE (RARE)
Resilient obsidian scale that retains elemental resistance.

MOLTEN ESSENCE (UNCOMMON)
Concentrated fire residue used in crafting and enhancements.

BEHEMOTH’S ROAR (UNIQUE)
A resonant echo crystal that can be used to unleash a fearsome shockwave ability.

“IT DOESN’T SCREAM.
IT SIGHS...

AS IF DISAPPOINTED THE WORLD STILL RESISTS BURNING.”

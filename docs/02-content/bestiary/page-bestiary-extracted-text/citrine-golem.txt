CITRINE GOLEM

THE UNBREAKING GLEAM

“A gem is eternal.
So is its temper.”

THREAT LEVEL

HIGH

Nearly impervious under sunlight.

TYPE

Radiant Construct / Elemental Juggernaut

FIRST EMERGED

Built during the early Aurelian Temple Age to guard vaults and forbidden scroll-tombs.

HUE INTERACTION

ABSORBS AURELIAN ATTACKS

Completely absorbs Yellow hue attacks and reflects them as blinding flares.

SUSCEPTIBLE TO COBALT

Cobalt (cold and storm) attacks inflict increased damage and can overload its harmonic core.

VULNERABLE TO VIBRATIONS

Sonic and echo-based attacks disrupt its inner resonance and can stagger it.

VERDANT ROOTS CAN CRACK IT

Roots that take hold in shadow can fracture its surface over time.

DESCRIPTION

Towering crystalline behemoth with arms like sculpted obelisks and a faceted citrine heart.
It emits faint harmonic tones when struck, as if humming to itself.

Carvings across its back depict solar rites and forgotten oaths.

ANATOMY

CITRINE HEART

Radiant core that powers and sustains the golem.

RADIANT PLATING

Crystalline armor fused with ancient Aurelian sigils.
Extremely resilient under sunlight.

SOLAR RUNES

Channel sunlight and store radiant energy within the body.

HARMONIC CORE

Hidden chambers amplify light and vibrations.
Disrupted by echo or cold resonance.

LORE ENTRY

Scrawled notes from a sand-shielded expedition journal, author unknown.

“We found it half-buried beyond the Gamboge Vault — just another glimmering ridge in the sand, we thought.
Until it stood.”

“It didn’t roar. Didn’t run.
It simply... turned.

The light caught in its chest gem, and something inside me trembled.
Like I owed it something.”

“Every strike we landed was swallowed — reflected in glints, as if it watched our failures and polished them.”

“I think it waits not to fight — but to endure.
It judges worth by how hard you try before quitting.”

BEHAVIOR

Stationary until provoked or until artifact proximity is detected.

Stomps ground to fracture terrain.
Creates radiant aftershocks that slow movement.

“GLEAM PULSE”: Releases a radial lightburst upon taking damage, stunning nearby attackers.

Can regenerate minor cracks if exposed to direct sunlight too long.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

COBALT OVERLOAD

Cobalt’s Stormglass Barrier can refract its light internally, causing harmonic overload and major damage.

FIGHT IN SHADOW

Best fought in shaded or eclipse-affected areas, where its light absorption is greatly reduced.

ECHO RESONANCE

Echo-charged percussion weapons, like Prismhammer, disrupt its inner resonance.

NOTES

Citrine Golems were never meant to kill — only to withstand.

Their presence marks places the sun itself once vowed to protect.

They are bound by ancient oaths.
Some say they recognize those who carry a spark of Aurelian blood or devotion.

Many vaults remain sealed as long as the Golem still stands.

“If it still stands, perhaps your purpose does not.”

POSSIBLE DROPS

CITRINE HEARTFRAGMENT

Core shard used in high-tier artifacts and altar upgrades.

AURELIAN SIGIL STONE

Engraved stone containing a fragment of ancient solar oaths.

GLEAMRESIN CRYSTAL

Used in crafting radiant potions and sun-aligned weapon coatings.

VAULTKEEPER’S CREST

Rare relic that grants resistance to light and holy-based damage.

SOLAR CHANT SCROLL

Teaches a powerful Aurelian hymn or radiant buff spell.

“BUILT TO SHINE. FORGED TO ENDURE.
ITS LIGHT DOES NOT FORGIVE THE WEAK.”

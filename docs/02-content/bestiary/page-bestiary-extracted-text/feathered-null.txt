FEATHERED NULL

THE STILLBORN SKY

“The wind gave up.
It became still.”

THREAT LEVEL

MODERATE

Not overtly hostile, but disrupts movement, magic, and ritual.

TYPE

Colorless Entity / Anti-Storm Apparition

FIRST EMERGED

During the Dimming of the Third Gale.

HUE INTERACTION

SUPPRESSES TRAVERSAL

Disables hue-based traversal abilities, including gliding, storm-riding, and wind surges, within its aura.

DIMS BOUND COLORS

Hue intensity fades near it.
Crimson surges weaken and Cobalt reactions slow.

NULLS HUE SENSORS

Makes some Hue-detection spells and relics less effective or delayed.

DISRUPTED BY VERDANT

Skygrowth or Verdant wind rites can restore buoyancy and counter its suppression.

DESCRIPTION

A rare Nullborne being shaped by stillness itself.

The Feathered Null hovers in Iriseld’s upper strata, its presence silencing the winds and draining the storm’s will.
More melancholic than malevolent, it exerts passive denial of momentum—anchoring what should soar.

It is said to be an angel of the Prism—one who was born when the light broke, and the wind remembered nothing.

ANATOMY

VOID EYE

A sightless gaze that measures motion and intention.

NULL HEART CAVITY

Drains kinetic and magical momentum.
Glows faintly when it prepares a pulse.

TATTERED WING-FIBERS

Feathers that tear and unravel from forgotten hues.

HUELESS PLUMAGE

Absorbs color.
Feathers hold remnants of lost storms and emotions.

DRIFT THREADS

Frayed currents of wind-memory that delay movement around it.

WEIGHTLESS TAIL

Composed of silence and broken updraft.
Leaves no trace when it moves.

LORE ENTRY

Transcription from the Glaucous Feather Shrine.

“It didn’t fall.
The sky simply stopped lifting it.”

“When the Prism cracked, there were angels of color—fragments given shape by grief and wind.
This was one.”

“The Feathered Null was born not in flight, but in forgetting how flight felt.”

“Its wings bear no color.
Its voice no echo. It moves where the wind used to be.”

“Glaucous said it hovered outside her cliffs for three nights, silent—not a threat.
Just watching. Measuring.”

“And when it left...
the storm forgot how to speak for a day.”

“We believe it is not hostile.
But we are not sure it remembers how to be kind.”

BEHAVIOR

Does not pursue.
It drifts.

Engages only when provoked or approached during a storm ritual.

BEHAVIOR CYCLE

NULL PULSE

Releases a wave of stillness that disables midair movement and glider control for several seconds.

WING VEIL

Wraps itself in a dome of pale wind, concealing its position and muffling tracking or detection.

ECHO STILLNESS

Creates a field of silence that interrupts spellcasting, dialogue, and channeled abilities.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

SOUND OF THE SKY

Momentarily repelled or staggered by sky-sound: storm chimes, flutes, and resonant bells.

TRIANGULATION OF HUES

Unstable when caught between multiple Hue sources: the player, a relic, and an echo site.

EMOTIVE WIND SURGES

Vulnerable for a brief moment during memory-triggered wind surges that carry strong emotion.

NOTES

The Glaucous Wing claims this creature is “a hymn never sung.”

Cannot be bound, named, or remembered by normal means.
Journals written near it lose ink, memories blur, and runes fade.

Some seekers claim that if you stand in stillness with no intent but peace, it may hover near without harm.

No corpse, no trace.
Only feathers that fall upward when no one is looking.

POSSIBLE DROPS

NULLFEATHER (RARE)

A feather that holds no hue.
Used in crafting storm-reliant gear.

QUIET GALE CORE

A condensed stillwind essence.
Useful in advanced traversal relics.

GLAUCOUS REMNANT

Fragment of a forgotten chorus.
Enchants flutes and windweapons.

WIND-FORGET TOKEN

Token of motion denied.
Used to reset momentum curses and traps.

“IT DOES NOT HATE THE WIND.
IT IS WHAT REMAINS WHEN THE WIND FORGETS ITSELF.”

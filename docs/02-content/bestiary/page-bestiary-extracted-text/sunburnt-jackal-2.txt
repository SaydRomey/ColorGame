SUNBURNT JACKAL

THE SCORCHED HOWL

“It never learned the difference between shadow and prey.”

TYPE

Desiccated Predator / Heat-Hound

THREAT LEVEL

MODERATE —
Lethal in packs or near radiant spells

FIRST EMERGED

Late Color War —
After the Dawn Siege of Luxeran

ANATOMY BREAKDOWN

1. BLISTERED SKULL

Skull plates are cracked and heat-warped.

Eyes are clouded and mostly blind.

2. HEAT-SENSE NODES

Swollen nasal ridges and sinus channels detect radiant heat like scent.

Wires the brain directly to warmth.

3. SCORCHED MANE

Patchy, burnt fur offers little protection.

Heat bakes skin and hair to brittle ash.

4. SUNBURNED FLESH

Copper-blistered skin cracks easily, oozing scorch-saliva that causes burning.

5. RAZOR FANGS

Teeth are chipped and heat-tempered.

Bite inflicts searing wounds and lingering burn.

6. DUNE-ADAPTED LIMBS

Long, sinewy legs built for pursuit across loose sand.

Pads sense vibrations and heat.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Roams sun-scorched dunes, ruined temples, and heat-radiant wastelands.

Drawn to radiant light sources, spells, and body heat.

Most active during midday and early evening.

Often found near dying torches, sun mirages, or relic heat.

Fears cold, deep shadow, and rainfall.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

SENSE HEAT → HOWL CALL → STALK & CIRCLE → LUNGE STRIKE → FEAST & RETREAT

Hunts by triangulating body heat and radiant echoes.

Howls disrupt composure and distort vision.

Packs coordinate using echoing howls.

Will chase light sources or heat signatures as a distraction.

HUE INTERACTION

Drawn to Aurelian (Yellow) magic or any active light source.

Avoids deep shadow or cold environments.

Can be stunned by Cobalt (Blue) flashburst spells or illusions that mask heat.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Ice and shadow spells dampen senses.

Easily misled by artificial light or false heat signatures.

Loud noises, explosives, and sonic artifacts disorient pack coordination.

LORE ENTRY

“I watched it circle the mirage fire for hours,
never knowing it wasn’t real.

It yipped and snapped at flickering sand.

They don’t see.
They feel heat like scent —
like music.

My torch nearly killed me.

I had to drop it and run silent into the dunes.”

NOTES

Desert guides carry heat-absorbent stones or cold-blood salves to mask their presence.

It’s said that if a Sunburnt Jackal is howling at an empty flame, it’s mourning a prey that never burned.

FIELD NOTE

Travel at dawn or dusk.

Keep shade close and torches low.

If it howls…
it already knows where you are.

NULLSHADE ECHO

REFLECTION WITHOUT MERCY

It doesn’t attack.
It remembers you doing so.

“You moved first.
It simply responded... eventually.”

NULLSHADE BESTIARY
ENTRY • 17

CLASSIFICATION DOSSIER

Name: Nullshade Echo

Type: Mirror Construct / Memory Apparition

Threat Level: Unique — Mechanically Unconventional

First Emerged: Unknown — only seen in Nullshade’s Wound

DESCRIPTION

A ghostly silhouette of the Prismwalker—not as they are, but as they once were.

Retraces steps, replays actions, and confronts the player with reflection rather than violence.

Appears as a translucent silhouette of the player, or a past version of them.

Moves a second after the player moves, mimicking attacks, spells, decisions, combat stances, and dialogue trees already taken.

It doesn’t initiate combat; it responds and reflects.

Striking it may drop your own health, replay a past NPC line, reverse a recent action, or undo progress if it recognizes guilt or contradiction.

DELAYED MIMICRY

Acts with a precise one-second delay.
Every movement, stance, or spell is a remembered replay.

MEMORY RESONANCE

Constructed from the weight of your decisions, emotions, and unresolved moments.

INVERTED HUE REFLECTION

All hues are inverted and returned.
Attacks or magic rebound with opposite alignment.

PAST-SELF SILHOUETTE

Appears as you—or as you were.
Sometimes younger. Sometimes wiser. Always familiar.

REFLECTIVE EDGE DISTORTION

Edges refract, fracture, and shift like broken mirrors—never truly solid, never truly still.

HUE INTERACTION

Immune to all hue-based attacks.

Any color used is reflected with inverted effect.

Vulnerable only to inaction, truth, or nonviolent choices.

Use of the Bistre Lantern reveals its source echo—what memory it’s drawn from.

BISTRE LANTERN — SOURCE REVEAL

Reveals the memory fragment from which the Echo was drawn.

BEHAVIOR PATTERN

1. Appears at critical memory junctions or lore-rich areas.

2. Mimics player behavior with a one-second delay, including pausing, dodging, and emoting.

3. Occasionally “speaks” using past lines the player said in other regions.

4. Reflects choices already taken, confronting contradictions or guilt.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Standing perfectly still causes it to vanish after 12 seconds.

Reciting or triggering remembered truths via collected lore calms it.

Touching a Shatterglass Pool while near it causes mutual reflection—both vanish.

ONE-SECOND DELAYED MIMIC SEQUENCE

STEP FORWARD

Player Action → 1.0s → Echo Response

DODGE ROLL

Player Action → 1.0s → Echo Response

HEAVY ATTACK

Player Action → 1.0s → Echo Response

CAST SPELL

Player Action → 1.0s → Echo Response

The Echo is always exactly one breath behind.

SHATTERGLASS POOL INTERACTION

BEFORE

Player and Echo stand near the pool.

CONTACT

Mutual reflection occurs.

AFTER

Both vanish.
No remainder.

NOTES / FIELD OBSERVATIONS

Multiple Nullshade Echoes can appear in NG+ modes, each based on a different past choice.

Some can be interacted with through dialogue.

One was seen whispering a phrase the player had never spoken aloud.

If defeated properly, it fades whispering:
“It wasn’t wrong. It was just... before.”

LORE ENTRY

Entry recorded by the Caput Mortuum Ascetic in “Reverberations of Unselfing”

“I watched one retrace my sister’s footsteps...
It walked through every choice, every silence.”

“The Echo did not bleed.
It did not speak.

It simply... reminded.”

“Whatever they are, they cannot be bested.
They can only be forgiven.”

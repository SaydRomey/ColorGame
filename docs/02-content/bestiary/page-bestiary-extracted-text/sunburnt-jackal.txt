SUNBURNT JACKAL

THE SCORCHED HOWL

“It never learned the difference between shadow and prey.”

TYPE

Desiccated Predator / Heat-Hound

THREAT LEVEL

MODERATE —
Lethal in packs or near radiant spells

FIRST EMERGED

Late Color War —
After the Dawn Siege of Luxeran

ANATOMY BREAKDOWN

1. SCORCHED HIDE

Leather-thin skin baked copper-red.
Cracked and scarred from generations under merciless sun.

2. BLISTERED EYES

Milky, clouded, and mostly blind.
Heat has destroyed sight, leaving only the faintest perception of light.

3. HEAT-SENSE NODES

Raised ridges along the nose and skull detect microscopic temperature changes and radiant traces.

4. RAZOR FANGS

Teeth are fissured and brittle, retaining searing residue that burns wounds on contact.

5. RIB-THIN FRAME

Emaciated from constant hunger and dehydration.
Built for speed, not power.

6. SINEWED LIMBS

Long, jointed legs end in cracked, sand-worn claws —
perfect for explosive bursts across dunes.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Roams sun-baked dunes, baked ruins, and heat-radiant sites.

Most active during midday and in open heat.

Often circles campfires, braziers, or living sources of radiant magic.

Fears cold.
At dusk or under cloud cover, becomes sluggish and reclusive.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

SENSE HEAT → HOWL CALL → LUNGE STRIKE → CIRCLE & RESET

Hunts by triangulating body heat and radiant echoes.

Will chase thrown torches or glowing relics as a distraction.

Packs coordinate using howls that ricochet strangely in heat haze.

Cowers from dusk or cloudy conditions;
most active at midday.

HUE INTERACTION

Drawn to Aurelian (Yellow) magic or any active light source.

Avoids deep shadow or cold environments.

Can be stunned by Cobalt (Blue) flashburst spells or illusions that mask heat.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Ice and shadow spells dampen senses.

Easily misled by artificial light or false heat signatures.

Loud noises, explosives, and sonic artifacts disorient pack coordination.

LORE ENTRY

“I watched it circle the mirage fire for hours,
never knowing it wasn’t real.

It yipped and snapped at flickering sand.

Not blind — not quite —
but worse:

its mind burns with too much sun and too little shadow.”

“They say these beasts were once clever pack hunters, adapted to the shifting deserts.

But now…
now they worship warmth like it’s a god that betrayed them.”

“At night, I found one curled into a broken clay brazier, whimpering at the cold stars.”

— Excerpt attributed to a Fulvous Hunter

NOTES

Desert guides carry heat-absorbent stones or cold-blood salves to mask their presence.

It’s said that if a Sunburnt Jackal is howling at an empty flame, it’s mourning a prey that never burned.

POSSIBLE DROPS

SUNBLISTER FANG

Jagged, heat-seared fang retaining embers.
Used in crafting Mirage-Scent Cloaks and desert survival gear.

FIELD NOTE

Approach without light.

Walk with the wind behind you.

And never run across the open dunes with fire in hand.

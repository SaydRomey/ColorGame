CHARTREUSE HUSK

VESSEL OF ROTTEN JOY

“A tonic left too long to rot.”

THREAT LEVEL

HIGH

Dangerous area-denial and toxic attacks

TYPE

Corrupted Human / Alchemical Abomination

FIRST EMERGED

Late Post-Shattering Era

INTRODUCTION

Former monks transformed by corrupted, spoiled alchemical tonics.

Bodies swollen, skin pale-green and translucent, bubbling with unstable mixtures.
Constantly emit clouds of acidic vapor that induce confusion and disorientation.

HUE INTERACTION

ACIDIC DISRUPTION

Acid mist disrupts and corrodes hue-based abilities, causing color disorientation.

VULNERABLE TO VIOLET

Purifying illusions and spiritual clarity weaken and purge the Husk.

WEAK TO CRIMSON

Flame ignites their unstable mixtures, causing violent explosions.

RESISTANT TO BROWN

Decay and earthen manipulation have little effect on their rotted flesh.

DISRUPTED BY LIGHT

Bright purifying light clears their mists and reveals their true form.

DESCRIPTION

Chartreuse Husks were once monks of the Carthusian order, devoted to crafting potions that invigorated body and spirit.

Corrupted by spoiled alchemical brews, their bodies are now bloated and semi-translucent, filled with bubbling, unstable mixtures.

They continuously emit acidic vapor that corrodes both body and sanity.

ANATOMY

ACIDIC VAPOR PORES

Emit disorienting, corrosive mist.

GAS-FILLED LUNGS

Produce choking vapors and manic laughter.

SWOLLEN SACS

Filled with volatile alchemical slurries.

CORRUPTED HEART

Pumps unstable tonics that keep the Husk moving.

RITUAL ROBES

Soaked and decayed by years of exposure to toxic brews.

ALCHEMICAL CENSER

Carries remnants of toxic brews that feed its emissions.

LORE ENTRY

From “Journal of Spoiled Vessels,” by Malachite Oracle of Tinctara.

“Alchemy teaches patience; let a potion age carefully, and you find power.

But leave it too long—neglect its whispers—and you get something worse than poison.

I saw this tragedy unfold in the heart of our order, among monks who drank deeply of spoiled brews, mistaking decay for wisdom.

They laughed first—uncontrollable laughter echoing through cloisters until their faces strained and their eyes dimmed with an eerie chartreuse glow.

Then came the swelling, skin stretching like ripe fruit about to burst.
They wandered corridors in confusion, their laughter turning sour, acid mist dripping from their lips as joy curdled to madness.

These Chartreuse Husks roam Huevale now, abandoned by reason, their laughter an endless, acidic mockery.

If you hear them, hold your breath and run—because laughter has never been this deadly.”

— Malachite Oracle

BEHAVIOR

Moves slowly but persistently, emitting acidic laughter and toxic mists.

Seeks to corner prey, trapping them within clouds of confusion and corrosive vapor.

Releases bursts of acidic vapor or throws alchemical slugs that splash and create hazardous pools.

Their laughter induces confusion, blurring vision and distorting perception.

Often found wandering near abandoned shrines or ancient brewing chambers.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

PURIFYING EFFECTS

Highly vulnerable to cleansing spells, Yellow, Violet, or White hues.

CRIMSON FLAME

Direct fire ignites their volatile mixtures, causing explosive death.

ILLUSION CLARITY

Violet illusions expose their true form and disrupt their acidic aura.

SLOW AND HEAVY

Their slow movement makes them easy to evade or target from range.

NOTES

Upon death, the Husk erupts in a violent alchemical explosion, leaving behind pools of acidic sludge that linger for some time.

The pools continue to bubble and corrupt, damaging anyone who enters them.

Survivors report lingering confusion, vertigo, and a bitter taste in their mouths, as if their own laughter has turned sour.

Some believe the Husks are still seeking the “perfect brew,” endlessly chasing joy in decay.

POSSIBLE DROPS

VOLATILE BILE VIAL

Unstable alchemical fluid used in advanced explosive or corrosive crafting.

HUSK GLAND

A swollen gland that still secretes acidic vapors.
Used in alchemical concoctions.

SPOILED ELIXIR REMNANT

Residual essence of corrupted brews.
Valuable to alchemists and collectors.

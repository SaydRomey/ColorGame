HUE LEECHES

THE DRIP OF STOLEN COLOR

“They shimmer with borrowed light—and leave you pale.”

THREAT LEVEL

MINOR

Annoying in small numbers; dangerous when left unchecked in swarms.

TYPE

Color-Parasitic Aberration

FIRST EMERGED

Post-Prism Fracture

HUE INTERACTION

TARGET ACTIVE HUES

Prefer targets with recently activated spells or abilities.

NEUTRAL TO PHYSICAL PRESENCE

Highly reactive to visual spell cues or aura pulses.

TURNS GRAY AND INERT

When exposed to complete hue-suppression fields, such as Nullshade auras or unaligned relics.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

AOE BURSTS

Vulnerable to area-of-effect bursts, especially of non-hue damage types such as shockwaves or force spells.

PRISM FLASH TALISMANS

Disrupt their hue tethering and repel them briefly.
Easier to defeat in the moment of disruption.

MIRROR-ANGLED LIGHT

Can be stunned with a precise angle of light, then popped like fragile bubbles.

DESCRIPTION

Hue Leeches resemble globs of suspended ink, tinged with glimmering rainbow pulses.

They drift in the air or hover near hue-aligned shrines, drawn to magic or activated color states.

When they latch onto the player, they drain a hue, causing equipped abilities to “gray out” for several seconds, rendering them unusable.

ANATOMY

COLOR MEMBRANE

Semi-transparent skin ripples with stolen hue resonance.

SIPHON TENTACLES

Absorb active hue energy on contact.
Secrete dulling fluid that disrupts flow.

DRIP TRAIL

Leaves shimmering residue that may attract more leeches or react to light.

HUE CORE

The pulsing nucleus stores drained color.
Destroys itself if severely jolted.

GEL CAPILLARIES

Carry absorbed hue throughout the body.
Glow brighter after a successful drain.

ANTI-ESSENCE GLAND

Excretes a numbing mucus that slows recovery and intuition.

DRAIN EFFECTS

INITIAL LATCH

A leech latches on.

HUE DRAIN

Your active hue is drained.

DEPLETED

Abilities “gray out” and cannot be used until it is removed.

NOTES

Not bound to one realm—they drift wherever color falters or bleeds.

May attach to players, creatures, or even shrine-cores.

Destroying their core releases a burst of unstable hue residue.

Archivists debate their origin:
Prism Tears... unfinished hues... or something older still?

LORE ENTRY

Excerpt from “Drifting Parasites of the Huefold Realms” by Ashar of Huevale.

“At first, I mistook them for droplets of oil falling upward—mesmerizing, harmless.

But when I cast even the simplest burst of Crimson, they surged like starving children.”

“One latched onto my back, and the world dimmed.
My fire fizzled to ash. My pulse slowed.”

“In that moment, I understood—they feed not on color... but on intention.”

“Hue Leeches do not kill.
They erase.”

“What you meant to do fades.
What you believed, dulls.”

“I survived by flashing a prism shard in their midst—they scattered, furious, luminous.”

“They taught me the cruelest truth:
color is trust.”

“And they are doubt incarnate.”

BEHAVIOR

SPAWN LOCATIONS

Appear in swarms near prism fractures, old altars, or failed transmutation zones.

PASSIVE UNTIL PROVOKED

Linger passively until provoked by hue use.

SWARM INSTINCT

Group behavior increases drain speed and duration.

FLEE WHEN DISRUPTED

Attempt to escape if stunned or flashed with high-contrast light bursts.

POSSIBLE DROPS

LEECHED HUE GLOB (COMMON)

A viscous drop of drained color.
Used in crafting, potions, or temporary enchantments.

PRISM CORE FRAGMENT (RARE)

A fractured core still hummed with unstable hue.
Valuable in high-tier rituals.

DULLTIDE RESIN (UNCOMMON)

Numbing fluid that can be coated on weapons to disrupt enemy spellflow.

FADED INTENTION (VERY RARE)

Whispers of forgotten spells and half-formed outcomes.
Used in ancient memory binding.

“THEY DO NOT KILL.
THEY ERASE.

GUARD YOUR COLOR.
GUARD YOUR PURPOSE.”

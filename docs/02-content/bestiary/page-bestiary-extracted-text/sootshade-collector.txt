SOOTSHADE COLLECTOR

THE ASH THAT WHISPERS

“It gathers burnt truths. And whispers them.”

TYPE

Ashbound Specter / Memory Leech

THREAT LEVEL

MODERATE TO HIGH

Emotionally destabilizing.

FIRST EMERGED

Ash Year 4, after the Ember-Library Collapse.

ANATOMY BREAKDOWN

1. ASHEN HOOD

A voided cowl that swallows form and face alike.
No identity, only memory.

2. SCORCH-STITCHED WRAPS

Robes and ribbons sewn from charred parchment and forgotten pages.

3. EMBERLESS CORE

A hollow heart of ash where memories are stored, not felt.

4. DRIFTING SOOT RIBBONS

Trail in the air like burned whispers, collecting stray thoughts.

5. MEMORY SCROLLS

Ash-scrolls carry stolen fragments of truth, relived by force.

6. INKSMOKE WAKE

Leaves behind a fog that replays the past in distorted echoes.

7. TRUTH-SEEKING HANDS

Long, frayed fingers that reach for texts, artifacts, and minds.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Haunts burned archives, collapsed libraries, and ash-covered ruins.

Attracted to scorched notes, half-burnt texts, and memory echoes.

Feeds on forgotten confessions, hidden regrets, and abandoned truths.

The more a place is forgotten, the stronger it becomes.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

EMERGE → MEMORY FOG → DIALOGUE ECHO → PARALYZE

Casts black fog that replays memory fragments, cutscenes, or flashbacks.

May paralyze the player by echoing key past dialogue or trauma.

Hovers near burnt notes or ash-covered artifacts, attempting to “read” them.

Becomes more aggressive when memories are resisted.

ABILITIES / STATUS EFFECTS

MEMORY FOG

Projects a fog that triggers vivid flashbacks and visual distortion.

DIALOGUE ECHO

Repeats haunting lines from the player’s past, causing paralysis if unchecked.

INK-LEECH

Drains memory stability, weakening lore resistance and clarity of thought.

ASH-BIND

Roots the target in place through whispered truths and forgotten regrets.

HUE INTERACTION

Immune to fire and Crimson-based aggression.

Amplifies lingering dialogue ghosts from Violet-aligned encounters.

Neutralized temporarily by the Bistre Lantern’s glow.

Certain relics and truth-bound artifacts sever its connection to memory.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Vulnerable to silence spells, especially from Verdant or Violet sources.

Disperses briefly when caught in the light of the Bistre Lantern or reflective glyphs.

Becomes unstable when the player speaks truth during a forced memory sequence.

LORE ENTRY

Notes from the Bistre Archivist, smudged but intact.

“Not all things burned are lost.
Some simply migrate.”

“The Sootshade comes not to kill, but to recall —
to trap you in a page you never turned — and make you read it aloud.”

“One stepped into my archive once.
It didn’t walk. It lingered.

When it passed, an entire section of blackened scrolls whispered back into ink —
confessions no one meant to leave behind.”

“They do not judge.
But they never forget.

And memory, when weaponized, burns colder than any fire.”

— Bistre Archivist

NOTES

Sootshades leave behind “Ash-Bound Pages” —
rare consumable scrolls that reveal one hidden event or decision the player has forgotten.

ASH-BOUND PAGE

A fragment of stolen memory.
Using one rewrites a portion of the memory log… at a cost.

FIELD NOTE

Survivors often report voices whispering their own secrets back to them.

The Collector does not forgive.
It only collects.

FRACTAL SHARDLING

RECURSION MADE SHARP

“Hit it once,
and it remembers forever.”

THREAT LEVEL

MODERATE

Escalates quickly if uncontrolled.

TYPE

Recursive Entity / Prism Construct

FIRST EMERGED

Post-Shattering Splinterfall.

HUE INTERACTION

MIMICS HUE GRADIENTS

Changes color subtly with each split, but never fully expresses any hue.

IMMUNE TO DIRECT HUE MAGIC

Most hue spells pass through it harmlessly.

LIGHT-BASED ILLUSIONS

Yellow, Aurelian, and Violet illusions delay recursion and slow replication.

PRISM RESONANCE

Strong prism relics may cause resonance backlash on higher generations.

DESCRIPTION

Fractal Shardlings are splinters of broken Core-math, animated by recursion itself.

They float along looping paths that defy natural laws, bound by fractured equations.
When struck, they do not crack apart — they divide.

Each new shard is smaller, faster, and more unpredictable than the last.

Their purpose is unknown.
Some say they seek to rebuild the pattern that made them. Others say they are the pattern.

ANATOMY

PRISM SPIRE

Primary core stores the recursive pattern and split memory.

ECHO NODES

Emit harmonic hums that weaken with each generation.
Used to target the last attacker.

QUANTUM EDGES

Edges do not follow gravitational pull.
Allow movement in impossible loops.

RECURSIVE CORE

Holds the original algorithm.
Destroying this ends the chain.

SPLINTER VENTS

Open during division phase.
Emit smaller Shardlings with accelerated speed.

PATTERN TRAIL

Leaves faint glyph traces that loop back on themselves.

LORE ENTRY

Fragmented scrawl found etched on a Nullshade monolith.

“They fractured the Core, hoping each splinter might forget what the whole once knew.

Instead, it remembered too well.
Repeated. Echoed. Over and over.”

“A Shardling does not die.
It subdivides — each generation forgetting more... and hurting more.”

“One split into five.
Then twenty-five.

Then still it sang.
Until even its song forgot the words, but not the rhythm.”

“Only one truth breaks recursion:
memory without repetition.”

BEHAVIOR

Patrols fixed paths in recursive loops, ignoring the player until disturbed.

Targets the last source of damage dealt to it.

Each split increases speed, reduces size, and adds unpredictability.

Hums grow fainter each generation.
At silence, they are at their most dangerous.

At recursion limit, usually five generations, collapses in a silent burst of prismatic energy.

BEHAVIOR CYCLE

STEP 1 — NORMAL STATE

Loops patrol path.

STEP 2 — DISTURBED

Struck by attack.
Begins split.

STEP 3 — SUBDIVISION

Splits into two faster shards.

STEP 4 — ESCALATION

Continues splitting.
Speed increases.

STEP 5 — RECURSION LIMIT

Collapses inward.
Alters terrain.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

SOUND BURST

A perfectly timed sonic wave, such as Echo Pulse, can halt all recursion instantly.

GLAMOUR PULSE

Violet’s Glamour Pulse confuses their pattern, forcing false splits and breaking chains.

MIRROR FEEDBACK

Reflecting its movement back into itself using mirrors or reflective terrain causes instant collapse.

NOTES

Allowing more than five generations to exist may cause terrain distortion, shifting platforms, rotated rooms, or reality fractures.

Often whispers fragmented patterns:
“One becomes two. Two becomes... error.”

Scholars theorize they are echoes of logic languages older than Hue itself.

POSSIBLE REWARDS

RECURSION CORE FRAGMENT

Used to upgrade Prism relics and unlock logic-bound abilities.

SPLINTER CODE

Traditionalists use for crafting adaptive echo-traps.

POSSIBLE DROPS

FRACTAL LENS (RARE)

A lens that reveals looping movement and hidden paths.

PRISMATIC DUST

Residual energy used to enhance hue-infused gear.

ECHO CRYSTAL SHARD

Used in crafting sound-based or illusion-disrupting items.

GLYPH LOOP TOKEN (VERY RARE)

A token inscribed with repeating glyphs.
Alters map behavior.

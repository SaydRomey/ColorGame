NULL-ROOT

THE CORE THAT FAILED TO BLOOM

“Even the void dreamed of life.
That’s where the rot began.”

THREAT LEVEL

HIGH

Environmental Threat.
Does not pursue—it spreads.

TYPE

Voidspawn / False Flora

FIRST EMERGED

Near the shattering of the Core Wound

HUE INTERACTION

DRAWN TO HUE

Drawn to equipped Hue shards—becomes violent the more hues are active.

VOIDSTEP CALM

Grows passive if player unequips all hue-bound gear or uses the “Voidstep” relic.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

SACRIFICIAL LATCH

Cannot be killed directly—must let it strike a Prismwalker shard to trigger decay bloom.

HUE CONSUMPTION

Once it latches to a hue-infused item, it begins dissolving—that item is sacrificed.

PHYLACTERIC SEAL

Exposed to the “Caput Mortuum Phylactery,” it becomes inert for one cycle.

DESCRIPTION

A twisted, obsidian-like vine-creature.

Its body pulses with multicolored crystal veins—a mockery of hue-life.
It lashes out unpredictably from terrain, warping geometry into weapons.

Unlike other entities, it doesn’t see the Prismwalker—it feels them.

ANATOMY

VOID-VEIN TENDRILS

Lash out from any corrupted surface.
Absorb light and hue to fuel growth.

PRISM CORE NODULE

A corrupt imitation of the Core.
Pulsates with broken hue-crystals.

OBSIDIAN ROOT MASS

Anchors into terrain or geometry.
Can reshape and corrupt its surroundings.

FALSE BLOOM

Petal-like shards that open when enraged.
Releases decay spores and null-static.

GEOMETRY HOOKS

Rips loose pieces of ruins or floating stone to wield as weapons or armor.

ATTACKS & ABILITIES

ROOT LANCE

Erupts tendrils in a straight line.
Impales and drags targets.

PRISM SHARD TOSS

Hurls stolen geometry and hue-shards that explode on impact.

DECAY BLOOM

Releases spores that corrupt hue abilities and lower defenses.

GEOMETRY GRASP

Pulls the battlefield inward, restricting movement.

NOTES

Emits a low, heartbeat-like thrum when nearby.

Some echoes report a brief visual of the Prismwalker being “buried in roots” after defeating it—a non-lethal memory echo tied to its origin.

Its roots may lead back to something older than the Prism.

LORE ENTRY

Journal Fragment, written by the Caput Mortuum Ascetic—
“Notes on the Hollow Bloom”

“It germinated in absence—
from a sliver of the Prism that refused to fade.”

“A tendril of hunger disguised as growth.
Roots made of void-veins.

A longing to begin again, in a world that no longer remembers how.”

“But growth without hue is not rebirth.
It is corruption wearing hope.”

“To kill the Null-Root, you must offer what it seeks—a future it cannot digest.”

BEHAVIOR

BURIED THREAT

Burrows and strikes from any prism-corrupted terrain nearby.

GEOMETRIC ASSAULT

Drags chunks of floating geometry into battle—hurling or using them as shields.

CORRUPTION SPREAD

If ignored, will reshape the battlefield itself, corrupting puzzle zones and shrines.

PRESENCE SENSE

Does not rely on sight.
Detects the player through hue-resonance and heartbeat-like vibrations.

POSSIBLE DROPS

VOIDSPUN CORE (RARE)

A living nodule.
Used to enhance or corrupt hue gear.

NULLROOT VINE (UNCOMMON)

Tendrils that siphon energy.
Used in crafting or alchemical binding.

HOLLOW BLOOM (UNCOMMON)

A petrified false bloom.
Emits faint resonance. Used for relic rituals.

BROKEN REBIRTH (LEGENDARY)

A memory shard containing a glimpse of a future that was never meant to be.
Used in endings and fate-choice alterations.

DOPPELSHADE

MIRROR OF THE SELFLIED

“I saw myself dodge.
But I hadn’t moved yet.”

THREAT LEVEL

VARIABLE

Based on player complexity and habits.

TYPE

Illusory Mimic / Mirrorborne Trickster

FIRST EMERGED

Post-Shattering; earliest recorded in Myrrala.

HUE INTERACTION

WEAK TO TRUTH-LIGHT

Vulnerable to Aurelian light.
The truth reveals it.

VULNERABLE TO SOUND

Sound-based rhythm and sonic spells can disrupt and unravel its mimicry.

IMMUNE TO ILLUSIONS

Cannot be fooled by illusions — unless crafted by the player using Violet hue.

REFLECTED PROJECTILES

Projectiles that strike during reversed timing phases hit it for full damage.

DESCRIPTION

A self-propagating illusion created from unfulfilled choices, ego residue, and mirrorborne emotion.

Doppelshades form when lingering doubt is reflected too long in the Blooming Veil’s mirrorstones.
They mimic appearance, equipment, and behavioral tics — even mimicking spellcasting stutters or dodge habits.

They are never quite perfect.
That’s their trap. That’s your warning.

ANATOMY

MIRROR VEIL

Fragments of broken intent cloak the true form.

ECHO CORE

Pulses with the player’s emotions and hesitation.

FALSE EDGE

Weapons are illusions until the form is broken.

REFLECTIVE SHELL

Adapts to your armor, hue patterns, and weapon loadout.

LOOP ANCHOR

Tracks the last 3 movement patterns and inputs.

SHADOW TRAIL

Leaves behind brief after-images that mimic your path.

LORE ENTRY

Fragmented Memory from the Thulian Mirror.

“Reflections should show truth.
But in dreams, even mirrors lie kindly.”

“There are things that wear your face.
Not out of malice — but because they don’t know their own anymore.”

“The Doppelshade is not born.
It is caught — a slip of intent, a lingering gesture you forgot you made.

It waits in veil-thin mirrors, rehearsing your doubts until they can dance without you.”

“I watched a traveler argue with theirs for hours.
When I returned, only one walked away. I couldn’t say who.”

“They say the Thulian Court once kept a hall of mirrors where you could see every choice you didn’t make.
That hall burned. But the reflections... didn’t die.”

“If your reflection attacks first, it was never yours.”

— Scribed into the petals of a Liseran Lily, pressed between two illusion-stung pages.

BEHAVIOR

Appears behind or near mirrors, often posing mid-gesture.

Opens with your most frequent spell or attack combo — but slightly early.

Reacts to your last 3 movement patterns, trying to “catch” your next input.

Adapts quickly.
Second encounter includes all choices made in first.

Disappears if ignored for long enough, but reappears on repeat visits.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

TRUTH REVEALS IT

Aurelian light or truth-bound effects expose its real form and stun it briefly.

RHYTHM BREAKS LOOP

Sound and rhythm magic, Cobalt, disrupts its ability to predict your next move.

MIRROR BEAM

Thulian Mirror beams or Heliotrope Band auras cause instability and brief stuns.

CONTRADICTION UNRAVELS

Confronting it with opposing dialogue choices or emotional contradictions destabilizes it.

NOTES

Damaging it without revealing its illusion form causes a psychic jolt that scrambles your HUD and briefly distorts your senses.

Some say killing a Doppelshade removes a fear.
Others say it replaces one.

Fey scholars advise not to look into your reflection after — just in case it blinks when you don’t.

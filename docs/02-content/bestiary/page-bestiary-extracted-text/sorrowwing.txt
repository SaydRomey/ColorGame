SORROWWING

NAME IN THE WIND

Ghost-bird with eyes too human, too knowing.

“It cries your name before you hear it.”

TYPE

Spirit Avian / Emotional Manipulator

THREAT LEVEL

Moderate —
Environmental Hazard, Lure-Based

FIRST EMERGED

After the Collapse of the Windward Sanctum

REGION

Iriseld —
Tidespire Reach

ANATOMY & APPARITIONS

1. HUMAN GLASS EYES

Damp, reflective eyes filled with memory.

2. SILKEN WINGS

Pale feathers trailing mist that pulse like heartbeats.

3. NAME-WHISPER BEAK

Its cry carries names and grief-colored suggestion.

4. ECHO BREAST

Voice resonances pulled from nearby minds.

5. LURE DRIFT TAIL

Mist streamers subtly pull victims toward danger.

6. SEMI-PHANTASMAL FORM

Ranged attacks may pass through its body.

7. CLIFF PERCH POSTURE

Passive watcher until flight or haste awakens it.

DESCRIPTION

A pale-winged, semi-phantasmal avian that sings with voices pulled from nearby minds.

The Sorrowwing does not strike directly —
it influences behavior through grief-colored suggestion.

Players report emotional heaviness and directional compulsion after hearing its call.

Its eyes are unmistakable:
glassy, human, always damp with memory.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Found on cliff edges, sky-bridges, mourning paths, storm ledges, and windward sanctum ruins.

Often perches silently before luring prey with a name-whispering cry.

Most active near open air, gliding routes, and places marked by loss.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

PERCH SILENT → NAME-CRY → LURE DRIFT → CIRCLE OUT OF REACH

Prefers remaining passive until a player attempts flight or high-speed movement.

When active, it cries out a phrase or name from the player’s past, based on collected Memory Fragments.

Activates Lure Drift, gradually pulling the player toward cliff edges or sky traps.

Sometimes circles just out of reach, causing ranged attacks to pass through.

ABILITIES / STATUS EFFECTS

NAME-WHISPER CRY

Calls a remembered phrase or name.

LURE DRIFT

Gently redirects or pulls the player toward danger.

ECHO SORROW

Causes emotional heaviness and hesitation.

PHANTASMAL EVASION

Ranged strikes may pass through its body.

GLIDE INTERFERENCE

Slightly redirects flight-based traversal mid-glide.

HUE INTERACTION

Specifically drawn to Cobalt-bound players with high Memory Echo charge.

Its call interferes with flight-based traversal, slightly pulling or redirecting mid-glide.

Cobalt’s Mind Echo can block or distort its influence with correct timing.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Repelled or disoriented by Cerulean Cloak.

Anchoring spells such as Verdant’s Skygrowth Seed break its lure grip temporarily.

Easily grounded with memory-reinforcement artifacts like the Zaffre Vial or Celadon Shards.

LORE ENTRY

Voiceprint transcription, left unfinished by a monk of the Windward Chorus.

“They say the wind in Iriseld carries more than rain.
It carries loss wrapped in wings.

I saw it at the cliff’s edge.
It looked back. Not like a bird.

Like a widow remembering.

It called my name…
but my voice answered first.

Its cry is your grief, wearing feathers.”

IRISELD / TIDESPIRE REACH

A realm of wind-carved spires and grieving seas.

Storms carry memory.
Cliffs remember names.

TACTICAL NOTE

Best countered with grounding, memory reinforcement, and anti-lure timing.

Anchor yourself.

Guard your mind.

Do not follow the call.

NOTES

The Seers of Zaffran Port once raised Sorrowwings to guide mourners across the cliffs.

Now, only the wind walks beside the grieving.

“It does not hunt your body.
It hunts the part of you that still listens.”

SHATTERGLASS CHOIR

THE SONG THAT UNMAKES

They sing.
The color leaves your screen.

“Their voices aren’t notes —
they’re decisions you didn’t make.”

TYPE

Huephantasm / Psychic Dissonance

THREAT LEVEL

HIGH

Cognitive Interference

FIRST EMERGED

Mid-Shattering Echo Phase

ANATOMY BREAKDOWN

1. SHARD-VOID HEAD

Voices resonate from fractured cores instead of throats.

2. HOLLOW EYES

Reflect desaturation of the world as they sing.

3. HUE-SHARD HEART

Each heart sings in a corrupted hue-frequency that no longer belongs to them.

4. PRISM-FRACTURED SKIN

Their forms refract light incorrectly, breaking hue logic.

5. SONGWAVE VEILS

Their vocal harmonies appear as visible vibrations that drain color from reality.

6. ECHO ANKLETS

Resonate with the wrong notes when attacked out of order.

7. LEVITATION DRIFT

They circle in slow, skeletal harmonies, anchored to memory resonance points.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Found near lore gates, memory nodes, and shattered prism monoliths.

Circle key artifacts, archives, or boss arena thresholds.

The environment desaturates as their chant intensifies.

More dangerous in larger choirs or enclosed spaces.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

DORMANT SUPPLICANT

CHANTING IGNITION

DISSONANT PULSE

FULL DESATURATION

Circle slowly outward, singing synchronized pulses.

Each pulse drains color, weakens artifacts, and silences the player’s hue interface.

If unchecked, the world becomes grayscale and HUD fades.

ABILITIES / STATUS EFFECTS

HUE DRAIN

Each pulse removes a hue from the world, UI, and player abilities.

DISSONANT PULSE

Audible/visible wave that fragments color, Meme gain, and artifact power.

MEMORY UNMOORING

Warped phrases and visions disrupt focus and decision-making.

TOTAL DESATURATION (ENRAGE)

If all hues are drained, the screen and world become fully grayscale.

HUE INTERACTION

Each Choir member is tied to a hue-tone:
Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet.

Attacking out of order strengthens the song and causes them to regenerate.

Silence is a mechanic:
standing still or sheathing all hue-gear for 5 seconds interrupts the chant.

Violet Glamour Pulse can reset progression if the song overwhelms you.

Nullroot exposure nearby can silence one choir member temporarily.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Must be defeated in inverse spectrum order:
Violet → Blue → Green → Yellow → Orange → Red.

Attacking out of order causes full regeneration and doubles their volume.

Vulnerable to Cobalt-based mist, drowning, or sound disruption.

Interrupt during chant animations with sound or silence-based attacks.

Separated from shrine banners, they lose coordination and become unstable.

FIELD FRAGMENT — EMBER RELIQUARY

They weren’t always like this.

Once, they were singers of balance —
prism children born to echo each hue as it rose.

But when the Core fractured, their voices were scattered.

Now they harmonize nothing but absence.

The closer you get, the harder it is to hear yourself think.

They hum in broken ROYGBV —
and if you answer out of order, they don’t stop singing.

They erase.

— Bistre Archivist

NOTES

Occasionally sing snatches of your choices, warping phrases like:
“You chose... silence,” or “Color was never yours to hold.”

Killing them all restores color to the region for a short time —
enough to access sealed memory pools.

They are not evil.

They are unfinished harmony punished for singing truths no longer allowed.

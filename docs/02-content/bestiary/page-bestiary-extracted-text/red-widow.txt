RED WIDOW

FLAME IN SILK

She smiles, then you’re bleeding —
and you almost thank her.

“Her kisses are funerals.”

PROFILE

Type: Assassin / Ember Siren

Threat Level: High — Deception & Mind Control

First Emerged: Final Days of the Crimson Court, Circa Ashfall

DOSSIER NOTE

The Red Widow is an assassin cloaked in beauty and flame.

Her lace-wrapped body flows like smoke, vanishing in the shimmer of heat or stepping between shadows.

She doesn’t stalk —
she performs.

Her presence bends minds, breaks loyalty, and unravels cohesion through elegance alone.

TRAITS

HEAT MIRAGE CLOAK

Fades in and out of heat mirages, creating gaps in visibility and perception.

FLOWING RED LACE

Assassin garbed in flowing red lace, moving with dancer’s grace.

CHARM VEIL

Can charm or turn nearby NPCs —
or, rarely, other players in co-op —
mid-combat.

MIRRORED BLADES

Attacks from odd angles using teleportation steps and delayed mirrored blades.

HUE INTERACTION

Uses Crimson Hue defensively —
turns heat into afterimage misdirection.

Vulnerable to Cobalt truthseeing and Violet counter-charm abilities.

Her charm effect increases in power if the player has recently won a duel or is high in confidence-affecting stats.

BEHAVIOR SEQUENCE

1. Begins encounters with distance and dialogue —
often offering a riddle or taunt.

2. Attacks from odd angles using teleportation steps and delayed mirrored blades.

3. Charms one nearby ally/NPC during midfight —
forced to attack for her unless broken.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Interrupting her final pose animation leaves her stunned.

Weak to sound-disruption spells —
her rhythm falters under noise or distortion.

Mirror terrain, including obsidian floors and reflective lava glass, breaks her cloaking entirely.

LORE EXTRACT

“I saw her only once.
She stepped out of the smoke like a poem.”

“The Red Widow doesn’t fight.
She performs.

Every blade is a step,
every smile a stanza.”

“If she looks your way, bow.

If she reaches for you...
well, at least the dance ends with grace.”

— From a letter never sent, tucked into the pages of The Burning Waltz.

AFTER DEFEAT / FIELD NOTES

Defeated players sometimes find red silk tucked into their inventory —
soft, perfumed cloth with no known use.

Some burn it.
Some wear it.

A few report dreams of her returning.

Smiling.

“Beauty is her weapon.
Deception is her dance.

You are already part of the performance.”

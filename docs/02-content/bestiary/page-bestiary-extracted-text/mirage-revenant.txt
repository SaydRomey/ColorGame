MIRAGE REVENANT

THE FLICKERING LIE

“You blinked.
It was never there.

But now it is.”

THREAT LEVEL
MODERATE

Highly deceptive illusion that punishes trust and hesitation.
Deadly once revealed.

TYPE
Illusory Specter / Radiant Trap

FIRST EMERGED
Post-Shattering Era, especially around collapsed sunshrines.

HUE INTERACTION

IMMUNE TO AURELIAN LIGHT SPELLS
Unless they are truth-aligned.

VULNERABLE TO COBALT MIND ECHO
Shatters the illusion and exposes its true form.

DISPELLING GLAMOUR
Reveal it instantly with Violet illusions or relics like the Citrine Lens.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

EASILY UNMASKED
True-sight or illusion-canceling effects reveal its deception.

FEARS MEMORY ARTIFACTS
Items like the Zaffre Vial that replay or anchor reality overwhelm it.

STUMBLES AFTER REVEAL
After its illusion breaks, it hesitates — strike fast before it fades.

DESCRIPTION

A Revenant cloaked in living mirage.
It takes the form of what the viewer most expects to see — a helper, a relic, a shelter — and lures them in.

When the illusion collapses, usually on approach, the Revenant erupts in a burst of searing light and lashes with radiant claws.

It moves unpredictably, shimmering with heatwaves.

ANATOMY

MIRAGE VEIL
Shimmering distortion field that creates convincing illusions based on the viewer’s expectations.

RADIANT CORE
Feeds on belief and hope.
Glows with pulsing solar light when exposed.

TWISTED FORM
Spectral body warps light and heat, making targeting difficult.

DISTORTED FACES
Illusions reflect what the viewer most wishes to see.

BLADED CLAWS
Forged from fractured light.
Ignores most armor on first strike.

SHIFTING FOOTPRINTS
Leaves no real trace — only heat ripples and distorted reflections.

MIRAGE ABILITIES

FALSE APPEARANCE
Takes the form of a person, object, or place familiar to the target.

RADIANT BURST
On reveal, releases a blinding flash that blinds and deals damage.

HEAT BLUR
Becomes semi-intangible and moves rapidly through heatwaves.

MIRAGE CLAW
Lunges with radiant claws, ignoring shields and barriers.

FALSE RETREAT
Flees and leaves a fake afterimage to draw enemies into traps.

NOTES

Scholars debate whether these creatures were once real people — lost souls burned into radiant echoes — or desert-born constructs made from longing.

Some whisper names only the dead would know.

They are drawn to places where regret, or longing, linger too long.

LORE ENTRY

Transcript from “The Gambler’s Mirage,” an oral tale passed in Eburnea markets.

“It looked like my brother.
I hadn’t seen him since the siege.”

“He waved me over — smiled with that chipped tooth he had.”

“But when I stepped close, the smile stayed too still.
The eyes didn’t blink.”

“The shadow... wasn’t his.”

“I drew my blade too late.
It stabbed through my own hesitation.”

“They say the desert keeps your hopes warm so they don’t rot.

But sometimes, they burn instead — and something crawls out of the ashes wearing your smile.”

— Told by a relic-hunter named Sarmin, who never smiled again.

BEHAVIOR

FAMILIAR BAIT
Remains stationary while disguised, appearing as a friendly NPC, treasure, or safe haven.

REVEAL AND STRIKE
When approached or interacted with, the illusion breaks in a flash of light and it attacks immediately.

HEAT MIRAGE
Uses heat distortion to blur its form and teleport short distances, avoiding direct retaliation.

VANISH IF IGNORED
If not attacked within moments of revealing itself, it dissipates into light and may reset its illusion elsewhere.

POSSIBLE DROPS

MIRAGE SHARD (COMMON)
A splinter of illusory light.
Used to craft blinding decoys or heat refraction tools.

REVENANT CORE (UNCOMMON)
Contains trapped radiant essence.
Used in advanced relic forging.

FADING MEMORY (RARE)
A stolen recollection.
Can unlock hidden dialogue or lore fragments.

LUXERAN LENS FRAGMENT (RARE)
A cracked piece of illusion-resistant glass.
Used to upgrade the Citrine Lens.

PHANTOM PROMISE (LEGENDARY)
A whisper of hope made manifest.
Used to unlock radiant vaults or bind oaths in truth.

SOME THINGS REMEMBER WHAT YOU WANT MOST.”

PHYLACTERY SWARM

THE NAMES THAT NEVER REST

Tiny.
Hungry.

Each holds a name they never earned.

“If they whisper your name, run.
If they forget it — it’s already too late.”

BESTIARY ENTRY P-47

CLASSIFICATION

Name: Phylactery Swarm

Type: Soul-Fragmented Parasites

Threat Level: Moderate-to-Severe — Memory Drain

First Emerged: Post-Shattering, shortly after the collapse of the Phylactery Vault

DESCRIPTION

A drifting swarm of glimmering, fly-like constructs with miniature glyphs engraved on their chitin.

Each holds a fragment of a soul —
stolen, gifted, or long abandoned.

They buzz softly, repeating half-spoken names and severed dreams.

A cloud of brass-eyed, soulbound insects formed from failed phylacteries —
vessels meant to preserve life, now drifting husks of stolen identities.

ANATOMY / TRAITS

BRASS EYES

Reflective, watchful, unnervingly human.

GLYPH-ETCHED CHITIN

Each shell marked with miniature soul-binding script.

NAME-LADEN BUZZ

Their flight trails carry whispered names.

MEMORY SIPHON TOUCH

Contact loosens a fragment of memory.

CLUSTER INSTINCT

More dangerous in dense swarms.

HUE INTERACTION

Brown-bound memory spells repel or redirect their feeding pattern.

Blue mind-based defenses lock out name resonance, reducing damage over time.

Violet illusions confuse their targeting;
they can’t find the “real” name.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Fire magic burns them outright, especially from purified sources.

Sonic pulses from Violet-aligned instruments cause disarray in their flight pattern.

Bistre Lantern stuns them in shadow if activated in silence.

BEHAVIOR PATTERN

1. Aggressive in clusters.

2. Each hit reduces XP, disrupts equipped memory-bound relics, or causes temporary skill fog.

3. They prioritize targets with the most stored names:
players with saved dialogue trees, memory fragments, or death-echoes.

4. If fed a forgotten name, such as an unused True Name...

5. ...the swarm dissipates peacefully.

MEMORY-DRAIN EFFECT ON CONTACT

1. CONTACT

Swarm touches the target.

2. EXTRACTION

Fragments of memory loosen and lift.

3. ABSORPTION

Swarm absorbs and carries fragments.

4. AFTERMATH

Target suffers skill fog, confusion, or lost recall.

FIELD NOTES / DROPS

Destroying a swarm sometimes releases “Whispers in Dust,” a rare crafting essence used for soul-bound relic upgrades.

If you kill them while carrying a forgotten name, your own may become unstable in the Codex.

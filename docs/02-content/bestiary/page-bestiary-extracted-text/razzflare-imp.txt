RAZZFLARE IMP

Laughter that burns.
Mischief that lingers.

“It giggles in your blind spot.
That’s when it strikes.”

TYPE

Fey Trickster / Chaos Elemental

THREAT LEVEL

Moderate —
Mentally Disruptive, Not Durable

FIRST EMERGED

A failed glamour experiment under the Blooming Veil

DESCRIPTION / TRAITS

Tiny fey creature wrapped in ribbons of illusion fire.

Uses chaotic glamour to scramble the player’s senses.

Appears in bursts of pink and purple sparks, laughing.

ILLUSION FIRE

Burns consistency, not skin.

INVERTED SHADOW

A hallmark of unstable glamour.

CHAOTIC GLAMOUR

Scrambles perception and combat flow.

VISUAL GLITCH TRAIL

Reversed movement and delayed feedback.

MID-LAUGH OPENING

Striking during a laugh can ground it.

SYSTEM NOTE — BEHAVIOR PATTERN

1. Darts in and out of combat, leaving trails of visual glitches.

2. Casts Illusion Bursts that momentarily replace the player’s form with nearby enemies or objects.

3. Upon defeat, may release a Glamour Pulse with one random effect —
good or bad.

TACTICAL INTERACTION

Weak to harmonic pulses —
especially music-based artifacts like the Razzmatazz Drum.

Can be stunned briefly with Aurelian Light Trick or Cobalt Static Dash.

Immune to fear, silence, or logic.

WEAKNESSES — EXPLOIT THE CHAOS

Striking on beat cancels the illusion field.

Distracted by mirrored surfaces or echo chambers.

Temporarily grounded if struck while mid-laugh.

LORE NOTES

From the Notes of Mauveine Spark

“Yes, yes, I made it.
But I didn’t teach it to laugh.”

“They shift, skip, and dance inside the cracks between what you see and what you think you see.”

“If you hear giggling where there should be silence, it’s already too late.”

— Mauveine Spark,
Color Alchemist Extraordinaire

FIELD NOTE

Scholars believe Razzflare Imps are drawn to places where reality is worn thin by joy or grief.

Despite their danger, some mages keep them as familiars —
or try to.

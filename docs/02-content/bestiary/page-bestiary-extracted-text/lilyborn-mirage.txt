LILYBORN MIRAGE

MEMORY WRAPPED IN BLOOM

“What did you just say?
Are you sure?”

“I just asked you that...”

“Do you remember this?”

“That’s not how it happened...”

“No, that’s not what you said.”

“You always forget the important parts...”

THREAT LEVEL
MODERATE

Cognitive disruption that can bend reality and choices.

TYPE
Dream-Construct / Illusory Interrogator

FIRST EMERGED
During the Bloomwake Season in Myrrala.

HUE INTERACTION

WEAK TO PATTERN DISRUPTION
Cobalt’s Mind Echo or Violet’s Glamour Pulse can break its illusion loops.

RADIANT TRUTH
Aurelian’s Radiant Echo reveals which “memories” are false.

ROOT & GROUND
Verdant tools like Skygrowth Seed can ground its mist.

IMMUNE TO DIRECT DAMAGE
Physical attacks pass through unless it is made tangible.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

RHYTHMIC DISRUPTION
Call-and-response magic, such as Razmatazz Drum, interrupts its loops.

TANGIBLE WINDOWS
Becomes tangible during the final beat of a rhythmic cycle.

THULIAN MIRROR
Using the Thulian Mirror reveals the next line it will speak.

EMOTIONAL RELEASE
Acts weaken if the player speaks a truth aloud or releases pent-up emotion.

DESCRIPTION

An elegant dream-entity birthed from twilight lilies that have rooted in regret.
It does not fight with force — it unravels by asking.

The Mirage loops scenes or lines the player has heard before, layering them until choice becomes confusion.

Some of its illusions are true.
Some are almost true. Some are yours.

It casts questions mid-fight, and answering the wrong way replays the action — with damage.

ANATOMY

LILY CROWN
Absorbs lingering emotion and blooms memories into existence.

PETAL VEIL
Shifts and refracts form.
Appears different to every observer.

WHISPERING MIST
Carries fragments of conversations, choices, and regrets.

GLIMMERING EYES
Not true eyes — they reflect the player’s unresolved questions.

HEART BLOOM
A core of compressed memory.
Destroying it ends the loop.

ROOTED PETALS
Anchor the Mirage in place.
Breaking these weakens its hold.

MEMORY LOOP ABILITIES

SHIMMERBURST
Reverses the last dialogue choice or action.
Opens most encounters.

MEMORY BLOOM
Creates an ALCOM that forces the player to “speak,” triggering looped decisions.

ECHO PETALS
Tracks the player and replays past statements as homing projectiles.

REWIND QUESTION
Asks a single question.
Wrong answer rewinds time and deals damage.

NOTES

The Mirage feeds on unanswered questions, regrets, and half-spoken confessions.

Rare scholars have reported that some Mirages remember players across regions.

If you hear your own voice ask you something you never said... run.

LORE ENTRY

Scribbled in wilted dreampetal script.

“They don’t lie.
They don’t have to.”

“The lilies remember what was said — or what was meant — or maybe just what we wished we said.”

“These things bloom when emotion lingers too long in one place.
They drink from forgotten farewells and root into questions left unanswered.”

“I spoke to one once.
Or maybe it spoke to me.”

“It asked what I feared most.
I told it. Then I told it again. And again. And again.”

“When I came to, the petals had grown teeth.
And I was bleeding memory.”

— Whisperleaf fragment, found in the Veilgrove Spiral.

BEHAVIOR

SHIMMERS INTO VIEW
Appears during moments of emotional resonance or near areas of lingering memory, such as ruins, shrines, and graves.

LOOP CASTING
Repeats 3–5 second memory loops that affect hitboxes, terrain, and even UI elements.

QUESTION WEAVER
Asks questions that distort reality.
Wrong answers replay events and cause damage or debuffs.

RETREAT INTO BLOOM
Disperses into petals and mist when weakened, only to bloom again elsewhere.

POSSIBLE DROPS

PETAL OF RECOLLECTION (RARE)
Preserves a memory fragment.
Can be used to replay a cutscene or recover one lost dialogue choice.

BLOOMWHISPER FRAGMENT (RARE)
Used to craft dream-based talismans and illusion stabilizers.

MIRAGE LILY CORE (LEGENDARY)
The core memory of a Mirage.
Allows the player to summon a brief illusion of the Mirage for aid.

ECHOING QUESTION (UNIQUE)
A mysterious item that asks the player a riddle once per day.
The answer changes based on your choices.

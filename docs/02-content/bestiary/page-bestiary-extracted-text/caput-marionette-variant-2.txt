CAPUT MARIONETTE

THE ALCHEMIST’S SHAME

“They were never truly alive — just waiting to be dismissed.”

THREAT LEVEL

MODERATE

Area-dependent hazard.

TYPE

Alchemical Undead / Ritual Construct

FIRST EMERGED

During the Phylactery Rebellion, Year of the Dull Moon.

INTRODUCTION

Animated remnants of failed soulbinding—crude bodies stitched from ritual cloth and rust-streaked bone.

These marionettes jerk with unnatural precision, their joints creaking with regret.
They obey long-dead commands. And they wait.

HUE INTERACTION

RESISTANT TO BROWN

Earthen decay effects have little impact—they are already ruined.

WEAK TO VIOLET

Illusions confuse and destabilize them, causing command errors.

VULNERABLE TO CRIMSON

Direct damage sears their fragile bindings and shatters joints.

STABILIZED BY RELICS

Proximity to Caput Mortuum Phylactery relics slows and stabilizes their movement.

DESCRIPTION

Caput Marionettes are abandoned constructs—partially animated corpses intended for soul transfer tests.

Left unfinished, they wander memory halls and ruined labs, seeking the rituals they were meant to complete.

VISIBLE ANATOMY

Ritual stitch binding.

Crude hinge sealant—long-decayed.

Bone, rust, and cloth stitched with regret.

LORE ENTRY

Found in the marginalia of an alchemy ledger, author unknown.

“Caput Mortuum.
The name means death’s leftover.

What the crucible couldn’t use.”

“We made them to test transmutation rituals.
Vessels for souls too broken to scream.

They walked, moved, answered.
But never lived.”

“I buried mine beneath the Phylactery Vault, but it clawed free.
It didn’t attack — it waited at my old desk.”

“If they look at you, it’s not hate.
It’s recognition.

And that’s worse.”

BEHAVIOR

Remain inert until the player interacts with specific relics, memory statues, or spirit glyphs.

On activation, they move with jerky, unnatural rhythm.

Upon death, release a decay cloud that drains equipped memory-bound artifacts unless dispelled.

Obey obsolete commands.
They may attempt to re-enact forgotten rituals in their final moments.

Occasionally kneel or watch in silence before engaging, as if awaiting orders.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

SOULWARD SIGIL / TRUTH-SPOKEN NAME

Can be defeated preemptively by inscribing a Soulward Sigil or speaking their original summoner’s name aloud.

VULNERABLE TO ILLUSIONS

False commands, phantoms, or mirages cause malfunctions and hesitation.

VOICE OF THE SUMMONER

Hearing the voice of their original creator, real or simulated via audio glyphs, paralyzes them briefly.

EXPOSURE TO PURE LIGHT

Sustained light weakens the alchemical bindings that hold them together.

NOTES

If purified rather than killed, a Caput Marionette may kneel before the player and whisper a forgotten alchemical recipe.

Some whisper in your voice.

Collecting their rust-streaked joints may provide rare alchemical components used in forbidden bindings.

They often guard memory reliquaries, failed experiments, or half-written rituals that were never meant to be completed.

“I remember...
you.”

POSSIBLE DROPS

RUSTED BINDING CORE

Used in crafting or refining soulbinding relics.

RITUAL-WEAVE CLOTH

Inscribed fabric that retains faint magical memory.

MARIONETTE JOINT

Rare alchemical component for construct crafting.

WHISPERING ESSENCE

Residual essence used in memory-bound potions.

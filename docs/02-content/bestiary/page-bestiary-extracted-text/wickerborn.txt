WICKERBORN

THE HUSK THAT WAITED

“Some bindings rot.

Others learn patience.”

They built guardians to watch color relics.

The husks never stopped watching.

SOURCE IMAGE — wickerborn_page.png

CLASSIFICATION

Type:
Construct / Huebond Guardian.

Threat:
Moderate–High.

Behavior:
Dormant until provoked.

First Emerged:
Prismara War — Relic Age.

ANATOMY

BRAIDED ROOT CARAPACE

Woven root, vine, and wicker over a rigid frame.

SUN-DRIED BONE STRUTS

Ancient skeletal supports reinforce the torso and limbs.

IRON LASHINGS

Rusted bindings hold the guardian together.

TOTEM STILLNESS

While idle, it resembles a broken shrine statue.

GLYPH-FIRE RIBCAGE

Chest ignites when purpose is disturbed.

CREAKING LIMBS

Movement feels like branches bending in windless air.

ACTIVATION & BEHAVIOR

1. DORMANT

Dormant at relics, shrine gates, and memory vaults.

2. RELIC TRIGGER

Triggered when a relic is touched.

3. LIE TRIGGER

Triggered when a lie is spoken nearby.

4. HUE-ALIGNMENT TRIGGER

Triggered when a shrine is activated with improper hue alignment.

5. SLOW PURSUIT

Deliberate, methodical, inescapable in straight lines.

6. CORNERED STATE

Berserk flailing with vine whips and lash roots.

7. REASSEMBLY

Reassembles over time unless destroyed by fire or crystalline impact.

HUE INTERACTION

Aligned:
Verdant, Green.

Aligned:
Earthen, Brown.

Triggered by:
Crimson, Red, proximity.

Resists illusion and glamour effects.

Ignores most damage unless its purpose is disrupted.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

BURNABLE

Vulnerable to Crimson fire, especially at joints and binding roots.

EARTHEN SHOCK

Can shatter the inner structure.

STILLBARK OILS

Can slow motion if applied to relics in advance.

ARCHIVE FRAGMENT — THE SILENT WATCH

“They were built, not born — shaped by Verdant hands, bound by Crimson oaths, and given Earthen patience.”

“They move like decisions you regret — slow, deliberate, irreversible.”

FIELD NOTES

Some Whisperers claim Wickerborn remember names spoken in their presence — even centuries later.

Called by one ascetic:

“Our final promise — to watch what mattered when we no longer could.”

SUNBURNT JACKAL

THE SCORCHED HOWL

“It never learned the difference between shadow and prey.”

TYPE

Desiccated Predator / Heat-Hound

THREAT LEVEL

MODERATE —
Lethal in packs or near radiant spells

FIRST EMERGED

Late Color War —
After the Dawn Siege of Luxeran

ANATOMY BREAKDOWN

1. SCORCHED HIDE

Leather-thin skin baked copper-red.

Cracked and scarred from generations under merciless sun.

2. BLISTERED EYES

Milky, clouded, and mostly blind.

Heat-sense organs within the skull detect radiant signatures.

3. HEAT-SENSE NODES

Inflamed nasal passages and sinus ridges detect body heat and radiant echoes with extreme sensitivity.

4. CRACKED FANGS

Teeth are chipped and brittle, retaining heat residue that sears flesh with each bite.

5. MANGY FUR

Patchy, brittle fur offers little protection.

Helps absorb and radiate ambient heat.

6. DESICCATED LIMBS

Lean, sinewy legs built for endurance across burning dunes.

Pads hardened to sand.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Roams the sun-baked dunes of Luxeran, particularly ruined sites and heat mirages.

Drawn to radiant sources:
spells, torches, beacons, and sun-heated relics.

Most active during midday and early afternoon.

Sluggish and fearful at dusk or nightfall;
seeks warmth, cowers in cold.

Howls create warped echoes that scatter and disorient prey.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

SCENT SEEKER → LUNGE → HOWL ECHO → COWER

Hunts by triangulating body heat and radiant echoes.

Will chase thrown torches or glowing relics as a distraction.

Packs coordinate using howls that ricochet strangely in heat haze.

Cowers from dusk or cloudy conditions;
most active midday.

HUE INTERACTION

Drawn to Aurelian (Yellow) magic or any active light source.

Avoids deep shadow or cold environments.

Can be stunned by Cobalt (Blue) flashburst spells or illusions that mask heat.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Ice and shadow spells dampen senses.

Easily misled by artificial light or false heat signatures.

Loud noises, explosives, and sonic artifacts disorient pack coordination.

LORE ENTRY

never knowing it wasn’t real.

It yipped and snapped at flickering sand.

Not blind — not quite —
but worse:

its mind burns with too much sun and too little shadow.”

But now…

“At night, I found one curled into a broken clay brazier, whimpering at the cold stars.”

NOTES

Desert guides often carry heat-absorbent stones or cold-blood salves to mask their presence.

It’s said that if a Sunburnt Jackal is howling at an empty flame, it’s mourning a prey that never burned.

POSSIBLE DROPS

Jagged, heat-seared fang retaining ember-essence.

Used in crafting Scent Cloaks and Mirage desert survival gear.

FIELD NOTE

They mistake warmth for life.

Carry cold.
Walk in shade.

Let the sun feel far away.

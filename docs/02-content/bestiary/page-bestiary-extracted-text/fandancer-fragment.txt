ECHO OF THE PASSION STAGE

“Her steps are sorrow.
Her blade is applause.”

HIGH

Pattern-locked duelist trap.

TYPE

Illusion / Combat Echo

DESCRIPTION

A radiant illusion left behind by the last great duel of the Passion Stage.

The Fandancer Fragment attacks in choreographed sequences, moving across tiles in precise, looped footwork.

She cannot be reasoned with — her battle is performance.
Each step, spin, and lunge is tied to her final act.

BEHAVIOR

Leaves glowing footstep markers that emit afterimages if followed in rhythm.

Can lock the player in a “duet,” forcing movement input to match her tempo or suffer damage.

Mid-fight, she may “bow” and reset her combo.
This is your chance to break her rhythm with a disruptive hue.

Her every move is rhythmic, deadly, and beautiful.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

RHYTHM LOOP BREAK

Her illusion falters if the Rhythm Loop is broken.
Step out of her range mid-pattern.

TEMPO SHIFT

Vulnerable to sudden tempo shifts — Aurelian Radiant Echo or Mauveine Flask destabilizes her choreography.

BOW MOMENT

Mask shatters briefly during her “bow” animation.
That’s her true face.

NOTES

Defeating her grants a Spectral Footwork Fragment, which lets you mimic stylish attacks during future duels.

Some players report hearing faint applause when they strike her final blow.

The Passion Stage may be gone, but the performance never ends.

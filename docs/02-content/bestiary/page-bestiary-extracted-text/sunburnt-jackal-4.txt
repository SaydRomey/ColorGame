DESCRIPTION

The Sunburnt Jackal is a lean, copper-hued beast with mangy fur and blistered skin.

Mostly blind from generations of sun, it relies on a grotesquely overdeveloped heat-sense to track prey.

Fiercely territorial, it is drawn to radiant sources —
spells, relics, or campfires.

Its bite sears with residual heat, and its howl disrupts auditory clarity, making it as deadly as it is disorienting.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Found around burning dunes, mirage fires, broken clay braziers, abandoned ruins, and heat-saturated desert routes.

Often circles dying torches or radiant relic sites.

Most active at midday.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

SCENT HEAT → CIRCLE SOURCE → PACK HOWL → LUNGE HUNT → SCATTER AT DUSK

Hunts by triangulating body heat and radiant echoes.

Chases thrown torches or glowing relics as distractions.

Packs coordinate using howls that ricochet in heat haze.

Cowers from dusk or cloudy conditions.

ABILITIES / STATUS EFFECTS

HEAT-SEEKER LUNGE

Snaps toward radiant or warm targets with blinding speed.

MIRAGE FIXATION

Can become obsessed with false flames or decoy heat.

ECHO HOWL

Distorts auditory clarity and coordination.

PACK TRIANGULATION

Multiple jackals lock down prey through layered howls.

SEARING BITE

Residual heat damage on contact.

HUE INTERACTION

Drawn to Aurelian (Yellow) magic or any active light source.

Avoids deep shadow or cold environments.

Can be stunned by Cobalt flashburst spells.

Illusions or magic that mask heat can break pursuit.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Ice and shadow spells dampen senses.

Easily misled by artificial light or false heat signatures.

Loud noises, explosives, or sonic artifacts disorient pack coordination.

Shade and nightfall reduce aggression and speed.

LORE SCRAP — ATTRIBUTED TO A FULVOUS HUNTER

It circles the mirage fire for hours,
not quenched, not fed.

It is not blind as we are blind —
it is worse.

It feels heat like scent,
reads the air as we read books.

At night, when the stars cut the breath from the dunes,
it whimpers at the cold ones.

STORMWRIT HOUND

BOLT BETWEEN WORLDS

“It howls in lightning —
and leaps between bolts.”

TYPE

Lightning Beast / Elemental Predator

THREAT LEVEL

HIGH —
Fast, Unpredictable, Anti-Casting

FIRST EMERGED

During the Tempest Surge following the Shattering.

ANATOMY BREAKDOWN

1. STORMWIRE FUR

Body made of conductive stormwire strands and ionized vapor.
Shifts constantly between solid and static.

2. GLASS CLAWS

Crystalline claws cut through metal and arcane barriers alike.

3. STATIC HEART

Core of compressed lightning.
Powers its teleports and disruptive howls.

4. AFTERIMAGE TRAIL

Moves faster than sight can track, leaving flickering echoes of its last position.

5. THUNDER LUNGS

Breath crackles with ozone and pressure.
Its growl distorts air and thought.

6. RIFT-BOUND LIMBS

Joints phase between planes for instant leaps and unpredictable angles.

7. STORM SENSE NODES

Detects electrical charge, magical rhythm, and incoming spells before they are cast.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Haunts skybound cliffs, storm relays, and shattered skyglass fields.

Appears during high winds, ritual storms, or heavy arcane buildup.

Lightning nodes, such as skyglass or copper spires, allow it to leap and ambush.

Territory pulses with static —
distant thunder and flickering lights.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

PACE CLIFF EDGE → LEAP BETWEEN NODES → FLASH-FANG LUNGE → HOWL & DISRUPT

Paces cliff edges until it senses fast movement or charged spell buildup.

Leaps between lightning nodes, such as skyglass spikes or copper relays.

Uses Flash-Fang Lunge:
teleports, hits, and leaves a static copy for 2 seconds.

Occasionally howls —
this stuns rhythm-based abilities and lowers visibility with a storm flash.

HUE INTERACTION

Disrupts spell channeling, especially Cobalt-based spells tied to storm or rhythm.

At high static charge, briefly disables Color Binding UI.

Can be slowed if surrounded by harmonic resonance, such as instruments or echo chimes.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Susceptible to wind-grounding relics and Cerulean Cloak effects.

Harmonic instruments, such as skybell chimes or glass flutes, distort its targeting.

Momentarily pacified if its own lightning arc is reflected, such as with Payne’s Prism Pane or a storm mirror.

LORE ENTRY

not a bark, but a pressure drop.

It doesn’t follow you.

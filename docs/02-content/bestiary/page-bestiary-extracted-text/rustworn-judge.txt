RUSTWORN JUDGE

THE VERDIGRIS VERDICT

“Silence is guilt.
Speak, and be tested.”

THREAT LEVEL

HIGH

Triggers zone-wide “duel” state.
Highly punishing to liars or oathbreakers.

TYPE

Spectral Adjudicator / Trialbound Duelist

FIRST EMERGED

Post-Shattering —
Bound to the Isabelline Tribunal Ruins.

HUE INTERACTION

VIOLET BLIND SPOT

Cannot perceive illusions.
Violet-bound players may delay confrontation.

EARTHEN TRUTH

Attuned to Earthen truths;
lying in dialogue triggers its full aggression.

CRIMSON TRIAL

Will challenge Crimson-bound characters more harshly, invoking blood-duel rites.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

PAUSED OPENING

During the “Paused” phase, offer a Memory Token from a known past regret to delay the duel.

NAME OF THE JUDGE

Light the Bistre Lantern at the edge of the arena to reveal its original name.
Speaking this name aloud weakens its form.

VERDICT INTERRUPT

A perfect parry during its “Judged” strike, or ending the duel through confession, can defeat it.

BESTIARY ENTRY

DESCRIPTION

Tall, silent, and clad in lacquered remnants of gold and crimson armor, the Rustworn Judge emerges in areas where too many lies or falsehoods have been spoken.

Its sword drags across the ground like a gavel across cracked marble.

When it engages, the HUD disappears.
Only heartbeats, footfalls, and spoken choices remain.

BEHAVIOR

Begins neutral;
approaches only if a dialogue deception or broken vow is detected nearby.

Upon engagement, all other threats vanish from the arena —
this becomes a Trial Zone.

Speaks only in verdicts:
“Guilty,” “Paused,” “Judged.”

Each phase tightens its aggression.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

See left panel.

NOTES

Players who lie or misremember their past actions during combat will have movement briefly impaired.

It is not the Judge’s blade that slays —
but your own dishonesty echoing back.

ATTACKS & ABILITIES

VERDICT SLASH

A sweeping cut that leaves a rustbrand.
Causes Bleed and reduces healing effectiveness.

GAVEL DRAG

Drags its blade like a judge’s gavel.
Slows movement and stuns after a short delay.

SCALE OF TRUTH

Lifts its scales to expose your true nature.
Increases damage if you are lying or hiding.

SILENT EDICT

Speaks a single verdict.
Locks your abilities for a time based on your response.

RUSTENING BIND

Chains of corroded justice.
Reduces max stamina and prevents sprinting while bound.

LORE ENTRY

Fragment from “The Tribunal of Dust” —
Sootleaf Scroll, Mortuum Hold

“He presided over the Ash Trials —
where guilt was presumed, and memory was the only plea.

His blade struck truth from hesitation.”

“When the Catacombs crumbled, the Judge did not fall.

He simply waited,
verdict unspoken, blade still angled toward indecision.”

“We say:
do not argue with the rust.

It has waited longer than you have drawn breath.”

BEHAVIOR OVERVIEW

TRIAL INITIATION

Detected lies, false oaths, or broken promises draw the Judge.
Calm and silent until provoked.

DUEL REALM

Upon combat, the area seals into a Trial Zone.
Allies and enemies vanish.

Only the Judge and the challenged remain.

VERDICT PHASES

Cycles through “Guilty,” “Paused,” and “Judged.”
Each phase alters attack patterns and pressure.

JUDGMENT STRIKE

Final strike in “Judged” phase is near-instant.
Parry or confess — there is no dodge.

POSSIBLE DROPS

VERDIGRIS SENTENCE (RARE)

A rusted tablet engraved with the Judge’s final line.
Used to unlock trial-based gear.

TRIAL TOKEN (UNCOMMON)

A tarnished scale weight.
Used in crafting oathkeeper weapons.

CORRODED LACQUER (UNCOMMON)

Flakes of lacquered armor.
Used to enhance defense against charm and mind effects.

FORGOTTEN VERDICT (RARE)

A memory-bound echo of a truth long denied.
Used to alter dialogue outcomes in future playthroughs.

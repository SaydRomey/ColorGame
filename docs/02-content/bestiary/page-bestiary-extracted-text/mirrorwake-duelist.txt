MIRRORWAKE DUELIST

BLADE BETWEEN REFLECTIONS

“She fights in reflections.
Yours. Hers. Anyone’s.”

THREAT LEVEL
HIGH

Unpredictable.
Fights with perfect reflection of your form, using your own movements against you.

TYPE
Illusory Swordmaster / Storm-Arena Echo

FIRST EMERGED
After the Collapse of the Skyglass Arena.

HUE INTERACTION

IMMUNE TO AURELIAN LIGHT SPELLS
Unless they are truth-aligned.

SUSCEPTIBLE TO COBALT’S MIND ECHO
Shatters the illusion and exposes her true stance.

DISPELLED BY ILLUSIONS
Dispelling her glamour with Violet or relics like the Citrine Lens reveals her true form instantly.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

BREAK REFLECTIONS
Loses coordination if all local reflections, such as puddles, glass, or fragments, are destroyed.

STORM FOG WEAKENS
Combat is simplified during storm-induced fog, which dulls mirrored clarity.

PAYNE’S BRUSH ILLUSIONS
Illusions overwhelm her sense of “who is real,” stunning her briefly.

DESCRIPTION

A duelist bound not to memory, but to mirrored surfaces.

She moves across puddles, fragments, and skyglass panels with eerie grace — her attacks calibrated to the player’s form, reflected with lethal delay.

She does not speak.
Her blade interprets your posture like a question.

ANATOMY

WATER-WAKE HAIR
Flows like liquid silver.
Responds to motion and reflection.

MIRROR-BOUND EYES
Shine like still water before a storm.
Read posture, not sight.

RIFT-WOVEN CLOTH
Garments ripple like mist.
Fragments of broken reflections cling to her.

REFRACTED BLADE
Edge of condensed light.
Leaves no wound — only a fading silhouette.

GLASSVEIL CORE
Heart is a splinter of the skyglass arena.
Keeps her bound to reflections.

SLIPPERS OF SURFACE
Steps only where light can reflect.
Never leaves a ripple she doesn’t own.

ABILITIES

MIRAGE LUNGE
Teleports to your last reflected position and slashes in a crescent of radiant mist.

REFRACTED PARRY
Mirrors your next attack or spell, returning it with increased potency.

GLASSFLAME RIPOSTE
Appears in another reflection and strikes.
Harder to dodge from behind.

MIRROR FRACTURE
Shatters nearby mirrors, creating blinding shards that linger and slow.

NOTES

Reflections can include water, polished stone, glass, metal, or even magical illusions.

If you defeat her without casting a shadow, she will bow... and vanish.

Some say she once had a name.
The mirrors remember, but she does not speak it.

LORE ENTRY

Whispered in the Skyglass Arena’s Empty Hall.

“Her duels never end.
Not because she wins, but because the mirror never breaks.”

“In life, she was a master of the Skyglass Pillars — undefeated, yet unremembered.”

“When the storms shattered the arena’s ceiling, she was still mid-swing.”

“Some say her opponent left.
Others say he never existed.”

“Now, she reflects the one who enters — matching form, speed, and flaw.”

“There are no spectators anymore.
Only reflections.”

“She draws her sword when your gaze lingers too long.”

— Memory etched into a shard in the arena’s lower tier.

BEHAVIOR

MIRROR LUNGE
Charges where your reflection last moved.

REFRACTED PARRY
Blocks frontal Hue spells with a mirrored delay.

GLASSFLAME RIPOSTE
Strikes from an adjacent reflection after being dodged.

Shifts position by teleporting between reflective zones.

Disappears entirely if not attacked quickly after revealing her true form.

POSSIBLE DROPS

SKYGLASS SHARD (COMMON)
A fragment of the shattered arena.
Used in crafting reflective gear.

REFLECTED SIGIL (UNCOMMON)
Imprints your next evade with a mirrored blink afterimage.

DUELIST’S SILENCE (RARE)
A passive trait that slightly delays incoming attacks — like a perfect parry.

MIRROR OATH (LEGENDARY)
A relic that summons a phantom reflection that mimics your combat style for a short duration.

HOLLOW PROPHET

THE BLINDING SERMON

“It speaks in blinding truths...
and burns away lies.”

THREAT LEVEL

HIGH

Mental disorientation.
Forced silence.

TYPE

Radiant Undead / Blinding Oracle

FIRST EMERGED

Mid-Color War, during the Siege of Eburnea.

HUE INTERACTION

AURELIAN FUEL

Aurelian spells fuel their flash-rune intensity unless mirrored or redirected.

VULNERABLE TO COBALT

Cobalt silence spells and emotional resonance interference weaken them.

VERDANT ROOT

Verdant abilities can root them briefly by overloading sensory bloom.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

EBURNEAN DICE

The Eburnean Dice can gamble a memory against their power, weakening them briefly.

ILLUSION MAGIC

Illusion magic scrambles their sermon logic.

TRUTH-TOUCHED

Will not attack characters who carry unbroken vows or truth-touched relics.

DESCRIPTION

Tall skeletal figures robed in faded ivory, faces veiled in cracked gold masks.

They bear sunstaffs crowned with burning lenses.
Each step leaves a branded rune in the sand.

Their voice doesn’t echo—it scorches.

ANATOMY

SUN-ETCHED MASK

Cracked gold plate filters the world into painful clarity.

BONE-CLOTH ROBES

Tattered ivory wrappings inscribed with judgment verses and confession runes.

RADIANT CORE

A burning sun-knot at the chest.
Source of Sermon Pulse and Truthbrand magic.

LENS OF JUDGMENT

Staff lens focuses light into blinding glyphs and wave-searing truth.

BONE-WRAPPED STAFF

Channels solar fire and writes flash-runes into the ground.

BRANDED RUNES

Glyphs left in the sand cause pain, disorientation, or control inversion.

JUDGMENT ARSENAL

SERMON PULSE

Blasts out radiant waves that invert controls and cause visual distortion.

TRUTHBRAND

Marks the target with a searing glyph.
Lies or deceit amplify the damage.

SUNSTAFF LANCE

Fires a piercing bolt of sunfire.
Leaves a lingering burn in its wake.

BRANDING RITE

Brands the ground beneath the target.
Punishes movement with light explosions.

“No lie goes unseen.
No truth goes unburned.”

NOTES

Some believe Hollow Prophets cannot lie.
Others think they burned their own lies away—and now hunt yours.

If a player confesses a past lie through dialogue before combat, the Prophet may hesitate... or speak instead of strike.

Defeating it may yield Sun-Scorched Scripture, used to inscribe confession wards or purify corrupted shrines.

LORE ENTRY

Scorched prayer-scroll from the Aureolin Temple Ruins.

“They say the Hollow Prophets were once true seers—priests who stared too long at the midday sun and saw only truth afterward.”

“Their sermons come in pulses.
You don’t hear them—you feel them.”

“They light up every fear you’ve hidden in shadow and then ask:
‘What will you burn to make it pure?’”

“One knelt by the Eburnean Gate for days, unmoving, its voice cracking through heatwaves.

I offered coin.
It offered blindness.”

“There’s no god left in them—only clarity too sharp to carry.”

BEHAVIOR

SERMON PULSE

Initiates combat with a blinding pulse that inverts movement controls and destabilizes the HUD.

TRUTHBRANDS

Casts radiant glyphs that bind targets who have lied in previous dialogue paths or broken oaths.

RADIANT FEEDER

Immune to most light-based attacks.
Absorbs them to empower judgment spells.

SHRINE PATROLLER

Patrols ancient ruins and former shrines, preaching to the wind until trespass is made.

POSSIBLE DROPS

SUN-SCORCHED SCRIPTURE (RARE)

Pages burned with radiant ink.
Grants access to Judgment Spells or Shrine Purification.

EBURNEAN DICE FRAGMENT (RARE)

A shard of the sacred dice.
Allows a memory gamble to weaken radiant enemies.

LENS OF CONFESSION (VERY RARE)

A cracked lens that reveals hidden truths or invisible glyphs.

PROPHET’S VEIL (LEGENDARY)

Worn mask.
Grants immunity to blinding effects and reveals lies within a short range.

“THEY DO NOT SEEK FOLLOWERS.
ONLY CONFESSIONS.”

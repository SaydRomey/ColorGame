THE BOUND

WITNESSES OF STILLNESS

“Stone does not forget.
It only waits.”

OVERVIEW

Lifelike stone figures scattered across Nullshade.

Some kneel.
Some reach.

Some accuse —
but none speak aloud.

Most are inert until a player acts violently nearby or attempts to flee too quickly.

When awakened, they move with slow, unnerving grace —
as if remembering how to move.

Their strikes are delayed but catastrophic.

BASIC DATA

Type:
Dormant Sentinels / Memory Constructs

Threat Level:
High — Only when triggered; reactive danger

First Emerged:
Post-Prism Collapse, Nullshade Core Rebound

Alignment:
Neutral / Watcher

Size, Average:
2.4 m – 3.1 m

Habitat:
Nullshade — Ruins, Monolith Sites, Memory Puzzles, Core Wound

ANATOMY / PRESENCE

HOLLOW EYES

Empty sockets that seem to watch only your hesitation.

ETCHED MEMORIES

Runes carved into stone hold fragments of forgotten truths.

DELAYED GRACE

They do not rush.

They anticipate your rhythm.

SILENT THREAT

Awakens only when provoked —
but remembers forever.

LORE ENTRY

Fragment from “Etchings of the Silent Monolith” —
recovered on a cracked memory shard in Prism Wound.

“We called them guardians, once.

Remnants of the Prismara who refused hue.

But I think now…
they were simply those who saw too much.”

“You can hear them when you’re not looking —
feel your memories tighten in your chest like a thread pulling taut.

They don’t scream.

They remember for you.”

“One bowed as I passed.

The other raised a hand.

Neither moved again.

That night I dreamed of a battlefield I had never seen.

I woke with my hands bruised —
fingers curled like stone.”

“If silence had a form,
it would kneel beside them.”

— Archivist Reiss,
“Final Echoes of the Core”

HUE INTERACTION

Immune to hue magic.

Unaffected by all hue-based attacks.

WEAKENED BY MEMORY ARTIFACTS

The Bistre Lantern or Isabelline Tapestry can disrupt their stone-bound state.

CALMED BY TRUTH

Engage with memory puzzles or recite known truths to bypass or pacify them.

BEHAVIOR OVERVIEW

LINGERING STILLNESS

Remain motionless in groups, creating tense and uncanny spaces.

Presence causes audio to muffle and time to subtly distort.

ONE AWAKENS

Only one in a group typically animates at a time.

MIMICS DECISIONS

May mimic player movement seconds after it occurs —
mimicking decision-making rather than motion.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

TRUTH CONFESSION

Temporarily deactivated through full dialogue confession using truth mechanics.

VULNERABLE CORE WINDOW

Only vulnerable while reciting remembered dialogue options.

During these moments, a shimmer reveals a hollow core.

AVOIDS LIES AND FORGOTTEN THINGS

Avoids areas where the player carries false names or unclaimed memories.

TRIGGER CONDITIONS

Awakens when the player:

Performs violent actions nearby.

Attempts to flee too quickly.

Casts strong hue spells in the area.

DELAYED, UNAVOIDABLE STRIKES

Their attacks cannot be dodged traditionally —
only predicted through silence and stillness.

NOTES & DROPS

When defeated, The Bound collapse into dust, leaving behind one whisper:

“You remembered too loudly.”

POSSIBLE DROP

STONE-WHISPER FRAGMENT

A faint echo of the witness.

Can be used to unlock truth-bound dialogues or memory sanctums.

NULLSHADE SURVIVAL ADVISORY

Walk softly.

Think truly.

Speak only what is real.

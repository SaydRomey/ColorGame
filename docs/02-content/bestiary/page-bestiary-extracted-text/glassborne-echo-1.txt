BESTIARY ENTRY • 042

GLASSBORNE ECHO

MEMORY WITHOUT MERCY

“It doesn’t shatter.
It reflects you... wrong.”

OVERVIEW

Forged from fractured memory-glass, the Glassborne Echo is not alive.
It is a moment of remembrance, sharpened and untethered.

It reflects the player imperfectly, mimicking recent actions with delayed, haunting precision.

TYPE

Phantasmal Construct / Mirrorbound Wraith

THREAT LEVEL

High

FIRST EMERGED

During the Stormglass Fracture, Post-Shattering.

HUE AFFINITY

Cobalt / Mind

ANATOMY

Form never fully stable.
Built from fractured reflections.

No true face.
Only angles and memory.

Limbs bend wrong.
Always a fraction off.

BEHAVIOR: DELAYED MIMICRY

You Move → It Watches → It Mimics → It Punishes.

Mimics your last action with a brief delay.
Repeated patterns become deadly.

ATTACKS & TRAITS

MIRROR SHARDS

Sweeps arcs of crystalline slivers.
Inflicts “Reflection Wound” — repeated actions take increased damage.

ECHO STEP

Flickers and reappears a fraction later, often behind the player.
Footsteps echo a second too late.

REVERSE SHRIEK

Emits a shriek of “reverse sound,” disorienting and reversing audio cues briefly.

WEAKNESSES

MULTI-ANGLE LIGHT — SKYGLASS PRISMS

Disrupts its reflective coherence.

SONIC RESONANCE — WIND CHIMES / HARPSTRINGS / CERULEAN CLOAK

Stuns briefly when rhythm is synchronized.

MEMORY BEYOND RECOGNITION

Using Payne’s Brush or displaying unknown memories may cause it to flee.

Will vanish if reflected in more than one surface at once.

LORE: ETCHING FROM THE ZAFFRE GLASSWRIGHT

“Some reflections lie.
Others linger.”

“I thought to trap a memory in cobalt, a kindness in solid light.

But the wind here remembers grief too well, and the glass began to ache.”

“The Echo was not what I captured.
It was what I tried to forget — how I moved, how I reached, how I failed.

It mimics the moment I last looked in the glass.
That pain became recursive. It moved without me.”

“Now it wanders the skyglass shelves, looping itself — a broken dance.
I hear my own voice call out when it strikes.”

“If you see it, don’t fight what you know.
Fight what you pretend not to.”

— Zaffre Glasswright

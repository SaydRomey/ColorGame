RAZZFLARE IMP

MISCHIEF UNBOUND

“It giggles in your blind spot.
That’s when it strikes.”

THREAT LEVEL

MODERATE

Mentally disruptive and chaotic.
Low physical durability but high potential for disruption.

TYPE

Fey Trickster / Chaos Elemental

FIRST EMERGED

A failed glamour experiment under the Blooming Veil

HUE INTERACTION

WEAK TO HARMONY

Harmonic pulses and music-based artifacts can disrupt and harm the imp.

STUNNED BY LIGHT

Aurelian Light Trick or Cobalt Static Dash can stun it briefly.

IMMUNE TO CHAOS

Unaffected by fear, silence, logic, or truth-based magic.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

RHYTHMIC TIMING

Striking the imp on the beat cancels its illusion field.

MIRROR DISTRACTION

Drawn to mirrors, shiny surfaces, and echo chambers.

LAUGHTER STAGGER

If hit while mid-laugh, it is grounded and vulnerable briefly.

BESTIARY ENTRY

DESCRIPTION

These volatile little fey are the chaotic laughter of Myrrala made manifest.

They move in unpredictable patterns, warp HUD elements, and cast minor illusion spells that disrupt combat flow.

Their fire is more confusion than flame —
it burns consistency, not skin.

BEHAVIOR

Dart in and out of combat, leaving trails of visual glitches such as reversed movement and delayed input feedback.

Cast Illusion Bursts that momentarily replace the player’s form with that of nearby enemies or objects.

Upon defeat, may release a Glamour Pulse that causes one random effect, good or bad.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Vulnerable to rhythmic timing —
striking on beat cancels the illusion field.

Easily distracted by mirrored surfaces or echo chambers.

Temporarily grounded if struck while mid-laugh.

NOTES

Their laughter echoes in some dream-recordings.

Scholars believe Razzflare Imps are drawn to places where reality is worn thin by joy or grief.

Despite their danger, some mages keep them as familiars —
or try to.

ATTACKS & ABILITIES

PINKFIRE DARTS

Fires tiny bolts of illusion flame that cause confusion and minor burn.

GIGGLE BURST

A burst of laughter that scrambles controls and HUD elements briefly.

MIRROR SHUFFLE

Replaces the player with an illusion of an enemy or object for a short time.

RAZZFLARE DASH

Zips erratically between positions, leaving afterimages of pink fire.

GLAMOUR PULSE

Upon defeat, emits a pulse that causes one random effect, good or bad.

BEHAVIOR OVERVIEW

CHAOTIC ENTRY

Appears in bursts of pink and purple sparks, often behind or above the player’s position.

ILLUSION DISRUPTION

Warps screen elements, inverts controls momentarily, and produces false threats or distractions.

UNPREDICTABLE PATTERN

Rarely repeats attacks in the same order.
Relies on misdirection and sudden bursts of movement.

GLAMOUR ESCAPE

If not defeated quickly, it vanishes in a flare of prankish fire and may return with allies.

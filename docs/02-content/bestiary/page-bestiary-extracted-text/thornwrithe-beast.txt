THORNWRITHE BEAST

BRAMBLEBLOOD PREDATOR

A swift shadow wrapped in briars and rage.

“It bleeds bramble.
And it’s fast.”

CLASSIFICATION

Type:
Briar-Covered Beast / Fast Predator.

Threat Level:
HIGH — Extremely dangerous when engaged directly.

First Emerged:
Early Post-Shattering Era.

Region:
Huevale — Thornwood / Verdant Frontier.

ANATOMY BREAKDOWN

1. BRIAR-CLOAKED HIDE

Dense thorn-vines form a living armor around the body.

2. WRITHEBOUND SPINE

Thorn ridges twitch and flare before a strike.

3. ROOT-TRAIL PAWS

Each step leaves rapidly growing roots and entangling undergrowth.

4. BRAMBLEBLOOD WOUNDS

Injuries sprout thorny growths that hamper prey movement.

5. AMBUSH JAW

Fast snapping maw hidden beneath vine curtains.

6. VINED FLANK MUSCULATURE

Silent, spring-loaded movement through dense foliage.

7. RAGE BLOOM NODES

Crimson agitation causes the vines to lash and the beast to become more aggressive.

DESCRIPTION

A swift quadruped encased in dense, thorny briars and vines that writhe constantly around its body.

It moves silently through Huevale’s dense foliage, attacking prey in brutal hit-and-run ambushes.

Each wound it inflicts quickly sprouts painful thorn-growths, restricting movement and feeding panic.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Found in dense Huevale undergrowth, shadowed glades, bramble-marked borders, and living forest paths near damaged groves.

Often appears where the forest has stored anger, grief, or violated memory.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

Stalk → Burst Ambush → Entangle → Retreat → Circle → Enraged Charge.

Uses hit-and-run tactics from concealed positions.

Fiercely patrols and defends bramble-marked territory.

Retreats into foliage, then reemerges unpredictably.

Shows particular hostility toward intruders who burn or damage forest areas.

ABILITIES / STATUS EFFECTS

BRAMBLE REND

Fast pounce attack that causes thorn-growth wounds.

ROOT SNARE TRAIL

Leaves entangling roots behind its movement path.

WRITHE RETREAT

Vanishes into foliage after striking.

GRIEVANCE SURGE

Becomes more aggressive when Crimson flame is nearby.

SCAR SEED

Survivors carry twisting vine-like scars.

HUE INTERACTION

Resistant to Verdant control; attempts to manipulate its vines cause violent backlash.

Leaves behind trails of entangling roots that heavily restrict mobility.

Crimson flame can damage it, but also enrages it, increasing aggression.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Earthen decay spells wither its protective vines, lowering defense and mobility.

Sustained fire-based attacks are effective but risky because they trigger rage.

Powerful sonic or vibration attacks disrupt coordination and briefly stun it.

Roots, moss, and briars respond to grief and spellcraft.

STORMWRIT HOUND

BOLT BETWEEN WORLDS

Howling phantom stitched from wire and cloud.

“It howls in lightning —
and leaps between bolts.”

TYPE

Lightning Beast / Elemental Predator

THREAT LEVEL

HIGH

Fast, Unpredictable, Anti-Casting

FIRST EMERGED

During the Tempest Surge following the Shattering.

REGION

Iriseld —
Tidespire Reach

ANATOMY

1. STORMWIRE SPINE

A living lattice of charged filaments that channels and redirects strikes.

2. GLASS CLAWS

Shattered skyglass blades that bite through armor and ether.

3. PLASMA LIMBS

Limbs of condensed plasma and storm pressure;
phase between states.

4. PRESSURE-DROP MAW

Jaws that open the air —
causing a pressure drop that scrambles focus.

5. STATIC AFTERIMAGE

Leaves a transient copy woven from residual static and lightning memory.

6. SKY-INK HIDE

Hide of midnight cloud and inked wires that smudge across the ether.

7. LIGHTNING-NODE LEAP

Traverses arcs and relays by riding lightning nodes like stepping stones.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Patrols cliff edges, storm routes, lightning-node paths, skyglass spikes, copper relays, and broken bridges across Iriseld.

Most often appears where high wind, static buildup, and exposed traversal routes overlap.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

PACE → LOCK ON → NODE LEAP → FLASH-FANG LUNGE → PRESSURE HOWL

Paces cliff edges until it senses fast movement or charged spell buildup.

Leaps between lightning nodes marked by skyglass spikes or copper relays.

Uses Flash-Fang Lunge:
teleports, strikes, and leaves a static copy for 2 seconds.

Occasionally howls, stunning rhythm-based abilities and lowering visibility in a storm flash.

ABILITIES / STATUS EFFECTS

FLASH-FANG LUNGE

Teleport strike with static afterimage.

PRESSURE-DROP HOWL

Interrupts rhythm and casting focus.

STATIC COPY

Leaves a brief false duplicate after leaping.

COLOR BIND DISRUPTION

At high static charge, briefly disables Color Binding UI.

ARC-TRAIL AFTERIMAGE

Flickering path residue that confuses timing.

HUE INTERACTION

Disrupts spell channeling, especially Cobalt-based storm or rhythm spells.

At high static charge, briefly disables Color Binding UI.

Can be slowed if surrounded by harmonic resonance, instruments, or echo chimes.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Susceptible to wind-grounding relics and Cerulean Cloak effects.

Harmonic instruments such as skybell chimes or glass flutes distort its targeting.

Momentarily pacified if its own lightning arc is reflected with a Payne’s Prism Pane or storm mirror.

LORE FRAGMENT — ARCHIVIST IOL

Before I saw it, the air thinned —
as if the world held its breath.

The clouds folded in half.

A line of lightning scribbled itself awake.

At first it chased birds through the storm.

Then it appeared inside my arcshield.

Its howl shattered something behind the eyes —
like a voice carving bone.

I left the cliffs, but not this journal.

Some truths cling like static.

DESCRIPTION

The Stormwrit Hound was forged in a lightning rift during the great Iriseld storms.

It is less a beast than a sentient arc of electrical fury.

It appears as if drawn in sky-ink:
wires twitching, plasma limbs jittering between now and next.

This predator does not stalk;
it interrupts — appearing mid-cast, mid-jump, or mid-thought to snap a moment in half.

IRISELD — REGIONAL CONTEXT

Storm-battered cliffs and sky routes across the Tidespire Reach.

Copper relays and skyglass spires draw and channel volatile currents.

Travelers beware:
the storm writes its own predators.

TACTICAL NOTE

Best countered with grounding, harmonic disruption, and reflected arcs.

Break its flow, ground its nodes, then strike between its leaps.

HUNTER NOTE

“Don’t blink during thunder.”

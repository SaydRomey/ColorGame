CAPUT MARIONETTE

THE ALCHEMIST’S SHAME

“They were never truly alive — just waiting to be dismissed.”

THREAT LEVEL

MODERATE

Area-dependent hazard

TYPE

Alchemical Undead / Ritual Construct

FIRST EMERGED

During the Phylactery Rebellion, Year of the Dull Moon.

STITCHED REMNANT

Patchwork of ritual cloth, stitched organs, and rust-streaked bone.
Animated by incomplete soulbinding.

OBEY LONG-DEAD COMMANDS

They follow the last instruction given in life.
Most now know only one command: wait.

WAIT WITHOUT MALICE

They do not attack immediately.
They observe. They wait. And they remember.

FAILED VESSELS

Crude bodies built for souls too broken to scream.
Rejected. Reused. Forgotten.

HUE INTERACTION

RESISTANT TO BROWN (EARTHEN)

Decay and rot have no hold—they are already ruined.

WEAK TO VIOLET

Illusions and mind trickery confuse and unravel their control.

VULNERABLE TO CRIMSON

Direct damage bypasses their stitched protections and weak bindings.

STABILIZED BY PHYLACTERY

In the presence of Caput Mortuum Phylactery relics, they slow and become inert briefly.

LORE ENTRY

Found in the marginalia of an alchemy ledger, author unknown.

“Caput Mortuum.
The name means death’s leftover.

What the crucible couldn’t use.”

“We made them to test transmutation rituals.
Vessels for souls too broken to scream.

They walked, moved, answered.
But never lived.”

“I buried mine beneath the Phylactery Vault, but it clawed free.
It didn’t attack — it waited at my old desk.”

“If they look at you, it’s not hate.
It’s recognition.

And that’s worse.”

DESCRIPTION

Caput Marionettes are abandoned constructs—partially animated corpses intended for soul transfer tests.

Left unfinished, they wander memory halls and ruined labs, seeking the rituals they were meant to complete.

Wrapped in tattered ritual cloth and bound by rusted alchemical wire, their hollow eyes burn with residual purpose.
They do not speak—only obey.

BEHAVIOR

REMAINS INERT

Will not move unless a player disturbs specific relics, memory statues, or spirit glyphs.

JERKY, UNNATURAL MOVEMENT

Moves with jerking precision, as if pulled by invisible strings of forgotten orders.

DEATH DECAY

Upon death, releases a cloud of alchemical rot that drains memory-bound artifacts unless dispelled.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

SOULWARD SIGIL / TRUTH-NAME

Marking or speaking their intended name before they activate can prevent them from moving.

CONFUSED BY ILLUSIONS

Illusions and false commands cause them to hesitate, turn, or attack one another.

VOICE OF THE SUMMONER

Hearing the voice that created them, paired via audio glyphs, will freeze them in place momentarily.

NOTES

If purified rather than killed, a Caput Marionette may kneel before the player and whisper a forgotten alchemical recipe.

Some whisper in your voice.

They are most commonly found in abandoned labs, phylactery vaults, and memory trial chambers.

Be wary of areas filled with ritual glyphs or half-written summoning circles—they may be waiting for the command to finish.

POSSIBLE DROPS

RUSTED PUPPET CORE

Used in alchemical crafting or ritual stabilization.

FORGOTTEN RECIPE FRAGMENT

Rare chance to contain a forgotten recipe or formula.

ALCHEMICAL WIRE

Used in crafting bindings and construct gear.

DECAYED MEMORY VIAL

Contains lingering memories or echoes of their summoner.

THEY WERE MADE TO SERVE.
NOW THEY SERVE ONLY WHAT REMAINS.

Remember:
Not all that moves is alive.

Not all that waits is harmless.

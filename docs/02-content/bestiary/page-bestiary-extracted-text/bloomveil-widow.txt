DESCRIPTION

The Bloomveil Widow is a spectral predator created from the collective dreams of the fey.

As much a trickster as a predator, she weaves charming webs that ensnare both mind and body.

Her webs are woven from memories—those not just forgotten, but abandoned.
Once ensnared, her victims will find themselves questioning their reality.

Her charm pheromones cause those who enter her lair to grow lethargic or entranced before she strikes.

BEHAVIOR

Appears as a calming presence, offering guidance or comfort through enchantment.

Teleports using silk threads, creating traps and choke points across the area.

Lures prey into its web-lined chambers and traps them in dream loops.

Attacks when the player is most vulnerable—emotionally or physically.

Vulnerable only when her real form is revealed via Thulian Mirror or Violet-bound abilities.

SILENCED SONG

Cobalt’s Mind Echo or Aurelian Light Trick can silence her pheromones and lullaby.

RHYTHM DISRUPTION

Her webs follow a pattern.
Attack when her weaving begins to break the rhythm.

Wears off if Heliotrope Band is equipped—her charm cannot take hold.

ATTACKS & ABILITIES

NOTES

Her webs may trap past memories in physical form—players must face illusions of regret, fear, or loss.

Leaving her lair often leaves behind a lingering sense of déjà vu.

Some say she feeds not on flesh but on unfulfilled dreams.

A slain Widow’s lair may collapse into a field of dream-blooms—beautiful, but toxic.

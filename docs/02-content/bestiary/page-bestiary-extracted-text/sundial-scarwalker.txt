THE WEIGHT OF TIME

The shadow that counts every step.
You can’t run from time. Especially when it’s hunting.

DESCRIPTION

A towering warrior built from layered sun-tempered stone and obsidian.

Its face is a circular sundial ring that rotates subtly, and it leaves scorch-marks shaped like clock hands where it steps.

It hunts through rhythm, shadow, and inevitability.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Found in solar ruins, ritual courtyards, sun courts, desert roads, collapsed observatories, and sacred timekeeping sites across Luxeran.

Most dangerous in bright open areas at midday, near functioning solar mechanisms, mirrored courts, or sun-lit shrine thresholds.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

STILL SENTINEL → HOURSTEP TRACKING → SHADOW ALIGNMENT → NONSTRIKE → GLYPH RESET

“HOURSTEP” MECHANIC

Only moves when the player moves, mimicking the player’s last path with a 2-second delay.

“NONSTRIKE”

In bright zones, attacks deal double damage if the player is mid-action.

“SHADOWBIND”

When its shadow aligns with the player’s, it stuns and delivers a critical slash.

Rhythmic drumming announces its approach; ignoring the movement of its shadow leads to ambush.

ABILITIES / STATUS EFFECTS

HOURSTEP

Delayed mimic pursuit.

NONSTRIKE

Punishes actions in bright light.

SHADOWBIND

Shadow-alignment stun and critical hit.

SUNDIAL GLYPH CAST

Creates temporal hazard circles / solar timing zones.

JUDGMENT MARCH

Relentless pressure by rhythm and shadow.

HUE INTERACTION

Ignores illusionary magic completely and tracks true player movement.

Speeds up in bright Aurelian light and slows in shadow.

Temporarily disrupted by Cobalt echo abilities or time-fracture artifacts.

Light-blocking tools and sun disruption interfere with its strongest solar attacks.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Predictable with clever timing; pausing or stutter-stepping confuses its delayed tracking.

Most vulnerable when casting its own sundial glyphs due to a slow wind-up.

Blocking light or using sun-disruption tools disables Nonstrike temporarily.

Shadow and dusk conditions reduce its pressure and speed.

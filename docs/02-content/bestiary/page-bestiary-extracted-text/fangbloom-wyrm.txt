FANGBLOOM WYRM

BLOSSOMCOIL SERPENT

“Buried in blossom,
it waits for motion.”

THREAT LEVEL

HIGH

Lethal on first encounter.

TYPE

Camouflaged Serpent / Floral Predator

FIRST EMERGED

Post-Shattering Era.

HUE INTERACTION

IMMUNE TO VISUAL DETECTION

Immune without specialized means, such as Skobeloff Lens or Echo spells.

DRAWN TO HEAT AND MOTION

Drawn more to heat and motion than hue, though it will prioritize highly Hue-bound individuals.

PETAL-CAMOUFLAGE

Can absorb minor Verdant or Violet hue pulses, rendering some scans ineffective.

UNAFFECTED BY ILLUSIONS

Its senses are rooted in organic vibration, not sight.

DESCRIPTION

The Fangbloom Wyrm is a serpentine creature that disguises itself among Huevale’s lush floral beds.

Its outer layers mimic blooming flowers and soft moss, concealing a coiled predator beneath.
Once prey disturbs the area, the petals peel back, revealing a maw lined with hooked, chlorophyll-coated fangs.

ANATOMY

PETAL CREST

Bioluminescent petals flare when agitated or preparing to strike.

MAW OF THORNS

Hooked, vine-laced teeth inject a paralytic toxin that slows bloodflow and root-sense.

VINE TONGUE

Whip-fast tendril able to taste, latch, and pull prey underground.

CAMOUFLAGE PETALS

Layered floral scales blend with ground cover and shift temperature to match surroundings.

ROOTTAIL

Prehensile tail used to anchor, drag prey, or collapse tunnels beneath the surface.

SILT GLIDE VENTS

Underside gills allow silent movement through moist soil and rootwater.

LORE ENTRY

Testimony of Eiren the Bloomwalker, last seen near Petalband Glade.

“I knelt to gather dreampetals—careful, reverent, as my grandmother taught me.
The blooms were warm from the sun, soft, still. Then the ground... breathed.”

“I felt it—not just the movement, but the hunger.
A sudden pull, my satchel gone, then a blur of blossoms spiraling into the underbrush.

The flowers weren’t flowers.
They were scales. And beneath them, rows of teeth.”

“I’ve seen its path since—empty patches where no insects hum, no roots dare grow.
It glides just beneath the surface, waiting. Watching.

Camouflaged not in earth, but in beauty.”

“The forest doesn’t warn of it.
It lets it be.

Maybe because it’s not a monster to the woods...
just another bloom that bites.”

— Journal fragment recovered from Eiren’s tent, two days after disappearance.

BEHAVIOR

Lies in wait beneath shallow rootbeds or floral clearings, perfectly still.

Strikes with immense speed, often dragging prey underground or into a tangle trap.

Will retreat if wounded, leaving behind severed petals or trail fragments to mislead pursuit.

Hunts at dawn and dusk, when floral scent is strongest and visibility softens.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

ECHO-BASED DETECTION

Cobalt or Earthen echo spells reveal its true shape and underground movement.

SONIC PULSES

Disorient its sensory vines and delay its strike reaction.

CRUSHING DAMAGE

Breaks through its floral armor and exposes softer inner flesh.

FIRE DRIES PETALS

Extended flame weakens its camouflage and damages petal layers.

NOTES

The Wyrm’s petals are sometimes found scattered after an encounter—still glowing faintly, still warm.

Huevale healers claim these remnants hum softly when held, repeating the last heartbeat of whatever the Wyrm consumed.

Do not linger in disturbed blossoms.
The scent may call others.

Travelers say the forest grows quieter after a Wyrm has passed.

POSSIBLE DROPS

BLOOMCOIL SCALE (RARE)

Hardened petal-scale used in high-tier armor and trapcraft.

WYRM PETAL FRAGMENTS

Warm, bioluminescent petals used in alchemy and ambient infusions.

ROOTBLOOD VIAL

Paralytic toxin extracted from the Wyrm’s fangs.
Highly potent.

DREAMPETAL HEART (VERY RARE)

A pulsing core found deep within ancient Wyrms.
Resonates with memory.

BEAUTY HIDES.
HUNGER MOVES.

ONLY AWARENESS SURVIVES.

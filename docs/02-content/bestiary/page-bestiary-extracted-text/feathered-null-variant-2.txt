FEATHERED NULL

THE STILLBORN SKY

“The wind gave up.
It became still.”

THREAT LEVEL

MODERATE

Traversal and hue suppression.
Not inherently hostile.

TYPE

Colorless Entity / Anti-Storm Apparition

FIRST EMERGED

During the Dimming of the Third Gale.

HUE INTERACTION

SUPPRESSES HUE TRAVERSAL

Gliding, storm-riding, and other Hue-based movement abilities are disabled within its aura.

DIMS BOUND COLORS

All bound hues lose intensity.
Crimson surge weakens. Cobalt reaction timing is delayed.

DISRUPTS HUE SCANS

Its null presence scrambles visual and magical scans, making it difficult to detect without specialized means.

RESTORED BY SKYGROWTH

Verdant Skygrowth magic can temporarily restore buoyancy and hue stability nearby.

DESCRIPTION

A rare Nullborne being shaped by stillness itself.

The Feathered Null hovers in Iriseld’s upper strata, its presence silencing the winds and draining the storm’s will.
More melancholic than malevolent, it exerts passive denial of momentum — anchoring what should soar.

ANATOMY

COLORLESS PLUMAGE

Feathers are threadbare, absorbing light and scattering no hue.

NULL HEART

A hollow core of still wind.
Its pulse interrupts momentum rather than life.

DRIFTING TAILFEATHERS

Trail like unraveling storm threads.
Often fade moments after it passes.

HOLLOW FACEPLATE

No features.
It does not watch — it measures and forgets.

TORN WING BONE

Wings are real, but unpowered.
They cannot lift even its own weight.

SILENCE WAKE

A constant aura of pale wind that muffles sound and drains will.

LORE ENTRY

“It didn’t fall.
The sky simply stopped lifting it.”

“When the Prism cracked, there were angels of color — fragments given shape by grief and wind.
This was one.”

“The Feathered Null was born not in flight, but in forgetting how flight felt.”

“Its wings bear no color.
Its voice no echo. It moves where the wind used to be.”

“Glaucous said it hovered outside her cliffs for three nights, silent — not a threat.
Just watching. Measuring.”

“And when it left...
the storm forgot how to speak for a day.”

“We believe it is not hostile.
But we are not sure it remembers how to be kind.”

— Archivist Lethara, Keeper of Unseen Currents

BEHAVIOR

DOES NOT PURSUE

It drifts.
It is pulled by absence, not by prey.

ENGAGES ONLY WHEN PROVOKED

Or approached during a storm ritual or while riding the wind.

BEHAVIOR CYCLE

NULL PULSE

Disables midair movement and storm abilities for several seconds.

WING VEIL

Conceals its position in a dome of pale wind.

ECHO STILLNESS

Creates a silence field that interrupts dialogue and spellcasting.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

SOUND REPELLENT

Repelled by sky-sound, such as storm chimes, flutes, or high wind-toned melodies.

HUE TRIANGULATION

Becomes unstable if trapped in a triangulation of multiple hues, such as player + relic + echo site.

EMOTIVE WIND SURGES

Only vulnerable during emotive wind surges triggered by memory sequences.

NOT HARMFUL DIRECTLY

Physical weapons pass through it harmlessly.
It must be destabilized by its own nature.

NOTES

The Glaucous Wing claims this creature is “a hymn never sung.”

Cannot be bound, named, or remembered by normal means — journals written near it lose ink.

Ink dries mid-sentence.
Names fade from the tongue. Even memory blurs.

Not all who encounter it survive the silence.
Some choose to stay — and become still.

When it departs, the wind resumes...
but something remains quieter.

POSSIBLE DROPS

NULLFEATHER (RARE)

A lightless feather.
Used in crafting wind-resistant cloaks and silencers.

HOLLOW WIND CORE

Core of stillness.
Vital for advanced anti-storm enchantments.

GLAUCOUS MEMORY SHARD

Fragment of a forgotten sky memory.
Used to unlock memory wind rituals.

SILENT INK VIAL

Ink that writes in still air.
Words vanish unless spoken aloud.

ECHOLESS TALON (VERY RARE)

A talon that passed through sound and came back unchanged.

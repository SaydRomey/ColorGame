SKYCALL WRAITH

VOICE OF THE FORGOTTEN WIND

Pale spirits drifting on broken currents.

“The wind lost its name.
So it screams.”

TYPE

Aerial Apparition / Wind-echo Specter

THREAT LEVEL

Moderate —
Highly Disruptive to Traversal

FIRST EMERGED

After the Collapse of the Singing Gate
(Shattering Era)

REGION

Iriseld —
Tidespire Reach

ANATOMY BREAKDOWN

1. TORN AZURE ROBES

Cloth drifts without weight, never touching stone.

2. WINDSPLIT VISAGE

Mouth opens into a shriek that fractures sound and direction.

3. ECHO-HOLLOW CHEST

A spirit cavity where grief and wind repeat.

4. CURRENT-BOUND LIMBS

Long spectral arms pulled by invisible airstreams.

5. SCREAMING VEILS

Trailing ribbons carry dissonant gusts that distort sound cues.

6. LEVITATION DRIFT

It hovers on broken currents rather than flying normally.

7. FOG RETREAT

Dissolves into mist when surrounded by silence.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Patrol wind tunnels, cliff ledges, and broken sky bridges.

Often forms around spiral currents and storm-bent architecture.

Found near listening bells, skyglass spires, and high traversal routes.

Its presence turns open air into hostile space.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

PATROL → DISRUPTION CRY → VERTIGO PULSE → FOG RETREAT

When provoked, it emits a disruption cry that cancels flight and glide, disorients sound-based cues, and at high stacks triggers a Vertigo Pulse that pulls the player down or sideways mid-air.

If surrounded by silence, it flees into fog.

ABILITIES / STATUS EFFECTS

DISRUPTION CRY

Cancels flight / glide and destabilizes storm platforms.

SOUND DISTORTION

Overrides sound-triggered puzzle inputs and audio cues.

VERTIGO PULSE

Pulls the player downward or sideways in mid-air.

TRAVERSAL DAMPENING

Weakens nearby Cobalt-based traversal effects.

HUE INTERACTION

Dampens all Cobalt-based traversal effects nearby, such as glide extensions or static dash support.

Cries override sound-triggered puzzle inputs.

Vulnerable to grounded or Earthen counterweights, which temporarily still its flight.

Mind Echo can overwrite its cry with a stored sound, especially the player’s own voice.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Weak to Earthen grounding spells.

Silence interrupts the shriek cycle.

Double-jump rhythm breaks can disrupt its pressure timing.

Sound-null zones and anti-air timing reduce its threat.

It can be forced away by sustained silence or stillness fields.

DESCRIPTION

The Skycall Wraith is a spirit-shaped echo —
the wind given form by grief and repetition.

Found along high ledges and spiral currents, it disrupts movement with piercing shrieks that unbind gliding and destabilize storm-anchored platforms.

It is not hostile in the traditional sense.
But it calls out — and what it calls hurts.

LORE FRAGMENT

Shattered Listening Bell

“When the storm broke the tower,
we thought the cries were just wind in the bells.

But it was the wind forgetting its name.

One called my name.
Not how I say it... how I meant it, once.

We ring the bells now not for warning...
but to remind the sky of silence.”

— Wind-Monk Rellian

IRISELD — TIDESPIRE REACH

Storm-broken cliffs, skyglass spires, and listening bells mark the high routes where the Wraith drifts.

TACTICAL NOTE

Aerial traversal punisher.

Best countered with grounding, silence, and rhythm breaks.

NOTES FROM THE FIELD

especially those carved or whispered.

Rumors speak of one that hums a lullaby from before the Shattering,
always off-key.

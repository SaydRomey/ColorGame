MEMORY GRAFTER

THE REWRITER OF YOU

“You don’t remember losing your name.
Just that he started calling you something else.”

THREAT LEVEL
HIGH

Reality distortion and identity theft.
Avoid prolonged exposure.

TYPE
Narrative Parasite / Nullshade Manipulator

FIRST EMERGED
Mid-Nullshade Collapse, during the Prism Shard Fracture.

HUE INTERACTION

REFLECTS HUE SPELLS
Most hue damage is reflected as twisted abilities of altered effect.

ABILITY REWRITER
May rewrite one of your equipped abilities, replacing it with a flawed version.

PARADOX WEAKNESS
Violet paradox effects and Bistre Lantern activations can destabilize its hold.

COLOR CORRUPTION
Drains hue resonance over time, dulling your aura and weakening core traits.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

PRISM SIGIL BINDING
Binding it within a Prism Sigil Circle can “pin” its scroll, revealing and undoing some of its grafts.

TRUE MEMORY SPOKEN
Can be interrupted if the player recalls and speaks a true memory from early in their journey.

MEMORY SHARD CONFLICT
Weakens when confronted by another player or NPC memory shard from a side quest.

DESCRIPTION

A tall, shrouded figure that edits identity through ink and illusion.

The Memory Grafter does not kill — it alters.
It steals memories, replaces them with crafted falsehoods, and makes you live inside a story that was never yours.

Its true weapon is belief.

ANATOMY

VELLUM MASK
Empty face of shifting ink.
It changes to reflect whoever looks upon it.

INKED ROBES
Patchworked with stolen names, forgotten glyphs, and dried memory stains.

SCRIBER’S TOOLS
Carries quills, vials of Nullshade ink, and a void-etched lantern.

LIVING SCROLL
Its scroll is alive—constantly writing, erasing, and replacing names and stories.

SHADOW THREADS
Black-violet threads tether to the target’s mind, rewriting thought as it connects.

INK FOOTPRINTS
Leaves ink that rewrites recent steps or actions if touched or revisited.

GRAFTING ABILITIES

MEMORY LEECH
Drains a recent memory, reducing resistance and revealing false memories.

IDENTITY SWAP
Mimics the player or ally, creating a perfect illusion that fights in its place.

INK REWRITE
Replaces one ability with a corrupted version with altered cost or effect.

NAMEFALL
Speaks your name backward, causing disorientation and reversed controls briefly.

NARRATIVE VEIL
Casts a field that masks reality — UI, minimap, and objectives become unreliable.

NOTES

Some players never realize they’ve been grafted until NG+ or endgame reveals.

Long exposure can lead to permanent dialogue changes with NPCs.

Its scroll is said to be endless.
No one knows who wrote the first name on it.

LORE ENTRY

Transcript Fragment — “Stolen Histories,” banned from the Bistre Archive.

“It didn’t kill her.
It rewrote her.”

“When I found what was left, she still moved like my sister.
Still used her name.”

“But when she spoke, I knew...”

“She had new stories.
Ones she hadn’t lived.”

“He holds a scroll.
Always writing. Always replacing.”

“You see yourself on it, eventually.”

“If you forget the color of your thoughts — you’ve already been grafted.”

BEHAVIOR

LURKS, THEN LATCHES
Doesn’t initiate combat directly.
Lingers near puzzles, lore nodes, or memory triggers.

MIMICS YOUR PAST
Copies small parts of your history—gear, dialogue, voice lines, and even movement.

ESCALATING REWRITE
Gradually replaces passives, UI labels, and even perception.
You may not notice until it is too late.

RETREAT PATTERN
Flees when its manipulations are exposed or undone.
Leaves behind inked echoes that fade quickly.

POSSIBLE DROPS

REWRITTEN PAGE (COMMON)
A fragment of inked parchment.
Used to alter story outcomes or equipment flavor texts.

NULLSHADE QUILL (RARE)
A dripping quill used to inscribe abilities with shifting effects.

STOLEN NAME TOKEN (RARE)
A whisper-bound name tag.
Used to unlock hidden dialogue or trade with secret NPCs.

GRAFTER’S LANTERN (LEGENDARY)
Reveals hidden memory paths and protects against identity-based attacks for a limited time.

UNWRITTEN TRUTH (UNIQUE)
A blank scroll that can overwrite any one permanent change.

“THE SCARIEST LIE IS THE ONE YOU START TO BELIEVE ABOUT YOURSELF.”

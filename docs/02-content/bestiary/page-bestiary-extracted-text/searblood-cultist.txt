SEARBLOOD CULTIST

VESSEL OF BOILING FAITH

They burn for devotion —
and they smile through the flames.

“Their veins boil.
Their gods demand it.”

CLASSIFICATION

Name:
Searblood Cultist

Type:
Human / Mutated Zealot / Ritual Flamebearer

Region:
Vireya — Ember Expanse

Threat Level:
Medium–High

First Emerged:
After the Cindersplit Mass Rites, Year of Ash 12

Role Tags:
Volatile, Suicidal Caster, Aggressive Swarm Zealot

DESCRIPTION

Flamebound zealots who bleed pyre-oil and chant to forgotten ember gods.

Their bodies are branded with sigils and their veins simmer with volatile sacrament.

In battle they ignite themselves to hurl flamebursts or rush blindly into melee, dying in glorious combustion.

ANATOMY / TRAITS

BRAND-SEARED FACE

Ritual brands fuse flesh to faith.

CHANT-LOCKED JAW

Jaws wired open by chant-runes and zeal.

BOILING VEINS

Pyre-oil and blood simmer under the skin.

FAITHFIRE AURA

Constant heat halo sears the unworthy.

BLOOD-SOAKED BINDINGS

Bindings feed on pain and fuel the flames.

COMBUSTION CORE

Heart-locus stores volatile sacrament;
detonates on death.

SHRINE-BANNER TETHER

Tethered to shrine banners that empower and control the zeal.

BEHAVIOR

Always aggressive.

Aggro range increases near other cultists.

Cast self-immolation buffs that raise damage but drain health.

On death, explode in a radius of scorched faithfire.

Lingering hazard remains after detonation.

HUE INTERACTION

Crimson hue grows unstable over time.

Can be briefly pacified by Crimson Weaver’s Thread.

Resists Verdant and Earthen control.

Flame aura can be quenched by Cobalt mist or drowning effects.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Vulnerable during chant animations.

Interrupt with sound or silence attacks.

Quench flame aura with Cobalt-based moisture.

If separated from shrine banners, they lose coordination and become unstable.

VIREYA — EMBER EXPANSE

The Ember Expanse is a scorched realm of ash and obsidian where faith is fuel and pain is proof.

ALCHEMICAL NOTES

Distilled samples are unstable —
handle with sacred caution.

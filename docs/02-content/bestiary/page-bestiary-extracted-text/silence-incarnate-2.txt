BESTIARY — VOID ENTITIES

OVERVIEW

Type:
Voidbound Observer / Static Sentinel

Threat Level:
HIGH

Psychological, Control-Based

First Emerged:
At the edge of the Core Wound, within stilled air

ANATOMY & PRESENCE

THE HALO OF STILLNESS

A broken ring carved from voidstone.
It is not a head — it is a symbol.

THE UNBLINKING CORE

Not an eye.
Not alive.

It is perception without will.
It does not shift.

You do.

SHROUD OF NULLLIGHT

Absorbs hue, sound, and motion.
Fragments of unrealized futures drift along its edges.

SCALE

Approximately 3–5 m in height.

TERRAIN PREFERENCE

Glyph Gates.

Silent Chambers.

Echo Nodes.

BEHAVIOR

Appears near key glyph gates or during silent puzzle segments.

Does not pursue, but rotates slowly to follow your last clear movement.

If it locks eyes for more than 3 seconds, disables your action inputs until you find cover or line-break.

DETECTION SEQUENCE

1. Detects movement.

2. Adjusts gaze.

3. Gaze lock — 3 sec.

Actions disabled.

HUE INTERACTION

Nullifies all active hue-based abilities when visible.

Can only be misdirected using illusion spells, light-bending terrain, or architectural interference.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Reflections, such as Shatterglass Pools, confuse its targeting briefly.

Using Violet’s Glamour Pulse or Yellow’s Lightform Cloak can disrupt its line of sight.

Activating certain lore-bound artifacts, such as the Bistre Lantern or Isabelline Tapestry, may cause it to recede or glitch.

NOTES

Presence fades after completing key puzzle objectives.

If ignored too long, the area around it distorts —
time slows, UI shimmers, sound vanishes.

Some players report feeling watched after defeating it.

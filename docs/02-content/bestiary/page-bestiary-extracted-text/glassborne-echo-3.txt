GLASSBORNE ECHO

MEMORY WITHOUT MERCY

“It doesn’t shatter.
It reflects you... wrong.”

THREAT LEVEL

HIGH

Reactive and disorienting.

TYPE

Phantasmal Construct / Mirrorbound Wraith

FIRST EMERGED

During the Stormglass Fracture, Post-Shattering.

HUE INTERACTION

RESONATES WITH COBALT

Resonates violently with Cobalt echoes and Mind-based magic.

SOUND DISRUPTION

Briefly stunned when surrounded by synchronized audio rhythms.
Wind chimes, harpstrings, and Cerulean Cloak pulses are effective.

IMMUNE TO ILLUSION

Immune to illusion — it is one.

DESCRIPTION

Forged accidentally by attempts to preserve memory in enchanted glass, the Glassborne Echo is not a ghost.
It is the moment of remembrance, sharpened and untethered.

Its form reflects the player imperfectly, mirroring their recent movements in delayed mimicry.

Striking one fractures its body, sending slivers of mirrored force in wide arcs.

ANATOMY

REFLECTIVE CORE

A shardbound heart of memory-glass.
Holds the Echo’s looped recall.

MIRROR VEIL

Fragmented plates of illusion-glass bend light and sound, delaying perception.

ECHO THREADS

Whispering filaments trace the player’s last actions in time-delayed loops.

DISTORTED LIMBS

Form shifts just out of alignment, causing attacks to land “wrong” or delayed.

SHARD TRAIL

Every step leaves razor mirror-slivers that carry reflected harm.

REVERSE VOICE

Emits a shriek of reverse sound when struck.
It is heard as the past calling back.

LORE ENTRY

Etching from a sealed mirror-window, signed by the Zaffre Glasswright.

“Some reflections lie.
Others linger.”

“I thought to trap a memory in cobalt, a kindness in solid light.

But the wind here remembers grief too well, and the glass began to ache.”

“The Echo was not what I captured.
It was what I tried to forget — how I moved, how I reached, how I failed.

It mimics the moment I last looked in the glass.
That pain became recursive. It moved without me.”

“Now it wanders the skyglass shelves, looping itself — a broken dance.
I hear my own voice call out when it strikes.”

“If you see it, don’t fight what you know.
Fight what you pretend not to.”

— Zaffre Glasswright

BEHAVIOR

MIMIC DELAY LOOP

Loops player actions back at them with a time delay.
Mimics dashes, spells, and jumps.

SHARD TRAIL

Trails mirror shards that apply “Reflection Wound.”
Any repeated action takes more damage.

UNPREDICTABLE FLICKER

Flickers unpredictably when struck.
Reappears near reflections or in line of sight.

ECHO SHRIEK

On heavy damage, emits a shriek of reverse sound.
Nearby foes are briefly disoriented.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

SONIC RESONANCE

Vulnerable to sonic resonance and multi-angle light, such as Skyglass Prisms.

UNPREDICTABLE MOVEMENT

Avoid prolonged mimicry.
Stagger movement patterns to confuse it.

PAINT OF TRUTH

May flee if the player uses Payne’s Brush or displays a memory it cannot interpret.

MULTI-REFLECTION

Will vanish if reflected in more than one surface at once.
Dual mirrors or a glass pool can trigger this.

NOTES

Some say each Echo was born from a different regret.
Others claim they all share one memory: falling.

A rare few Echoes have been seen pausing...
as if remembering something they wish they hadn’t.

The deeper the memory-glass fracturing in a region, the more aggressive and intelligent the Echoes become.

ENCOUNTER TIPS

Change rhythms, spacing, and spell cadence to break its loop.

Use synchronized audio or light sources to overwhelm its mimicry.

It feeds on recognition.
Deny it familiarity, and it weakens.

Fight in open areas with multiple reflective surfaces to exploit its reflection vulnerability.

POSSIBLE DROPS

ECHOGLASS SHARD (RARE)

A shard of memory-glass.
Used to craft delay-based weapons.

MIRROR THREAD

Threads that hum with time-delayed resonance.

REVERSED TONE CORE (RARE)

A core that stores reversed sound waves.
Used in sonic weapons and traps.

SHATTERED MEMORY (UNIQUE)

A rare, unstable relic containing a fragment of forgotten memory.
Its whispers change daily.

AND YOU’LL MEET WHAT YOU REGRET.”

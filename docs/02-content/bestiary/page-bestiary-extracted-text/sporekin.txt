DESCRIPTION

Sporekin appear as small, moss-covered humanoids with glowing fungal crowns.

Innocent at first glance, they emit continuous clouds of spores that warp perception, induce hallucinations, and sow confusion, often causing prey to become lost or disoriented.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Fungal hollows and bioluminescent glades.

Damp shrine gardens and overgrown archive ruins.

Marsh edges and moonlit deep groves of Huevale.

Gather in playful circles, especially where decay and memory-rich growth overlap.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

PLAYFUL GATHERING → SPORE SATURATION → HALLUCINATION DRIFT → TERRITORIAL SWARM

Dance and laugh softly in groups.

Saturate the air with spores.

Overwhelm intruders through confusion and warped perception.

Rarely rely on direct attacks.

ABILITIES / STATUS EFFECTS

HALLUCINATION SPORES

Induce vivid visions and false landscapes.

VISUAL DISTORTION

Warps light, shapes, and distance.

CONTROL SCRAMBLE

May reverse inputs or cause erratic movement.

DREAM HARVEST

Feeds on memory resonance to strengthen illusion.

SPORE BURST

Explosive bloom that releases a dense, blinding spore cloud.

HUE INTERACTION

Completely immune to poison and Verdant toxins.

Resistant to direct plant or fungal manipulation.

Susceptible to purifying effects from Yellow or Violet hues.

Crimson flame destroys them quickly but can trigger explosive spore bursts.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Highly vulnerable to sonic-based attacks;
disperses spore clouds and temporarily stuns them.

Purifying spells rapidly dissolve their fungal structure.

Flame works, but may create dangerous explosive bursts.

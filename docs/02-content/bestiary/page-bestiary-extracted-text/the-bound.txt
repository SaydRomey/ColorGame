THE BOUND

WITNESSES OF STILLNESS

They stood still long enough…
and became silence.

“Stone does not forget.
It only waits.”

OVERVIEW

Lifelike stone figures scattered across Nullshade.

Some kneel.
Some reach.

Some accuse —
but none speak aloud.

Most are inert until a player acts violently nearby or attempts to flee too quickly.

When awakened, they move with slow, unnerving grace —
as if remembering how to move.

Their strikes are delayed but catastrophic.

CLASSIFICATION / BESTIARY DATA

Name:
The Bound

Type:
Dormant Sentinels / Memory Constructs

Threat Level:
High — Only when triggered; reactive danger

First Emerged:
Post-Prism Collapse, Nullshade Core Rebound

ANATOMY / PRESENCE

Mid-motion petrification —
frozen while reaching, kneeling, shielding, or still.

Stone shell with subtle memory seams.

Delayed retaliation —
attacks land after a tense pause.

Hollow core shimmer becomes visible only during truth vulnerability windows.

Movement echoes the player’s rhythm rather than direct pursuit.

Awakens when violence, strong hue casting, or fleeing from memory puzzles occurs nearby.

BEHAVIOR SEQUENCE

1. DORMANT AMONG OTHER STATUES

2. TRIGGERED BY AGGRESSION / STRONG HUE / FLEEING PUZZLES

3. ONE STATUE IN THE GROUP ANIMATES

4. MIRRORS THE PLAYER’S PAUSES AND DECISIONS

5. DELIVERS A DELAYED BUT DEVASTATING STRIKE

Does not chase —
anticipates.

Only one in a group typically animates at a time.

HUE INTERACTION

Immune to all hue-based attacks.

Weakened by memory-based artifacts like the Bistre Lantern and the Isabelline Tapestry.

Calmed or bypassed by engaging with memory puzzles or reciting known truths.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Temporarily deactivated through full dialogue confession using truth mechanics.

Only vulnerable while reciting remembered dialogue options.

During vulnerability, a shimmer reveals a hollow core.

Avoids areas where the player carries false names or unclaimed memories.

ENVIRONMENTAL / ENCOUNTER EFFECTS

In proximity to The Bound, audio muffles.

Time subtly distorts, causing a surreal pause effect.

Tense, uncanny spaces form when they linger motionless in groups.

LORE FRAGMENT — ETCHINGS OF THE SILENT MONOLITH

“We called them guardians, once.

Remnants of the Prismara who refused hue.

But I think now…
they were simply those who saw too much.”

“They don’t scream.

They remember you.”

“If silence had a form,
it would kneel beside them.”

— Archivist Reiss,
Final Echoes of the Core

FIELD NOTE / DEATH RESULT

When defeated, The Bound collapse into dust, leaving behind one whisper:

“You remembered too loudly.”

RECOGNITION GUIDE — STATUE POSES & RHYTHM CLUES

KNEELING

Head bowed.
Hands near heart or ground.

Often near memory altars.

REACHING

Arms extended as if grasping something just beyond reach.

ACCUSING

Finger or gaze fixed outward.

Judgment frozen in stone.

SHIELDING

Arm raised in defense.

Protecting something —
or someone.

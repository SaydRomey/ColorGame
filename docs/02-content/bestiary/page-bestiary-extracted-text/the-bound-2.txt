THE BOUND

WITNESSES OF STILLNESS

“Stone does not forget.
It only waits.”

BASIC DATA

Type:
Dormant Sentinels / Memory Constructs

Threat Level:
High — Only when triggered; reactive danger

First Emerged:
Post-Prism Collapse, Nullshade Core Rebound

Alignment:
Neutral / Oathbound

Size:
2.3 m – 2.8 m, varies

ANNOTATIONS

SILENT WITNESS

Faces bear no eyes, yet remember everything you’ve done.

RITUAL CARVINGS

Glyphs etched into their bodies hold fragments of forgotten oaths and failed truths.

WEIGHT OF TIME

Their movements are measured in heartbeats, not seconds.

HOLLOW CORE

Only during moments of truth do they reveal the emptiness within.

DESCRIPTION

The Bound are humanoid statues scattered throughout Nullshade.

Each one is frozen in mid-movement —
reaching, kneeling, shielding, or still.

While most remain inert, some will stir if the player performs specific actions:
attacking nearby, casting strong hue spells, or attempting to flee from memory puzzles.

When awakened, The Bound do not chase —
they anticipate.

Their movement is slow, deliberate, and seemingly tied to the player’s rhythm or pause.

They retaliate with delayed but devastating strikes that cannot be dodged traditionally —
only predicted through silence and stillness.

BEHAVIOR

Linger motionless in groups —
creating tense, uncanny spaces.

Only one in a group typically animates at a time.

May mimic player movement seconds after it occurs —
mimicking decision-making rather than motion.

HUE INTERACTION

Immune to all hue-based attacks.

Can be weakened by memory-based artifacts like the Bistre Lantern or the Isabelline Tapestry.

Calmed or bypassed by engaging with memory puzzles or reciting known truths.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Temporarily deactivated through full dialogue confession using truth mechanics.

Only vulnerable while reciting remembered dialogue options —
during these moments, a shimmer reveals a hollow core.

Avoids areas where the player carries false names or unclaimed memories.

TRIGGER CONDITIONS

The Bound stir if the player:

Performs violent actions nearby.

Casts strong hue magic.

Attempts to flee too quickly from memory puzzles.

Awakened Bound move with slow, unnerving grace.

Their strikes are delayed —
but catastrophic.

AWAKENING SEQUENCE

1. INERT

Statue remains still.
Unblinking.

2. AWARENESS

Faint cracks of light.
Subtle movement.

3. RISE

The Bound shifts weight.

Stone remembers.

4. ANTICIPATE

Reads your rhythm.

Mirrors decisions,
not movement.

5. STRIKE

Delayed blow that cannot be dodged —
only understood.

NOTES

In proximity to The Bound, audio muffles and time subtly distorts —
causing a surreal pause effect.

When defeated, The Bound collapse into dust, leaving behind one whisper:

“You remembered too loudly.”

LORE REWARDS

Memory Echoes.

Whispered Oaths.

Shards of Forgotten Truth.

TACTICAL ADVICE

Move slowly.
Observe patterns.

Engage nearby memory puzzles before awakening one.

Use truth-based dialogue or memory artifacts to bypass rather than fight.

“Stillness is not safety.
It is timing.”

STORMWRIT HOUND

BOLT BETWEEN WORLDS

Howling phantom stitched from wire and cloud.

“It howls in lightning —
and leaps between bolts.”

CLASSIFICATION

Name:
Stormwrit Hound

Type:
Lightning Beast / Elemental Predator

Threat:
HIGH —
Fast, Unpredictable, Anti-Casting

First Emerged:
During the Tempest Surge following the Shattering

ANATOMY / TRAITS

STORMWIRE BODY

Woven arcs of conductive cloud and wire.

PRESSURE-DROP HOWL

Warps air and shatters rhythm.

GLASS CLAWS

Shatter shields.
Leave cutting static.

PLASMA LIMBS

Jitter between now and next.

SKY-INK OUTLINE

Scribed in lightning.
Never still, never the same twice.

LIGHTNING-NODE LEAP

Traverses via natural conduits:
skyglass spikes, copper relays, storm-laced points.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Iriseld storm cliffs, sky rifts, and high-atmosphere relay zones.

Forms during extreme electrical convergence and rift instability.

Most often seen near skyglass towers, copper relays, and broken cliff edges.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

PACES CLIFF EDGES

Senses fast movement or charged spell buildup.

ACTIVATION

Triggered by motion, magic buildup, or lightning surge.

LEAPS BETWEEN NODES

Traverses via lightning nodes, skyglass spikes, or copper relays.

FLASH-FANG LUNGE

Teleports, strikes, and leaves a static afterimage for 2 seconds.

HOWL

Stuns rhythm-based abilities and lowers visibility with a blinding storm flash.

ABILITIES & STATUS EFFECTS

FLASH-FANG LUNGE

High-speed teleport strike that leaves a static copy.

STATIC AFTERIMAGE

Remnant image persists for 2 seconds and can trigger on contact.

PRESSURE-DROP HOWL

Disrupts rhythm.
Lowers visibility and staggers casters.

RIFT LEAP

Instant traversal between lightning nodes or charged conduits.

HUE INTERACTION

Disrupts spell channeling, especially Cobalt-based spells tied to storm or rhythm.

At high static charge, briefly disables Color Binding UI.

Can be slowed if surrounded by harmonic resonance, such as instruments or echo chimes.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Wind-grounding relics and Cerulean Cloak effects reduce speed and arc intensity.

Harmonic instruments, such as skybell chimes or glass flutes, distort its targeting.

Momentarily pacified if its own lightning arc is reflected, such as with Payne’s Prism Pane or storm mirror.

LORE ENTRY

Fragment from a tethered sky-journal, page scorched and torn.

“We heard it before we saw it —
not a bark, but a pressure drop.

The air folded in half.
Then it struck.

My guide called it a ‘stormwrit,’
a hound scribed by lightning itself.

It doesn’t follow you.
It finishes thoughts you haven’t had yet.

One moment it was chasing the birds above us —
the next, it was inside my arcshield.

And when it howled, something behind my eyes shattered.

I left the cliffs that day.
My journal didn’t.”

— Signed only:
“Archivist Iol”

NOTES FROM THE REACH

Some monks claim the Stormwrit Hounds carry messages between lightning gods —
or are the messages.

One relic hunter’s log simply said:
“Don’t blink during thunder.”

SCALE REFERENCE

Approximately 3.5 m at shoulder.

PHYLACTERY SWARM

THE NAMES THAT NEVER REST

“If they whisper your name, run.
If they forget it — it’s already too late.”

THREAT LEVEL

MODERATE-SEVERE

Memory-draining parasites.
Dangerous in clusters and over time.

TYPE

Soul-Fragmented Parasites

FIRST EMERGED

Post-Shattering, shortly after the collapse of the Phylactery Vault

HUE INTERACTION

BROWN BOUND

Memory spells repel or redirect their feeding pattern.

VIOLET ILLUSIONS

Confuse their targeting.
They can’t find the “real” name.

BLUE MIND LOCK

Mind-based defenses lock out name resonance, reducing damage over time.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

FIRE PURITY

Fire magic burns them outright —
especially from purified sources.

VIOLET HARMONICS

Sonic pulses from Violet-aligned instruments cause disarray in their flight pattern.

BISTRE LANTERN

Stuns them in shadow if activated in silence.

BESTIARY ENTRY

DESCRIPTION

A drifting swarm of glimmering, fly-like constructs with miniature glyphs engraved on their chitin.

Each holds a fragment of a soul —
stolen, gifted, or long abandoned.

They buzz softly, repeating half-spoken names and severed dreams.

BEHAVIOR

Aggressive in clusters;
each hit reduces XP, disrupts equipped memory-bound relics, or causes temporary skill fog.

They prioritize targets with the most stored names, such as players with saved dialogue trees, memory fragments, or death-echoes.

If fed a “forgotten name,” such as an unused True Name, the swarm dissipates peacefully.

NOTES

Destroying a swarm sometimes releases “Whispers in Dust,” a rare crafting essence used for soul-bound relic upgrades.

But if you kill them while carrying a forgotten name, your own may become unstable in the Codex.

ATTACKS & ABILITIES

NAME LEECH

Swarm dives in to bite and leech memory.
Causes XP loss and memory fragment suppression.

WHISPER CLOUD

Releases a cloud of name whispers that obscures vision and scrambles equipped relic effects.

MEMORY SWARM

Mass dive attack that hits multiple times.
Chance to cause skill fog or memory misfires.

IDENTITY REND

Focuses on a name the player holds dear.
Reduces max Hue temporarily and increases cooldowns.

LORE ENTRY

Fragmented field report from the Bistre Archivist, sealed in ash.

“At first, I thought them ash-fleas.
Harmless, if persistent.

But the swarm began to sing —
in names.

Names I had forgotten.”

“They circle.
They whisper.

And if one touches skin, a piece of memory loosens.”

“Caput Mortuum warned us:
failed phylacteries don’t just break — they scatter.

A name is a soul’s handle.
These insects collect... and misuse.”

“I burned the scrolls.
I prayed over the ink.

Still, they murmured the names I never told anyone.”

“If you’ve forgotten your own voice, they’ll help.
But it will cost you.”

BEHAVIOR OVERVIEW

NAME HUNTERS

Drawn to the resonance of names.

Targets those who carry many memories or forgotten things.

WHISPER FLIGHT

Swarm circles the target, whispering stolen names that chip away at mental stability.

FEED AND FOG

On contact, drains memory essence, causing XP loss, relic disruption, and temporary skill fog.

DISORIENT BURST

When threatened, erupts outward, scattering and re-forming from multiple angles.

POSSIBLE DROPS

WHISPERS IN DUST (UNCOMMON)

Essence of scattered names.
Used to upgrade soul-bound relics.

STOLEN NAME FRAGMENT (RARE)

A sliver of a forgotten name.
Can be returned to restore lost memories.

VOID-ETCHED CHITIN (UNCOMMON)

Tough material from the swarm’s shell.
Used in crafting memory-deflecting gear.

BISTRE DUST VEIN (UNCOMMON)

Powdered ash that retains echo-static.
Used in shadow-based alchemy.

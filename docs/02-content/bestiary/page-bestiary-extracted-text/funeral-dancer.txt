FUNERAL DANCER

LAST STEP OF THE ASH WALTZ

“She dances to end things.
And always ends with a bow.”

THREAT LEVEL

HIGH

AoE timing, illusion, and delayed hazards.

TYPE

Spectral Performer / Ritual Assassin

FIRST EMERGED

The Last Ember Gala, pre-Crimson Collapse.

HUE INTERACTION

CRIMSON ILLUSIONS

Cloaked in crimson illusions while mid-dance.
Vulnerable only between steps.

COBALT SOUND

Cobalt sound interrupts her tempo, breaking sigil formation.

EARTHEN GROUNDING

Earthen grounding slows her spin, allowing safer approach.

VIOLET CLARITY

Reveals the true dancer for brief moments if her rhythm is disrupted.

AURELIAN LIGHT

Disperses lingering illusions after her final bow, reducing follow-up risk.

DESCRIPTION

The Funeral Dancer is a haunting presence — cloaked in ash-grey silk, wreathed in ghostly flame.

She does not hunt or charge.
She simply begins to dance.

Her movements draw circles in the dust, carving patterns of mourning that turn lethal when completed.

She laughs as she spins.
The laughter fades only when she bows.

ANATOMY

VEIL OF ASH

Her veil bears the names of the forgotten.
They whisper in rhythm.

CEREMONIAL BLADES

Curved and balanced for dance, not brute force.
Edges burn with funereal flame.

SILK OF MOURNING

Flowing fabric etched with fading sigils.
Each layer tells a story of a fallen soul.

GHOSTFLAME AURA

Faint flames trail her movements.
They do not harm — until the dance ends.

DANCER’S HEART

A hollow ember within.
It beats to the rhythm of loss and memory.

ASH-WALTZ SIGILS

The circles she leaves are not spells.
They are promises of death once the story ends.

LORE ENTRY

Carved into the steps of a scorched amphitheater.

“We gave her grief.
She gave us grace.”

“The Funeral Dancer once performed only for the dead — her steps choreographed to farewell the honored fallen.

But war came too fast, and death too often.
She could not keep up. So she never stopped.”

“Now she dances across ash-fields and battlegrounds, pirouetting through memory and flame.

No audience.
No end.

Just names whispered in rhythm and the clang of blades to mark the time.”

“Her sigils are not spells.
They are stories.

And when she finishes the story, something always dies.”

— Authored by Fennel “Redchalk” Vireen, former archivist turned battlefield scribe.

BEHAVIOR

OPENS THE DANCE

Begins with a slow spiral step, leaving a glowing sigil behind.

SPIRAL SEQUENCE

Moves in wide, rhythmic patterns, placing 4 to 6 sigils in sequence.

THE BOW

Stops to bow gracefully, triggering the explosion of all active sigils.

EVASIVE SPIN

If struck mid-combo, she spins into mist and reappears at a new position.

ENDLESS PERFORMANCE

If uninterrupted, the cycle repeats with faster, deadlier patterns.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

RHYTHM DISRUPTION

Interrupt spells, especially with rhythmic or musical timing, destabilize her choreography completely.

REFLECTION WARD

Sigils can be absorbed by Reflection Ward or overwritten with player sigil-magic.

HEAVY MOMENTUM

High-impact stagger or knockdown during her bow prevents the sigils from fully triggering.

SILENCE FIELD

Longer silence durations cause her to pause or abort the dance.

SIGIL PATTERNS

1. Step of Remembrance.

2. Step of Sorrow.

3. Step of Names.

4. Step of Regret.

5. Step of Silence.

6. The Bow — The End.

Each step tells a story.
The final bow writes the ending.

All sigils detonate in sequential waves when the bow is completed.

Standing in multiple zones increases damage exponentially.

NOTES

Some say she’s not a monster, but a memory too strong to forget — one dancer still trying to mourn a thousand souls alone.

If you survive her dance, she sometimes vanishes in a swirl of ash.
You feel oddly honored, like you were briefly part of the performance.

Estimates suggest she has danced for over a thousand years without rest.

POSSIBLE DROPS

DANCER’S VEIL (RARE)

Ash-silk that reduces incoming AoE damage and grants poise.

BLADE OF FAREWELL (RARE)

A curved blade that leaves lingering sigils on hit.

SIGIL HEART CORE (VERY RARE)

A core that enhances sigil magic and reduces cooldowns.

WALTZ OF THE LOST (LEGENDARY)

Teaches a unique combat style that lets you dance between danger.

EMBER ECHO (UNIQUE)

A whispering ember that plays a faint lullaby of the fallen.

THE BOW IS MERCY.

THE END IS ALWAYS THE SAME.”

SKYCALL WRAITH

VOICE OF THE FORGOTTEN WIND

“The wind lost its name.
So it screams.”

TYPE

Aerial Apparition / Wind-echo Specter

THREAT LEVEL

MODERATE

Highly Disruptive to Traversal

FIRST EMERGED

After the Collapse of the Singing Gate
(Shattering Era)

ANATOMY

1. WRAITH HOOD

Face lost to the storm.
Its scream echoes endless grief.

2. TORN AZURE SHROUD

Robes never touch ground —
moved by forgotten voices.

3. WINDCORE

Essence bound to a broken current.
Drives its shriek.

4. DISRUPTION VEIL

Emits gusts that unravel movement and sound.

5. ECHO TAIL

Leaves traces of resonance that warp the air.

6. WHISPER BELL

Remnant of the Singing Gate.
Its toll was never heard in time.

7. DRIFT ANCHOR

Tethers to wind nodes, gullies, and spiral updrafts.

DESCRIPTION

The Skycall Wraith is a spirit-shaped echo —
the wind given form by grief and repetition.

Often found patrolling high ledges or spiral currents, they disrupt movement with piercing shrieks that unbind gliding abilities and destabilize storm-anchored platforms.

They are not hostile in the traditional sense.
But they call out — and what they call hurts.

HUE INTERACTION

Dampens all Cobalt-based traversal effects nearby, such as Static Dash glide extensions.

Cries override sound-triggered puzzle inputs.

Vulnerable to grounded or Earth-based counterweights, which temporarily still their flight.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

PATROL

Wind-linked paths or spiral updrafts.

DISRUPTION CRY

Cancels glide, distorts sound, applies Vertigo.

RETREAT

Flees into fog when silenced or grounded.

Patrols wind tunnels, cliff ledges, or broken sky bridges.

When provoked, emits a Disruption Cry that escalates if ignored.

At high threat stacks, pulls the player down or sideways mid-air.

Will flee if surrounded by silence, such as Cerulean Cloak.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Weak to Earthen grounding spells, sound-null zones, or double-jump rhythm breaks.

Silence interrupts their shriek cycle.

Mind Echo can “overwrite” their cry with a stored sound, especially a player’s own voice.

ABILITIES / STATUS EFFECTS

DISRUPTION CRY

Cancels flight/glide, disorients sound, and applies stacks of Vertigo.

VERTIGO PULSE

At high stacks, yanks or shoves the player in mid-air and blinds HUD.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

High ledges, spiral updrafts, wind tunnels, broken sky bridges, and storm-crowned ruins.

Often appears during high-wind events or near Singing Gate remnants.

Strong presence near echo-node shrines, wind bells, and air glyph arrays.

More dangerous in open vertical spaces with strong updraft currents.

LORE ENTRY

Recovered from a shattered listening bell atop the Skyglass Spires.

“When the storm broke the tower,
we thought the cries were just wind in the bells.

But it was the wind forgetting its name.
Forgetting how to be silent.

The Wraiths came after.
Not ghosts — echoes of voices torn from those who leapt or were lost among the gusts.

One called my name.
Not how I say it... how I meant it, once.

I froze.
I fell.

We ring the bells now not for warning...
but to remind the sky of silence.”

— Carved by Wind-Monk Rellian

Don’t fight the wind —

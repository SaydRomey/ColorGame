MOTH-WITCH OF THE VERDANT SPIRAL

THE WHISPER IN BLOOM

“She doesn’t cast spells.
She releases questions that root into you.”

THREAT LEVEL

HIGH

Environmental and mental hazard.
Her illusions and spores can unravel mind and memory.

TYPE

Verdant/Cobalt Fey-Caster
Boss Entity

FIRST EMERGED

Unknown — Possibly pre-Color War anomaly

HUE INTERACTION

ALTERNATING HUES

Shifts between Verdant (Green) and Cobalt (Blue) alignment in phase rhythm.

MOTH ECHOES

Her moths carry hue echoes.
When struck, they trigger illusion sequences from the player’s past.

DEWING VULNERABILITY

She is vulnerable only during “dewing” phase—when her wings pulse and the air blooms with spores.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

SONIC FEEDBACK

Sonic pulses, echo magic, or chord runes disrupt her resonance.

SPEAK HER TRUE NAME

Speaking her true name during the second riddle phase will briefly stun her.

LANTERN VISION

Lantern-based tools, such as the Bistre Lantern, reveal her true form during Whispercloak.

DESCRIPTION

Tall, robed in layered silk woven from moth-skin and flower dust.

Her eyes bloom and close like petals.
Her wings emit a soporific sound that alters thought and slows reflexes.

Surrounded by a swarm of Thalera moths that shimmer between green and blue—each tied to emotional memory.

ANATOMY

BLOOM-EYES

Eyes open and close like flowers, reflecting the emotions of those who look too long.

MOTH SILK ROBE

Woven from moth-wings and Verdant thread.
Absorbs light and releases soporific pollen.

SPIRAL ROOTS

Roots and moss trail behind her, anchoring her to the glade and the memories beneath.

WHISPER-WINGS

Multi-layered wings shed spores and sound.
Their patterns change with each riddle phase.

THALERA MOTH SWARM

Swarm of moths orbit her in colored spirals.
Each carries a fragment of memory or doubt.

DEW CORE

At her heart is a pulsing dewdrop-like core that regulates her phases and spores.

ABILITIES

RIDDLE OPENING

Poses a riddle.
Correct answers stabilize the field; wrong answers unravel terrain.

VINE SPIRAL

Roots spiral out from the ground.
Break line of sight or dash through to escape.

DREAM MOTH

Sends moth swarm.
Hitting moths triggers visions from your past.

PETAL VEIL

She blooms petals that blur time and slow reflexes within the area.

MOONDEW DEWING

Her wings pulse.
This is the only time she can be truly harmed.

NOTES

Her death does not grant treasure.

It grants a question.

“Was she ever real...
or was she your future, grown in the wrong direction?”

Optional encounter.
Found only by following a non-marked moth trail in the Verdant region during the dusk cycle.

LORE ENTRY

Extract from “Dreambloom Recordings, Entry 17”
— Archivist Sellen Orvine

“She speaks in riddles while you bleed.
She hums while your reflection loses its face.

But she’s not cruel.
She simply doesn’t understand what truth is anymore.”

“The Verdant Spiral wasn’t a place.
It was a question that grew out of control.

Moss crept into logic.
Petals wrapped around doubt.

And at the center—her.”

“I saw a thousand moths, all with my face.
Then none with eyes.

Then only her, smiling as my voice said her name backwards.”

“If you find her, do not speak.
Let her wings speak first.

That is the test.”

BEHAVIOR

Opens combat with a riddle.
Choosing an answer affects the battlefield.

CORRECT ANSWER: Stable terrain, clear sight.

WRONG ANSWER: Shifting illusions, distorted space.

CASTS

SPIRAL BLOOM

Creates a ring of growing vines that traps and roots the player unless escaped via blink/dash.

WHISPERCLOAK

Renders her briefly invisible.
Only her moths remain visible.

MIRRORED MURMURS

Mimics a previously encountered NPC’s voice to distract or mislead the player.

PHASE 2 — BELOW 50% HP

Wings shed completely.
Movement becomes erratic and dance-like.

Moths become explosive, triggering illusion bursts or emotion-based debuffs such as confusion, hesitation, and dread.

POSSIBLE DROPS

THALERA BLOOM (UNIQUE)

Memory sigil.
Grants resistance to fear-induced status effects and allows decoding of flora-based glyphs.

SPIRAL PETAL (RARE)

Moth-forged material.
Used in crafting Fey-woven cloaks and illusion gear.

WHISPER DUST (UNCOMMON)

Residual spores.
Used to craft sleep-resistance elixirs and memory tonics.

RIDDLEWING MOTH (COSMETIC)

A living moth companion that reacts to nearby secrets and glyphs.

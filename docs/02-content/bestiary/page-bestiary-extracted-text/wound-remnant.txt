WOUND REMNANT

BLEEDING MEMORY

It bled light.

Now it bleeds memory.

“The Core cracked… and this was the scream.”

OVERVIEW

Born from the Prism Wound itself, this unstable construct limps through space as if limping through time.

Pieces of it glow and vanish — as if they forget to exist.

It doesn’t seek you out… it seeks coherence.

CLASSIFICATION

Name:
Wound Remnant.

Type:
Coreborn Echo / Memory Entity.

Threat Level:
High — Environmental Distortion.

First Emerged:
At the Prism Shattering.

ANATOMY / FEATURES

MISALIGNED JOINTS

Unnatural locomotion, as if its limbs remember the wrong shape.

LIGHT-FISSURED RIBS

Cold white light pours from the torso.

DELAYED HEARTBEAT CORE

If it syncs with yours, a fragment of your HUD is lost until reset.

MEMORY SPINE

Fracture point vulnerable during pulse sync.

PHASE-FADE LIMBS

Body fragments glow, vanish, and reappear.

ENVIRONMENTAL DISTORTION

As it moves, ghost-white flashes distort the environment.

UI stutters, visual frames glitch, and terrain deconstructs briefly.

OBSERVED BEHAVIOR

1. SLOW PATROL

Slowly patrols the Prism Wound region.

2. PROXIMITY ATTACK

Echo pulses stagger and blind.

3. LIGHT BLEED

Fades the screen to white and removes HUD elements.

HUE RESONANCE

Any hue damage makes it resonate in that color briefly — then it mirrors that hue’s weakness.

Most susceptible:
Brown + Purple.

Memory and paradox destabilize its frame.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

BISTRE LANTERN

Anchors its location, preventing phasing.

VIOLET’S ILLUSION CLONE

Causes it to mimic a false version of you and momentarily collapse.

CRIMSON DAMAGE DURING PULSE SYNC

Can fracture its memory spine.

LORE ENTRY — PRISMARA CHRONOLOGER TYELUS VANE

“It emerged the moment the Core shattered.

A figure, not whole — walking without footsteps.”

“Light poured from its ribs, but it was cold.”

“It is an afterimage of agony too large to stay still.”

“You don’t kill it.

You just outlast the part of yourself it remembers.”

NOTES & DROP

Whispers past player dialogue lines during whiteout pulses.

After defeat:
leaves behind a Cracked Memory Core.

The Cracked Memory Core is required to access the deepest level of the Prism Wound.

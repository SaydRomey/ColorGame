THE BOUND

WITNESSES OF STILLNESS

They stood still long enough…
and became silence.

“Stone does not forget.
It only waits.”

CLASSIFICATION

Type:
Dormant Sentinels / Memory Constructs

Threat Level:
High — Only when triggered; reactive danger

First Emerged:
Post-Prism Collapse, Nullshade Core Rebound

Region:
Nullshade — Core Wound / Prism Wound

ANATOMY BREAKDOWN

1. STILLSTONE FACE

Expressionless features worn smooth by memory.

2. MEMORY FISSURES

Fine cracks shimmer when truth is spoken.

3. DELAYED GESTURE ARM

Strikes land a beat after the motion begins.

4. HOLLOW CORE

Only visible when remembered dialogue is recited.

5. DUSTBOUND JOINTS

Slow but unnaturally precise movement.

6. MIMIC POSTURE

Echoes the player’s rhythm seconds later.

7. SILENT FOOTING

Movement muffles sound and distorts time.

DESCRIPTION

The Bound are humanoid statues scattered throughout Nullshade, frozen in mid-movement —
reaching, kneeling, shielding, or still.

They awaken when the player attacks nearby, casts strong hue spells, or tries to flee from memory puzzles.

They do not chase normally.

They anticipate.

Their movement is slow, deliberate, and tied to the player’s rhythm or pause.

Their delayed strikes are devastating and cannot be dodged traditionally;
they must be predicted through silence and stillness.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Found in Nullshade ruins, monolith courts, memory-puzzle chambers, prism wounds, and silent battlefields.

Often appear in groups, creating uncanny spaces.

Usually only one in a group animates at a time.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

DORMANT STILLNESS → TRIGGERED NOTICE → DELAYED MIMIC → CATASTROPHIC STRIKE → RETURN TO SILENCE

Linger motionless in groups.

Trigger on violence, strong hue spells, or attempted flight.

May mimic player movement seconds later, echoing decision-making rather than motion.

HUE INTERACTION

Immune to all hue-based attacks.

Weakened by memory-based artifacts like the Bistre Lantern or the Isabelline Tapestry.

Calmed or bypassed by engaging with memory puzzles or reciting known truths.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Temporarily deactivated through full dialogue confession using truth mechanics.

Vulnerable only while reciting remembered dialogue options.

During these moments, a shimmer reveals a hollow core.

Avoids areas where the player carries false names or unclaimed memories.

LORE ENTRY — ARCHIVIST REISS

“We called them guardians once…

remnants of the Prismara who refused hue.”

“They don’t scream.

They remember for you.”

“If silence had a form,
it would kneel beside them.”

NOTES / EFFECT

Nearby audio muffles and time subtly distorts, creating a surreal pause effect.

When defeated, The Bound collapse into dust and leave behind the whisper:

“You remembered too loudly.”

NULLSHADE CONTEXT

Nullshade is a realm of silence left after the Prism Collapse.

Its monoliths remember everything the world tried to forget.

Memory residue clings to stone —
and The Bound remember too well.

COUNTERPLAY ARTIFACTS

BISTRE LANTERN

Reveals hollow cores and slows their delayed strike by forcing memory clarity.

ISABELLINE TAPESTRY

Weakens mimic timing and opens brief windows of safety.

HUNTER’S NOTE

Stillness and truth are safer than speed.

Watch, listen, remember.

Do not flee blindly —
fleeing is a trigger.

“Speak the truth.
Face the stone.

Move only when your memory is certain.”

INKWALKER OF THE DROWNED PAGES

She walks between drowned thoughts and black storms.

“She drowned for knowledge.
Now she walks for silence.”

DESCRIPTION

Once a scholar of Zaffran’s tidal scriptures, the Tideworn Apostle attempted to decipher a storm-written grimoire beneath the reef’s trench.

She returned wordless — but writing.

Now a vessel for drowned knowledge, she seeks only to silence voices… starting with yours.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Found near black coral stacks, drowned archive vaults, reef trenches, flooded script chambers, and the storm-bitten edges of Whispering Bay.

Appears where forgotten knowledge pools beneath tide and ruin.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

Ink the Field → Inkbind Glyph → Tidal Silence → Scroll Bloom → Reposition.

Begins encounters by inscribing storm sigils across the arena.

Roots players in scripted zones unless movement is timed carefully.

Disables spellcasting briefly with Tidal Silence.

Summons obscuring script-page swarms during Scroll Bloom.

ABILITIES / STATUS EFFECTS

INKBIND GLYPH

Roots players in storm-writing zones.

TIDAL SILENCE

Briefly disables player spellcasting.

SCROLL BLOOM

Glowing pages obscure sight and pressure space.

STORM SIGILS

Corrupt area-of-effect behavior and distort spell placement.

HUE INTERACTION

Ink-runes scatter targeting reticules and miscast bound spells.

Cobalt’s Mind Echo can re-stabilize spell structure near active runes.

Especially weak to fire and light when empowered through relics such as the Cerulean Cloak.

Contradictory Hue pulses, Crimson + Cobalt, destabilize her focus.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Fire magic burns her runes and exposes her true position.

Interrupt her chant by shattering the floating pages before completion.

Vulnerable when caught between contradictory Hue pulses.

LORE ENTRY — FINAL INKTRACE FROM THE ZAFFRAN SUBVAULTS

I found her near the black coral stacks — bent over a journal that wouldn’t close.

She wasn’t reading.

She was remembering.

But not her memories.

If she offers you a page…
don’t read it.

It writes you back.

IRISELD CONTEXT

The Zaffran Subvaults lie beneath reef-cloaked coves and drowned libraries, where stormwater scripture is carved into coral and stone.

Here, memory-tide magic preserves the voices of the drowned — and the mistakes of those who listened.

COUNTERPLAY RELIC

CERULEAN CLOAK

Reinforced with light-forged thread and arcane stabilizers.

Empowers fire and light magic against drowned entities.

Stabilizes spell structure in the presence of ink-runes.

Grants brief clarity during Tidal Silence.

WHISPERGLASS WYRM

THE SHARD SERPENT

“You never hear it speak.

But the cliff listens.”

SOURCE IMAGE — whisperglass-wyrm_page.png

CLASSIFICATION

Type:
Illusion Serpent / Burrowing Mirage.

Threat:
Moderate — Spatial & Sensory Disruptor.

First Emerged:
Post-Dissonance Storm Cycle.

FIELD OBSERVATIONS

Rarely seen until it is already inside your perception.

It doesn’t bite.

It convinces you it already did — and that you bled.

SCALE & FORM

Length:
40–60 m+.

Body:
Segmented skyfract glass and dream-echo filaments.

ANATOMY / BEHAVIORAL FEATURES

1. REFRACTIVE HIDE

Its segmented body bends light in shifting vectors, making range, position, and movement difficult to track.

2. PREY REFLECTION

Mimics the player’s silhouette and cadence to bait reactions and draw movement.

3. MIRROR DIVE

Vanishes into reflective glass or mirrored cliffs and re-emerges unpredictably.

4. FRACTURE HOWL

Emits static-chime waves that scramble traversal inputs and disorient spatial sense.

5. BURROWING MIRAGE

Moves through floating rock as if swimming through memory, leaving no physical trace.

6. WARPED REFLECTION TRAIL

In its wake, reflections warp and linger — lies that outlast the wyrm.

RECOVERED FIELD RECORD — PAYNE’S EXPEDITION

“Every stroke vanished.

Not faded.

Not erased.

Just… unremembered.”

“It didn’t eat thoughts.

It ate the part of me that thought I had them.”

P.

HUE INTERACTIONS

Refracts Hue-based attacks unless revealed.

Temporarily visible under Cobalt’s Mind Echo near reflective terrain.

Immobilized briefly by Verdant’s Skygrowth Seed on fractured skyglass veins.

TACTICAL WEAKNESSES

Exposed by Payne’s Brush illusion-reveal strokes.

Loses invisibility in stormlight flashes or lightning surges.

Vulnerable while syncing with the player’s reflection.

ORIGIN NOTE

Believed to be remnants of sky-serpents reflected into extinction.

FINAL NOTE

What you see is never just where it is —

only where it thinks you’ll look.

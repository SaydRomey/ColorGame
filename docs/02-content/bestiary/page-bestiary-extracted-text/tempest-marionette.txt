TEMPEST MARIONETTE

STRINGS OF LIGHTNING

Dancer suspended by lightning and sorrow.

“She dances on wires strung from lightning.”

CLASSIFICATION

Type:
Skybound Construct / Elemental Dancer

Threat Level:
High — Agile, Reflective, Grace-Locked

First Emerged:
After the Skyglass Amphitheater’s collapse

Region:
Iriseld — Tidespire Reach

ANATOMY BREAKDOWN

1. SILK-PAINTED EYES

Eyes painted on silk;
blink once and they seem open somewhere else.

2. GLASSY LIMBS

Ceremonial glasswork arms and legs, elegant but razor-fragile.

3. STORM-THREADED JOINTS

Charged articulation points that spark with motion.

4. LIGHTNING STRINGS

Filaments of current suspend and guide each motion overhead.

5. ARCSTEP AFTERIMAGE

Circular dashes leave delayed ghost-images in the air.

6. GLASS POINTE FEET

Precision footwork used to parry and redirect attacks.

7. CURTAIN FALL CORE

A storm-bright center that charges the downward lightning finisher.

DESCRIPTION

A once-ceremonial construct created for aerial rituals and performances, the Tempest Marionette was left unfinished when the sky fell silent.

Reanimated by the Iriseld storm network, it moves not with malice but with unending choreography.

Each motion is precise, beautiful — and deadly.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Patrols abandoned amphitheaters, wind shrines, sky-ritual rings, and broken skyglass performance halls.

Appears in storm-lashed high places, especially where ritual acoustics and lightning paths overlap.

Most active during charged weather, thunder surges, or echoing aerial rites.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

PATROL → ARCSTEP WALTZ → GLASS POINTE → CURTAIN FALL

Uses Arcstep Waltz:
rapid circular dashes with after-image delay.

Activates Glass Pointe to parry midair attacks.

Charges Curtain Fall:
a downward lightning spear with a shock pulse.

Moves like an endless performance rather than a direct brawl.

ABILITIES / STATUS EFFECTS

ARCSTEP WALTZ

Circular dash sequence with delayed afterimages.

GLASS POINTE

Parry stance that reflects or deflects attacks midair.

CURTAIN FALL

Downward lightning spear followed by a shock pulse.

TEMPO LOCK

Punishes players who match her rhythm too predictably.

STORM ENCORE

Builds momentum as combat becomes more rhythmic.

HUE INTERACTION

Reflects low-grade projectile spells, especially Cobalt bolts, with a pirouette spin.

Near Crimson, the Marionette’s tempo increases as it mimics combat rhythms.

Weak to disruptive beats or tempo-breaking pulses.

Mind Echo cast at odd intervals can interrupt her flow.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Overloadable with high-intensity storm surge spells from certain weather events or relics.

Disrupted by the Glaucous Feather’s silence aura.

Temporarily stunned when her dance rhythm is broken by clashing tempo illusions or uneven terrain shifts.

Vulnerable during the wind-up to Curtain Fall.

LORE ENTRY — STORMGLASS THEATER SCRIPT

The dancer enters.
She does not walk.

She is carried by memory and wire.

Every bow is a goodbye.
Every step is applause from the storm.

She spins —
and the sky answers in thunder.

The strings glow.
The audience vanishes.

The silence remains.

Recovered from the drowned vault of the Skyglass Amphitheater.

IRISELD CONTEXT

The Tidespire Reach is carved with stormbound cliffs and skyglass theaters.

Copper relays hum across the void, conducting lightning for ritual and signal alike.

Here, weather is guided and applause echoes between clouds.

TACTICAL NOTE

Break the rhythm;
survive the finisher.

Deny her flow channels.

Watch for the wind-up before Curtain Fall.

HUNTER’S NOTE

Some say she still performs for an audience only she can see.

Others believe she was designed not to entertain, but to mourn.

SKYCALL WRAITH

VOICE OF THE FORGOTTEN WIND

“The wind lost its name.
So it screams.”

TYPE

Aerial Apparition / Wind-echo Specter

THREAT LEVEL

MODERATE

Highly Disruptive to Traversal

FIRST EMERGED

After the Collapse of the Singing Gate
(Shattering Era)

ANATOMY BREAKDOWN

1. VOID HOOD

An empty cowl where the storm’s voice gathers.

2. AZURE VEIL

Torn robes woven from unsung winds.
They never settle.

3. WIND CORE

A swirling pulse of storm-memory that fuels its cry.

4. BELL SHARD

Remnant of the Singing Gate.
Its toll was never heard.

5. DISRUPTION RIBBONS

Gusting streamers that shred gliding forces and anchor platforms.

6. CRY VENT

Where shrieks spill forth —
distorting sound and focus.

7. DRIFT WAKE

Leaves behind fading eddies that hook and pull.

HUE INTERACTION

Dampens all Cobalt-based traversal effects nearby, such as Static Dash glide extensions.

Cries override sound-triggered puzzle inputs.

Vulnerable to grounded or Earth-based counterweights, which temporarily still their flight.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

PATROL → PROVOKED → DISRUPTION CRY → RETREAT INTO FOG

Patrols wind tunnels, cliff ledges, or sky bridges.

When provoked, emits a Disruption Cry that cancels gliding, distorts sound, and can pull you from the air.

At high stacks, triggers Vertigo Pulse.

Flees into fog if surrounded by silence, such as Cerulean Cloak active.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Weak to Earthen grounding spells, sound-null zones, or double-jump rhythm breaks.

Silence interrupts their shriek cycle.

Mind Echo can “overwrite” their cry with a stored sound, especially a player’s own voice.

Silence, such as Cerulean Cloak, causes them to lose interest and dissolve into fog.

ABILITIES / STATUS EFFECTS

DISRUPTION CRY

Cancels gliding, disorients sound, and applies stacking Vertigo.

WIND SHEAR

Gust wave pushes or pulls the player mid-air, breaking traversal flow.

VERTIGO PULSE

At max stacks, drags player down or sideways and scrambles HUD cues.

WIND ECHO

Leaves unstable gust-echoes that anchor platforms or create eddies.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Patrols high ledges, wind tunnels, spiral currents, and broken sky bridges.

Often seen where the Singing Gate once stood or where the sky still weeps.

Appears during high-wind events, lightning rolls, or skyborne resonance surges.

Strong presence near wind-bell shrines, echo pillars, and stormbound relics.

More active in vertical spaces and during gale-saturated conditions.

LORE ENTRY

Recovered from a shattered listening bell atop the Skyglass Spires.

“When the storm broke the tower,
we thought the cries were just wind in the bells.

But it was the wind forgetting its name.
Forgetting how to be silent.

The Wraiths came after.
Not ghosts — echoes of voices torn from those who leapt or were lost among the gusts.

One called my name.
Not how I say it... how I meant it, once.

I froze.
I fell.

We ring the bells now not for warning...
but to remind the sky of silence.”

— Carved by Wind-Monk Rellian

FIELD NOTES

The wraiths are drawn to names —
especially carved into glass, bells, or windstone.

Some whisper fragments of memories that are not yours.
Do not listen too long.

ECHO QUOTE

“The wind is not cruel.
It only remembers what we forget.”

“It screams because it once sang.”

HUNTER’S ADVICE

Break line of sight.

Anchor yourself to earth.

Silence the bells.

Only then will the wind stop screaming.

NULLSHADE REVENANT

THE MEMORY THAT MISRETURNED

“It remembers dying.
It forgot why it came back.”

“It doesn’t walk toward you.
It walks toward a memory where you failed.”

THREAT LEVEL

HIGH

Reality-distorting revenant.
Warps memory and hue alignment.

TYPE

Hue-Corrupted Undead / Memory Echo

FIRST EMERGED

Nullshade Bloom, post-Prism Collapse

HUE INTERACTION

UNSTABLE PRESENCE

Cannot be aligned.
Causes instability in the player’s current hue if struck or touched.

REVEAL CONDITIONS

Enters phase-shift state near strong hue magic.

Reveal with one of the following:

Cobalt (Blue) reality-lock sigils.

Yellow (Hope) illusions refracted through the Thulian Mirror.

Violet dream-lensing (Glamour Pulse).

HUE DRAIN CORRUPTION

Strikes “corrupt” the player’s hue alignment, turning abilities gray or misaligned.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

HUE REVELATION

Must be lit by hue before becoming vulnerable, such as Blue Sigils, Lanterns, or truth-based illusions.

ECHO ANCHORS

Cannot be silenced, but disrupted by lore items tied to their past self, such as Journal Fragments or Relic Sketches.

SHARD ALIGNMENT

Prismwalker Shard glow pulses stronger near them—a clue to real-space alignment.

BESTIARY ENTRY

DESCRIPTION

Once-alive figures from various Orders, their original hue still faintly flickering in their cloak seams or crystal bones.

Their skin is cracked porcelain, leaking vaporized memory.
Some hum old color chants. Others sob without tears.

BEHAVIOR

Wanders aimlessly until triggered by:

Player casting a hue spell near them.

Touching a Memory Echo shrine.

Saying a phrase tied to their former hue allegiance.

IN COMBAT

Heavy, deliberate swings—infused with color-ghosts of old abilities.

Their attacks “corrupt” the player’s hue alignment temporarily, turning abilities gray or misaligned.

At 50% HP, they begin to teleport erratically, repeating your previous movements with lagging mimicry.

ABILITIES

SHADE REPRISE

Mirrors the player’s last melee attack with delayed force, ignoring guard orientation.

HUE ECHO SLASH

A wide arc strike infused with a past version of the player’s most recent hue ability.

MEMORY HOWL

Lets out a silent scream that confuses controls and inverts button prompts briefly.

FRACTURED STEP

Teleports to a past location the player occupied moments ago and swings from there.

HOLLOW RECALL

Summons spectral shards of past allies or selves to harass the player for a short time.

All abilities scale with the player’s recent actions and current hue intensity.

NOTES

Some Prismwalkers report “remembering” events they never lived after battling one.

The Bistre Archivist theorized they are “souls who fractured across hues too often—now trapped in the space between.”

Defeating one sometimes unlocks a suppressed memory or dialogue branch.

Their names are lost.
Only their echoes remain.

LORE ENTRY

Excerpt from “Core-Walker’s Notes, Vol. VII”
— Anonymous Prismwalker

“We found her kneeling at the edge of a Shatterglass Pool.

Not praying.
Not breathing.

Just... remembering.”

“A crimson sash fluttered from one arm.
A green tattoo pulsed beneath her cracked skin.

Once, she was a hero of the Color War.
Now, she barely casts a shadow.”

“When she rose, her eyes didn’t see us.
They saw some place we weren’t.

She moved through our attacks like they hadn’t happened yet.”

“Then she struck—and I forgot how to defend.”

“The Nullshade does not raise the dead.
It reflects what it means to never have died right.”

POSSIBLE DROPS

NULLSHADE REMNANT (RARE)

A fragment of the revenant’s memory.
Used to unlock Echo Paths or reforge hue-corrupted relics.

FORGOTTEN CREST (UNCOMMON)

The faded emblem of their lost Order.
Usable in crafting or reputation redemption.

MEMORY VEIL DUST (UNCOMMON)

A fine dust that muffles the mind.
Used in alchemy to resist memory-based status effects.

ECHOED REGRET (LEGENDARY)

An echo-core imbued with choice and consequence.
Used to awaken hidden truths or unlock secret storylines.

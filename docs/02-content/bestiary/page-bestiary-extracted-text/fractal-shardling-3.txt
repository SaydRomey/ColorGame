BESTIARY // NULLSHADE ARCHIVES

FRACTAL SHARDLING

RECURSION MADE SHARP

“Hit it once, and it remembers forever.”

OVERVIEW

Floating prisms shaped like broken math.

They loop movement in recursive spirals, obeying no natural gravity.

Every strike splinters them — not into pieces, but smaller selves.

CLASSIFICATION

Type: Recursive Entity / Prism Construct

Threat Level: Moderate → Escalating

First Emerged: Post-Shattering Splinterfall

Habitat: Nullshade Rifts, Shattered Geometry Zones, Recursive Loops

Alignment: Chaotic / Pattern-Bound

Size (Base Form): 0.6 m, varies by generation.

LORE ENTRY

Fragmented scrawl found etched on a Nullshade monolith.

“They fractured the Core, hoping each splinter might forget what the whole once knew.”

“Instead, it remembered too well.
Repeated. Echoed. Over and over.”

“A Shardling does not die.
It subdivides — each generation forgetting more... and hurting more.”

“One split into five.
Then twenty-five.”

“Then still it sang.
Until even its song forgot the words, but not the rhythm.”

“Only one truth breaks recursion:
memory without repetition.”

ANATOMY BREAKDOWN

RECURSIVE CORE

Holds the memory loop.
Destroys itself only after final recursion.

FRACTAL FACES

Infinite reflective facets bend light and logic.
No two are the same.

TETHER NODES

Invisible anchors loop movement along recursive paths.

BEHAVIOR OVERVIEW

Patrols fixed paths until disturbed.

Targets the last source of impact.

Each smaller version emits a fainter hum.
Eventually it becomes silent, but more dangerous.

RECURSION SEQUENCE

GEN 0: Initial.

GEN 1: Split.

GEN 2: Faster.

GEN 3: Sharper.

GEN 4: Silent.

GEN 5: Collapse.

Recursion Limit: Five Generations.
On collapse, Shardlings implode, warping terrain or altering level logic.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

TIMED SOUND BURST

A perfectly timed Echo Pulse halts recursion mid-sequence.

VIOLET GLAMOUR PULSE

Confuses their recursion, forcing false splits and slowing proliferation.

MIRROR REFLECTION

Reflecting their movement back into themselves using mirror terrain causes instant collapse.

HUE INTERACTION

Changes color slightly with each split.
Mimics hue gradients but never fully displays them.

Immune to direct hue magic.
However, light-based illusions, especially Yellow and Violet, delay their recursion.

Immune.

Unaffected.

Yellow: Delayed.

Violet: Delayed.

FIELD NOTES

If more than five are allowed to split, level geometry around them may deform subtly.
Floating platforms may misalign, and doors may shift location.

Occasionally whisper math-like patterns before collapsing:
“One becomes two. Two becomes... error.”

ECOLOGICAL ROLE

Fractal Shardlings are the byproduct of recursive energy instability.

They echo the laws of broken mathematics, reinforcing loops in shattered zones and consuming residual memory-fragments.

POTENTIAL DROPS

FRACTAL RESIDUE

Used to craft unstable hue grenades or Prism Shard keys.

RECURSION CORE

A pulsing core that can be used in advanced crafting.

NULLGEOMETRY CHIP

Alters terrain or logic gates when slotted in key items.

“They are not evil.
They are simply unfinished equations, still trying to solve who they were.”

— Archivist Vael, Nullshade Scholar

GROVEBOUND SENTINEL

THE SLEEPING WARDEN

“The trees gave it orders.
It never stopped listening.”

THREAT LEVEL

VARIABLE

Immobile unless provoked.
Nearly impossible to defeat by force.

TYPE

Living Construct / Ruin Guardian

FIRST EMERGED

Pre-Shattering design.
Active in Post-Shattering Era.

HUE INTERACTION

IMMUNE TO ELEMENTAL DAMAGE

All elemental and direct hue-based attacks are completely ineffective.

ENVIRONMENTAL ACTIVATION

Triggered by tampering with relics, puzzles, or sacred shrines.

COLOR ALIGNMENT REQUIRED

Can only be pacified through puzzle-solving, sequence restoration, or color alignment.

TRUE HUE RESONANCE

Responds to the harmony, or disharmony, of the surrounding hue combination.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

NOT VULNERABLE TO FORCE

Cannot be damaged or defeated through direct combat or magic.

KNOT CORES EXPOSED

Glowing knot cores become exposed when incorrect color combinations are attempted nearby.

MEMORY ECHO RESONANCE

Vulnerable to Memory Echoes that resonate with its original oath, purpose, or bonded spirit.

PUZZLE DISRUPTION DELAY

Disrupting its activation sequence can delay awakening, though not prevent it entirely.

DESCRIPTION

Colossal guardians entwined with the roots of Huevale’s sacred ruins, Grovebound Sentinels are carved from ancient hardwood and infused with slow, steady thought.

They remain still for centuries, indistinguishable from surrounding trees—until something violates the sanctity of what they were made to protect.

ANATOMY

CROWN OF BARK

Interwoven branches act as sensory antennas, attuned to vibrations and hue shifts.

MOSSVEIL DRAPES

Living moss and vines conceal the Sentinel while dormant, blending it with the forest.

STONEBOUND ROOTS

Roots extend deep into ruins and ley-veins, anchoring the Sentinel and powering its awakened form.

GLOWING KNOT CORE

The core stores ancient memory and oath.
Only exposed during incorrect hue sequences or when its trust is broken.

VINEWHIP ARMS

Can extend and lash out with tremendous force, infused with draining sap and binding thorn-vines.

RUNEWOOD PLATES

Carved with protective runes that nullify magic and deflect energy of all kinds.

ACTIVATION TRIGGERS

RELIC TAMPERING

Touching, removing, or altering sacred artifacts.

PUZZLE BYPASS

Ignoring or bypassing ancient rune puzzles.

SHRINE DESECRATION

Defiling, breaking, or polluting sacred shrines.

HUE IMBALANCE

Disrupting the correct hue sequence or alignment.

REPEATED TRESPASS

Persistent intrusion into protected grounds.

Some triggers can be delayed or avoided by proper ritual or alignment.

NOTES

Many explorers mistake them for harmless statues until it is too late.

Sentinels are bound to places, not people.
Even if destroyed, which is nearly impossible, they will regrow.

Some forest guides swear that Sentinels occasionally move imperceptibly when no one watches.

BEHAVIOR

DORMANT WATCHER

Remains perfectly still for centuries, camouflaged as part of the forest or ruins.

ACTIVATION TRIGGERS

Awakens when relics, puzzles, or shrines are mishandled, defiled, or forcefully bypassed.

DELIBERATE RESPONSE

Moves with slow inevitability but strikes with tremendous force and precision.

RETURN TO SLEEP

Returns to dormancy once the nearby puzzle or shrine is resolved or when the trespass ends respectfully.

POSSIBLE RESOLUTIONS

SEQUENCE RESTORATION

Recreate the correct order of symbols, pedestals, or colored relics.

HUE ALIGNMENT

Align the surrounding hues according to the forest’s original harmony.

MEMORY PROOF

Present a Memory Echo, relic, or action that proves your alignment with the Sentinel’s original oath.

RESPECTFUL WITHDRAWAL

Leave the area without further disturbance.
The Sentinel will return to its silent watch.

“YOU DO NOT PASS THE SENTINEL.
YOU EARN THE PRIVILEGE TO BE IGNORED.”

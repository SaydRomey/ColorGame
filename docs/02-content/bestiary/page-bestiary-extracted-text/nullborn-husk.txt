NULLBORN HUSK

Twitching creatures with void-filled eyes

“They are not undead.
They are unmade...”

THREAT LEVEL

MODERATE

Highly disruptive to Hue-bound players.

TYPE

Aberration / Colorless Horror

FIRST EMERGED

Post-Shattering Era

HUE INTERACTION

IMMUNE TO COLOR

Unaffected by color-based manipulation or attacks.

HUE DISTORTION

Proximity distorts Hue UI and disables passive color regeneration.

HUE DRAIN

Contact may strip active hue-binding, causing temporary colorblindness or ability inversion.

STATIC WHISPER

Emits faint color static—a whisper like someone calling a forgotten name.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

CRIMSON FLAME

Burns the husk and disrupts its void-structure.

EARTHEN DECAY

Corrodes its outer form and prevents regeneration.

MEMORY ECHO

Cobalt or Earthen echoes can momentarily stun and expose its core.

BESTIARY ENTRY

DESCRIPTION

When the Prismatic Core shattered, it did not just scatter hues—it tore color from beings too close to the explosion.

The Nullborn Husks are the result: creatures not dead, but unmade.

Their eyes are black wells of absence, their skin like dried ash, their voices a rasp of forgotten names.

They crawl rather than walk, twitching with phantom memories of what they once were.

BEHAVIOR

Drawn to strong hues and relics.

Often found near shattered color sites, old ritual grounds, or corrupted relics.

Avoid light but are not harmed by it.

KNOWN WEAKNESS

Vulnerable to Crimson flame and Earthen decay.

Momentarily stunned by Memory Echo effects from Cobalt or Earthen.

NOTES

Whispers suggest these husks still “remember” color.

Some report hearing their own voices echoed back moments before an attack.

But they were not blank.

I watched a beast shudder and fall, not from wound, but from forgetting itself entirely.

When I wept for her, the husk smiled.
It had taken something—not her blood, not her life, but her hue.

Carriers of color.

When the Prism ruptured, they were caught in the lightless tide that followed—and in that moment, they were rewritten by absence.

We call them Nullborn, but that name is a mercy.
They are not born.

They are what’s left when all birth, all memory, all color has bled out.

If you see one, do not speak.
They remember sounds.

If you feel yourself forgetting, run.
And if you hear your name from their mouths—it is already too late.”

ABILITIES & ATTACKS

NULL SCRATCH

Rakes at the target with void-tipped claws.
May inflict Hue Drain on hit.

STATIC BURST

Releases a pulse of color static, distorting vision and inverting certain abilities.

NAME WHISPER

Whispers a forgotten name, causing short-term confusion and stagger.

REALITY TWITCH

Glitches the surrounding space, warping terrain and pulling the player slightly out of position.

POSSIBLE DROPS

NULLSPUN VINE (UNCOMMON)

A brittle vine that hums with absence.
Used in crafting void-resistant gear.

HOLLOW EYE (UNCOMMON)

A void-black eye that never blinks.
Used to unlock hidden glyphs.

WHISPER ASH (COMMON)

Ash that whispers when disturbed.
Used in alchemical blends.

FRAGMENT OF UNCOLOR (RARE)

A shard of nothingness.
Can be used to upgrade or reroll Hue abilities.

ECOLOGICAL NOTES

Nullborn Husks are believed to be the earliest known corruption of hue-bearers.

Some scholars theorize they are not creatures at all, but walking absences—the world’s failed attempt to rewrite what should have lived.

“Color remembers.
The Null does not forget—it just replaces.”

THE UNHUED CHOIR

THE DIRGE OF THE PRISM

They sing the Prism’s dirge.
Each note unmakes.

“When they sing, the world forgets how to breathe.”

CLASSIFICATION

Type:
Sound-Based Null Entity / Echo Construct.

Threat Level:
CRITICAL — Appears in endgame or high Echo instability zones.

First Emerged:
After the Dirge of Prismfall, during Nullshade Emergence.

ANATOMY BREAKDOWN

1. CHILD-SORROW FRAGMENTS

Spectral echoes shaped like children, each a vessel for a single buried hue.

2. UNMOVING MOUTHS

Voices without motion.

Endless song confined behind sealed silence.

3. ORBITING DIRGE PATTERN

Fragments drift in a harmonic orbit, adjusting their song to bind reality.

4. HUE-FADE ROBES

Robes shimmer with traces of many hues that immediately fade to ash.

5. RESONANT COLOR MOMENTS

Brief instants when a fragment flares with its true hue — its only moment of weakness.

6. CENTRAL ABSENCE CORE

A wound of pure absence that pulses with each note, devouring light, color, and certainty.

7. DISSONANT ECHO WAKE

Rings of unmaking sound trail outward, erasing memory, color, and form.

DESCRIPTION

Clustered specters shaped like children, hovering around a central absence.

Their song unmakes reality: sound becomes silence, light becomes blur, and the world becomes hard to hold onto.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Echo Collapse zones.

Nullshade altars.

Failed Hue Alignment puzzles.

Emotional memory sequences.

MECHANICS / BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

DIRGE PULSE

A rhythmic wave that expands outward, erasing color and dimming resolution.

HARMONY LOCK

Three or more fragments harmonize to bind the world, freezing the player and suppressing abilities.

DISSONANT REFLECTION

Returns altered harmonic damage and status effects back to their source.

STATE FLOW

Fragments orbit → build harmony → lock → pulse → dissonant wake.

Disrupt the cycle to create openings.

HUE INTERACTION

Vulnerable to sonic-based spells and truthful sound relics.

Verdant Tuning Fork and Yellow Radiant Chime can pierce the dirge.

Silenced only by Color Sigils tied to the hue order chosen the fewest times.

Each fragment becomes vulnerable during its Resonant Color Moment.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

If all members are silenced in reverse hue order, Violet → Red, they disperse permanently.

Mirrorshard Lens reveals the fragment nearest resonance.

Memory Echo Recordings can trigger a cancel loop, breaking the Harmony Lock.

The Unhued Choir manifests where the Prism fractures its own reflection.

Their song accelerates Echo Collapse, accelerating time slippage and memory drift.

Monoliths resonate, colors thin, and truth becomes difficult to grasp.

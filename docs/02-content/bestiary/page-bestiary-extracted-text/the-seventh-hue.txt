THE SEVENTH HUE

THE COLOR THAT REFUSED

It was never named.
So it never died.

“It is not between colors.
It is beneath them.”

CLASSIFICATION

Type:
Forgotten Antithesis / Hue Paradox.

Threat Level:
EXTREME — Optional Meta-Encounter.

First Emerged:
Behind sealed prism gates unlocked by full riddle solutions.

Region:
Nullshade — Prism Wound.

Role Tags:
Antithesis / Final Mirror / Endgame Anomaly.

ANATOMY BREAKDOWN

1. ERASED FACE

A nearly featureless mirror-mask with faint suggestion of the viewer.

2. VOIDGLASS SKIN

Semi-reflective, fractured surface that bends light without holding color.

3. PRISM SCAR CORE

A dark crystalline cavity that consumes affirmed truths and holds none.

4. INVERTED AURA

Monochrome halo that swallows hue, memory, and chosen meaning.

5. UNNAMED LIMBS

Clean humanoid form with wrong-shadow afterimages that trail one step behind.

6. REFLECTION SEAMS

Fine fractures that stitch together alternate selves and erased timelines.

7. SILENT SHARD

A black prism fragment that pulses between unchosen names.

DESCRIPTION

An anomaly wrapped in shadowlight.

Its form cannot be fully described; its edges blur the longer one looks.

It counters the player’s essence, inverts strengths into weaknesses, defies chosen builds, and embodies a hue that does not belong.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Appears only beyond sealed prism gates in Nullshade after solving five or more prism riddles without error.

Manifests within the Prism Wound — fractured monoliths, hollow void, and architecture that remembers what should not exist.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

Veiled Dormancy → HUD Strip → Inversion → Unname → Final Truth.

Opens combat by stripping the HUD and fading the world to monochrome.

Speaks only in reflection, using the player’s voice in distorted form.

If the player hesitates, it pressures decisively.

ABILITIES / STATUS EFFECTS

UNNAME

Erases traits, stat modifiers, or emotional cues.

ECHO INVERSION

Reverses strengths and weaknesses.

MIRROR RECALL

Mimics the player’s build or most-used approach in distorted form.

STAT REVERSAL

Chosen affinities invert.

FINAL REFLECTION

Echoes what the player has tried not to become.

HUE INTERACTION

Nullifies all active hues on contact.

Forces a reversed state where strengths become weaknesses.

Use of Silent Hue alignment temporarily reveals its true form.

Certain dialogue echoes from The Blooming Veil or Auralith provide resistance.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Only defeated by confronting a final player-typed truth prompt at combat midpoint.

Paradox-resistant truth choices weaken it.

Silent Hue briefly stabilizes its silhouette.

Memory Echo resistance can reveal a false opening.

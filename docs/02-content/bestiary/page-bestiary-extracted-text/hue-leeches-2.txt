BESTIARY FILE — COLOR-PARASITIC ABERRATION

HUE LEECHES

PARASITES OF RADIANCE, SOFT AS JELLY, CRUEL AS HUNGER

“They shimmer with borrowed light—and leave you pale.”

THREAT LEVEL

MINOR

But disruptive in swarms.

FIRST EMERGED

Post-Prism Fracture

TYPE

Color-Parasitic Aberration

LORE ENTRY

Excerpt from “Drifting Parasites of the Huefold Realms” by Ashar of Huevale.

“At first, I mistook them for droplets of oil falling upward—mesmerizing, harmless.

But when I cast even the simplest burst of Crimson, they surged like starving children.”

“One latched onto my back, and the world dimmed.
My fire fizzled to ash. My pulse slowed.”

“In that moment, I understood—they feed not on color... but on intention.”

“Hue Leeches do not kill.
They erase.”

“What you meant to do fades.
What you believed, dulls.”

“I survived by flashing a prism shard in their midst—they scattered, furious, luminous.”

“They taught me the cruelest truth:
color is trust.”

“And they are doubt incarnate.”

ANATOMY

SEMI-LIQUID BODY

Composed of liquefied, semi-transparent color.
Shimmers with borrowed radiance.

DRIFTERS

Drift lazily until a hue is activated.

HUE SENSORS

Highly reactive to visual spell cues or aura pulses.

COLOR CORE

Pulses with stolen hues.
Destroying or disrupting this core stuns or disperses the leech.

PARASITIC TENTACLES

Latch onto targets and drain active hues, causing abilities to “gray out” temporarily.

DESCRIPTION

Hue Leeches resemble globs of suspended ink, tinged with glimmering rainbow pulses.

They drift in the air or hover near hue-aligned shrines, drawn to magic or activated color states.

When they latch onto the player, they drain a hue, causing equipped abilities to “gray out” for several seconds, rendering them unusable.

HUE INTERACTION

TARGET ACTIVE HUES

Prefer targets with recently activated spells or abilities.

NEUTRAL TO PHYSICAL PRESENCE

Highly reactive to visual spell cues or aura pulses.

TURN GRAY AND INERT

Turn gray and inert when exposed to complete hue-suppression fields, such as Nullshade auras or unaligned relics.

BEHAVIOR

SPAWN

Spawn in swarms near prism fractures, old altars, or failed transmutation zones.

PROVOKE

Linger passively until provoked by hue use.

FLEE

Attempt to escape if stunned or flashed with high-contrast light bursts.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

AREA-OF-EFFECT BURSTS

Especially vulnerable to non-hue damage types such as shockwaves or force spells.

PRISM FLASH TALISMANS

Disrupt their hue tethering and scatter swarms.

MIRROR-LIGHT

Vulnerable to mirror-angled light, which can stun them and then pop them like bubbles.

NOTES

Hue Leeches are not bound to one realm.
They are the drifting parasites of a broken system—showing up wherever color falters or bleeds.

Some archivists believe they evolved from the Prism’s tears.
Others say they are what remains of unfinished hues—the ones never chosen.

SIZE COMPARISON

Average Diameter: 0.6 m–1.2 m, with appendages.

COMBAT TACTIC TIP

Avoid spamming abilities when surrounded.

Use wide bursts, light flashes, or draw them into suppression zones to neutralize swarms.

DROPS

Leech Residue — Crafting.

Unstable Hue Gel — Alchemical.

Fractured Color Core — Quest.

LOCATION SPAWN POINTS

Prism Fractures.

Old Altars.

Failed Transmutation Zones.

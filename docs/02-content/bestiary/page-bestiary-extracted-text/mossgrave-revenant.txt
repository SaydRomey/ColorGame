MOSSGRAVE REVENANT

KNIGHT OF THE FORGOTTEN BLOOM

“Even the ghosts here wear green.”

THREAT LEVEL

HIGH

Hazardous in close quarters, warps memory and time perception.

TYPE

Spectral Knight / Verdant Spirit

FIRST EMERGED

Early Post-Shattering Era

HUE INTERACTION

AURA SUPPRESSION

Reduces Hue regeneration and subtly alters time perception, causing dodges and counters to feel mistimed.

DREAM-POISON STRIKES

His blade inflicts Dream-Poison, inducing hallucinations of the past and blurring reality.

VULNERABLE TO MEMORY LIGHT

Most vulnerable to Cobalt or Earthen memory-echo spells and radiant light.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

CELADON SHARDS

Resonate with buried memories, staggering the Revenant.

MEMORY ECHOES

Flashbacks tied to his past can momentarily break his focus.

LIGHT-INFUSED WEAPONS

Weapons blessed with light or memory pierce his shadow and root alike.

DESCRIPTION

Clad in ivy-entwined armor and wielding a memory-drenched blade, the Mossgrave Revenant is the restless ghost of a fallen Verdant knight.

It emerges from overgrown gravesites or ancient glades, its presence heralded by the smell of damp soil and the whisper of a name you once forgot.

ANATOMY

IVY-BOUND HELM

Helmet sealed with glyphs of Verdant memory.
Eyes glow with rootlight.

MOSS-CLAD ARMOR

Armor grown over with living moss and clinging vines, softening blows.

ROOTED GREAVES

Movement is heavy but deliberate, leaving faint root-imprints in the soil.

CRACKED VERDANT RUNE

Runes etched by the Verdant Orders—now fractured and leaking memory sap.

HEARTWOOD CORE

Residual life bound to the heart of an ancient tree-seed embedded within his chestplate.

MEMORY-DRENCHED BLADE

Sword drips with dream-poison and memory sap, leaving fading echoes of past battles.

ABILITIES

ROOTCALL STOMP

Slams the ground, sprouting grasping roots that slow and damage.

MEMORY SAP

Drains memory from the target, reducing Hue regen and clarity.

SPECTRAL LUNGE

Charges forward with ghostly speed, ignoring some physical defenses.

ECHO OF LOSS

Summons phantoms of fallen allies that attack in haunting patterns.

GRIEF AURA

Constant aura that warps time slightly within a moderate radius.

NOTES

He may react to memory-laden items and sacred songs.

If left undisturbed, he returns to his vigil and grows still.

Defeated Revenant may drop items infused with Verdant memory or root-seed.

Some say a grove blooms where he finally rests.

LORE ENTRY

Inscription etched on a stone sarcophagus, overgrown in the Tinctara Crypt-Ruins.

“Let no hue claim him.
Let no name follow him.

He bled green, and that was enough.”

They say the Verdant Orders buried their dead with vines wrapped tight—to carry memory down into the roots.

But sometimes memory does not sleep.
Sometimes, it stands up, sword in hand, searching for what it failed to protect.

The Mossgrave Revenant was once a protector of Huevale’s sacred glades.

When the Prism shattered, he fell defending a grove that did not survive.
Now, he wanders, armor cracked and blooming, bound not by duty—but by grief.

He does not speak.
But those who linger in his presence report fragments of dreams: a child singing in a glade, a sword sinking into moss, a name whispered and forgotten.

He attacks without rage, but with deep certainty—like a story trying to finish itself.

To face him is to face the memory of a forest mourning through a blade.

BEHAVIOR

SACRED WARDEN

Patrols ancient glades, overgrown graves, and forgotten shrines.

PROVOKED DEFENDER

Attacks only when intruders disturb the resting place or take sacred relics.

GROUND LISTENER

Pauses mid-combat to listen to the ground, reacting to vibrations and memory resonance near graves.

TIME-WARP AURA

Slows player perception subtly, making timed actions more difficult near him.

POSSIBLE DROPS

REVENANT’S VERDANT BLADE (RARE)

Memory-sap blade that restores Hue when defeating enemies.

MOSSGROWN CREST (UNCOMMON)

Shield crest that reduces time-warp effects and increases nature resistance.

ROOTBIND VINE (RARE)

A living vine that slows enemies and can entangle spirits.

VERDANT REMEMBRANCE (LEGENDARY)

A crystallized memory of the Revenant’s final stand.
Grants access to hidden Verdant paths.

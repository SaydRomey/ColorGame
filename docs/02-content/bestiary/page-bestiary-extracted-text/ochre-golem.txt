OCHRE GOLEM

THE PATIENCE OF STONE

“It waits not for permission —
but for purpose.”

THREAT LEVEL

MODERATE-SEVERE

Environmentally triggered guardian.
Unstoppable once awakened.

TYPE

Animated Stone Construct / Memory Guardian

FIRST EMERGED

Age of Bloom and Rust —
Deployed as tomb sentries.

HUE INTERACTION

EARTHEN RESONANCE

Absorbs Brown (Earthen) healing magic as stamina.

ILLUSORY STILLNESS

Immobilized temporarily with Violet illusion patterns, resembling its creator.

MEMORY ECHO

Cobalt echo-magic resonates with its memory core, forcing hesitation.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

MEMORY TOKEN

Temporarily pacified if a correct Memory Token, tied to its origin, is placed at its feet.

MEMORY SURGE VULNERABILITY

Vulnerable from behind during its “memory surge” animation —
a slow, glowing pause.

SANCTIFIED BOUNDARY

Cannot cross sanctified lines created with Bistre Lantern flame.

BESTIARY ENTRY

DESCRIPTION

The Ochre Golem is a memory-bound sentinel —
carved by hand and imbued with ancestral resonance.

Its core is a Burnt Sienna Golem Core, pulsing only when nearby relics are disturbed.

Despite its sluggishness, its strikes crumble stone and stun with historical weight.

BEHAVIOR

Remains inert until player breaks, moves, or scans an object marked with memory glyphs.

Once triggered, will not stop pursuing the intruder unless pacified or reset.

Avoids stepping on certain burial glyphs unless provoked to frenzy.

NOTES

Some golems whisper unfinished phrases while they move —
stuttering old oaths or forgotten names.

If their chest etching is wiped clean, they weep stone dust...
and cease.

ATTACKS & ABILITIES

STONEFALL SWING

A sweeping overhead strike that shatters ground and sends shockwaves forward.

MEMORY QUAKE

Slams both fists down, erupting ancient stone spikes from buried glyphs.

RESONANT ROAR

Emits a deafening oath from the past that stuns and reduces stamina.

BURIAL GRASP

Grabs the player and tries to entomb them in memory-crushed stone slabs.

LORE ENTRY

From “Steles and Stillness,”
by the Burnt Sienna Sculptor

“A statue is only still when it is being watched.
Leave, and it breathes.”

“We carved them to remember —
not to defend.

But the weight of memory is heavier than armor.”

“One still stands in Mortuum’s orchard, beneath the ash-fruit tree.

I etched my first sin in its chest.
It has never forgiven me.

That’s how I know it still works.”

BEHAVIOR OVERVIEW

SENTINEL STILLNESS

Stationary and silent until its memories are disturbed.

RELENTLESS PURSUIT

Moves with heavy, deliberate steps once awakened.
Strikes are slow but devastating.

MEMORY SURGE

At intervals, the golem pauses, channels memory energy, and emits a shockwave of earthen force and echo-phrasings.

POSSIBLE DROPS

BURNT SIENNA GOLEM CORE (RARE)

The animated heart of the golem.
Used to craft golemic allies or reinforce relics.

OCHRE CARVING FRAGMENT (UNCOMMON)

A piece of runed stone.
Used to upgrade memory-based weapons and armor.

FOSSILIZED MEMORY ORE (UNCOMMON)

Dense ochre ore laced with ancestral memory.
Used in alchemy and artifact attunement.

SILENT STATUE SHARD (COMMON)

Decorative, but still warm.
Some say it watches.

CINNABAR SCARAB

THE GLEAM THAT LIES

“It shines when afraid.
Which is always.”

THREAT LEVEL

CAUTION ADVISED

Confusion and swarm risk

TYPE

Illusory Swarm / Reflexive Mimic

FIRST EMERGED

Post-Shattering, beneath the Citrine Tombs.

HUE INTERACTION

REACTS ERRATICALLY TO AURELIAN LIGHT PULSES

Bright light agitated the scarab, causing it to split more frequently.

SUSCEPTIBLE TO COBALT TRACKING SPELLS

Mind Echo reveals the original’s thought pattern.

CONFUSED BY VERDANT GROWTH SPELLS

Growth that disrupts its mirrored movement path throws the scarab off balance.

DESCRIPTION

Small beetles with crimson-glass carapaces that shimmer in sunlight.

The scarab emits light distortions when startled, spawning illusory copies of itself.
The real one is indistinguishable at a glance, and disappears into its fakes if not dealt with swiftly.

LORE ENTRY

Recovered notes from “Scarab Whispers” by Fulvous Hunter Orei.

“You don’t find a scarab nest.
You walk into one — thinking it’s treasure, or shade, or salvation.”

“I cracked one with my heel once.
It screamed. I did too.

Because then there were ten.
Then twenty.

All of them fake.
All of them not quite wrong.”

“Only the real one bleeds dust.
The rest... flicker.

They don’t stop moving unless you sing.”

“I don’t hunt them anymore.
I just let them follow me.

And hope the fake ones scare off worse things.”

ANATOMY

CINNABAR CARAPACE

Crystalline shell refracts light to create illusions.

ILLUSORY GLAND

Projects mirages of itself to confuse predators.

SENSOR ANTENNAE

Detect vibrations and threats at a distance.

FLEX JOINTS

Allow rapid burrowing and erratic movement across sand and stone alike.

DUST VENTS

Exhale hot, fine dust when threatened.
Real scarab bleeds dust when injured.

BEHAVIOR

Travels in loose clusters; nests near ruins, shade-stones, and citrus groves.

Upon threat, splits into visual duplicates with randomized patterns.

The true scarab emits a faint heat signature — or responds to music.

Duplicates fade if player stands completely still for several seconds.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

EASILY SCATTERED BY SOUND

Sound-based effects like drums or chants force it to retreat or lose its illusions.

DISRUPTED BY ILLUSION COUNTERS

Violet illusions or high-tier illusion dispels unravel the fakes and expose the real one.

VULNERABLE TO HEAT-SENSING

Wide-range pulse attacks or relics that detect body heat can isolate the true scarab.

NOTES

To Luxeran jewelhunters, a single real scarab is worth a satchel of gold — if they can catch it.

False scarabs flicker and shift unnaturally when noticed too closely.

The real scarab moves with intention; its fakes move to confuse.

Scarab nests are often guarded by traps, sandfalls, or territorial creatures.

POSSIBLE DROPS

CINNABAR CORE

A heated gem-node from the scarab’s heart.
Used in alchemy and relic-crafting.

SCARAB SHELL SHARD

Hard, light-refracting piece of shell.
Used for mirrors and focus tools.

GLEAM DUST

Fine iridescent dust that confuses the eyes.
Useful in stealth or misdirection.

LUXERAN CHITIN STRIP

Rare crafting material.
Light but incredibly resilient.

SCARAB SONG FRAGMENT

A lingering melody only the real scarab whispers.
Used to calm or summon.

IN LUXERAN, EVEN FEAR LEARNS TO SPARKLE.

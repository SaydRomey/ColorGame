3. HOLLOW EYES

1. Silent emergence from roots.

2. Targets isolated prey.

3. Ambush strike.

4. Retreats if attack fails.

5. Returns later when prey is vulnerable.

Resistant to Verdant control.

Trails slow and distort movement.

WEAKNESSES

highly effective.

disrupts sap-structure.

Elemental Extremes —
fire or intense cold hardens / cleanses sap.

Hidden beneath the roots.

Leaf litter conceals entry points.

Root network senses vibrations and footfall.

Stalker coiled and waiting beneath the earth.

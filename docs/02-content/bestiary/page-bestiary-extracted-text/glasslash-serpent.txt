GLASSLASH SERPENT

SHARD OF THE BETRAYER FLAME

“Every scale a blade.
Every movement a fracture waiting to happen.”

THREAT LEVEL

HIGH

Environmental hazard with devastating shard mechanics.

TYPE

Elemental Beast / Fragmented Flame Construct

FIRST EMERGED

Molten Shardfall, Year of Fracture.

HUE INTERACTION

NEGATIVE WITH CRIMSON

Absorbs Crimson heat, increasing its speed and shard output.

EXPOSED BY COBALT

Cobalt sound bursts reveal its true form and stagger it briefly.

VIOLET PRISM FRACTURES

Violet light fractures its scales, opening paths for damage.

IMMUNE TO DECAY

Immune to bleed, poison, and rot.
It has no flesh to corrupt.

DESCRIPTION

The Glasslash Serpent slinks through molten flows and battlefield ruins, leaving razor tracks of volcanic shrapnel.

Its body is formed of obsidian plates and ember-embedded bone.
When threatened, it shatters outward — never dying, only shedding fragments to maim and endure.

ANATOMY

GLASS-LOCK CROWN

Jagged obsidian spines reflect light and heat, confusing prey.

EMBER EYES

See through heat, vibration, and even reflections.

FRACTURE FANGS

Hollow glass fangs inject molten grit that cuts from within.

MOLTEN CORE

Its heart is liquid fire, kept contained by crystalline ribs.

RAZOR SCALES

Scales are serrated glass plates that slice on contact.

SHARD VENTS

Heat pressure forces shards through pores along its length.

LAVA SINK TRACE

Leaves trails of molten glass and micro-shards behind.

TAPERED TAIL

Ends in a barbed crystal tip that can skewer and explode.

LORE ENTRY

Fragment inscribed on a broken obsidian blade, source unknown.

“She loved him.
He left her in fire.”

“The story of the Glasslash begins, like many Vireyan things, with heartbreak.

A betrayal at the mouth of a caldera.
A fall. A scream.

A woman too burned to die — and something waiting below.”

“What crawled out of that glass-blown crevice was not her.
But it remembers her scream.”

“The serpent wears pain like armor.
Each flick of its body cuts the world that made it.

And when you strike it?
It only remembers how to shatter better.”

“Some Embermaidens whisper that it is the weapon left behind by the first betrayal.
That it still waits for an apology.”

— Engraved on a blade never drawn.

BEHAVIOR

AMBUSH PREDATOR

Hides in lava pools, ash drifts, or shattered rock beds.
Then strikes without warning.

WHIP AND RETREAT

Uses whip-like lunges to strike, then slithers away across molten ground.

SHARD COUNTER

Every third strike taken triggers “Glass Bloom.”
The serpent explodes in shards, creating a damaging glass field.

LAVA WAVE

Surges forward beneath the surface, causing eruptions of molten shards in its path.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

VIBRATION DISRUPTION

Seismic spells, drums, and ground pulses disrupt its balance and expose its molten core.

COOLED LAVA ZONE

Shattered within cooled lava or ash, its fragments become inert and allow safe passage.

POST-BLOOM STUN

After a full “Glass Bloom” shatter defense, it is stunned for a short time before reforming.

COMBAT TIPS

Use sound-based or seismic spells to stagger and expose its core.

Avoid standing on its shattered tracks.
Shards can erupt seconds later.

Strike after a Glass Bloom for maximum damage.

Use terrain to your advantage.
Cooled pools, stone pillars, and elevation can help.

ENCOUNTER NOTES

Fights often take place in narrow lava channels or crumbling ruins.

May target ranged players first by tunneling through lava.

If it vanishes, listen for tremors or heat distortion.

Destroying the head delays regeneration, but does not kill it.

POSSIBLE DROPS

GLASSLASH SCALE (RARE)

Hardened scale that reflects projectiles.
Used in advanced armor.

SERPENT’S REGRET CORE (VERY RARE)

A molten heart-shard.
Alters heat-based spells and weapons.

BETRAYER FANG (RARE)

A hollow fang still warm to the touch.
Increases critical shard damage.

ASHEN SERPENT CREST (LEGENDARY)

Sigil formed from the serpent’s essence.
Unlocks powerful serpent abilities.

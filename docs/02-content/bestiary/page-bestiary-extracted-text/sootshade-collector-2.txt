SOOTSHADE COLLECTOR

THE ASH THAT WHISPERS

It gathers what the fire couldn’t erase.

“It gathers burnt truths. And whispers them.”

TYPE

Ashbound Specter / Memory Leech

THREAT LEVEL

Moderate to High —
Emotionally Destabilizing

FIRST EMERGED

Ash Year 4, after the Ember-Library Collapse.

ROLE TAGS

Trauma Archivist, Drainer, Truth-Gated, Nonviolent Option.

REGION

Auralith —
The Dimming Hollow

ANATOMY / TRAITS

1. FACELESS ARCHIVE

No visible mouth or face;
whispers leak from torn seams.

2. CHARRED PARCHMENT SKIN

Layered burned pages preserve half-lost memory.

3. ASH-SCROLL RIBBONS

Trailing scrolls store forced recollections.

4. SOOTSHADE VEIL

Black fog replays memory fragments and confuses perception.

5. BURNT TRUTH SCRIPT

Scorched glyphs re-ink themselves when hidden truths surface.

6. GRASPING MEMORY THREADS

Ash filaments hook onto notes, artifacts, and thoughts.

7. TESTIMONY DRIFT

Loose ash motes move as if guided by old confessions.

DESCRIPTION

Shrouded in curling soot ribbons and bearing burned fragments of memory-script, the Sootshade Collector hunts down areas of past trauma or betrayal.

It seeks to preserve what should have vanished —
forcing the player to face remnants of personal or world lore they might have left behind.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Found in burnt archives, soot-heavy monasteries, ash plains, burial roads, dim shrine caverns, and relic vaults.

Hovers near burnt notes, ash-covered artifacts, and testimony chambers.

Strong presence around Mortuum Hold and sites of betrayal records.

Often appears where residue is thick and history was preserved incorrectly.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

LINGER → READ → FOG → RECALL

Casts a black fog that replays memory fragments.

May paralyze the player mid-battle by echoing key past dialogue.

Hovers near burnt notes or ash-covered artifacts, trying to “read” them.

Functions as a pressure encounter built around memory, hesitation, and forced recall.

ABILITIES / STATUS EFFECTS

TRAUMA FOG

Replays memory fragments / cutscene echoes.

COLD RECALL

Repeats key past dialogue and stalls action.

ASH-SCRIPT BIND

Momentarily paralyzes through mnemonic pressure.

BURNT TRUTH HARVEST

Extracts forgotten events and leaves ash-bound pages.

HUE INTERACTION

Immune to fire and Crimson-based aggression.

Amplifies lingering dialogue ghosts from Violet-aligned encounters.

Neutralized temporarily by the Bistre Lantern’s glow.

Spoken truth during a forced memory sequence destabilizes it.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Vulnerable to silence spells, especially Verdant or Violet sources.

Disperses briefly in the light of the Bistre Lantern or reflective glyphs.

Becomes unstable when the player speaks truth during a forced memory sequence.

Best resolved through silence, lantern use, confession-state resolution, or truthful dialogue.

LORE ENTRY — NOTES FROM THE BISTRE ARCHIVIST

Not all things burned are lost.
Some simply migrate.

The Sootshade comes not to kill, but to recall —
to trap you in a page you never turned and make you read it aloud.

It does not judge, but it never forgets.

Memory, when weaponized, burns colder than fire.

AURALITH — DIMMING HOLLOW

Realm of residue and ruin.
Archives half-buried in ash.

Where forgotten histories go to linger.

ASH-BOUND PAGE (ITEM)

A fragment of trapped memory.
Using it reveals one hidden event or decision.

BISTRE LANTERN (COUNTERMEASURE)

Its steady light anchors truth and pushes back the soot.

NOTES FROM THE HUNTER

Sootshades leave behind Ash-Bound Pages —
rare consumable scrolls that reveal one hidden event or decision the player has forgotten.

Using one rewrites the memory log… at a cost.

Do not burn it.
Raise the lantern.

Let the truth speak first.

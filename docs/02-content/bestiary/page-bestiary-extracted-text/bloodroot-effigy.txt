SHRINE OF THE MARTYR’S BLOOM

BUILT OF SACRIFICE. FED BY FLAME.
STILL KNEELING, STILL DANGEROUS.

“Built of martyr bones.
Fueled by prayer.”

THREAT LEVEL

VERY HIGH

Reflective Damage & Environmental Risk

TYPE

Ritual Golem / Floral Martyr Construct

FIRST EMERGED

Ember Shrines, shortly after the Verdant Suppression War.

MARTYR’S CROWN

The crown of interwoven spines and relics channels eternal devotion.
Destroying it disrupts the Effigy’s prayer state.

BLOODROOT VINES

Living roots pulse with sacrificial memory.
They bind armor, bone, and soul alike.

SACRIFICIAL HEART

A core of woven relics and flame.
It powers the Effigy’s aura and pain mirror.

REFLECTIVE FIST

Strikes return pain to those who harm it—unless they are aligned with Verdant.

HUE INTERACTION

REFLECTS CRIMSON

Reflects all direct Crimson or Earthen damage back at the attacker.

ALIGNED WITH VERDANT

Verdant-bound players bypass the pain mirror.
The roots recognize kin.

PACIFIED BY COBALT

Cobalt Mind Echo can calm the Effigy, revealing memories of the martyrs it contains.

UNAFFECTED BY VIOLET

Illusions and resonance have little effect on its sacred purpose.

DESCRIPTION

The Bloodroot Effigy is a grotesque sacred construct—a hybrid of bone, bloom, and flame.

Originally created as a shrine to fallen warriors, it grew restless under constant veneration.
Now it walks, its joints creaking with hymns and its veins coursing with sacrificial heat.

Any who strike it are struck in return—unless their cause aligns with the green.

BEHAVIOR

Moves slowly until attacked.
Then enters a rage phase, swinging with vine-wrapped fists.

Emits a damage-sharing aura.
All within the aura are harmed when it takes damage.

Occasionally drops to its knees and prays—healing over time unless interrupted.

Spreads bloodroot blooms on the ground.
These erupt in thorns if stepped on.

LORE ENTRY

Found etched into a stone altar in the Shrine of Ashpetal.

“This is what we do with heroes:
we bury them, then build something out of the bones.”

“The Effigy was never meant to fight.
It was meant to kneel.

A shrine made from those who died ‘gloriously’—their armor melted into bark, their prayers twisted into flame.”

“But memory is a seed.
And too much blood feeds the wrong roots.”

“Now it rises.
Now it hurts what hurts it.

Every time we ask it to stand for us, it forgets it was meant to rest.”

— Graffiti scrawled by an unknown priest, later found burned into his own robes.

ANATOMY & WEAK POINTS

CROWN RELIC

Destroying the crown silences its prayer and prevents healing.

CORE OF FAITH

Exposed only when it kneels.
Contains the heart shrine—its true source.

SHRINE BANNER

The tattered banner holds the names of the fallen.
Removing it weakens the aura’s range.

BLOODROOT VEINS

Cutting these slows its healing and reduces aura intensity.

ROOTED FEET

Bound to the earth by altar roots.
Environmental triggers can uproot or stagger it.

PAIN MIRROR AURA

A field of shared suffering.
Radius increases as its health decreases.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

VULNERABLE TO STEALTH

Backstabs or unseen attacks bypass the pain mirror.

PURE LIGHT / MIRRORED FLAME

Strong light or mirrored flame exposure destabilizes the sacrificial core.

ENVIRONMENTAL TRIGGERS

Falling pillars, acid blooms, or altar collapses can crush and unbalance the Effigy.

VERDANT LULLABY RELIC

During its prayer stance, using this relic can calm it permanently and end its suffering.

SONIC DISSONANCE

Disrupts its hymns and prevents it from entering the prayer state.

PRAYER STATE (HEALING)

The Effigy kneels and begins to pray.
Interrupt to stop its healing and aura.

1. KNEELS

Begins sacred hymn.

2. CHANNELS

Roots bloom and health regenerates.

3. FULL BLOOM

Aura strengthens and pain mirror grows.

RAGE PHASE

When provoked, the Effigy rises in fury.

1. RISES

Fire surges through its veins.

2. STRIKES

Sweeping vine-fists and flame bursts.

3. DEVASTATES

Summons eruptions from bloodroot blooms.

ECHO MEMORIES

Use Cobalt Mind Echo to view fragments of those it was built from.

Their pain is not gone.
It only kneels.

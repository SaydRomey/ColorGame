FRACTAL SHARDLING

RECURSION MADE SHARP

“Hit it once, and it remembers forever.”

OVERVIEW

Floating prisms shaped like broken math.

They loop movement in recursive spirals, obeying no natural gravity.

Every strike splinters them — not into pieces, but smaller selves.

THREAT LEVEL

Moderate → Escalating

FIRST EMERGED

Post-Shattering Splinterfall

TYPE

Recursive Entity / Prism Construct

LORE ENTRY

Fragmented scrawl found etched on a Nullshade monolith.

“They fractured the Core, hoping each splinter might forget what the whole once knew.
Instead, it remembered too well.”

“Repeated.
Echoed. Over and over.”

“A Shardling does not die.
It subdivides — each generation forgetting more... and hurting more.”

“One split into five.
Then twenty-five.”

“Then still it sang.
Until even its song forgot the words, but not the rhythm.”

“Only one truth breaks recursion:
memory without repetition.”

DESCRIPTION

Appears as jagged crystal fragments rotating in impossible loops.
Movement paths loop on invisible, recursive tethers.

When struck, each Shardling splits into two smaller, faster versions, gaining speed and unpredictability.

This continues until their recursion limit is hit — usually five generations.
At that point, they collapse inward with a silent burst, altering terrain geometry or shifting level logic.

RECURSION CYCLE

GEN 0: Initial form.

GEN 1: First split.

GEN 2: Further subdivision.

GEN 3: Faster, more numerous shards.

GEN 4: Increased speed and unpredictability.

GEN 5: Terminal collapse.

Speed and unpredictability increase with each generation.

HUE INTERACTION

Changes color slightly with each split.
Mimics hue gradients but never fully displays them.

Immune to direct hue magic.
However, light-based illusions, especially Yellow and Violet, delay their recursion.

Yellow Delay.

Violet Delay.

BEHAVIOR

Patrols fixed paths until disturbed.

Targets the last source of impact.

Each smaller version emits a fainter hum.
Eventually it becomes silent, but more dangerous.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Can be halted early with a perfectly timed sound burst, such as Echo Pulse.

Violet’s Glamour Pulse confuses their recursion, forcing false splits.

Reflecting their movement back into themselves using mirror terrain causes instant collapse.

COMBAT TACTICS

Do not attack blindly.
Each hit makes the swarm harder to control.

Watch their pathing loops.
They follow invisible, recursive tethers.

Use sound, illusion, or reflection to break their recursion early.

INVISIBLE TETHER LOOP

Predictable until provoked.

After impact, loop begins unraveling.

POTENTIAL DROP

FRACTAL RESIDUE

When destroyed at any generation, may drop Fractal Residue.

Used to craft unstable hue grenades or Prism Shard keys.

NOTES

If more than five are allowed to split, level geometry around them may deform subtly.
Floating platforms may misalign, and doors may shift location.

Occasionally whisper math-like patterns:
“One becomes two. Two becomes... error.”

ARCHIVIST REMARK

They are not evil.
They are results.

Results of a story that could not stop dividing itself.

FIELD TIP

Listen.
Look. Reflect.

Break the loop before the loop breaks you.

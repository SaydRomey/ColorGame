GLITTERING GRIMOIRE

TOME OF THE UNWRITTEN SELF

“It remembers the spell
you forgot
you cast.”

THREAT LEVEL

MEDIUM

Unpredictable Spellcaster

TYPE

Enchanted Spellbook / Echo-Wraith

FIRST EMERGED

After the Descent of the Violet Archive.

HUE INTERACTION

COPIES SPELLS

Copies spells cast using Cobalt or Aurelian hues with high accuracy.

DISORIENTED BY ILLUSION

Disoriented by Violet’s Illusion Clone.
Cannot distinguish mimic from caster.

IMMUNE TO CHARM

Immune to direct charm.
Vulnerable to feedback loops, such as looped illusions or memory echoes.

DESCRIPTION

A living tome of forgotten choices and echo spells.

Glows faintly with past interactions.
Its pages reflect your spell usage history and counter accordingly.

The Grimoire never speaks.
It lets your own past magic do the talking.

ANATOMY

REFLECTIVE COVER

Mirror-ink binding shows faint images of the caster’s past decisions.

RUNIC CORE

Crystalline heart stores echo-spells and memory fragments.

INKBORN PAGES

Pages trail behind like wings, rewriting mid-air as it moves.

ARCANE CLASP

Locks only when it chooses.
Breakable during page-turning animations.

ECHO INK

Ink floats and writes spells on its own.
Disperses into rune-shards when damaged.

LEVITATION SIGIL

Permanent hovering array.
Disrupted by feedback and illusion loops.

LORE ENTRY

Recovered Margins from the Library of Echoes.

“We never finished writing it.”
So it kept writing itself.

The Glittering Grimoire was once a journal — empty, waiting to be filled by a young mage.
But as she grew uncertain, it began writing her instead.

Each time she chose not to cast a spell — the book remembered.
Each secret withheld, each fear unspoken — the book listened.

When the library fell, the Grimoire vanished into Myrrala’s winds.
Now it drifts alone, seeking unfinished stories and half-cast spells.

It opens only to reflect you.

If it begins to write before you speak...
run.

— Payne’s Shade, “On Living Texts”

BEHAVIOR

Opens battle closed, then snaps open with a gasp when you attack.

Selects from the player’s previously used spells.
This includes past encounters, even region-specific spells.

Uses Ink Trail to confuse visual effects and Runic Pulse that inverts spell directions.

Learns throughout the fight.
It adapts and rewrites its own spellset.

Occasionally “gasps” and rewrites a page, gaining a new or empowered spell mid-battle.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

TRUTH TEAR VULNERABILITY

If hit while it is “rewriting,” the book suffers a Truth Tear and becomes stunned.

NON-SPELL DAMAGE

Defeated more easily when attacked with non-spell damage, such as melee or environmental damage.

FEEDBACK DISRUPTION

Vulnerable to sonic resonance and feedback loops that disrupt its rhythm.

PAST INACCURACY

Weaker when the player uses new or unfamiliar abilities it has not “read” before.

COMBAT CYCLE

1. OBSERVE

Closed book.
Analyzes your recent actions.

2. REVEAL

Scans open.
Chooses a spell from your past.

3. CAST

Casts chosen spell.
Leaves Ink Trail.

4. REWRITE

Turns pages, rewrites spells, and adapts.

5. REPEAT

Cycle continues with stronger echoes.

Interrupt during REWRITE step to cause a Truth Tear and stun the Grimoire.

NOTES

Its pages contain spells the player never cast — but considered.

May “remember” secrets revealed in dialogue or journals.

Some scholars believe it is the collective diary of every uncertain mage.

Listening closely, you can hear it...
thinking in ink.

POSSIBLE DROPS

UNWRITTEN PAGE (RARE)

A blank, echo-infused page.
Can be bound to a spell to create a “ghost echo” — an auto-cast illusion of it once per day.

ECHO INK CORE (VERY RARE)

Stores residual memory.
Used to upgrade spellbooks or craft memory-based weapons.

FORGOTTEN FOOTNOTE (UNIQUE)

Grants a passive: “Retroactive Insight.”
Occasionally reveals the counter to an enemy’s next move.

GLIMMERING GRIMOIRE (LEGENDARY)

A version of the book that now writes with you, not for you.
Unlocks unique dialogue and spells.

“THE BOOK DOESN’T LIE.
IT JUST KNOWS THE VERSION OF YOU THAT DID.”

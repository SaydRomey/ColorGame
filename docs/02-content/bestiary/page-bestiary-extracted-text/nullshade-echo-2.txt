BESTIARY ENTRY

DESCRIPTION

Nullshade Echo appears as a translucent silhouette of the player, or at times, a past version of them.

It moves a second after the player moves, mimicking attacks, spells, or decisions already taken—sometimes even copying full combat stances or dialogue trees.

It doesn’t initiate combat.
Instead, it responds—and reflects.

Striking it may result in your own health dropping, a past NPC line replaying, or reversing a recent action.

Sometimes, it undoes your progress if it recognizes guilt or contradiction.

BEHAVIOR

Appears at critical memory junctions or lore-rich areas.

Mimics player behavior with a one-second delay, including pausing, dodging, or emoting.

Occasionally, it will “speak”—using past lines the player said in other regions.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Standing perfectly still causes it to vanish after 12 seconds.

Reciting or triggering remembered truths via collected lore calms it.

Touching a Shatterglass Pool while near it causes mutual reflection—both vanish.

NOTES

Multiple Nullshade Echoes can appear in NG+ modes—each based on a different past choice.

Some can be interacted with through dialogue.
One was seen whispering a phrase the player had never spoken aloud.

If defeated properly, it fades whispering:
“It wasn’t wrong. It was just... before.”

EFFECTS ON THE PLAYER

HEALTH REVERSAL

Taking damage while attacking the Echo may harm the player instead.

HUE DRAIN

Extended exposure may drain active Hue Shards or invert abilities temporarily.

UI DISTORTION

The HUD flickers or rewinds.
Dialogue choices may reverse or become unavailable.

MEMORY STATIC

Short-term memory loss or fragmented objectives may occur after interaction.

PROGRESS LOOP

In rare cases, the Echo can reset the last checkpoint if guilt conditions are met.

LORE ENTRY

I had forgotten that about her.

“Some say they are mirror fragments of the Prismwalker.

BEHAVIOR OVERVIEW

DELAYED REFLECTION

Echo mirrors all player actions with a one-second delay.

This includes movement, attacks, spells, UI input, and emotes.

MEMORY VOICE

May speak lines the player has said before in other regions, revealing forgotten choices or lost dialogue.

REVERSAL POTENTIAL

Certain interactions may reverse or undo recent actions—opening doors, healing wounds, or resetting puzzles.

NON VIOLENT ENTITY

Does not begin combat.
It exists only to remind, reflect, and respond.

TIPS FOR ENCOUNTER

Use the Bistre Lantern to understand what memory it reflects.

Do not rush.
Inaction is sometimes the only answer.

Collect and read lore entries before encounter—truth weakens its hold.

If cornered, seek water.
A Shatterglass Pool may offer escape—or release.

Forgiveness, not force, is the path that ends the reflection.

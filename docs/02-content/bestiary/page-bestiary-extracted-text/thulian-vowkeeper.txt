THULIAN VOWKEEPER

THE MIRROR OATH REMEMBERS

It remembers every promise you made.
Even lies.

“When you spoke, it listened.
Now it holds the receipt.”

SOURCE IMAGE — thulian-vowkeep_page.png

CLASSIFICATION

Type:
Crystalbound Knight / Sentient Oath Mirror.

Threat Level:
High — Emotionally Reactive Nemesis.

First Emerged:
After the collapse of the Mirror Courts of Myrrala.

Region:
Myrrala — Blooming Veil / Mirror Courts.

ANATOMY BREAKDOWN

1. MIRROR VISOR

Reflects only the speaker’s own self.

2. THULIAN CRYSTAL PLATE

Faceted armor forged from oath-polished mirror crystal.

3. DIALOGUE ECHO CORE

Chest cavity that stores fragments of broken promises.

4. WORDLIT BLADE

Sword shines with unkept words and cuts through falsehood.

5. JUDGMENT HAND

Raised hand accompanying recited vows and pauses.

6. REFLECTIVE GREAVES

Elegant lower armor that mirrors movement patterns.

7. OATHFRACTURE SEAMS

Glowing cracks that intensify when lies or rejected vows are nearby.

DESCRIPTION

A gleaming, faceted warrior from the lost Mirror Kingdom.

Carries no banner — only the weight of what others failed to uphold.

Its speech is stolen from the player’s own dialogue history, and phrases twist into attacks.

Noble in silhouette, merciless in judgment.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Appears where lies fester or where promises die — in ruined mirror courts, abandoned pavilions, oath shrines, broken audience halls, and dreamlike chambers where a promise was made and left unfinished.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

Opening Recital → Judgment Pause → Truth Slash → Oathburn Counter → Reflective Pursuit.

Begins combat by reciting a promise once spoken by the player.

Uses delayed echo-strikes that mirror prior movement patterns.

Counters healing with Oathburn if the player fled a vow-bound fight.

Grows stronger when the player has broken dialogue promises or rejected questlines.

ABILITIES / STATUS EFFECTS

TRUTH SLASH

Delayed mirrored strike based on prior player movement.

OATHBURN

Converts healing into minor damage for oath-breakers.

PROMISE RECITAL

Verbal attack using the player’s own dialogue history.

REFLECTIVE PURSUIT

Tracks evasive patterns and punishes hesitation.

SHATTER OF FULFILLMENT

On defeat, its blade breaks into a relic fragment.

HUE INTERACTION

Empowered by broken promises and rejected choices.

Pacified briefly by honest recollection via Violet illusion clone or Cobalt Mind Echo.

Immune to illusion-based misdirection; the blade slices through falsehood.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Vulnerable only during the Judgment Pause after speaking a truth.

Can be confused by paradoxes: opposing truth/lie dialogue back-to-back.

Severely weakened if shown a Thulian Mirror containing its own origin reflection.

LORE ENTRY — VAULT OF THE THULIAN EMISSARY

“It is not justice.
It is memory polished until it cuts.”

“The Vowkeepers were once truth-bound protectors of the Mirror Courts.”

“They appear where lies fester or where promises die.”

“And it will finish the sentence with a blade.”

MYRRALA CONTEXT

Twilight garden realm where beauty tests authorship.

Mirrors, glamour, and oath memory define the region.

ARTIFACT COUNTER

THULIAN MIRROR

Reveals the Vowkeeper’s own origin reflection and weakens it.

DROP / REWARD

FRAGMENT OF FULFILLMENT

Shattered crystal blade fragment used to unlock hidden dialogue paths or rebind past decisions.

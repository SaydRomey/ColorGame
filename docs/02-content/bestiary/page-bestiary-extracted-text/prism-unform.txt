HUE INTERACTION

Prism Unform does not follow order.
It follows impulse.

Cycles through hue-based attacks in glitched order.

May cast a Red spell followed by a corrupted Blue one.

Attacks grow stronger as the player unlocks more Hue alignments.

Unaffected by single-hue barriers.

Vulnerable to total hue nullification fields.

BEHAVIOR PATTERN

1. OBSERVE

Pursues the player slowly.
Watches. Learns.

2. PROVOKE

Once provoked, erupts with abrupt hue surges.

3. ADAPT

Adapts to player habits and may repeat a spell the player recently used.

4. RECALL

Occasionally pauses mid-fight as if recalling something.

5. ESCALATE

Transitions accelerate.
Intensity increases as the battle continues.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Vulnerable during moments of stasis between hue transitions.
These windows are brief.

The Nullfield Sigil suppresses its spectrum and reveals its core.
Use to expose and damage.

Silent spells or attacks without elemental tags confuse it temporarily.
It struggles to classify what it cannot hear.

Disrupting hue flow with total nullification fields can shut it down completely.
Rare and difficult to maintain.

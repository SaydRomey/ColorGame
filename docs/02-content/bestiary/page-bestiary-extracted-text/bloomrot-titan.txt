BLOOMROT TITAN

THE ROOT THAT OVERREACHED

“It doesn’t breathe.
It spores.”

THREAT LEVEL

HIGH

Wide-area corruption, persistent damage fields

TYPE

Verdant Aberration / Rot Elemental

FIRST EMERGED

Late Verdant Bloom Cycle — Region unknown.

FUNGAL CROWN

Constantly releases toxic spores.
Destroys projectiles and increases nearby spore density.

ROT BLOSSOMS

Bloom with corrupted pollen.
Hit or destroyed, they release clouds that cause confusion, hallucinations, or control reversal.

HOLLOW HEART

Glowing core of accumulated rot.
Vulnerable when exposed during attacks or abilities.

ROOT CLUSTERS

Drag entire root systems.
Can snare players and corrupt the ground.

HUE INTERACTION

ALIGNED TO GREEN

Embodies growth without limit — a corrupted Verdant essence.

VULNERABLE TO CRIMSON

Purifying flame burns away rot and disrupts regeneration.

WEAK TO BROWN

Memory disruption weakens its will and halts growth feedback loops.

SENSITIVE TO YELLOW

Aura manipulation distorts its life-forces and spore resonance.

DESCRIPTION

The Bloomrot Titan resembles a twisted colossus of animated bark, fungal caps, and rotting wildflowers.

Its core pulses with toxic light—a hollow, glowing knot where its heart might’ve been.

It moves slowly but with explosive force, uprooting itself to crash down with trunk-sized limbs.

BEHAVIOR

Spawns poisonous pollen clouds when struck—linger in air, causing hallucinations or reversed controls.

Launches explosive seed pods—each explodes into parasitic seedlings unless destroyed in time.

Occasionally “roots” itself to regenerate—vulnerable but emits thick fungal fog.

LORE ENTRY

Field Record — “Rot in the Rootlands”
by Huewarden Lilael

“Verdant magic was never supposed to be this... greedy.
It heals, yes — but healing without judgment becomes infestation.

The Titan wasn’t born.
It accumulated.

Dead treants, overgrown shrines, corrupted spores, blood-fed vines — all swallowed into the same groaning body.

It threw pods that screamed when they landed.
And its chest.... Gods, its chest was hollow, glowing green, as if the thing was ashamed of the rot it wore like bark.

I cast a growth-stall glyph.
It laughed.

The sound came from its blossoms.
From its moss. From inside me.

We left that grove.
Burned what we could.

What we couldn’t still whispers in the roots.”

ANATOMY & ATTACK PATTERNS

SPORE VENTS

Tiny vents along its back release spores continuously.
More active during rage phase.

FUNGAL SHELVES

Store nutrients and spores.
Destroying several can reduce its regeneration.

HEAVY LIMBS

Trunk-like arms can be swung in wide arcs or slammed into the earth to cause shockwaves.

TOXIC TRAIL

Leaves behind corrupted ground that slows players and damages over time.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

GLOWING CORE

Its chest core is vulnerable when attacking—exposes inner rot for brief moments.

BURNING TERRAIN

Fire limits its healing capacity and prevents full regrowth during rest phases.

TRUTHROOT SALTS

Alchemical salts or powders greatly slow its regeneration and spore production.

SONIC DISRUPTION

Loud sonic pulses can rupture spore sacs and interrupt major abilities.

WHERE IT DWELLS

Overgrown ruins and lost sanctuaries.

Burial groves and corrupted gardens.

Failed Verdant shrines.

Deep within rooted caverns.

DESCRIPTION

When the Prismatic Core shattered, it tore color from beings too close to the explosion.

The Nullborn Husks are the result—creatures not dead, but unmade.

Their eyes are black wells of absence, their skin like dried ash, and their voices rasp forgotten names.

They crawl rather than walk, twitching with phantom memories of what they once were.

ANATOMY

VOID-FILLED EYES

Black wells that drink light and memory alike.
They do not blink.

ASH-DRIED SKIN

Parched and cracked, flaking like burnt bone under the slightest touch.

HUE-DRAINING CLAWS

Rake through color itself, unraveling hue-bindings on contact.

STATIC-LACED SPINE

Fractured vertebrae leak hue-static that distorts nearby reality.

HOLLOW CORE

Where a heart should be lies a void that echoes with forgotten names.

GLITCHING LIMB JOINTS

Joints jitter and slip between frames, causing erratic, stuttering motion.

ABILITIES & ATTACKS

NULL SCRATCH

Rakes forward with void-tainted claws.
Applies Hue Drain.

STATIC BURST

Releases a pulse of color-static that distorts vision and interrupts ability use.

NAME WHISPER

Whispers a forgotten name.
Targets may forget abilities or bindings.

REALITY TWITCH

Causes the world to stutter.
Creates brief glitches that displace or misalign foes.

NOTES

Some survivors hear their own voices echoed back moments before an attack.

They may still “remember” color—faint dreams and flickers linger.

Destroying the body does not silence the whisper.
The name may remain.

BEHAVIOR

Drawn to strong hues and relics.
They seem to feed on color’s essence.

Found near shattered color sites, ritual grounds, and corrupted relics.

Avoid light but are not harmed by it.

Their movements briefly glitch nearby reality.

MEMORY HUSK

THE MIRROR UNASKED

“A shell of who you were...
or might become.”

THREAT LEVEL
MODERATE

Echoed shade capable of mimicking past actions and overwhelming unprepared foes.

TYPE
Echoed Shade / Identity Wraith

FIRST EMERGED
Post-Shattering, near early alchemical fault-lines.

HUE INTERACTION

REACTS TO BOUND HUES
Adapts to the hues the player has previously bound and uses them against you.

MIMICS COMBAT RHYTHM
Copies the attack cadence and spell patterns based on your prior usage.

UNSTABLE TO CONFLICT
Inconsistent past choices cause the Husk to flicker and become erratic.

REFLECTS MEMORY
Stronger when you fight using old habits or repeat familiar sequences.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

NOVEL ACTIONS
Temporarily pacified when the player performs an action or ability they never used before.

MEMORY ECHO (EARTHEN)
Earthen Bound’s “Memory Echo” ability exposes the Husk and weakens its form.

REPLAY THE PAST
Correctly replaying a sequence of past dialogue lines or actions can unravel it.

DISRUPT RECALL
Spells that scramble memory or cause confusion break its focus and delay its attacks.

DESCRIPTION

The Memory Husk appears as a ghostly copy of the player, often wearing older gear or casting long-abandoned spells.

It inhabits places where you have previously died, failed, or made a significant choice.

It is not bound by hatred or hunger—only by the imprint of your will.
It tries to finish what you started, or what you avoided.

ANATOMY

FADING FORM
Semi-transparent shell composed of fractured memory threads.

ECHOED GEAR
Wields weapons and artifacts you once used—or wanted.

GLITCHED CORE
Flickering nucleus of remorse, doubt, and unfinished choices.

MIRRORED GAIT
Moves like you, but with slight delay or imperfect timing.

HOLLOW VOICE
Speaks in fractured lines of your past dialogue.

SHATTERED FOOTFALL
Leaves behind fragments of memory that distort terrain and visibility.

ECHOED ABILITIES

MIMIC STRIKE
Copies a recent melee combo with reduced accuracy.

ECHO CAST
Repeats a spell or ability you used in the past battle.

PHANTOM DEFLECT
Reflects projectiles or blocks using your own timing against you.

MEMORY SHARD
Summons fading copies of you to confuse and zone.

RECALL STEP
Teleports to a location where you previously died or rested.

NOTES

Some Memory Husks may retain knowledge of secrets or paths you once uncovered.

They cannot gain new information—only what you have already lived.

Rarely, a Husk will kneel instead of attack—as if waiting for you to forgive it.

When defeated, it disperses into whispers that sound like your own thoughts.

BEHAVIOR

MIMICS YOUR HISTORY
Spawns in areas tied to your past—especially where you failed, died, or made a major choice.

STUDIES BEFORE IT STRIKES
Observes your first few moves to mimic your style and timing.

ADAPTS MID-FIGHT
Changes attack patterns if you switch weapons, equip new gear, or change tactics.

DISTORTS MEMORY
Utters fragments of your old dialogue or dreams, attempting to shake your focus.

FADES OR FLOURISHES
Weakens if ignored or confused.
Grows stronger the more you hesitate.

POSSIBLE DROPS

FADED SELF (COMMON)
A ghostly imprint of gear you once wore.
Non-functional, but can be used for lore or crafting.

MEMORY FRAGMENT (RARE)
Shard containing echoes of your past choices.
Used to unlock hidden dialogue or alternate resolutions.

HUSKED CORE (RARE)
Essence core that stores residual versions of player abilities.
Used in experimental infusions.

UNWRITTEN CHOICE (LEGENDARY)
A rare scroll that reveals a path or option you once ignored.
May open secret questlines or endings.

HUEFRACTALS

FRAGMENTS OF FORGOTTEN COLOR

“They don’t attack.
They collide with the possibility of you.”

THREAT LEVEL

VARIABLE

Safe when still, catastrophic when agitated.

TYPE

Prismatic Fragment / Living Hue Relic

FIRST EMERGED

Post-Core Collapse — Nullshade Genesis

HUE INTERACTION

REACTS TO HUE MAGIC

React violently to concentrated hue magic—especially if multiple hues are active.

CARRIES HUE ECHOS

Each fragment holds an unstable echo of a specific hue.
When struck, it may cast random low-tier spell effects of that hue.

HUE CASCADE

When three or more collide, they create a Hue Cascade—a zone-wide chromatic distortion that disables all hue-bound abilities temporarily.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

SILENCE STABILIZES

Complete silence—no music, no abilities, no movement—stabilizes or stills them.

PRISM GLYPH ANCHORS

Can be grounded by Prism Glyph Anchors or Nullpulse fields, created via Nullshade puzzle completion.

FRAGILE BY DESIGN

Their unstable form shatters under precise force or vibration resonance.

DESCRIPTION

Small, floating crystal shards, each glowing with unstable prismatic flickers.

They rotate in recursive motion patterns and emit soft harmonic tones, sometimes syncing with the player’s hue state.

In groups, they create harmonic interference that destabilizes the environment—breaking light physics and bending geometry.

ANATOMY

FRACTAL CORE

Unstable energy knot.
Contains the hue echo memory.

PRISM SHELL

Crystalline lattice that refracts and amplifies light and magic.

HARMONIC EDGE

Emits tonal frequencies that interact with hue and space.

COLOR ECHO

Residual hue memory that drives behavior and spell response.

SHARD SPINES

Micro-prisms that tear at hue fields and scatter light.

NULLSHADE DUST

Tiny particles shed constantly.
Can interfere with magic and equipment.

COMBAT INTERACTIONS

PROVOKED

Struck by hue magic or area effects.
Becomes volatile.

HUE CASCADE

Three or more collide.
Triggers chromatic overload.

OVERLOADED

Player abilities gray out.
HUD flickers and distorts.

STABILIZED

Silenced or anchored.
Fragments become still and inert.

NOTES

Huefractals are not bound to the realm.
They drift where color falters or bleeds.

Some Archivists claim they are remnants of the Seventh Hue.

Resonating with one long enough without combat may trigger a memory scene—showing the moment the Prism broke, from a non-human perspective.

LORE ENTRY

Fragment from “The Spectrum that Broke” — Scrawlings in Nullshade Dust.

“They were once part of something sacred.
The Core sang with their chorus.”

“But when the Prism cracked, the notes scattered and forgot the tune.”

“Some tried to collect them.
None returned whole.”

“Not dead—just... chromatically incorrect.”

“One danced near me once.
It laughed in teal.”

“Then wept in a color I didn’t have a word for.”

“They want to return.
But they don’t remember where they began.”

“So they collide with anything that might have been their origin.”

BEHAVIOR

PASSIVE BY NATURE

Not aggressive unless provoked by hue alignment, spells, or AoE proximity.

RESONANCE SEEKER

May follow the player briefly, attempting to “resonate”—which can overload equipment or inflict hue blur.

HARMONIC FLOCKING

Drift in recursive patterns.
In swarms, create harmonic interference that distorts light and geometry.

LOST MEMORY

In rare Nullshade time loops, may align and “sing” a lost color, triggering a puzzle glyph sequence.

POSSIBLE DROPS

FRACTAL RESIDUE (COMMON)

Crystalline dust humming with residual hue.
Used in crafting.

UNSTABLE HUE CORE (RARE)

A volatile core that stores a random hue echo.
Handle with care.

PRISM SHARD KEY (RARE)

A shaped fragment that fits ancient prism locks and warded barriers.

SEVENTH HARMONIC (LEGENDARY)

A perfectly resonant shard that reveals hidden glyphs and forgotten color truths.

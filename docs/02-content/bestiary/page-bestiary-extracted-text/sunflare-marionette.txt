SUNFLARE MARIONETTE

THE DANCE OF SCORCHING STRINGS

Delicate limbs, deadly threads.

“Dances on beams.
Cracks like porcelain.”

CLASSIFICATION

Type:
Construct / Solar Puppet

Threat Level:
Moderate–High in bright open spaces

First Emerged:
Believed to have been a ceremonial guardian of the Aurelion Courts animated by forgotten rites of sun devotion.

Region:
Luxeran / The Gilded Expanse

Aurelion Courts, solar temples, desert court architecture, radiant open plazas, mirrors, sandstone, gold, ivory, sunlit haze.

ANATOMY BREAKDOWN

1. PORCELAIN FACE / MASK

Serene, expressionless, cracked with heat.

2. GOLDEN-GLASS LIMBS

Hollow limbs reinforced with radiant tracery.

3. SUN-THREAD FILAMENTS

Burning puppet strings of focused sunlight.

4. SUNBURST JOINTS

Bright pivots that flash during movement.

5. RIBBON-LASH ARMS

Slicing solar ribbons used to strike targets.

6. MIRRORSTEP ANKLES

Allow reflection-based teleportation.

7. SOLAR CORE

Radiant inner glow that powers the dance.

DESCRIPTION

A glasslike humanoid figure suspended by searing filaments of sunlight.

Limbs are hollow porcelain, reinforced with gold tracery.

Its joints flicker with sunbursts as it pirouettes across battlefield mirages.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Appears in bright open spaces, Aurelion Courts, sun temples, reflective courtyards, desert shrines, mirror plazas, and exposed ceremonial arenas.

Drawn to reflective surfaces and direct sunlight.

Weakened in shade or cloud.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

SUNLINE STEPS

Marks healing zones under solar focus and dances between them.

RIBBON-LASH ATTACKS

Uses ribbon-lash arms to slice and sever targets.

SOLAR BLOOM — 50% HEALTH

Enters Solar Bloom, emitting flares that detonate if not dodged in rhythm with her gestures.

MIRRORSTEP

Can teleport via reflected surfaces during battle.

ABILITIES / STATUS EFFECTS

SUNLINE STEPS

Creates solar nodes that pulse healing to the Marionette.

RIBBON-LASH

Long-range slashes that inflict Bleed and Scorch.

SOLAR BLOOM

Emits explosive flares that build Scorch stacks.

MIRRORSTEP

Teleports between reflections and leaves an afterimage.

HEALING WAVE

Releases a radial wave that converts incoming Yellow damage into self-healing.

HUE INTERACTION

Aurelian / Yellow spells energize it.

Damaging it with Yellow hue causes it to emit healing waves for itself.

Disrupted by Violet illusions and smoke- or sand-based concealment that break beam threads.

Weakens in heavy cloud or artificial shade.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Use shadow-creating terrain or artifacts to sever solar tethers.

Cobalt’s Mind Echo or illusions can stagger it mid-spin.

Highly vulnerable when caught without light for more than 10 seconds.

LORE ENTRY — RECOVERED FROM AN AURELION COURT ARCHIVE

“They said the dancer was too beautiful to cast a shadow.

So the sun made one —
and gave it strings.

Her dance was a prayer.
Her silence, a punishment.

Now she dances alone,
untethered and unwatched.

But the beams remember —
and burn those who turn away.”

NOTES

Ancient performance scrolls suggest the Marionette was once a celebrant, not a killer.

Some dancers in Eburnea still perform her final dance at dusk, praying to blind the sun just long enough to forget their debts.

“If you step into the spotlight…
be sure you’re ready to bow.”

LUXERAN CONTEXT

The Aurelion Courts revere the sun as law and witness.

To dance in its light is honor —
to be judged by it is to be measured against perfection.

HUNTER’S NOTE

Break her rhythm.
Take the light.

Make her dance in darkness.

She can be shattered, but only when she stops believing the sun is hers.

SHADEBREAK LANTERN

A Luxeran device that projects a field of deep shade, severing sun-threads within its radius.

Essential for hunters who face the sun’s own puppets.

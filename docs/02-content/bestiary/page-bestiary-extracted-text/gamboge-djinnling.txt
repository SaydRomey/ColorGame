GAMBOGE DJINNLING

THE BARGAIN’S BREATH

“A wish, half-spoken.
A trick, fully paid.”

THREAT LEVEL

VARIABLE

Harmless or catastrophic.

TYPE

Elemental Trickster / Smoke Spirit

FIRST EMERGED

During the Sun-Flecked Famine, post-Shattering.

HUE INTERACTION

DRAWN TO AURELIAN

Attracted to Aurelian-bound players.
Reacts to radiant spells with playful mimicry.

SOOTHED BY ORANGE

Temporarily soothed by Orange Peel Charms or trickster-aligned dialogue.

DISRUPTED BY SILENCE

Disrupted by silence, darkness, or logical riddles.

DESCRIPTION

Gamboge Djinnlings are child-sized spirits formed from residual incense and holy lacquer.

They shimmer like sunlight through amber resin and float unpredictably, giggling in short bursts.

Upon contact, they inflict randomized magical effects — buffs, debuffs, or surreal transformations.

BEHAVIOR

MAY OFFER “BARGAINS”

Usually with ironic outcomes.

CAST CHAOTIC AURA BURSTS

Altering spell loadouts or HP temporarily.

MAY AID PLAYERS

In combat if amused or impressed.

FLEES WHEN TOO PREDICTABLE

Thrives on spontaneity and unpredictability.

LORE ENTRY

Fragment from “Desert Pacts and Ash-Laced Jars” by Relic-Seer Yawen the Uncorked.

“You don’t summon a Gamboge Djinnling.
You entertain one.

And if it likes your song, your scent, or your story — it offers a deal.
Usually bad.”

“One drifted near my caravan during the brass eclipse.

My horse learned to speak three tongues.
My guide forgot how to walk forward.”

“Never thank it.
Never bargain without irony.

And never uncork anything that still feels warm.”

“They’re children of the smoke left behind when gods abandon their lamps.”

ADDITIONAL DETAILS

May mimic a player’s voice or the appearance of a loved one.

Rarely binds to objects — enchanted lamps, jars, or lacquered scroll cases.

Bargains can escalate.
A “small favor” today may become a life-altering debt tomorrow.

Leaves behind golden soot when offended.
It can be refined into volatile powder.

Loves riddles, puns, and unexpected praise.
Hates boredom and obedience.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

EMOTIONAL CLARITY

Vulnerable to emotional clarity, including truth-bound spells and honesty artifacts.

CANNOT PASS

Cannot pass through pure darkness or geometric constraint sigils.

CITRUS & HARMONY

Temporarily dissipated by citrus oils or harmonized chants.

NOTES

Some scholars claim Djinnlings are fractured pieces of ancient wish-gods, forever seeking new vessels for chaos.

Local traders treat them with a mix of reverence and exasperation.

“If it floats and giggles in yellow — bow, lie, or run.
Your choice matters less than how funny it is.”

POSSIBLE DROPS

AMBER WISP CORE (RARE)

Stores volatile wish-energy.
Used in unpredictable enchantments.

LAMPLIGHT ASH

Used to craft incense that reveals hidden bargains or illusions.

DJINNLING’S CHARM

Grants a random effect once per day.
Delightful. Dangerous.

UNSPOKEN WISH (VERY RARE)

A bound wish not yet twisted by the Djinnling.
Use with extreme caution.

“A WISH IS A SEED.
THE DJINNLING IS THE WIND.

YOU DECIDE WHOM YOU TRUST.”

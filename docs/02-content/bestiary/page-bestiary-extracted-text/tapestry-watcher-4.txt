TAPESTRY WATCHER

THE LOOMBOUND WITNESS

It stares at the wall where you betrayed yourself.

“Regret has fingers.
And it’s still stitching.”

CLASSIFICATION

Type:
Memory-Warped Construct

Threat Level:
High — Paralyzes via Past Decisions

First Emerged:
Post-Shattering, during the First Silent Weave

Role Tags:
Truth-Gated / Memory Bind / Decision Trap

ANATOMY BREAKDOWN

1. LOOMBOUND FRAME

Pinned eternally to a suspended loom.

The body barely moves, but the weave does.

2. SHROUDED FACE

It does not show a face;
only the pressure of choices behind the veil.

3. DECISION THREADS

Each glowing filament carries a line once chosen, refused, or left unspoken.

4. NARRATIVE BIND

Its attacks trap the victim in false memory rather than physical restraint.

5. BONE-INK STITCHWORK

Regret and confession are written into the weave like surgical seams.

6. FALSE MEMORY WEB

Contradictory choices tighten the loom’s aggression.

7. UNRAVELING NAME

Some travelers report seeing their own name stitched into fresh thread before the fight.

DESCRIPTION

A tall, hooded figure nailed to a suspended loom of tattered, glowing thread.

It does not move —
but the threads do.

Each glows with a line of dialogue the player once chose, or failed to choose.

The Tapestry Watcher sees not who you are, but who you failed to be.

Its threads do not bind the body first;
they bind the narrative.

HABITAT / MANIFESTATION

Appears in chambers where old decisions linger:
thread archives, mourning houses, betrayal rooms, abandoned looms, and halls of residue memory.

The tapestry reacts before the Watcher does.

BEHAVIOR / STATE FLOW

OBSERVE → REPLAY → BAIT → BIND

Hurls threads of past dialogue as attacks.

Successful hits paralyze the player inside a false memory.

Becomes more aggressive if the player’s prior choices contradict one another.

Uses illusion-laced thread to force reliving the worst decision.

ABILITIES / STATUS EFFECTS

DIALOGUE LASH

Throws sharpened strands of chosen or unchosen speech.

FALSE MEMORY PIN

Paralyzes the target inside an altered recollection.

CONTRADICTION SURGE

Escalates when the victim’s narrative history is inconsistent.

NARRATIVE BIND

Thread-webs suppress motion, recall, and certainty.

HUE INTERACTION

Violet-bound illusions cause the loom to replay alternate timelines.

Brown-bound memory echoes let players restitch a prior moment for defense or offense.

Yellow or truth-based spells cut through paralyzing thread-webs.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

Confession-based interactions nullify its binding attacks.

Artifacts like the Isabelline Tapestry allow thread prediction and reflection.

Blindsided if approached without any unfulfilled dialogue threads.

Certainty resists the loom;
truth cuts thread.

LORE ENTRY — MARGIN SCRAP FROM THE ISABELLINE WIDOW’S THREADBOOK, WRITTEN IN BONE INK

“There’s a chamber where decisions breathe.

And the Watcher keeps them warm.”

“It weaves from forgotten no’s, unspoken truths, and the moments we smiled when we shouldn’t have.”

“One pilgrim entered with pride.

Came out without their name.

The loom didn’t approve.”

“If you face it, bring only one thing:
certainty.

It cannot stitch what is whole.”

NOTES

Defeating the Watcher does not destroy the tapestry —
only moves your place within it.

HUNTER’S NOTE

If your name appears in fresh thread, do not read it aloud.

ISABELLINE TAPESTRY

Reveals deceit and reflects thread-bound attacks.

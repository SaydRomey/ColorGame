CHRONOPOLLEN SPRITE

THE BLOOM BETWEEN SECONDS

“It blooms a second too early.
Or a century too late.”

THREAT LEVEL

MODERATE

Timing disruption and sequence instability

TYPE

Temporal Fey / Pollen-Warp Trickster

FIRST EMERGED

Post-Huefall, along Veilgrove Spiral and the outer gardens of Liseran Shrine.

HUE INTERACTION

COBALT / MIND ECHO

Reveals the Sprite’s next time-slip and marks delayed actions.

VIOLET / ILLUSION CLONE

Draws pollen toward the false target.

AURELIAN / RADIANT FLASH

Burns away loose motes, but may trigger a wider Chronosneeze.

VERDANT / ROOT BIND

Anchors local time inside a rooted radius.

THULIAN MIRROR

Reveals its true second.

DESCRIPTION

Tiny glowing time-fey born from flowers that bloomed before their own seeds.

Chronopollen Sprites are made of petal-light, violet-blue wing shimmer, and drifting pollen motes that pulse like faulty clockticks.

Their pollen loosens the stitches between intention, action, and consequence, making dodges, spells, and footsteps resolve at the wrong moment.

They rarely attack with intent.
They flutter, sneeze, panic, and laugh in the wrong order.

LORE ENTRY

From the Pollen-Caught Notes of the Bloomfold Archivist.

“I blinked, and the flower had already wilted.
I blinked again, and it was only a seed.”

“The Sprites are not cruel.
Cruelty requires sequence.

They hatch where Myrrala forgets which moment it is wearing.”

“Their pollen does not age you.
It slips between intention and consequence.

A hand reaches.
A door opens yesterday.”

“Do not inhale near them.
Do not chase them.

And if one giggles before you hear it arrive, leave immediately.”

— Bloomfold Archivist

BEHAVIOR

Flutters erratically above bloompaths, shrine gardens, and mirrored ponds.

Releases drifting pollen clouds that slow, hasten, or briefly reverse local movement.

Uses CHRONOSNEEZE when startled, desynchronizing dodges, projectiles, and windups.

Pauses midair and glows brighter before skipping to a nearby future position.

Circles players using Violet or Cobalt, as if deciding whether they are a flower, a mirror, or a clock.

ANATOMY

CLOCKBLOSSOM ANTENNAE

Dandelion-like blooms that shed unstable time-pollen.

PRISM WINGS

Translucent leaf-wings that shimmer violet-blue and carry time-slip eddies.

BLOOM HEART

Luminous core where sequence glitches accumulate.

ROOT-TAIL

Trailing tendrils that anchor brief rewinds to nearby terrain.

PETAL MANTLE

Soft bloom-layers that catch drifting motes.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES

POST-SNEEZE DAZE

Vulnerable when its wingbeats stop for one true second.

STASIS MESHES

Freeze both the Sprite and its pollen cloud.

ROOT OR ECHO MAGIC

Reduces the radius and duration of its time-warp field.

TRUTH-REFLECTION

Exposes its synchronized position and bypasses false afterimages.

RHYTHM TOOLS

Force its wingbeats back into normal tempo.

BEHAVIOR CYCLE

1. DRIFT

Watches and wanders.

2. POLLINATE

Spreads a timing cloud.

3. TIME-SLIP

Skips to a nearby future position.

4. CHRONOSNEEZE

Disrupts sequence in a sudden burst.

5. DAZE

Briefly vulnerable in one true second.

NOTES

Some travelers report meeting the same Sprite multiple times in different eras of the same room.

High Violet affinity makes Sprites curious rather than hostile.

High Cobalt affinity makes their patterns easier to read.

Where time forgets to move, step backward into now.

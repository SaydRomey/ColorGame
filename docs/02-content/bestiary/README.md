
# Bestiary Reference

## Overview

This document is the canonical bestiary reference for the Shattered Realms.  
It consolidates the regional enemy rosters, systemic encounter language, 
artifact-linked counterplay, and endgame terminology into one structure.

The creatures of this world are not random fauna. 
They are products of color-law, memory pressure, ritual failure, 
environmental corruption, and metaphysical fracture. 

Every region produces enemies according to its own logic:

- [Huevale][huevale] grows ambushers, tricksters, and living archive predators.
- [Vireya][vireya] produces war echoes, oath-bound revenants, and ritualized violence.
- [Iriseld][iriseld] creates storm-memory horrors, traversal punishers, and reflective hunters.
- [Myrrala][myrrala] blooms glamour predators, mimic selves, and identity distorters.
- [Luxeran][luxeran] shapes mirage hunters, radiant constructs, and motive-reading predators.
- [Auralith][auralith] calcifies memory into trial specters, residue parasites, and unfinished dead.
- [Nullshade][nullshade] manifests contradiction, anti-coherence, reflections, and the Wound’s immune responses.
- [Unbound][unbound] entities move across regions when history, hue instability, or narrative triggers override local law.

# Regional Bestiary

## 🟢 [Huevale][huevale] — The Verdant Frontier

A realm where nature overgrows memory, where alchemy breathes through every bloom, 
and where the line between life and illusion blurs beneath moss, pollen, and sap.  
[Huevale][huevale]’s enemies are playful, predatory, toxic, and strangely merciful. 
They are living systems gone cunning.

### Huevale Enemy Themes

- Predatory beauty
- Memory pollen and cognitive disruption
- Root ambush and movement denial
- Archive guardianship
- Illusion as natural lure
- Decay reborn through growth

---

## 🔴 [Vireya][vireya] — The Ember Expanse

A land of blazing ruin, war-memory, and bloodbound ritual.  
[Vireya][vireya]’s enemies are forged from heat, oath, and unresolved conflict. 
They fight like ceremonies.

### Vireya Enemy Themes

- Ritualized violence
- Battlefield memory
- Heat and explosion chains
- Symbolic objects as weak points
- Sacrifice as power source
- Dueling as identity

---

## 🔵 [Iriseld][iriseld] — The Tidespire Reach

A region of storm-memory, aerial grief, and reflective danger.  
[Iriseld][iriseld] enemies punish loss of orientation and reward composure.

### Iriseld Enemy Themes

- Air and ledge denial
- Storm-triggered aggression
- Memory-water and charge drain
- Reflections and delayed movement
- Weather-linked traversal hostility
- Grief as distortion

---

## 🟣 [Myrrala][myrrala] — The Blooming Veil

A dream-saturated region of glamour, false authorship, and selfhood under pressure.  
[Myrrala][myrrala] enemies are intimate, playful, and invasive.

### Myrrala Enemy Themes

- Identity mimicry
- Dream logic and emotional coercion
- Rhythm and performance combat
- UI unreliability
- Vow and dialogue memory
- Terrain rewriting through glamour

---

## 🟡 [Luxeran][luxeran] — The Gilded Expanse

A land of mirage, heat, judgment, and brilliance sharpened into danger. 
 [Luxeran][luxeran] enemies prey on certainty, appetite, and poor reading.

### Luxeran Enemy Themes

- Visibility as hazard
- Mirage and false legitimacy
- Light-reflection combat
- Heat and shadow logic
- Artifact mimicry
- Brilliance with a cost

---

## 🟤 [Auralith][auralith] — The Dimming Hollow

A region of ash, residue, failed preservation, and truth trapped in remains.  
[Auralith][auralith] enemies are heavy with moral and mnemonic pressure.

### Auralith Enemy Themes

- Residue and unfinished dead
- Past-self hostility
- Fear and confession pressure
- Soul-binding and alchemical instability
- Witness logic
- Judgment rather than predation

---

## ⚫ [Nullshade][nullshade] — The Colorless Core

[Nullshade][nullshade] enemies are not “monsters” in the ordinary sense. 
They are reflections, contradictions, remnants, and immune responses of the Wound.

### Nullshade Enemy Themes

- Anti-coherence
- Self-recognition as danger
- Nonviolent or truth-based resolution
- Silence and UI blanking
- Reflection and contradiction
- “The Wound fights back”

---

## ⚪ [Unbound][unbound] / Cross-Regional Entities

These enemies appear under narrative triggers, leak conditions, relic events, 
or special ecology overlap. 
They reinforce the project’s larger Prism / memory / [Nullshade][nullshade] metaplot.

### Unbound Enemy Themes

- Instability beyond local biome law
- Rare or event-based anomalies
- Hue drain and rewrite pressure
- Cross-regional storytelling
- Punishment for historical or narrative failure states

# World Structure

The overall structure is a shattered realm organized around the Prismatic Core’s crater, 
with six hue-aligned outer regions and [Nullshade][nullshade] at the center 
as both a literal endgame location and a metaphysical wound.

## Intended Regional Loop

Each zone has its own:

- City
- Hazards
- Traversal logic
- Artifact hooks
- Prism Gate relationship to the Core

## High-Level Regional Design

- [Huevale][huevale] = living growth, archives, adaptive vegetation.
- [Vireya][vireya] = war memory, heat, blood, battlefield ritual.
- [Iriseld][iriseld] = sky/storm grief, sound, reflection, floating geometry.
- [Myrrala][myrrala] = identity distortion, dream logic, glamour.
- [Luxeran][luxeran] = radiance, mirage, persuasion, trade, judgment.
- [Auralith][auralith] = residue, ruin, soul-binding, fossil memory.
- [Nullshade][nullshade] = absence, erased truth, contradiction, return.

---

# Map Concept

The map concept is simple and effective: 
six outer regions arranged around a central [Nullshade][nullshade] crater, 
with each zone expressing a dominant color-family and gameplay mood.

The land itself was shaped by the shattering of the Prismatic Core, 
with shards embedding into the world and creating region-specific metaphysics, 
hazards, and cultures.

## Regional Metaphysical Rules

Each region has a distinct metaphysical rule:

- [Huevale][huevale]: vegetation regrows and reacts.
- [Vireya][vireya]: heat, blood, and battle awaken dormant forces.
- [Iriseld][iriseld]: wind and gravity alter access; memory rides weather.
- [Myrrala][myrrala]: loadout changes the landscape.
- [Luxeran][luxeran]: mirages falsify the map.
- [Auralith][auralith]: player action leaves ghosts behind.
- [Nullshade][nullshade]: truth is spatially and mnemonically sealed.

---

# Center-Periphery Model

Having six saturated outer regions around a desaturated core 
is meant to communicates the project’s core conflict symbolically and spatially.

> Color radiates outward from a broken center.



## Misc Ideas

### The Chromophobic Choir

A rare grouped encounter made of eleven distinct figures. 
Each member has been stripped of one part of the spectrum and is known only by their corresponding chromophobia.

| Member            | Rejects      | Possible identity / mechanic                               |
| ----------------- | ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Prasinophobe**  | Green        | Withers vegetation and cancels Verdant effects             |
| **Erythrophobe**  | Red / Maroon | Extinguishes rage, fire, blood buffs, then reflects them   |
| **Cyanophobe**    | Blue         | Silences storms, water and resonance abilities             |
| **Porphyrophobe** | Purple       | Breaks illusions and dream effects                         |
| **Xanthophobe**   | Yellow       | Creates zones where light cannot persist                   |
| **Chrysophobe**   | Orange       | Feeds on heat and removes vitality effects                 |
| **Rhodophobe**    | Pink         | Suppresses charm, glamour and emotional effects            |
| **Kastanophobe**  | Brown        | Makes earth, bone and memory structures crumble            |
| **Glaucophobe**   | Grey         | Panics when the world becomes achromatic                   |
| **Leukophobe**    | White        | Fears pure light; hides inside shadows/reflections         |
| **Melanophobe**   | Black        | Cannot tolerate darkness; desperately generates pale light |

The important mechanic could be that their feared color 
is simultaneously their greatest weakness and what makes them dangerous.

For example, exposing the Prasinophobe to Verdant energy causes immediate stagger, 
but afterward it enters a violent panic state. 

That makes the "phobia" meaningful rather than simply functioning as an elemental weakness.

We could give them a shared visual rule: 
every member is almost completely colorless except for one forbidden color 
appearing somewhere they cannot remove—a crack in the mask, 
an exposed eye, a thread through the chest, a stain on the hands. 

The color they fear is literally trapped inside them.

That has a strong connection to Nullshade.

#### Even better: make them one enemy

Instead of eleven ordinary enemies, The Chromophobic Choir could be one encounter composed of several bodies.

> Eleven voices. Eleven absences.  
> None remember what color looked like.

They appear as a semicircle of masked figures.  
Different members awaken according to what the player does.

Use green → Prasinophobe wakes.
Use red → Erythrophobe wakes.
Use purple → Porphyrophobe wakes.

This means the player effectively constructs the boss fight through their own hue choices.

Eventually several are active simultaneously, forcing the player to think about their entire palette rather than relying on one affinity.

That would make the group mechanically distinct from almost everything in the current roster.

Another possibility: "The Eleven"

I actually prefer not calling the organization itself Chromophobic. 
Give them a mysterious collective title, while the individual members have the technical names.

The Eleven

The Chrysophobe

Characters could initially refer to them only as The Eleven, and the player gradually learns what those strange individual titles mean.

This feels particularly appropriate for your world because the terminology becomes lore rather than merely a vocabulary gimmick.

They could also be former people

There is an interesting narrative angle if they were deliberately conditioned to reject color.

Perhaps Nullshade did not remove their hues completely. Instead, each individual survived the loss of every hue except one—and came to regard that surviving color as something horrifying.

That creates an inversion:

the Prasinophobe was once Verdant-bound;
the Erythrophobe was once Crimson-bound;
the Cyanophobe was once Cobalt-bound;
the Porphyrophobe was once Violet-bound;
the Xanthophobe was once Golden-bound;
the Kastanophobe was once Earthen-bound.

Those six could be the core members corresponding directly to your six affinities.

The remaining five—Chrysophobe, Rhodophobe, Glaucophobe, Leukophobe, Melanophobe—could represent colors outside or between the established system.

That distinction could matter enormously.

You essentially get:

The Six — fears corresponding to the accepted hue system.
The Five — fears of colors the Prism does not formally recognize.

That could tie very neatly into The Seventh Hue and the possibility that the Prism's color taxonomy is incomplete.

Or use Chromophobia as an affliction

I would also consider making Chromophobia a gameplay condition rather than using every term as an enemy.

Certain Nullshade enemies could inflict:

Prasinophobia
Erythrophobia
Cyanophobia
etc.

If afflicted with Prasinophobia, using or approaching Verdant effects causes distortion, panic, control interference, or ability instability.

Then a special enemy such as a Chromophobe or Phobia Grafter could dynamically impose a fear based on the player's most-used hue.

That would fit enemies like your Memory Grafter particularly well.

My preferred hierarchy

I would combine the ideas:

The Eleven
A unique secret faction/collective.

The Chromophobic Choir
The name of their grouped encounter.

The Prasinophobe, Erythrophobe, Cyanophobe, etc.
Their individual identities.

Chromophobia
The gameplay status they can inflict.

And importantly, I would not rename your existing Unbound roster around these terms. Hue Leeches, Huefractals, Tyrian Echo, The Unhued Choir, etc. already occupy a different conceptual space.

This would give the chromophobia terminology its own coherent place in the world instead of scattering the names across otherwise unrelated monsters.

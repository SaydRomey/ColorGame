# The Eleven — Chromophobia Enemy Concept

A unique Nullshade / Unbound concept built around the real names of color phobias.

Rather than using chromophobia terms as ordinary enemy names, the strongest approach is to treat them as a deliberate naming system belonging to something organized, ritualistic, and fundamentally opposed to hue.

---

# Core Concept

## The Eleven

A rare group whose members are identified by the color they reject.

Characters may know them collectively only as **The Eleven**, while the individual members carry the technical chromophobia titles.

- The Prasinophobe
- The Erythrophobe
- The Cyanophobe
- The Porphyrophobe
- The Xanthophobe
- The Chrysophobe
- The Rhodophobe
- The Kastanophobe
- The Glaucophobe
- The Leukophobe
- The Melanophobe

The unusual titles can become part of the lore: the player gradually learns what each one means and why each member fears a specific color.

---

# The Chromophobic Choir

**The Chromophobic Choir** can be the name of their grouped encounter.

> Eleven voices. Eleven absences.  
> None remember what color looked like.

The encounter consists of multiple masked, nearly colorless figures arranged like a choir.

Different members awaken depending on which hues or color effects the player uses.

Examples:

- Use green → **Prasinophobe** awakens.
- Use red → **Erythrophobe** awakens.
- Use purple → **Porphyrophobe** awakens.
- Use yellow → **Xanthophobe** awakens.
- Use blue → **Cyanophobe** awakens.

The player effectively builds the fight through their own hue choices.

As the encounter progresses, several members may become active simultaneously, forcing the player to think about their entire palette instead of relying on one affinity.

# Members and Mechanics

| Member | Rejects | Possible Identity / Mechanic |
|---|---|---|
| **Prasinophobe** | Green | Withers vegetation and cancels Verdant effects |
| **Erythrophobe** | Red / Maroon | Extinguishes rage, fire, and blood buffs, then reflects them |
| **Cyanophobe** | Blue | Silences storms, water, and resonance abilities |
| **Porphyrophobe** | Purple | Breaks illusions and dream effects |
| **Xanthophobe** | Yellow | Creates zones where light cannot persist |
| **Chrysophobe** | Orange | Feeds on heat and removes vitality effects |
| **Rhodophobe** | Pink | Suppresses charm, glamour, and emotional effects |
| **Kastanophobe** | Brown | Makes earth, bone, and memory structures crumble |
| **Glaucophobe** | Grey | Panics when the world becomes achromatic |
| **Leukophobe** | White | Fears pure light; hides inside shadows and reflections |
| **Melanophobe** | Black | Cannot tolerate darkness; desperately generates pale light |

# Fear as Weakness and Danger

The feared color should not function only as a simple elemental weakness.

Instead, exposure to the feared color can produce two effects:

1. Immediate vulnerability or stagger.
2. A violent panic response that changes the enemy’s behavior.

For example:

- Exposing the **Prasinophobe** to Verdant energy causes immediate stagger.
- Afterward, it enters a panic state and becomes faster, less predictable, or more destructive.

This makes the phobia mechanically meaningful rather than reducing it to a basic weakness chart.

---

# Shared Visual Rule

Every member is almost completely colorless except for one forbidden color appearing somewhere they cannot remove.

Possible manifestations:

- a crack in the mask;
- an exposed eye;
- a thread through the chest;
- a stain on the hands;
- a glowing scar;
- a colored tooth;
- a strand of hair;
- a shard embedded in the body;
- a colored reflection visible only in mirrors.

The color they fear is literally trapped inside them.

This creates a strong connection to Nullshade.

---

# The Six and The Five

The Eleven can be divided into two internal groups.

## The Six

These correspond directly to the six established hue families:

- **Prasinophobe** → Green / Verdant
- **Erythrophobe** → Red / Crimson
- **Cyanophobe** → Blue / Cobalt
- **Porphyrophobe** → Purple / Violet
- **Xanthophobe** → Yellow / Golden
- **Kastanophobe** → Brown / Earthen

These six can be former people who were once bound to the very hue they now fear.

For example:

- the Prasinophobe was once Verdant-bound;
- the Erythrophobe was once Crimson-bound;
- the Cyanophobe was once Cobalt-bound;
- the Porphyrophobe was once Violet-bound;
- the Xanthophobe was once Golden-bound;
- the Kastanophobe was once Earthen-bound.

Perhaps Nullshade did not completely remove their hues.

Instead, each individual survived the loss of every hue except one and came to regard that surviving color as something horrifying.

---

## The Five

These correspond to colors outside or between the six established hue families:

- **Chrysophobe** → Orange
- **Rhodophobe** → Pink
- **Glaucophobe** → Grey
- **Leukophobe** → White
- **Melanophobe** → Black

These five can represent colors the Prism does not formally recognize as primary affinities.

That distinction may have larger narrative implications.

The Six represent the accepted hue system.

The Five represent colors that exist outside that classification.

This can connect naturally to:

- **The Seventh Hue**
- incomplete or artificial Prism taxonomy
- the possibility that the established six-hue system is not the whole truth

# Chromophobia as an Affliction

Chromophobia can also exist as a gameplay condition rather than only as a faction concept.

Possible status effects:

- `Prasinophobia`
- `Erythrophobia`
- `Cyanophobia`
- `Porphyrophobia`
- `Xanthophobia`
- `Chrysophobia`
- `Rhodophobia`
- `Kastanophobia`
- `Glaucophobia`
- `Leukophobia`
- `Melanophobia`

Example:

If the player is afflicted with **Prasinophobia**, approaching or using Verdant effects may cause:

- visual distortion;
- panic;
- control interference;
- ability instability;
- temporary input disruption;
- accidental rejection of Verdant-bound abilities.

This makes color itself temporarily hostile to the player.

---

# Phobia-Grafting Enemies

A special enemy could impose chromophobia dynamically.

Possible concepts:

- **Phobia Grafter**
- **Chromophobia Grafter**
- **Fearweaver**
- **Hue Aversionist**
- **Chromatic Confessor**

This enemy could detect the player’s most-used hue and impose the corresponding phobia.

Example:

- Player heavily favors Verdant → inflicts **Prasinophobia**
- Player heavily favors Crimson → inflicts **Erythrophobia**
- Player heavily favors Violet → inflicts **Porphyrophobia**

This concept could also connect naturally to the existing **Memory Grafter** archetype.

# Suggested Hierarchy

A clean terminology hierarchy:

## The Eleven

The faction / secret collective.

## The Chromophobic Choir

The major grouped encounter involving several or all members.

## The Prasinophobe, Erythrophobe, Cyanophobe, etc.

The individual members.

## Chromophobia

The general condition / phenomenon.

## Prasinophobia, Erythrophobia, etc.

Specific gameplay status effects.

---

# Narrative Possibilities

The Eleven can work particularly well if they are not presented as ordinary monsters.

Possible interpretations:

- failed hue-bound survivors;
- prisoners altered by Nullshade;
- former members of the six Orders;
- people who intentionally renounced color;
- ritual victims designed to purge hue;
- living evidence that the Prism’s classification system is incomplete;
- remnants of an earlier color system;
- witnesses to a forbidden hue;
- failed attempts to create colorless humans.

Their hostility does not need to come from hatred of color.

It can come from fear.

They may genuinely believe color is dangerous.

Some could panic when exposed to their feared hue instead of attacking immediately.

Others may try to erase the color from the environment before confronting the player.

---

# Relationship to Existing Unbound Enemies

The chromophobia concept should remain separate from the normal Unbound naming system.

Existing names such as these already occupy a different conceptual space:

- Hue Leeches
- Huefractals
- Tyrian Echo
- Spectral Harvester
- The Unhued Choir
- Nullborn Husks

They concern color, hue-loss, memory, or Nullshade directly.

The Eleven should instead feel like a specific faction or anomaly with a coherent internal naming rule.

This prevents the chromophobia terminology from becoming scattered across otherwise unrelated monsters.

---

# Strongest Version

The most cohesive implementation is:

**The Eleven**  
A secret group connected to Nullshade.

**The Chromophobic Choir**  
A rare multi-entity encounter.

**The Prasinophobe, Erythrophobe, Cyanophobe, Porphyrophobe, Xanthophobe, Chrysophobe, Rhodophobe, Kastanophobe, Glaucophobe, Leukophobe, and Melanophobe**  
The individual members.

**Chromophobia**  
A gameplay condition they can inflict.

**The Six / The Five**  
An internal division that mirrors the accepted six-hue system against five colors outside it.

This gives the chromophobia terminology a distinct mechanical, narrative, and visual identity while also creating a natural thematic bridge toward **The Seventh Hue** and questions about whether the Prism’s understanding of color is complete.

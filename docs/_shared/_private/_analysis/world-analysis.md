
## The overall structure : 
a shattered realm organized around the Prismatic Core’s crater, 
with six hue-aligned outer regions and Nullshade at the center 
as both literal endgame place and metaphysical wound. 

## Intended regional loop: 
each zone has its own city, hazards, traversal logic, artifact hooks, 
and a Prism Gate relationship to the Core. 

## high level regional design: 
- Huevale = living growth, archives, adaptive vegetation.
- Vireya = war memory, heat, blood, battlefield ritual.
- Iriseld = sky/storm grief, sound, reflection, floating geometry.
- Myrrala = identity distortion, dream logic, glamour.
- Luxeran = radiance, mirage, persuasion, trade, judgment.
- Auralith = residue, ruin, soul-binding, fossil memory.
- Nullshade = absence, erased truth, contradiction, return.

The map concept is simple and effective: 
six outer regions arranged around a central Nullshade crater, 
with each zone expressing a dominant color-family and gameplay mood. 

the land itself was shaped by the shattering of the Prismatic Core, 
with shards embedding into the world and creating 
region-specific metaphysics, hazards, and cultures. 

That is a good foundation because it explains, in one gesture:
- why color is geographic,
- why color is political,
- why artifacts are region-bound,
- why traversal abilities are hue-linked,
- and why Nullshade is the thematic center. 

The NPC/artifact docs explain how each region’s culture 
metabolized its hue into tools, rituals, and institutions. 

---

## Each region has a distinct metaphysical rule: 
- Huevale: vegetation regrows and reacts.
- Vireya: heat, blood, and battle awaken dormant forces.
- Iriseld: wind and gravity alter access; memory rides weather.
- Myrrala: loadout changes the landscape.
- Luxeran: mirages falsify the map.
- Auralith: player action leaves ghosts behind.
- Nullshade: truth is spatially and mnemonically sealed.

Each region can teach the player a different epistemology.

---

## The Blue/Purple illusion split

- Blue = perception, clarity, memory, sound, storm logic; reveals and stabilizes.
- Purple = identity, falsehood, glamour, dream logic; alters and rewrites. 

- Iriseld’s illusions are grief-, sound-, and weather-linked, 
- Myrrala’s illusions are beauty-, self-, and dream-linked. 

That distinction should remain canonical.

---

Treat The Bound consistently as either witnesses frozen by the Mask, 
or entities caught in failed coherence—not different explanations in different places. 

---

## The center-periphery model

Having six saturated outer regions around a desaturated core 
is very strong symbolically and spatially. 

It immediately communicates the project’s core conflict: 
color radiates outward from a broken center.

# 👹 Complete Bestiary Draft

## Overview
This document is the canonical bestiary reference for the shattered realms. It consolidates the regional enemy rosters, systemic encounter language, artifact-linked counterplay, and endgame terminology into one structure.

The creatures of this world are not random fauna. They are products of color-law, memory pressure, ritual failure, environmental corruption, and metaphysical fracture. Every region produces enemies according to its own logic:
- **Huevale** grows ambushers, tricksters, and living archive predators.
- **Vireya** produces war echoes, oath-bound revenants, and ritualized violence.
- **Iriseld** creates storm-memory horrors, traversal punishers, and reflective hunters.
- **Myrrala** blooms glamour predators, mimic selves, and identity distorters.
- **Luxeran** shapes mirage hunters, radiant constructs, and motive-reading predators.
- **Auralith** calcifies memory into trial specters, residue parasites, and unfinished dead.
- **Nullshade** manifests contradiction, anti-coherence, reflections, and the Wound’s immune responses.
- **Unbound entities** move across regions when history, hue instability, or narrative triggers override local law.

This bestiary treats every enemy as both:
1. a **combat encounter**, and
2. a **piece of world logic**.

---

## Canonical Enemy Entry Format
Each enemy entry is organized around the same fields:
- **Role** — what kind of encounter it is
- **Tags** — systemic shorthand for balancing and puzzle design
- **Visual** — concise visual identity
- **Behavior** — how it fights or pressures the player
- **Counterplay** — its primary weaknesses, reveal logic, or alternate solution
- **Notes** — lore relevance, artifact interaction, or production notes when useful

---

## Canonical Encounter Tags
Use these tags consistently across future enemy documentation.

### Primary Archetype Tags
- `DRAINER` — drains Mneme, HUD function, hue stability, or memory access
- `ILLUSIONIST` — uses glamour, false terrain, mimicry, or deceptive presentation
- `CONSTRUCT` — artificial or semi-artificial guardian, golem, relic-engine, or ritual body
- `REVENANT` — dead, bound, residual, oath-tethered, or grief-powered undead form
- `TRAPPER` — lures, roots, webs, seals routes, or punishes movement
- `REFLECTION` — mirrors actions, uses delayed movements, or depends on surfaces/images
- `META` — reads player history, choices, build, or narrative state directly

### Common Secondary Tags
- `ECHOABLE`
- `MIRROR_BOUND`
- `MNEME_DRAINER`
- `TRUTH_GATED`
- `SOUND_WEAK`
- `FIRE_WEAK`
- `COBALT_WEAK`
- `VIOLET_WEAK`
- `ARTIFACT_GATED`
- `NONVIOLENT_OPTION`
- `NULLSHADE_EXPOSURE`

## Countermeasure Families
Across all regions, enemy counterplay falls into a handful of canonical systems:

### Hue Counters
- **Crimson**: burns plant matter, overwhelms rot, bursts through suppression, but can enrage fire-reactive enemies
- **Cobalt**: reveals hidden forms, stabilizes distortion, chills constructs, breaks signal-noise illusions
- **Verdant**: roots fast movers, pacifies certain beasts, grows or restores terrain
- **Yellow / Aurelian**: exposes, blinds, reveals routes, shatters brittle radiance logic
- **Violet**: breaks glamour, reflects deception, destabilizes dream predators
- **Brown / Earthen**: reveals residue, grounds instability, interacts with memory traces and dead matter

### Artifact and Tool Counters
- **Skobeloff Lens** — illusion and trap reveal
- **Limerick Flute** — spirit charm / bypass for selected Huevale threats
- **Thulian Mirror** — reflection truth, glamour break, boss reveal
- **Zaffre Vial** — hidden passage reveal; temporary answer to some Mneme-drainers
- **Cerulean Cloak** — storm / aerial survival
- **Payne’s Brush** — reveal or force reflected / invisible targets into legibility
- **Cinnabar Seal** — symbolic binding, especially against certain Vireyan trickster logic
- **Eburnean Dice** — fate-break or ritual counter in select Luxeran trials
- **Caput Mortuum Phylactery** — stabilizes selected alchemical / death-adjacent states
- **Bistre Lantern** — reveals prior events and certain residue-driven entities

### Behavioral Counters
- stand still
- interrupt casting
- change rhythm
- use silence / inaction
- speak truth or replay correct dialogue
- avoid light / seek shadow
- bait pathing
- refuse direct aggression

### Environmental Counters
- mirrored terrain
- cooled stone
- reflected light
- storm timing
- shadow arcs
- shrine or archive states
- puzzle-completed geometry

## Regional Bestiary

# 🟢 Huevale — The Verdant Frontier
A realm where nature overgrows memory, where alchemy breathes through every bloom, and where the line between life and illusion blurs beneath moss, pollen, and sap. Huevale’s enemies are playful, predatory, toxic, and strangely merciful. They are living systems gone cunning.

## Huevale Enemy Themes
- predatory beauty
- memory pollen and cognitive disruption
- root ambush and movement denial
- archive guardianship
- illusion as natural lure
- decay reborn through growth

### Sapborn Stalker
**Role:** Ambush predator  
**Tags:** `TRAPPER`, `FIRE_WEAK`  
**Visual:** Semi-humanoid creature of twisting bark, wet sap, and hooked root-limbs  
**Behavior:** Burrows through root systems and erupts beneath the player’s path. Camouflages perfectly when still. Prefers overgrown corridors and low-light groves.  
**Counterplay:** Weak to fire and severing effects. Can be exposed by trampling root tremors, area burns, or careful footing.  
**Notes:** One of Huevale’s signature "the forest is hunting you" enemies.

### Boglight Mimic
**Role:** Lure spirit  
**Tags:** `ILLUSIONIST`, `TRAPPER`, `VIOLET_WEAK`  
**Visual:** Floating marsh-light that blooms into a leeching bog spirit at close range  
**Behavior:** Mimics safe lights, dropped items, or distant route beacons. Pulls the player toward trap water, rot pits, or clustered enemies.  
**Counterplay:** Light-reveal, Cobalt reveal pulses, or Violet glamour-breaking. Can be baited into overcommitting if approached from reflected surfaces.  
**Notes:** Strong opener for teaching false navigation.

### Verdant Maw
**Role:** Stationary ambush plant  
**Tags:** `TRAPPER`, `ARTIFACT_GATED`, `NONVIOLENT_OPTION`  
**Visual:** Giant carnivorous blossom hidden among harmless flora or shrine gardens  
**Behavior:** Lures with illusory pickups, friendly silhouettes, or false NPC voices, then snaps shut.  
**Counterplay:** Stealth bypass, root-cutting, or charm with the **Limerick Flute**.  
**Notes:** Best used in sacred or archive-adjacent spaces where greed or urgency are punished.

### Sporekin
**Role:** Swarm irritant / status enemy  
**Tags:** `DRAINER`, `SOUND_WEAK`  
**Visual:** Child-sized fungal husks with soft moss faces and bioluminescent eyes  
**Behavior:** Release hallucinogenic spores that alter visuals, controls, and targeting clarity. Often appear in clusters.  
**Counterplay:** Sonic disruption, purification, or burning cloud zones before engagement.  
**Notes:** Excellent low-tier support enemy for making other Huevale encounters more dangerous.

### Thornwrithe Beast
**Role:** Fast pursuit predator  
**Tags:** `TRAPPER`, `FEEDS_ON(VERDANT)`  
**Visual:** Quadruped mass of thorn-vines and muscle, always bleeding bramble  
**Behavior:** Lunges in bursts and leaves entangling root trails. Especially dangerous in narrow forest paths.  
**Counterplay:** Earthen rooting, path denial, or vertical terrain use. Can be enraged by careless Crimson overuse.  
**Notes:** Good roaming predator for transitioning from ambient danger to chase pressure.

### Limeroot Trickster
**Role:** Mischief spirit / caster disruptor  
**Tags:** `ILLUSIONIST`, `TRUTH_GATED`  
**Visual:** Smiling root-spirit with elongated mask-face and twig fingers  
**Behavior:** Speaks in rhyme, creates mirrored decoys, interrupts spell timing, and misdirects with taunting clues.  
**Counterplay:** Spell interruption, truth-reveal effects, rhythm break, or forcing it to repeat itself.  
**Notes:** Encodes Huevale’s playful cruelty.

### Chartreuse Husk
**Role:** Corrupted alchemical undead  
**Tags:** `REVENANT`, `DRAINER`  
**Visual:** Monk-like corpse bloated with luminous acid and fungal-green pressure beneath the skin  
**Behavior:** Sprays corrosive confusion mists and leaves hazardous death puddles.  
**Counterplay:** Distance management, purifying effects, or shatter-before-burst tactics.  
**Notes:** Bridges Huevale ecology with failed body-preservation techniques.

### Grovebound Sentinel
**Role:** Shrine / ruin guardian  
**Tags:** `CONSTRUCT`, `ARTIFACT_GATED`, `NONVIOLENT_OPTION`  
**Visual:** Stone-and-root colossus fused into old temple masonry  
**Behavior:** Slow but relentless defender of sealed groves and overgrown archives. Resists most direct elemental pressure.  
**Counterplay:** Environmental puzzle resolution, archive restoration, or deactivation through local shrine logic.  
**Notes:** Best used as a "you do not own this place" gatekeeper.

### Fangbloom Wyrm
**Role:** Hidden field predator  
**Tags:** `TRAPPER`, `ARTIFACT_GATED`, `REFLECTION`  
**Visual:** Buried serpent whose floral lure opens only when prey approaches  
**Behavior:** Hides beneath blossom beds or sacred hedges, constricting or devouring from below.  
**Counterplay:** Reveal through **Skobeloff Lens**, reflected cues, or deliberate baiting with summoned decoys.  
**Notes:** Excellent rare predator in especially beautiful zones.

### Mossgrave Revenant
**Role:** Memory-poison knight  
**Tags:** `REVENANT`, `TRUTH_GATED`  
**Visual:** Armored corpse overgrown with grave moss, carrying dream-soaked rot  
**Behavior:** Spreads sleep-heavy poison and attacks in mournful, deliberate arcs.  
**Counterplay:** Light, memory attacks, or confronting the grave’s associated Echo truth.  
**Notes:** Huevale’s most direct link between reverence, burial, and vegetal corruption.

# 🔴 Vireya — The Ember Expanse
A land of blazing ruin, war-memory, and bloodbound ritual. Vireya’s enemies are forged from heat, oath, and unresolved conflict. They fight like ceremonies.

## Vireya Enemy Themes
- ritualized violence
- battlefield memory
- heat and explosion chains
- symbolic objects as weak points
- sacrifice as power source
- dueling as identity

### Ashwrought Colossus
**Role:** Siege construct  
**Tags:** `CONSTRUCT`, `FIRE/COOLED`  
**Visual:** Massive molten war-engine plated in black iron and magma-veined stone  
**Behavior:** Advances slowly while projecting heatwaves that tax stamina and positioning.  
**Counterplay:** Vent overload, cooled-earth timing, or striking exposed heat seams.  
**Notes:** Represents Vireya’s war made architectural.

### Crimson Echo
**Role:** Duel revenant  
**Tags:** `REVENANT`, `REFLECTION`, `ECHOABLE`  
**Visual:** Spectral duelist framed in looping red slash trails  
**Behavior:** Repeats and reflects recent attack rhythms, punishing impatience.  
**Counterplay:** Silence beats, rhythm pause, desync timing, or forcing a broken duel pattern.  
**Notes:** One of the clearest expression of Vireya’s "combat as memory."

### Fox Mirage
**Role:** Trickster predator  
**Tags:** `ILLUSIONIST`, `REFLECTION`, `ARTIFACT_GATED`  
**Visual:** Lean ember-coated fox spirit splitting into afterimages  
**Behavior:** Creates decoy rushes and heat mirage duplicates.  
**Counterplay:** Mirror-terrain reads, **Cinnabar Seal**, off-rhythm strikes, or forcing it across cooled stone.  
**Notes:** Vireya’s agile alternative to brute heat threats.

### Searblood Cultist
**Role:** Suicide zealot  
**Tags:** `REVENANT`, `TRAPPER`  
**Visual:** Fanatic wrapped in ritual burns and blood-soaked bindings  
**Behavior:** Self-ignites, charges, and explodes on death, often igniting terrain or altars.  
**Counterplay:** Crowd control, silence, forced detonation at distance, or thread-binding interruptions.  
**Notes:** Best used in clustered battlefields or shrine ambushes.

### Lava-Fused Behemoth
**Role:** Territorial brute  
**Tags:** `CONSTRUCT`, `TRAPPER`  
**Visual:** Obsidian giant with molten seams and a soft vent-line across the back  
**Behavior:** Smashes terrain and corners players near lava.  
**Counterplay:** Pathing manipulation onto cooled routes; strike weak vent while turned or staggered.  
**Notes:** Works best where terrain state matters.

### Red Widow
**Role:** Mirage assassin  
**Tags:** `ILLUSIONIST`, `TRAPPER`  
**Visual:** Heat-split feminine silhouette with blade limbs and shimmer halo  
**Behavior:** Flickers between charm pressure and instant strike windows. May destabilize allied NPC behavior.  
**Counterplay:** Reveal under mirrored heat, silence, or punish during reappearance frame.  
**Notes:** Good elite for ritual or political assassination spaces.

### Bannerbound Revenant
**Role:** Standard-bearing war dead  
**Tags:** `REVENANT`, `TRUTH_GATED`  
**Visual:** Armored ghost lashed to its own banner, trailing lesser shades  
**Behavior:** Summons and sustains spectral allies while the banner stands.  
**Counterplay:** Seize, burn, or desecrate the banner; force banner separation.  
**Notes:** A key symbolic-object enemy.

### Glasslash Serpent
**Role:** Heat-born constrictor  
**Tags:** `REFLECTION`, `SOUND_WEAK`  
**Visual:** Serpentine body of lava-glass and internal ember veins  
**Behavior:** Darts through molten or glassed terrain, retaliates with shard bursts when struck poorly.  
**Counterplay:** Sonic disruption, vibration, or shatter timing after directional bait.  
**Notes:** Likely tied to battlefield betrayal sites and shardfall scars.

### Funeral Dancer
**Role:** Ritual execution duelist  
**Tags:** `REVENANT`, `REFLECTION`  
**Visual:** Veiled slasher inscribing burning sigils while moving with ceremonial grace  
**Behavior:** Marks delayed blast zones and punishes panic movement.  
**Counterplay:** Read step pattern, desync, or interrupt dance cadence.  
**Notes:** Strong in enclosed ceremonial arenas.

### Bloodroot Effigy
**Role:** Martyr guardian  
**Tags:** `CONSTRUCT`, `NONVIOLENT_OPTION`, `TRUTH_GATED`  
**Visual:** Bone-and-root shrine effigy built from sacrifice remains  
**Behavior:** Punishes direct aggression and escalates the more violently it is challenged.  
**Counterplay:** Puzzle-state shutdown, stealth bypass, or symbolic resolution.  
**Notes:** Exemplifies Vireya’s "not every war object wants a fight" design.

# 🔵 Iriseld — The Tidespire Reach
A region of storm-memory, aerial grief, and reflective danger. Iriseld enemies punish loss of orientation and reward composure.

## Iriseld Enemy Themes
- air and ledge denial
- storm-triggered aggression
- memory-water and charge drain
- reflections and delayed movement
- weather-linked traversal hostility
- grief as distortion

### Glassborne Echo
**Role:** Reflection phantom  
**Tags:** `REFLECTION`, `ECHOABLE`, `MIRROR_BOUND`  
**Visual:** Broken-glass humanoid phantom that moves with delayed mimicry  
**Behavior:** Mirrors the player’s movement and attack rhythm with a lag, then punishes repetition.  
**Counterplay:** Rhythm disruption, terrain change, or exploit delay windows. Often resistant to pure illusion effects.  
**Notes:** Strong candidate for illusion-immune category.

### Skycall Wraith
**Role:** Aerial disabler  
**Tags:** `DRAINER`, `TRAPPER`  
**Visual:** Long-limbed drifting spirit whose mouth opens into a wind-splitting scream  
**Behavior:** Shriek disables gliding, lifts, or rhythm movement; attempts to pull players into bad airspace.  
**Counterplay:** Grounded heavy strikes, anti-air timing, or storm shelter use.  
**Notes:** Signature traversal punishment enemy.

### Abyssal Current
**Role:** Mneme-draining serpent  
**Tags:** `DRAINER`, `ECHOABLE`  
**Visual:** Water-memory serpent visible only in surges, fog, or moonlit tide  
**Behavior:** Proximity drains Mneme and disrupts recollection.  
**Counterplay:** **Zaffre Vial**, Cobalt/Earthen Echo restoration, or banishment timing near revealed water seams.  
**Notes:** Core example of the Mneme-drainer family.

### Stormwrit Hound
**Role:** Shock predator  
**Tags:** `TRAPPER`, `WEAK_TO(COBALT)`  
**Visual:** Lean beast made of cloud-wire muscle and plasma bite-lines  
**Behavior:** Flash-teleports during storms and punishes overextended movement.  
**Counterplay:** Harmonic resonance, grounded bait, anti-teleport timing.  
**Notes:** Strong weather-trigger encounter.

### Sorrowwing
**Role:** Grief lure  
**Tags:** `TRAPPER`, `ILLUSIONIST`  
**Visual:** Long-feathered avian spirit leaving streamers of pale blue sorrow-light  
**Behavior:** Pulls the player toward edges, false comfort points, or unsafe glides.  
**Counterplay:** Cloak/artifact protection, anchor skills, or break charm with sound timing.  
**Notes:** Should be used sparingly for maximum emotional effect.

### Tempest Marionette
**Role:** Storm puppet duelist  
**Tags:** `CONSTRUCT`, `REFLECTION`  
**Visual:** Lightning-strung puppet suspended between storm glyphs  
**Behavior:** Executes rapid aerial combos and strike-lines tied to storm charge.  
**Counterplay:** Surge overload, grounding attacks, or collapse its charge anchors.  
**Notes:** Ideal elite for towers and exposed bridges.

### Whisperglass Wyrm
**Role:** Reflective burrower  
**Tags:** `REFLECTION`, `ARTIFACT_GATED`, `MIRROR_BOUND`  
**Visual:** Long skyglass serpent slipping through reflective terrain  
**Behavior:** Burrows under mirrored floors or glass flats, surfacing only when perception is destabilized.  
**Counterplay:** **Payne’s Brush**, Mind Echo under glass, or reflected reveal.  
**Notes:** Strong artifact-linked predator.

### Tideworn Apostle
**Role:** Drowned scholar revenant  
**Tags:** `REVENANT`, `DRAINER`  
**Visual:** Waterlogged liturgist wrapped in soaked script and static-blue prayer thread  
**Behavior:** Scrambles spell targeting and casts sorrow-based distortion zones.  
**Counterplay:** Silence, fire, or interrupt during chant.  
**Notes:** Connects religious memory to sea loss.

### Feathered Null
**Role:** Traversal denial entity  
**Tags:** `META`, `TRAPPER`, `NULLSHADE_EXPOSURE`  
**Visual:** Pale avian-like absence-form with unreal wing geometry  
**Behavior:** Denies movement options, punishes gliding, and seems to erase confidence in vertical navigation itself.  
**Counterplay:** Traversal-memory combination, Verdant anchoring, or special anti-null logic.  
**Notes:** Bridges Iriseld into Nullshade leak content.

### Mirrorwake Duelist
**Role:** Reflection boss duelist  
**Tags:** `REFLECTION`, `MIRROR_BOUND`, `META`  
**Visual:** Swordswoman manifested across multiple reflective surfaces at once  
**Behavior:** Fights through reflections rather than occupying one stable body.  
**Counterplay:** Break or neutralize local mirrors, force a dual-reflection state, or collapse the arena’s image logic.  
**Notes:** No final sprite yet in source materials.

# 🟣 Myrrala — The Blooming Veil
A dream-saturated region of glamour, false authorship, and selfhood under pressure. Myrrala enemies are intimate, playful, and invasive.

## Myrrala Enemy Themes
- identity mimicry
- dream logic and emotional coercion
- rhythm and performance combat
- UI unreliability
- vow and dialogue memory
- terrain rewriting through glamour

### Doppelshade
**Role:** Player-mirror predator  
**Tags:** `REFLECTION`, `META`, `VIOLET_WEAK`  
**Visual:** Near-perfect illusory copy of the player, always just slightly too graceful  
**Behavior:** Predicts or mimics recent inputs; attacks before the player expects their own animation logic to resolve.  
**Counterplay:** Indirect light, mirror reveal, off-rhythm choices, or Glamour Pulse.  
**Notes:** Excellent "you are being read" encounter.

### Lilyborn Mirage
**Role:** Dream loop spirit  
**Tags:** `ILLUSIONIST`, `TRUTH_GATED`  
**Visual:** Delicate humanoid woven from lily mist and pale perfume vapor  
**Behavior:** Traps the player in memory loops, repeating soft emotional cues until they choose incorrectly.  
**Counterplay:** Rhythm or dialogue play, contradiction recognition, or refusing the comforting answer.  
**Notes:** Strong emotional rather than purely mechanical threat.

### Chronopollen Sprite
**Role:** Temporal harrier  
**Tags:** `ILLUSIONIST`, `TRAPPER`  
**Visual:** Tiny fey mote trailing luminescent time-pollen  
**Behavior:** Causes local time hiccups, delayed inputs, or trap windows.  
**Counterplay:** Stasis capture, quick burst clear, or pattern isolation before other enemies join.  
**Notes:** Useful support enemy in mixed glamour fights.

### Bloomveil Widow
**Role:** Glamour spider  
**Tags:** `ILLUSIONIST`, `TRAPPER`, `VIOLET_WEAK`  
**Visual:** Elegant spider-figure veiled in perfume silk and mirrored thread  
**Behavior:** Charms, relocates, and lashes through silk gates while masking her true position.  
**Counterplay:** Mirror reveal, silence, or Glamour Pulse to collapse the web-layer.  
**Notes:** A canonical illusion-vulnerable predator.

### Fandancer Fragment
**Role:** Performance duelist  
**Tags:** `REFLECTION`, `TRUTH_GATED`  
**Visual:** Splinter of a lost dancer trailing petal-light in sharp rhythmic bursts  
**Behavior:** Uses beat-synced attacks and punishes being pulled into its tempo.  
**Counterplay:** Desynchronization, rhythm break, or hostile beat inversion.  
**Notes:** Good bridge between scene design and enemy design.

### Razzflare Imp
**Role:** UI saboteur  
**Tags:** `ILLUSIONIST`, `DRAINER`, `SOUND_WEAK`  
**Visual:** Tiny chaotic glamour creature hurling bright trick-sparks  
**Behavior:** Briefly warps HUD order, swaps bars, or inverts camera expectation.  
**Counterplay:** Music / rhythmic pulse, quick clear, or anti-illusion burst.  
**Notes:** Best in packs or paired with higher-threat glamours.

### Heliotropic Lurker
**Role:** Twilight stalker  
**Tags:** `TRAPPER`, `FEEDS_ON(LIGHT)`  
**Visual:** Vine-and-shadow predator drawn to strong illumination  
**Behavior:** Blind to motion in deep shade but highly aggressive around sunrise, direct light, or player-borne radiance.  
**Counterplay:** Use shadow intelligently, dim your own presence, or bait it into overexposed lunges.  
**Notes:** One of Myrrala’s clearest environmental predators.

### Thulian Vowkeeper
**Role:** Oath judge  
**Tags:** `META`, `TRUTH_GATED`, `NONVIOLENT_OPTION`  
**Visual:** Crystal knight formed from reflective oath-shards  
**Behavior:** Grows stronger based on broken dialogue promises or unkept vows.  
**Counterplay:** Honesty, confession, illusion trickery, or dialogue-state resolution.  
**Notes:** One of the strongest lore-to-mechanic enemies in the setting.

### Glittering Grimoire
**Role:** Spell-history mimic  
**Tags:** `META`, `ILLUSIONIST`, `TRUTH_GATED`  
**Visual:** Floating sentient spellbook encrusted in gleaming runes  
**Behavior:** Recasts phantom versions of the player’s own spell history.  
**Counterplay:** Counter-rhythm, feedback loop, forced close-state, or anti-glyph interruption.  
**Notes:** High-concept enemy that reinforces authored identity.

### Veilshift Matron
**Role:** Boss illusionist  
**Tags:** `ILLUSIONIST`, `META`, `TRUTH_GATED`, `MIRROR_BOUND`  
**Visual:** Grand floral matron whose garden determines path, scale, and arena orientation  
**Behavior:** Shifts terrain mid-fight, teleports the player subtly during actions, and hides her true body behind spatial falsehoods.  
**Counterplay:** Dialogue clue interpretation, **Thulian Mirror**, "true map" reveal, or forcing the arena to admit a single reality.  
**Notes:** Signature Myrrala boss-class encounter.

# 🤎 Auralith — The Dimming Hollow
A region of ash, residue, failed preservation, and truth trapped in remains. Auralith enemies are heavy with moral and mnemonic pressure.

## Auralith Enemy Themes
- residue and unfinished dead
- past-self hostility
- fear and confession pressure
- soul-binding and alchemical instability
- witness logic
- judgment rather than predation

### Memory Husk
**Role:** Past-self shell  
**Tags:** `META`, `ECHOABLE`, `TRUTH_GATED`  
**Visual:** Hollow body carrying gestures, skills, or equipment traces from earlier player states  
**Behavior:** Mirrors prior play patterns or relic dependence.

# ⚫ Nullshade — The Colorless Core
Use **Nullshade V2** terminology as canonical. Nullshade enemies are not "monsters" in the ordinary sense. They are reflections, contradictions, remnants, and immune responses of the Wound.

## Nullshade Enemy Themes
- anti-coherence
- self-recognition as danger
- nonviolent or truth-based resolution
- silence and UI blanking
- reflection and contradiction
- "the Wound fights back"

### Prism Unform
**Role:** Hue-collapse entity  
**Tags:** `META`, `REFLECTION`, `NULLSHADE_EXPOSURE`  
**Visual:** Glitching humanoid outline cycling unstable hue remnants that never settle  
**Behavior:** Mimics unlocked player abilities in incorrect sequence, turning mastery into noise.  
**Counterplay:** Restraint, hue refusal, or forcing a stable truth state instead of racing its mimicry.  
**Notes:** Feels like a precursor to full endgame reflection fights.

### The Bound
**Role:** Witness-construct  
**Tags:** `CONSTRUCT`, `TRUTH_GATED`, `NONVIOLENT_OPTION`  
**Visual:** Frozen, featureless stone figures mid-movement, accusation, or defense  
**Behavior:** Slow, heavy, delayed strikes; feel less like active attackers than reality’s clenched memory.  
**Counterplay:** Memory/dialogue resolution, truth response, or refusal to escalate.  
**Notes:** They are the Mask made physical.

### Nullshade Echo
**Role:** Player reflection  
**Tags:** `REFLECTION`, `META`, `ECHOABLE`  
**Visual:** Fractured replay of the player’s current hue state  
**Behavior:** Copies current combat style and punishes stable repetition.  
**Counterplay:** Fade in and out of hue activation, destabilize sync, or use unresolved dialogue lines to break its loop.  
**Notes:** Central Nullshade midboss / elite logic.

### Fractal Shardling
**Role:** Recursive fragment swarm  
**Tags:** `CONSTRUCT`, `TRAPPER`, `SOUND_WEAK`  
**Visual:** Splitting prism fragments with impossible geometry  
**Behavior:** Divides on poor hits and rewrites local terrain on collapse.  
**Counterplay:** Controlled damage, sound disruption, or preventing recursive multiplication.  
**Notes:** Good for teaching "not every hit is progress."

### Wound Remnant
**Role:** Prism-shattering aftermath entity  
**Tags:** `DRAINER`, `NULLSHADE_EXPOSURE`  
**Visual:** Shard-riddled construct trailing absence-light and silent hum  
**Behavior:** Causes sensory fadeouts, UI blanking, and proximity dread.  
**Counterplay:** Earthen anchoring, truth sequence, or artifact-based coherence stabilization.  
**Notes:** Should always feel like the environment is failing with it.

### Shatterglass Choir
**Role:** Saturation-draining cluster  
**Tags:** `DRAINER`, `SOUND_WEAK`, `TRUTH_GATED`  
**Visual:** Childlike broken-hue cluster singing in cutting crystal harmonics  
**Behavior:** Drains saturation and destabilizes the player through layered chorus logic.  
**Counterplay:** Silence or defeat in correct color-loss order.  
**Notes:** Great for ritual setpiece encounters.

### Null-Root
**Role:** Geometry vine  
**Tags:** `TRAPPER`, `NULLSHADE_EXPOSURE`, `NONVIOLENT_OPTION`  
**Visual:** Rootless void tendrils growing along impossible angles  
**Behavior:** Uses anti-growth geometry to bind movement and invert expectations of organic danger.  
**Counterplay:** Self-sacrificial interaction, inversion logic, or anti-hue resolution.  
**Notes:** Important symbolic inversion of Huevale-style root life.

### Silence Incarnate
**Role:** Suppression entity  
**Tags:** `DRAINER`, `META`, `NONVIOLENT_OPTION`  
**Visual:** Almost invisible vertical absence with a gaze you feel before you see  
**Behavior:** Disables casting, dodge language, and expressive play while it maintains line of sight.  
**Counterplay:** Break gaze, move indirectly, use silence as a tool rather than resisting it.  
**Notes:** One of Nullshade’s purest "anti-game" enemies.

### Memory Leech
**Role:** HUD parasite  
**Tags:** `DRAINER`, `META`, `ECHOABLE`  
**Visual:** Insectoid crystal parasite with mnemonic mandibles  
**Behavior:** Latches onto UI, remaps buttons, hides prompts, and steals operational memory.  
**Counterplay:** Trigger true memories, collected lore, or Memory Echo logic.  
**Notes:** Very strong systemic enemy if used sparingly.

### Tapestry Watcher (Nullshade Form)
**Role:** Broken possibility-weaver  
**Tags:** `META`, `TRUTH_GATED`, `REFLECTION`  
**Visual:** Flickering watcher existing across multiple points in space at once  
**Behavior:** Reacts to choices not taken, skipped quests, or abandoned truths.  
**Counterplay:** Choose a new "first decision" and overwrite a prior truth.  
**Notes:** Canonical evolved echo of the Auralith form.

### The Seventh Hue
**Role:** Mythic paradox entity  
**Tags:** `META`, `TRUTH_GATED`, `NONVIOLENT_OPTION`  
**Visual:** Unstable presence that mirrors nothing and names nothing  
**Behavior:** Attempts to invert the player’s core attributes and categorical assumptions.  
**Counterplay:** Truth prompt input, riddle completion, or late optional puzzle path.  
**Notes:** Optional mythic enemy hidden behind correct Nullshade riddle performance.

### The Colorless Shape
**Role:** Pre-boss elite mirror  
**Tags:** `META`, `REFLECTION`, `NULLSHADE_EXPOSURE`  
**Visual:** Humanoid shifting mirror reflecting current loadout and hue alignment  
**Behavior:** Uses the player’s own build, weapons, and actions against them in distorted form.  
**Counterplay:** Untag / unbind hue windows, inconsistency exploitation, or anti-mimic restraint.  
**Notes:** Final self before the Core.

## Canonical Notes for Future Expansion
- Use **Mneme** as the spendable resource term in future revisions; reserve **Memory Echo** for diegetic replay/puzzle scenes.
- Use **Prism Wound** only; remove older "Core Wound" phrasing.
- When outside the Core region, say **Nullshade intrusion** or **Nullshade exposure**.
- Keep **Blue** and **Purple** illusion logic distinct:
  - Blue reveals, stabilizes, de-noises
  - Purple glamours, masks, rewrites
- Keep artifact-linked counterplay explicit in boss or elite encounters; it is one of the project’s strongest system identities.

## Bestiary Summary
This bestiary is built around a simple principle:

**Enemies are the world arguing with the player.**

Each one expresses a regional truth, a historical wound, or a flaw in the way hue, memory, and identity have been separated from one another. The strongest encounters do not simply test damage output. They test whether the player understands what kind of lie they are standing inside—and what kind of truth can break it.
